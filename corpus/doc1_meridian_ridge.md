## SECTION 1. SUMMARY OF PRINCIPAL TERMS

The following summary is qualified in its entirety by the more detailed information appearing elsewhere in this Memorandum and by the terms of the Limited Partnership Agreement. In the event of any inconsistency between this summary and the Limited Partnership Agreement, the Limited Partnership Agreement shall control.

### 1.1 The Partnership

Meridian Ridge Global Opportunities Fund, L.P. (the "Partnership" or the "Fund") is a limited partnership organized under the Delaware Revised Uniform Limited Partnership Act on September 8, 2024. The Partnership has been formed to engage in the speculative trading of a broad range of financial instruments, including but not limited to listed equities, equity derivatives, sovereign and corporate debt securities, currencies, exchange-traded futures, and over-the-counter derivative instruments.

The Partnership operates as a "master-feeder" structure. Substantially all of the assets of the Partnership are invested in Meridian Ridge Global Opportunities Master Fund, Ltd., a Cayman Islands exempted company (the "Master Fund"). Offshore and tax-exempt investors may invest through Meridian Ridge Global Opportunities Offshore Fund, Ltd., a Cayman Islands exempted company, which also invests substantially all of its assets in the Master Fund.

### 1.2 The General Partner and the Investment Manager

The general partner of the Partnership is Meridian Ridge GP, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (the "General Partner"). The investment manager of the Partnership and the Master Fund is Meridian Ridge Capital Management, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company registered as an investment adviser with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (the "Investment Manager").

The Investment Manager was founded in 2019 and, as of December 31, 2024, managed approximately $1.4 billion in regulatory assets under management across the Partnership and two separately managed accounts.

### 1.3 Investment Objective

The investment objective of the Partnership is to generate attractive risk-adjusted returns with low correlation to broad equity indices over a full market cycle, principally through a global macro and relative value strategy. There can be no assurance that the Partnership will achieve its investment objective, and an investment in the Partnership involves a substantial degree of risk, including the risk of total loss.

## SECTION 3. INVESTMENT PROGRAM

### 3.1 Strategy Overview

The Investment Manager pursues a discretionary global macro strategy complemented by relative value positions in rates and credit. The strategy is implemented across four principal sleeves:

**Directional Macro.** Expressions of top-down views on growth, inflation and policy, implemented primarily through liquid futures, foreign exchange forwards and index options.

**Rates Relative Value.** Curve, basis and swap spread trades in G10 sovereign markets, generally implemented on a duration-neutral basis.

**Credit Relative Value.** Long/short positioning in corporate credit indices, single-name credit default swaps and cash bonds, with an emphasis on capital structure arbitrage.

**Opportunistic.** Event-driven and dislocation-driven positions, including sovereign distressed situations, which may be less liquid than the balance of the portfolio.

### 3.2 Leverage

The Partnership may employ leverage without limitation, whether through margin borrowing, repurchase agreements, securities lending, or the inherent leverage embedded in derivative instruments. The Investment Manager targets gross exposure in the range of 300% to 700% of net asset value under normal market conditions, though actual exposure may be materially higher or lower. Gross notional exposure is not a reliable indicator of economic risk given the prevalence of offsetting positions.

### 3.3 Concentration

The Investment Manager does not maintain formal concentration limits at the individual position level. As a matter of internal policy, the Investment Manager generally seeks to limit the estimated stress loss attributable to any single thematic exposure to 8% of net asset value. This policy may be modified or waived at any time without notice to Limited Partners.

### 3.4 Illiquid Investments and Side Pockets

The Partnership may designate certain investments as "Special Investments" and allocate them to a side pocket account. Special Investments may not exceed 15% of the Partnership's net asset value, measured at the time of designation. Interests attributable to Special Investments are not redeemable until the relevant Special Investment is realized or re-designated as a liquid investment by the General Partner.

## SECTION 4. FEES AND EXPENSES

### 4.1 Management Fee

The Partnership pays the Investment Manager a management fee (the "Management Fee") calculated and accrued monthly and payable quarterly in arrears.

With respect to **Class A Interests**, the Management Fee is charged at an annual rate of **1.75%** of the net asset value of each Limited Partner's capital account, determined before accrual of the Performance Allocation and before giving effect to any redemptions effective as of such date.

With respect to **Class B Interests**, the Management Fee is charged at an annual rate of **1.25%** of the net asset value of each Limited Partner's capital account, determined on the same basis.

The Management Fee is prorated for any partial period. The Investment Manager may, in its sole discretion, waive or reduce the Management Fee with respect to any Limited Partner, including any Limited Partner that is an affiliate or employee of the Investment Manager, without notice to or the consent of any other Limited Partner.

### 4.2 Performance Allocation

At the end of each Performance Period, the General Partner is entitled to receive a performance-based allocation (the "Performance Allocation") from each Limited Partner's capital account equal to **20% of the Net Profits** allocated to such capital account for such Performance Period, subject to the Loss Carryforward Account described in Section 4.3.

The Performance Allocation is **not** subject to a preferred return, hurdle rate or benchmark. Net Profits are calculated after deduction of the Management Fee and all Partnership expenses allocable to the relevant capital account.

The "Performance Period" is each calendar year, provided that a Performance Period shall also end with respect to any amount withdrawn or redeemed by a Limited Partner on the effective date of such withdrawal or redemption, and shall end with respect to the entire capital account upon the dissolution of the Partnership.

The Performance Allocation applies identically to Class A Interests and Class B Interests.

### 4.3 Loss Carryforward Account (High Water Mark)

The Partnership maintains a loss carryforward account (a "Loss Carryforward Account") with respect to each Limited Partner's capital account, which operates as a perpetual high water mark on a per-investor basis.

Net Losses allocated to a capital account in any Performance Period are debited to the Loss Carryforward Account. No Performance Allocation will be taken with respect to such capital account in any subsequent Performance Period until Net Profits have been allocated in an amount sufficient to restore the Loss Carryforward Account balance to zero. The Loss Carryforward Account is reduced proportionately upon any partial redemption, such that a redeeming Limited Partner does not carry forward loss recovery credit attributable to redeemed capital.

The high water mark does **not** reset upon any change in the Management Fee, transfer of Interests to an affiliate, or the passage of time.

### 4.4 Organizational Expenses

Organizational and initial offering expenses of the Partnership, the Offshore Fund and the Master Fund, which the Investment Manager estimates at approximately $675,000 in the aggregate, are borne by the Partnership and amortized over sixty (60) months from the commencement of operations. This amortization treatment is not in conformity with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles, and the Partnership's auditors may require an adjustment for financial reporting purposes.

### 4.5 Operating Expenses

The Partnership bears its own operating expenses, including but not limited to: brokerage commissions and other transaction costs; interest and financing charges; custodial and administration fees; research and market data expenses; legal, audit, tax preparation and regulatory filing fees; insurance premiums including director and officer liability coverage; expenses of the Partnership's risk and portfolio management systems; and extraordinary expenses including indemnification and litigation costs.

The Investment Manager bears its own overhead, including compensation of its personnel and rent for its offices.

## SECTION 5. REDEMPTIONS AND WITHDRAWALS

### 5.1 Lock-Up

**Class A Interests** are subject to a **twelve (12) month** lock-up period commencing on the date of each subscription. Each subscription is separately locked up on a "first-in, first-out" basis.

**Class B Interests** are subject to a **twenty-four (24) month** lock-up period commencing on the date of each subscription, on the same first-in, first-out basis.

### 5.2 Redemption Frequency

Following expiration of the applicable lock-up period, Interests may be redeemed as of the last business day of each calendar quarter (each, a "Redemption Date").

### 5.3 Redemption Notice

A Limited Partner wishing to redeem must deliver written notice to the Administrator not less than **ninety (90) calendar days** prior to the applicable Redemption Date. Redemption notices are irrevocable once delivered, except with the consent of the General Partner in its sole discretion.

### 5.4 Early Redemption Fee

A Limited Partner may, with the consent of the General Partner, redeem Interests prior to the expiration of the applicable lock-up period. Any such early redemption is subject to an early redemption fee of **3%** of the redemption proceeds, which fee is paid to the Partnership and not to the General Partner or the Investment Manager, and which therefore inures to the benefit of the remaining Limited Partners.

### 5.5 Gate

The General Partner may, in its sole discretion, limit aggregate redemptions effective as of any single Redemption Date to **20% of the Partnership's net asset value** as of such Redemption Date (the "Gate"). If redemption requests for any Redemption Date exceed the Gate, each redemption request will be reduced pro rata in proportion to the amount requested, and the unsatisfied portion of each request will be carried forward and given priority as of the next succeeding Redemption Date, subject to the Gate then in effect.

The Gate is an investor-level gate at the Partnership level and is applied after giving effect to any suspension under Section 5.6.

### 5.6 Suspension

The General Partner may suspend redemptions, suspend the calculation of net asset value, or suspend payment of redemption proceeds for the whole or any part of any period during which, in the reasonable judgment of the General Partner: (a) any exchange or market on which a substantial portion of the Partnership's investments are traded is closed other than for ordinary holidays, or trading thereon is restricted or suspended; (b) circumstances exist as a result of which it is not reasonably practicable to dispose of investments or to determine fairly the value of the Partnership's assets; or (c) a breakdown occurs in the means of communication normally employed in determining the value of the Partnership's assets.

### 5.7 Payment of Redemption Proceeds

The Partnership will generally pay not less than 90% of estimated redemption proceeds within thirty (30) calendar days following the applicable Redemption Date, with the balance, subject to audit adjustment, paid within thirty (30) calendar days following completion of the Partnership's annual audit for the fiscal year in which the redemption occurred.

### 5.8 Compulsory Redemption

The General Partner may compel the redemption of all or any portion of any Limited Partner's Interests at any time, for any reason or no reason, upon not less than five (5) business days' written notice.

## SECTION 10. VALUATION

### 10.1 Valuation Policy

Net asset value is calculated by the Administrator as of the last business day of each calendar month, and at such other times as the General Partner may determine, in accordance with the Partnership's written valuation policy.

Exchange-traded instruments are valued at the last reported sale price on the principal exchange. Over-the-counter derivatives are generally valued using a mid-market price derived from at least two independent dealer quotations or, where unavailable, from a recognized third-party pricing service.

### 10.2 Level 3 Assets and Manager Valuation

Where neither dealer quotations nor third-party pricing are available, or where the General Partner determines in good faith that available prices are not representative of fair value, instruments are valued at fair value as determined by the **Investment Manager** in good faith, subject to review by the Partnership's valuation committee.

The valuation committee consists of the Chief Operating Officer, the Chief Risk Officer and one non-affiliated member. **Because the Management Fee and the Performance Allocation are calculated on the basis of net asset value, the Investment Manager has an inherent conflict of interest in determining the fair value of Level 3 instruments.** Special Investments allocated to side pockets are valued at cost until a subsequent financing, realization or impairment event provides a basis for re-marking.

## SECTION 11. TAX CONSIDERATIONS

The Partnership intends to be treated as a partnership for U.S. federal income tax purposes and not as an association taxable as a corporation. Each Limited Partner will receive a Schedule K-1 reflecting its distributive share of the Partnership's income, gain, loss, deduction and credit.

The Partnership does not expect to make distributions. Limited Partners should anticipate an annual tax liability in respect of their allocable share of Partnership income notwithstanding the absence of any cash distribution, and should ensure they have sufficient liquidity from other sources to satisfy such liability.

The Partnership will use commercially reasonable efforts to furnish Schedule K-1 information to Limited Partners by **September 15** following the end of each fiscal year, and expects that Limited Partners will be required to file extensions of their personal and entity returns. Estimates sufficient to permit the calculation of quarterly estimated payments will be furnished on request.

Tax-exempt investors should be aware that the Partnership's use of leverage will generate unrelated business taxable income ("UBTI") that is attributable to debt-financed property. Tax-exempt U.S. investors are strongly encouraged to invest through the Offshore Fund, which is treated as a corporation for U.S. federal income tax purposes and is therefore expected to block UBTI.

## SECTION 13. SELECTED RISK FACTORS

*The following is a summary only. Prospective investors should review Appendix B for a fuller discussion.*

**Loss of Capital.** An investment in the Partnership is speculative and involves a high degree of risk. A Limited Partner may lose all or substantially all of its investment.

**Leverage.** The use of leverage magnifies both gains and losses and may result in losses exceeding the amount invested at the position level.

**Illiquidity of Interests.** There is no secondary market for Interests, transfers require General Partner consent, and redemption is restricted by lock-ups, notice periods and the Gate.

**Reliance on Key Personnel.** The Partnership depends substantially on Dr. Vasquez-Hartmann. The Key Man Event provision in Section 6.2 provides a redemption right but does not protect against loss of value.

**Counterparty Risk.** The Master Fund is exposed to the credit of its Prime Brokers and derivative counterparties, including with respect to rehypothecated assets.

**Valuation Risk.** As described in Section 10.2, the Investment Manager values Level 3 instruments and has a conflict of interest in doing so.

**Conflicts of Interest.** The Investment Manager manages separately managed accounts alongside the Partnership. Allocation of investment opportunities among the Partnership and such accounts is subject to the Investment Manager's allocation policy, and the Partnership may receive a smaller allocation of a desirable opportunity than it otherwise would.

**Regulatory Risk.** Changes in law or regulation, including with respect to position limits, margin requirements, and the treatment of carried interest, could materially and adversely affect the Partnership.
