## ARTICLE I — EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### 1.1 The Fund

Calder & Wyeth Private Credit Fund III, L.P. (the "Fund") is a closed-end, drawdown private investment fund organized as a limited partnership under the laws of the State of Delaware on November 3, 2024. The Fund is the third in a series of private credit funds sponsored by Calder & Wyeth Capital Partners, LLC (the "Sponsor" or the "Investment Adviser").

Unlike an open-end hedge fund, the Fund is a **closed-end vehicle with a finite life**. Limited Partners make binding capital commitments that are drawn down over time, and there are **no redemption rights, no withdrawal rights, and no liquidity** prior to distributions made in the ordinary course or the final liquidation of the Fund.

### 1.2 The General Partner

The general partner of the Fund is **C&W PC III GP, L.P.**, a Delaware limited partnership (the "General Partner"). The General Partner is controlled by the Sponsor.

### 1.3 Predecessor Funds

The Sponsor previously sponsored Calder & Wyeth Private Credit Fund I, L.P. (2018 vintage, $340 million of commitments) and Calder & Wyeth Private Credit Fund II, L.P. (2021 vintage, $760 million of commitments). Performance of predecessor funds is set forth in Appendix C and is not indicative of the results the Fund may achieve.

## ARTICLE II — TARGET SIZE AND COMMITMENTS

### 2.1 Target Fund Size

The Fund is seeking aggregate capital commitments of **$1,250,000,000**, with a hard cap of **$1,500,000,000**. The General Partner may, in its discretion, accept commitments in excess of the hard cap solely to accommodate commitments from the General Partner, the Sponsor and their respective affiliates and employees.

### 2.2 Minimum Commitment

The minimum capital commitment for a Limited Partner is **$10,000,000**. The General Partner may accept a lesser commitment from any investor in its sole discretion, and in particular expects to accept commitments of not less than $1,000,000 from individuals and family investment vehicles affiliated with the Sponsor's existing investor base.

### 2.3 Closings

The Fund held an initial closing on February 28, 2025. The General Partner may hold one or more subsequent closings until the date that is **eighteen (18) months** following the initial closing.

A Limited Partner admitted at a subsequent closing will be required to fund its pro rata share of prior capital contributions, together with an **equalization charge equal to 8% per annum** on the amount of such catch-up contributions, calculated from the date on which each such prior contribution was funded. Equalization charges are paid to the Fund for the benefit of existing Limited Partners and are not treated as capital contributions.

### 2.4 Capital Calls

Capital will be drawn down as needed upon not less than **ten (10) business days'** prior written notice. The General Partner does not expect any single capital call to exceed 25% of aggregate commitments.

### 2.5 Defaulting Partners

A Limited Partner that fails to fund a capital call when due and does not cure within ten (10) business days is subject to remedies including: forfeiture of up to **50%** of its capital account; forced sale of its interest; loss of voting rights; and interest on the unfunded amount at the lesser of 15% per annum or the maximum rate permitted by law.

## ARTICLE III — INVESTMENT STRATEGY

### 3.1 Strategy

The Fund will originate and acquire senior secured loans, unitranche facilities and, opportunistically, junior debt and structured credit instruments issued by middle-market companies domiciled principally in North America and Western Europe. Target borrowers generally have EBITDA between $15 million and $100 million.

The Fund expects to act as sole or lead lender in the majority of its transactions, and to negotiate financial maintenance covenants, information rights and board observation rights.

### 3.2 Diversification Limits

Without the consent of the Advisory Committee, the Fund will not:

(a) invest more than **10% of aggregate commitments** in the securities or obligations of any single portfolio company or group of affiliated portfolio companies;

(b) invest more than **25% of aggregate commitments** in any single industry, as classified by the Sponsor in good faith;

(c) invest more than **30% of aggregate commitments** in borrowers domiciled outside of the United States and Canada;

(d) invest more than **15% of aggregate commitments** in instruments that are junior to a first lien position at the time of investment; or

(e) invest in publicly traded equity securities other than as incidental to a debt investment, or as received in connection with a restructuring.

### 3.3 Fund-Level Leverage

The Fund may incur leverage through one or more subscription credit facilities and one or more asset-based credit facilities.

Subscription facility borrowings are limited to the lesser of 30% of aggregate unfunded commitments or the borrowing base, and any individual borrowing under a subscription facility must be repaid within **180 days**.

Asset-based leverage at the Fund level is limited to **1.25x** the Fund's net asset value. This limit is measured at the time of incurrence and a subsequent decline in net asset value will not constitute a breach.

### 3.4 Recycling

The General Partner may recycle proceeds attributable to the return of capital from investments realized within **twenty-four (24) months** of the date of investment, provided that aggregate contributions drawn from any Limited Partner shall not exceed **115% of its capital commitment**.

## ARTICLE IV — TERM AND INVESTMENT PERIOD

### 4.1 Investment Period

The "Investment Period" commences on the initial closing date and continues until the **fifth (5th) anniversary of the final closing date**, unless earlier terminated.

Following expiration of the Investment Period, the Fund may make follow-on investments in existing portfolio companies, complete transactions committed to prior to expiration, and draw capital for expenses, management fees and the repayment of Fund indebtedness.

### 4.2 Term of the Fund

The Fund shall continue until the **eighth (8th) anniversary of the final closing date** (the "Initial Term").

The General Partner may extend the Initial Term for up to **two (2) consecutive one-year periods**, the first with the consent of the Advisory Committee and the second with the consent of Limited Partners holding a majority in interest of aggregate commitments. Accordingly, the maximum term of the Fund is **ten (10) years** from the final closing date, subject to a further extension solely for the purpose of winding up, which shall not exceed twelve (12) months.

### 4.3 Suspension and Termination of the Investment Period

The Investment Period will be suspended automatically upon a Key Person Event, as described in Article VII. Limited Partners holding **two-thirds (66⅔%) in interest** of aggregate commitments may terminate the Investment Period at any time without cause, and Limited Partners holding **a majority in interest** may terminate the Investment Period for cause.

### 4.4 No Redemption Rights

**The Fund does not offer redemption or withdrawal rights of any kind.** There is no lock-up period as that term is used in open-end funds, because there is no mechanism by which a Limited Partner may redeem. There is no redemption notice period, no redemption frequency, and no gate provision, because no redemptions are permitted.

A Limited Partner seeking liquidity prior to the dissolution of the Fund must transfer its interest in a secondary transaction pursuant to Article X, which requires the consent of the General Partner.

## ARTICLE V — MANAGEMENT FEE

### 5.1 Rate and Basis

The Fund pays the Investment Adviser a management fee (the "Management Fee") payable **quarterly in advance**.

**During the Investment Period**, the Management Fee is charged at an annual rate of **1.50%** of each Limited Partner's **aggregate capital commitment**, whether or not drawn.

**Following expiration or termination of the Investment Period**, the Management Fee is charged at an annual rate of **1.50%** of each Limited Partner's **invested capital in unrealized portfolio investments**, determined at cost and reduced as investments are realized or written off.

### 5.2 Commitment-Based Discounts

The Management Fee rate is reduced for larger commitments on the following schedule, applied to the entire commitment on a "cliff" basis rather than marginally:

| Aggregate Commitment | Management Fee Rate |
|---|---|
| Less than $50,000,000 | 1.50% |
| $50,000,000 to $99,999,999 | 1.35% |
| $100,000,000 or greater | 1.25% |

An investor that committed to Calder & Wyeth Private Credit Fund II, L.P. receives an additional **10 basis point** reduction from the rate that would otherwise apply.

### 5.3 Fee Offset

**100%** of all transaction fees, origination fees, arrangement fees, amendment fees, commitment fees, monitoring fees, consulting fees, break-up fees and directors' fees received by the Investment Adviser or its affiliates from or with respect to portfolio companies of the Fund shall be applied to reduce the Management Fee otherwise payable, on a dollar-for-dollar basis, net of unreimbursed out-of-pocket expenses incurred in connection with the transaction giving rise to such fee.

Any offset amount that exceeds the Management Fee payable in a given quarter is carried forward and applied against Management Fees payable in subsequent quarters, and any amount remaining at dissolution is credited against amounts otherwise distributable to the General Partner.

### 5.4 Organizational Expenses

The Fund bears organizational and offering expenses, including placement agent fees, up to a cap of **$2,500,000**. Amounts in excess of the cap are borne by the Sponsor. Placement agent fees, if any, that are borne by the Fund reduce the Management Fee on a dollar-for-dollar basis.

## ARTICLE VI — DISTRIBUTIONS AND CARRIED INTEREST

### 6.1 Distribution Waterfall

Distributions in respect of each portfolio investment shall be apportioned among the Limited Partners and the General Partner and distributed in the following order of priority:

**First — Return of Capital.** 100% to the Limited Partners until each Limited Partner has received cumulative distributions equal to (i) its capital contributions used to fund such investment, plus (ii) its capital contributions used to fund the Management Fee, Fund expenses and any written-off or written-down investments allocable to it.

**Second — Preferred Return.** 100% to the Limited Partners until each Limited Partner has received a cumulative **preferred return of 7% per annum**, compounded annually, on the amounts described in the First tier.

**Third — General Partner Catch-Up.** **80% to the General Partner and 20% to the Limited Partners** until the General Partner has received 20% of the aggregate amount distributed under the Second tier and this Third tier.

**Fourth — Residual Split.** Thereafter, **80% to the Limited Partners and 20% to the General Partner**.

### 6.2 Carried Interest

The General Partner's share of distributions under the Third and Fourth tiers constitutes the "Carried Interest." The Carried Interest is **20%** of profits, subject to the 7% preferred return and the 80/20 catch-up described above.

The Carried Interest is calculated on a **whole-of-fund (European) basis**, not on a deal-by-deal basis. No Carried Interest will be distributed to the General Partner until Limited Partners have received distributions equal to all capital contributions made in respect of all investments, fees and expenses of the Fund, plus the preferred return thereon.

**There is no high water mark.** A high water mark is a feature of open-end funds that charge a periodic performance allocation on unrealized appreciation; the Fund charges Carried Interest only on realized distributions through the waterfall described above.

### 6.3 Clawback

Upon dissolution of the Fund, the General Partner shall return to the Fund, for distribution to the Limited Partners, an amount equal to the excess, if any, of (a) the aggregate Carried Interest distributions received by the General Partner, over (b) the aggregate Carried Interest the General Partner would have received had all distributions been made at the time of dissolution on an aggregate whole-of-fund basis.

The clawback obligation is:

(a) calculated on an **after-tax basis**, assuming the General Partner and its beneficial owners were subject to tax at the highest combined marginal federal, state and local rate applicable to an individual resident in New York, New York;

(b) **guaranteed severally, and not jointly**, by each individual beneficial owner of the General Partner that received Carried Interest distributions, in proportion to the amount received; and

(c) supported by an interim clawback test performed as of the **sixth anniversary of the final closing date**, and every two years thereafter, requiring the General Partner to return amounts that would be owed if the Fund were liquidated at then-current fair value.

### 6.4 Tax Distributions

The Fund will make tax distributions to the General Partner in respect of Carried Interest allocations at an assumed rate equal to the highest combined marginal rate described in Section 6.3(a). Tax distributions are treated as advances against future distributions and are subject to the clawback.

### 6.5 Distribution Timing

The Fund expects to distribute current income quarterly and realization proceeds within **ninety (90) days** of receipt. The General Partner may retain amounts reasonably required for Fund expenses, obligations and reserves.

A Key Person Event does not give rise to any redemption right, because the Fund does not permit redemptions.

### 7.4 Removal of the General Partner

Limited Partners holding **75% in interest** of aggregate commitments may remove the General Partner for cause. Limited Partners holding **80% in interest** may remove the General Partner without cause, in which case the removed General Partner retains Carried Interest in respect of investments made prior to removal, reduced by 25%.

## ARTICLE VIII — ADVISORY COMMITTEE

### 8.1 Composition

The General Partner shall establish an advisory committee (the "Advisory Committee") consisting of not fewer than five and not more than nine representatives of Limited Partners selected by the General Partner, with a preference for the largest Limited Partners and for Limited Partners representing a diversity of investor types.

### 8.2 Role

The Advisory Committee shall: review and approve conflicts of interest, including principal transactions and cross-trades between the Fund and other Sponsor vehicles; review valuations of portfolio investments not less than annually; approve any deviation from the diversification limits in Section 3.2; approve the first one-year extension of the Initial Term; and consider such other matters as the General Partner may refer to it.

The Advisory Committee **does not have authority over investment decisions** and owes no fiduciary duty to any Limited Partner. Approval by the Advisory Committee of a conflicted transaction is binding on all Limited Partners.

## ARTICLE X — TRANSFERS AND SIDE LETTERS

### 10.1 Transfers

No Limited Partner may transfer all or any portion of its interest without the prior written consent of the General Partner, which may be withheld in its sole discretion. The General Partner will not unreasonably withhold consent to a transfer to an affiliate of a Limited Partner that does not give rise to adverse tax, regulatory or ERISA consequences to the Fund.

Any transferee must make representations as to its status as an accredited investor and qualified purchaser and must assume the transferor's unfunded commitment.

### 10.2 Side Letters

The General Partner may enter into side letters with Limited Partners granting rights that differ from those set forth herein, including reduced Management Fee or Carried Interest, Advisory Committee seats, co-investment rights, enhanced reporting, excuse rights in respect of particular investments, and confidentiality accommodations for public pension investors.

### 10.3 Most Favored Nation Election

**The Fund provides a most favored nation ("MFN") election.** Within sixty (60) days following the final closing, the General Partner shall deliver to each Limited Partner a schedule summarizing the material terms granted in all side letters.

Each Limited Partner shall then have thirty (30) days to elect to receive the benefit of any such term, subject to the following commitment-tier restrictions:

| Limited Partner Commitment | MFN Scope |
|---|---|
| $100,000,000 or greater | May elect any side letter term |
| $50,000,000 to $99,999,999 | May elect terms granted to Limited Partners with commitments of $100,000,000 or less |
| Less than $50,000,000 | May elect terms granted to Limited Partners with commitments equal to or less than its own |

Economic terms (Management Fee and Carried Interest reductions), Advisory Committee seats, and capacity or co-investment priority rights are **excluded from the MFN election** at all commitment levels.

## ARTICLE XIII — SELECTED RISK FACTORS

**No Liquidity.** Limited Partners have no redemption rights and must hold their interests for the life of the Fund, which may extend to ten years or longer.

**Blind Pool.** Limited Partners will not have the opportunity to evaluate investments prior to their being made.

**Credit and Default Risk.** Middle-market borrowers are more likely to default than larger, rated issuers, and recoveries on defaulted loans are uncertain.

**Covenant Erosion.** Competition in middle-market direct lending has resulted in weaker covenant packages across the market, which may reduce the Fund's ability to intervene early in a deteriorating credit.

**Leverage.** The Fund's use of subscription and asset-based facilities magnifies losses and may accelerate capital calls in a stressed environment.

**Valuation of Illiquid Assets.** Loans held by the Fund are not traded on an exchange and are valued using models and judgment, subject to the process in Section 9.3.

**Conflicts of Interest.** The Sponsor manages other vehicles that pursue overlapping strategies, and allocation among them is subject to the Sponsor's allocation policy.

**Interest Rate Risk.** The Fund's loans are predominantly floating rate, and a decline in base rates would reduce current income.
