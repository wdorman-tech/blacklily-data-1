## PART ONE — ORIGINAL OFFERING MEMORANDUM (JUNE 1, 2023)

## SECTION 1. THE PARTNERSHIP

### 1.1 Organization

Ashgrove Special Situations Fund, L.P. (the "Partnership") is a limited partnership organized under the laws of the State of Delaware on January 19, 2023. The Partnership commenced operations on July 1, 2023.

The Partnership invests directly and does not operate as a feeder into a master fund. There is no offshore parallel vehicle. Non-U.S. and tax-exempt investors invest directly in the Partnership and should consult their own advisers regarding the consequences of doing so.

### 1.2 General Partner and Investment Manager

The general partner is **Ashgrove SS GP, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company. The investment manager is **Ashgrove Asset Management, LP**, a Delaware limited partnership registered as an investment adviser with the Securities and Exchange Commission (the "Investment Manager").

### 1.3 Investment Objective

The Partnership seeks to generate absolute returns through investment in corporate special situations, including post-reorganization equities, stressed and distressed credit, liquidations, litigation claims, and event-driven equity situations. The Partnership may take concentrated positions and may participate in creditors' committees and restructuring negotiations.

---

## SECTION 2. CLASSES OF INTERESTS

The Partnership offers two classes of limited partnership interests:

**Class F Interests** ("Founders Class") were offered exclusively during the period from July 1, 2023 through December 31, 2023 to investors subscribing not less than $5,000,000. The Founders Class is closed to new subscriptions. Existing Class F Limited Partners may make additional subscriptions into Class F.

**Class I Interests** ("Institutional Class") are offered on a continuing basis to qualifying investors and constitute the class principally offered by this Memorandum.

Except as expressly set forth herein, Class F and Class I Interests have identical rights, and participate pro rata in the profits and losses of the Partnership.

---

## SECTION 3. SUBSCRIPTIONS

### 3.1 Minimum Investment

The minimum initial subscription for Class I Interests is **$1,000,000**, and the minimum additional subscription is **$100,000**. The General Partner may waive either minimum in its sole discretion, subject to applicable investor eligibility requirements.

### 3.2 Eligibility

Subscribers must be both "accredited investors" under Rule 501(a) of Regulation D and "qualified clients" within the meaning of Rule 205-3 under the Investment Advisers Act of 1940.

### 3.3 Subscription Dates

Interests are offered as of the first business day of each calendar month, with subscription documents and funds due five business days in advance.

---

## SECTION 4. FEES

### 4.1 Management Fee

The Partnership pays the Investment Manager a management fee accrued monthly and payable quarterly in arrears at an annual rate of **2.00%** of the net asset value of each Limited Partner's capital account, determined prior to accrual of the Incentive Allocation.

The Management Fee rate is identical for Class F and Class I Interests.

### 4.2 Incentive Allocation

At the close of each Incentive Period, the General Partner receives an incentive allocation (the "Incentive Allocation") from each Limited Partner's capital account.

With respect to **Class F Interests**, the Incentive Allocation is **15% of Net New Appreciation**.

With respect to **Class I Interests**, the Incentive Allocation is **20% of Net New Appreciation**.

The "Incentive Period" is each calendar year, and also ends with respect to any redeemed amount as of the effective date of redemption.

### 4.3 Hurdle Rate

As set forth in the original Memorandum, the Incentive Allocation is **not subject to any hurdle rate, preferred return or benchmark**, for either Class F or Class I Interests. Net New Appreciation is measured on an absolute basis.

### 4.4 High Water Mark

The Partnership maintains a **perpetual high water mark** on a per-Limited-Partner, per-subscription basis. "Net New Appreciation" means the increase in the value of a capital account for an Incentive Period in excess of the highest value of such capital account as of the close of any prior Incentive Period (the "High Water Mark"), adjusted for subscriptions and redemptions.

The High Water Mark does not reset over time, does not reset upon a change in the Management Fee or Incentive Allocation rate, and does not reset upon a change of control of the Investment Manager. This provision applies identically to Class F and Class I Interests and has not been amended.

### 4.5 Expenses

The Partnership bears brokerage and transaction costs, interest and financing expense, administration and custody fees, research and data costs, legal and audit fees, insurance, regulatory filing fees, and expenses of participation in creditors' committees and restructuring processes, including fees of counsel and financial advisers retained by such committees where such fees are not borne by the estate.

The Investment Manager bears its own compensation and overhead.

---

## SECTION 5. REDEMPTIONS

### 5.1 Soft Lock-Up

Interests are **not subject to a hard lock-up**. Instead, Interests redeemed within **eighteen (18) months** of the date of the relevant subscription are subject to an early redemption charge of **2.00%** of the redemption proceeds, payable to the Partnership for the benefit of remaining Limited Partners.

Subscriptions are treated on a first-in, first-out basis for purposes of determining whether the eighteen-month period has elapsed. No early redemption charge applies to redemptions occurring more than eighteen months after the relevant subscription.

### 5.2 Redemption Frequency

Interests may be redeemed as of the last business day of each calendar quarter.

### 5.3 Redemption Notice

Written notice of redemption must be received by the Administrator not less than **sixty (60) calendar days** prior to the applicable redemption date.

### 5.4 Gate

The Partnership applies a redemption gate. The mechanics and thresholds of the gate are set forth in **Appendix A — Schedule of Redemption Limitations**, attached to this Memorandum.

### 5.5 Suspension

The General Partner may suspend redemptions and the determination of net asset value where, in its reasonable judgment, market conditions make orderly disposition of assets impracticable, or where a substantial portion of the Partnership's positions are subject to trading restrictions arising from the Partnership's participation in a restructuring process or receipt of material non-public information.

### 5.6 In-Kind Distributions

The General Partner may satisfy any redemption in whole or in part through a distribution in kind of securities, or through the issuance of a non-transferable liquidating interest in respect of positions that cannot be liquidated in an orderly manner. Liquidating interests bear no Management Fee and no Incentive Allocation and are distributed as the underlying positions are realized.

**Ingrid Solheim-Achebe**, Director of Research.

**Cormac Delahunty**, Chief Financial Officer and Chief Compliance Officer.

### 6.2 Investment Process

Mr. Brannigan-Tate has sole discretion over portfolio construction and position sizing. There is no investment committee and no formal vote is required for any investment decision.

### 6.3 Absence of Key Person Protection

Prospective investors should note that **the Partnership does not provide any key person provision, key man clause, or associated redemption right**. The departure, death, disability or incapacity of Mr. Brannigan-Tate, or of any other person, does not trigger any right of redemption, any suspension of the Partnership's activities, any notice obligation beyond that required by law, or any other remedy in favor of Limited Partners.

Limited Partners seeking key person protection must negotiate it individually by side letter. The Investment Manager has historically declined to grant such provisions.

### 6.4 General Partner Investment

**The General Partner has not committed, and does not commit, to maintain any minimum investment in the Partnership.** There is no required general partner commitment, no stated minimum dollar amount, and no stated minimum percentage of net asset value.

As of the date of this Memorandum, Mr. Brannigan-Tate and his family vehicles hold interests in the Partnership with an aggregate value of approximately $4,200,000, representing roughly 3.1% of the Partnership's net asset value. **This holding is disclosed as a matter of fact as of the date hereof and does not constitute a commitment.** It may be redeemed in whole or in part at any time, without notice to Limited Partners, subject only to the same redemption terms applicable to Limited Partners generally.

## SECTION 10. VALUATION

Net asset value is determined monthly by the Administrator. Liquid exchange-traded positions are valued at last sale. Bank debt and other loan positions are valued using the average of available dealer quotations, with a minimum of two quotations required.

Post-reorganization equities and other positions for which no market quotation is available are valued at fair value as determined by the Investment Manager in good faith. Litigation claims and similar contingent assets are held at **the lower of cost or estimated recoverable value**, and are not written up in advance of realization except upon a definitive, non-appealable adjudication or a binding settlement agreement.

---

## SECTION 11. REPORTING

Limited Partners receive estimated monthly performance within seven business days of month end, final capital account statements within twenty business days of month end, a quarterly letter, and audited annual financial statements within 120 days of fiscal year end. Position-level transparency is not provided.

## SECTION 12. SELECTED RISK FACTORS

**Concentration.** The Partnership may hold a small number of large positions. A loss in any single position may materially affect the Partnership.

**Illiquidity of Investments.** Distressed and post-reorganization securities may trade infrequently, in small size, and at wide bid-offer spreads.

**No Key Person Protection.** As described in Section 6.3, Limited Partners have no remedy upon the departure of Mr. Brannigan-Tate.

**Restrictions Arising from Material Non-Public Information.** Participation in creditors' committees may result in the Partnership's receipt of material non-public information, restricting its ability to trade affected securities for extended periods.

**Valuation Risk.** The Investment Manager values positions for which no market quotation exists and has a conflict of interest in doing so.

**Litigation Risk.** The Partnership may become a party to litigation arising from restructuring processes, the costs of which are borne by the Partnership.

This Supplement No. 1 ("Supplement") amends the Confidential Offering Memorandum of Ashgrove Special Situations Fund, L.P. dated June 1, 2023 (the "Original Memorandum"). Capitalized terms used but not defined herein have the meanings given in the Original Memorandum.

**In the event of any inconsistency between the Original Memorandum and this Supplement, this Supplement controls.** Except as expressly amended hereby, the Original Memorandum remains in full force and effect.

---

### Section S-1. Effective Date

Except as otherwise stated in a particular Section below, the amendments set forth in this Supplement are effective as of **January 1, 2025**.

---

### Section S-2. Amendment to Management Fee (amends Section 4.1)

Section 4.1 of the Original Memorandum is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

> *"The Partnership pays the Investment Manager a management fee accrued monthly and payable quarterly in arrears at an annual rate of **1.60%** of the net asset value of each Limited Partner's capital account, determined prior to accrual of the Incentive Allocation. The Management Fee rate is identical for Class F and Class I Interests."*

Accordingly, effective January 1, 2025, the Management Fee for all Interests is **1.60% per annum**. The rate of 2.00% stated in the Original Memorandum is no longer in effect and should be disregarded.

---

### Section S-3. Addition of Preferred Return on Class I Incentive Allocation (amends Section 4.3)

Section 4.3 of the Original Memorandum is amended as follows.

With respect to **Class I Interests**, the Incentive Allocation of 20% of Net New Appreciation is, effective January 1, 2025, subject to a **preferred return of 4.00% per annum**, calculated on the opening capital account balance for each Incentive Period and adjusted pro rata for subscriptions and redemptions during the period.

No Incentive Allocation shall be taken with respect to a Class I capital account for an Incentive Period unless and until Net New Appreciation for such period exceeds the preferred return. The preferred return operates as a **soft hurdle with full catch-up**: once Net New Appreciation exceeds the preferred return, the Incentive Allocation applies to the entirety of Net New Appreciation and not solely to the excess above the preferred return. The preferred return does not compound across Incentive Periods and any shortfall is not carried forward.

With respect to **Class F Interests**, no hurdle rate or preferred return applies. The Class F Incentive Allocation of 15% of Net New Appreciation continues to be measured on an absolute basis, as set forth in the Original Memorandum.

---

### Section S-4. Amendment to Redemption Notice Period (amends Section 5.3)

Section 5.3 of the Original Memorandum is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

> *"Written notice of redemption must be received by the Administrator not less than **forty-five (45) calendar days** prior to the applicable redemption date."*

Effective January 1, 2025, the redemption notice period is **45 calendar days**. The 60-day notice period stated in the Original Memorandum is no longer in effect.

For the avoidance of doubt, the redemption frequency remains quarterly as set forth in Section 5.2, the soft lock-up and 2.00% early redemption charge remain as set forth in Section 5.1, and the gate remains as set forth in Appendix A.

---

### Section S-5. Change of Independent Auditor (amends Section 9.2)

Effective for the fiscal year ending December 31, 2024, the Partnership has appointed **Wexley Sandoval Partners LLP** as its independent registered public accounting firm, replacing Ardmore Pike & Levinson LLP.

The change resulted from the Investment Manager's determination to engage a firm with a larger alternative investment audit practice and was not the result of any disagreement with Ardmore Pike & Levinson LLP on any matter of accounting principle, financial statement disclosure, auditing scope or procedure.

Ardmore Pike & Levinson LLP continues to provide tax preparation services to the Partnership, including preparation of Schedule K-1s. Appendix B of the Original Memorandum is amended accordingly.

---

### Section S-6. Increase in Minimum Additional Subscription (amends Section 3.1)

The minimum additional subscription for Class I Interests is increased from $100,000 to **$250,000**. The minimum **initial** subscription for Class I Interests remains **$1,000,000** and is not amended by this Supplement.

---

### Section S-7. Continuing Effect

Except as expressly amended by Sections S-2 through S-6 of this Supplement, the Original Memorandum remains in full force and effect in accordance with its terms.
