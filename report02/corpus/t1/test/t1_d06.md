## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

### 1.1 Definitions

"Advisory Committee" means the committee constituted under Section 6.4.

"Affiliate" means any Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with another Person; no Portfolio Company is an Affiliate of the General Partner.

"Capital Commitment" means the amount a Partner has agreed to contribute to the Partnership, as recorded in the register of Partners and as adjusted under this Agreement. "Capital Contribution" means cash contributed by a Partner under Article III; Additional Amounts are not Capital Contributions.

"Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized to close.

"Carried Interest" means distributions to the General Partner under Sections 5.3(c) and 5.3(d)(ii).

"Fee Income" means all transaction, closing, monitoring, directors', break-up and similar fees received by the General Partner, the Manager, any Principal or any of their Affiliates from any Portfolio Company or prospective Portfolio Company, net of unreimbursed expenses of unconsummated transactions.

"Final Closing Date" means the date of the last Subsequent Closing under Section 3.1. "Initial Closing Date" means February 20, 2024.

"GP-Affiliated Limited Partner" means any Limited Partner that is the Manager, a Principal, an employee of the Manager, or an Affiliate or estate-planning vehicle of any of them.

"Interest" means the entire partnership interest of a Partner, including its Capital Account and its rights to distributions.

"Invested Capital" means, as of any date, the aggregate Capital Contributions used to fund the acquisition cost of Portfolio Investments (including related fees and expenses) that have not been Disposed of as of such date.

"Majority in Interest of the Limited Partners" means Limited Partners (other than Defaulting Partners and GP-Affiliated Limited Partners) whose Capital Commitments represent more than fifty percent (50%) of the aggregate Capital Commitments of all such Limited Partners, and "Supermajority in Interest of the Limited Partners" means such Limited Partners holding at least seventy-five percent (75%) thereof, in each case voting together with the partners of any Parallel Fund.

"Manager" means Fenwright Colquist Management, L.P., a Delaware limited partnership registered as an investment adviser under the Investment Advisers Act of 1940, as amended (the "Advisers Act").

"Parallel Fund" means Fenwright Colquist Capital Partners III (Cayman), L.P., a Cayman Islands exempted limited partnership, and any other entity organized under Section 2.2.

"Portfolio Investment" means any investment made by the Partnership, and "Portfolio Company" means the issuer of a Portfolio Investment. "Disposition" means any sale, exchange or other transaction by which the Partnership realizes a Portfolio Investment, and "Disposed of" has a correlative meaning.

"Preferred Return" means, with respect to any Limited Partner, an amount equal to a return of eight percent (8%) per annum, compounded annually, on such Limited Partner's Capital Contributions, calculated from the date each Capital Contribution is due until the date it is returned through distributions under Section 5.3(a).

"Principals" means the Key Persons and such other senior investment professionals of the Manager as the General Partner designates by notice to the Limited Partners.

"Successor Fund" means any pooled investment vehicle, other than a Parallel Fund or co-investment vehicle, sponsored by the General Partner or its Affiliates with an investment strategy substantially similar to that of the Partnership.

## ARTICLE II. ORGANIZATION

### 2.1 Name; Purpose; Office

The Partnership is a limited partnership organized under the laws of the State of Delaware. The name of the Partnership is "Fenwright Colquist Capital Partners III, L.P." Its purpose is to make, hold and dispose of control and significant minority equity investments in middle-market business services, specialty industrial and healthcare services companies headquartered principally in the United States and Canada. Its principal office is in Charlotte, North Carolina, and its registered office in the State of Delaware is as stated in the Certificate. The fiscal year of the Partnership ends on December 31.

### 2.2 Parallel Funds and Alternative Investment Vehicles

The General Partner may organize Parallel Funds for investors for whom legal, tax or regulatory considerations make investment through the Partnership impracticable. Each Parallel Fund shall invest alongside the Partnership pro rata to available commitments, on substantially the same terms, and shall dispose of its investments at substantially the same time. The Partnership shall not invest through a master fund or other intermediate pooled vehicle (other than alternative investment vehicles and holding companies formed for specific Portfolio Investments), and no Parallel Fund shall hold an interest in the Partnership.

### 2.3 Term

The term of the Partnership (the "Term") commenced on the filing of the Certificate and shall continue until the tenth (10th) anniversary of the Final Closing Date, unless the Partnership is dissolved earlier under Article X; provided that the General Partner may extend the Term for up to three (3) successive periods of one (1) year each to permit the orderly Disposition of Portfolio Investments, the first such extension in the discretion of the General Partner and each of the second and third such extensions only with the consent of the Advisory Committee. The General Partner shall notify the Limited Partners of any extension not later than sixty (60) days before the date on which the Term would otherwise expire.

### 2.4 Investment Period

(a) The "Investment Period" commences on the Initial Closing Date and ends on the earliest of (i) the fifth (5th) anniversary of the Initial Closing Date, (ii) the date designated by the General Partner by notice to the Limited Partners, and (iii) the date on which the Investment Period terminates under Section 6.3, Section 9.1 or Section 9.2.

(b) After the Investment Period, Capital Contributions may be called only to (i) complete Portfolio Investments that were the subject of a binding written agreement entered into before the end of the Investment Period, (ii) make follow-on investments in existing Portfolio Companies, not to exceed fifteen percent (15%) of aggregate Capital Commitments in the aggregate, and (iii) pay Management Fees, Partnership Expenses and indemnification obligations.

## ARTICLE III. CAPITAL COMMITMENTS AND CONTRIBUTIONS

### 3.1 Closings

The General Partner may admit additional Limited Partners, or accept increased Capital Commitments, at one or more closings after the Initial Closing Date (each, a "Subsequent Closing"), the last of which shall occur not later than the first (1st) anniversary of the Initial Closing Date or, with the consent of the Advisory Committee, eighteen (18) months after the Initial Closing Date. Each Limited Partner admitted at a Subsequent Closing shall contribute its pro rata share of all prior Capital Contributions, plus an amount computed at six percent (6%) per annum on that share (the "Additional Amount") for distribution to the previously admitted Partners. Aggregate Capital Commitments of the Partnership and the Parallel Funds shall not exceed $900,000,000.

### 3.2 Minimum Capital Commitment

The minimum Capital Commitment of a Limited Partner is $5,000,000, provided that the General Partner may, in its sole discretion, accept Capital Commitments of lesser amounts.

### 3.3 General Partner Commitment

The General Partner shall, directly or through its Affiliates (including GP-Affiliated Limited Partners), make Capital Commitments to the Partnership and the Parallel Funds in an aggregate amount equal to not less than two percent (2%) of the aggregate Capital Commitments of all Partners and all partners of the Parallel Funds (the "GP Commitment"). The GP Commitment shall be funded in cash pro rata with the Limited Partners and shall not be satisfied through any waiver of the Management Fee. Interests held by GP-Affiliated Limited Partners bear no Management Fee and no Carried Interest.

### 3.4 Capital Calls; Default

(a) The General Partner may call Capital Contributions pro rata to Unfunded Commitments by written notice (a "Drawdown Notice") delivered at least ten (10) Business Days before the due date. The Partnership may borrow under a subscription credit facility secured by Unfunded Commitments, provided that each borrowing is repaid within one hundred eighty (180) days, and the Preferred Return on Capital Contributions used to repay a borrowing accrues from the date the borrowing was incurred.

(b) A Limited Partner that fails to fund a Capital Contribution within ten (10) Business Days after notice of the failure may be declared a "Defaulting Partner", charged interest at twelve percent (12%) per annum, required to forfeit up to fifty percent (50%) of its Capital Account and suspended from voting.

### 3.5 No Withdrawal or Redemption

No Limited Partner shall have the right to withdraw from the Partnership, to withdraw or redeem all or any portion of its Interest or its Capital Account, or to demand the return of any Capital Contribution, in each case before the dissolution and winding up of the Partnership, except as the General Partner may require under Section 11.2. The Interests are not redeemable at the option of the holder at any time. The Partnership does not maintain redemption dates or a redemption procedure, and no right of redemption or withdrawal shall be implied from any provision of this Agreement. A Limited Partner will realize liquidity on its Interest only through distributions under Article V and Transfers permitted under Article VIII.

### 3.6 Excuse and Exclusion

A Limited Partner may be excused from a Portfolio Investment on delivery of an opinion of counsel that its participation would violate applicable law or an investment policy disclosed at its admission, and the General Partner may exclude a Limited Partner whose participation would have a material adverse legal, tax or regulatory effect. Amounts not contributed by an excused or excluded Limited Partner may be called from the other Partners, up to their Unfunded Commitments.

## ARTICLE IV. MANAGEMENT FEE AND EXPENSES

### 4.1 Management Fee

(a) The Partnership shall pay the Manager a management fee (the "Management Fee") quarterly in advance on the first Business Day of each fiscal quarter, prorated for partial quarters, borne by the Limited Partners other than GP-Affiliated Limited Partners.

(b) The Management Fee for each fiscal quarter attributable to each Limited Partner shall equal:

(i) for each fiscal quarter commencing during the Investment Period, one-quarter of one and three-quarters percent (1.75%) (that is, 0.4375%) of such Limited Partner's Capital Commitment; and

(ii) for each fiscal quarter thereafter, one-quarter of one and one-half percent (1.50%) (that is, 0.375%) of such Limited Partner's share of Invested Capital as of the first day of such fiscal quarter.

(c) The rate and basis in Section 4.1(b)(ii) shall apply from the earlier of the end of the Investment Period and the first day on which a management fee accrues for any Successor Fund.

(d) A Limited Partner admitted at a Subsequent Closing shall pay the Management Fee it would have paid had it been admitted on the Initial Closing Date, plus the Additional Amount thereon. No Management Fee is payable for any period after the tenth (10th) anniversary of the Final Closing Date or after dissolution.

### 4.2 Management Fee Offset

The Management Fee for each fiscal quarter shall be reduced by one hundred percent (100%) of the Partnership's share of Fee Income received in the preceding quarter, allocated among the Partnership, the Parallel Funds and any co-investment vehicle by relative investment in the relevant Portfolio Company. Any reduction exceeding the Management Fee then payable shall be applied in subsequent quarters, and any excess remaining at dissolution shall be paid by the Manager to the Limited Partners.

### 4.3 Organizational Expenses; Partnership Expenses

The Partnership shall bear organizational and offering expenses up to $2,000,000 for the Partnership and the Parallel Funds together; any excess, and all placement fees, shall be borne by the Manager. The Partnership shall also bear its operating expenses ("Partnership Expenses"), including expenses of unconsummated transactions, the fees of the Administrator and the independent auditor, legal and tax expenses, interest on borrowings, Advisory Committee expenses (including counsel it retains), insurance and indemnification expenses. The Manager bears its own overhead.

## ARTICLE V. ALLOCATIONS AND DISTRIBUTIONS

### 5.1 Capital Accounts; Timing

A capital account shall be maintained for each Partner in accordance with Treasury Regulations Section 1.704-1(b)(2)(iv) (each, a "Capital Account"), and net income and net loss shall be allocated so that each Capital Account equals, as nearly as possible, the amount distributable to that Partner under Section 5.3 on a hypothetical liquidation at book value. Net proceeds of a Disposition shall be distributed within ninety (90) days after receipt, and current income not less often than annually, subject to reasonable reserves.

### 5.2 Apportionment

Distributable proceeds shall be apportioned among the Partners in proportion to their participation in the Portfolio Investment giving rise to them. Amounts apportioned to the General Partner and to GP-Affiliated Limited Partners shall be distributed to them. Amounts apportioned to each other Limited Partner shall be distributed under Section 5.3.

### 5.3 Distribution Waterfall

Amounts apportioned to each Limited Partner (other than a GP-Affiliated Limited Partner) shall be distributed in the following order of priority:

(a) Return of Capital: first, 100% to such Limited Partner until it has received cumulative distributions under this Section 5.3(a) equal to its aggregate Capital Contributions for all purposes, including Capital Contributions used for Portfolio Investments that have been Disposed of or written off and Capital Contributions used to pay Management Fees and Partnership Expenses;

(b) Preferred Return: second, 100% to such Limited Partner until it has received cumulative distributions under this Section 5.3(b) equal to its Preferred Return;

(c) General Partner Catch-Up: third, 100% to the General Partner until the General Partner has received cumulative distributions under this Section 5.3(c) equal to twenty percent (20%) of the aggregate amount distributed under Section 5.3(b) and this Section 5.3(c); and

(d) 80/20 Split: thereafter, (i) eighty percent (80%) to such Limited Partner and (ii) twenty percent (20%) to the General Partner.

### 5.4 Calculation of Carried Interest

The Carried Interest is determined on a cumulative basis over the life of the Partnership, taking into account all Capital Contributions made by and all distributions made to each Limited Partner from the Initial Closing Date. The Carried Interest is not determined by reference to any annual or other periodic performance period and is not subject to a high-water mark, loss carryforward account or similar mechanism; realized losses, write-downs, Management Fees and Partnership Expenses are instead taken into account through the cumulative requirements of Section 5.3 and the clawback in Section 5.5.

### 5.5 General Partner Clawback

If, after the final distribution on dissolution, the General Partner has received Carried Interest with respect to any Limited Partner in excess of the amount it would have received had all distributions been made under Section 5.3 on a cumulative basis, the General Partner shall restore the excess to that Limited Partner, limited to the Carried Interest received net of taxes at an assumed combined rate. Each Principal receiving Carried Interest guarantees that obligation severally, and not jointly, to the extent of the Carried Interest distributed to him or her. Tax distributions to the General Partner are advances against subsequent Carried Interest.

### 5.6 Distributions in Kind

Before dissolution, the Partnership shall not distribute securities in kind other than freely tradeable listed securities, valued at their average closing price over the five (5) trading days before distribution.

### 5.7 Reinvestment

The General Partner may retain or recall (a) distributions representing a return of Capital Contributions used to pay Management Fees and Partnership Expenses and (b) proceeds of Portfolio Investments Disposed of within eighteen (18) months of acquisition, provided that Capital Contributions invested in Portfolio Investments shall not exceed one hundred twenty percent (120%) of aggregate Capital Commitments. Recallable amounts are added back to Unfunded Commitments.

## ARTICLE VI. MANAGEMENT

### 6.1 Authority; Investment Limitations

(a) The management and control of the Partnership are vested exclusively in the General Partner, which may delegate investment advisory functions to the Manager. No Limited Partner shall take part in the management or control of the Partnership or have authority to bind it.

(b) Without the consent of the Advisory Committee, the Partnership shall not invest more than fifteen percent (15%) of aggregate Capital Commitments, at cost, in any single Portfolio Company, or more than twenty percent (20%) in companies headquartered outside the United States and Canada, and shall not invest in any pooled vehicle charging a management fee or carried interest.

### 6.2 Successor Funds

Until the earlier of the end of the Investment Period and the date on which at least seventy-five percent (75%) of aggregate Capital Commitments has been invested, committed or reserved, neither the General Partner nor its Affiliates shall hold a first closing of a Successor Fund.

### 6.3 Key Persons

(a) The "Key Persons" are Graham Oduya and Celia Brandvold, and each of them is a "Key Person". Each Key Person shall, during the Investment Period, devote substantially all of his or her business time to the Partnership, the Parallel Funds, Fenwright Colquist Capital Partners I, L.P., Fenwright Colquist Capital Partners II, L.P. and their respective portfolio companies (the "Required Time").

(b) A "Key Person Event" occurs if, at any time during the Investment Period, either Key Person dies, becomes permanently disabled, ceases to be a managing member of the General Partner or ceases to devote the Required Time.

(c) Upon a Key Person Event, the Investment Period shall be suspended automatically, effective as of the date of the Key Person Event (the "Suspension Period"), and the General Partner shall notify the Limited Partners and the Advisory Committee within ten (10) Business Days. During the Suspension Period the Partnership shall not make new Portfolio Investments, other than those subject to a binding written agreement entered into before the Key Person Event and follow-on investments permitted under Section 2.4(b)(ii).

(d) The Suspension Period shall end, and the Investment Period shall resume, if within one hundred eighty (180) days after the Key Person Event either (i) a Majority in Interest of the Limited Partners elects to reinstate the Investment Period or (ii) the Advisory Committee approves, and a Majority in Interest of the Limited Partners consents to, an individual proposed by the General Partner to replace the departing Key Person, in which case this Agreement shall be amended to name that individual as a Key Person. If neither occurs within that period, the Investment Period shall terminate automatically on the last day of that period.

### 6.4 Advisory Committee

The General Partner shall establish an Advisory Committee of three (3) to seven (7) representatives of Limited Partners that are not Affiliates of the General Partner. The Advisory Committee shall approve or disapprove conflict of interest transactions, review the valuation policy annually and give the consents reserved to it by this Agreement. It acts by majority, may retain independent counsel at the expense of the Partnership, and has no authority to manage the Partnership.

### 6.5 Exculpation and Indemnification

None of the General Partner, the Manager, the Principals, the members of the Advisory Committee or their Affiliates (each, a "Covered Person") shall be liable to the Partnership or any Partner except for fraud, willful misconduct, gross negligence, a material breach of this Agreement or a knowing violation of law ("Disabling Conduct"), and the Partnership shall indemnify each Covered Person against losses not resulting from its Disabling Conduct.

## ARTICLE VII. SERVICE PROVIDERS AND REPORTS

### 7.1 Independent Auditor

The financial statements of the Partnership shall be audited as of the end of each fiscal year by Aldwin Treloar LLP, independent certified public accountants, or by such other independent accounting firm registered with the Public Company Accounting Oversight Board as the General Partner may select on thirty (30) days' prior notice to the Advisory Committee.

### 7.2 Administrator

The General Partner has engaged Kinnaird Stroud Fund Services, LLC (the "Administrator") to maintain the books of the Partnership and the register of Partners, calculate Capital Accounts, prepare Drawdown Notices and distribution notices, and conduct anti-money laundering and sanctions screening of investors. The fees of the Administrator are Partnership Expenses.

### 7.3 Custody; No Prime Broker

Cash of the Partnership is held in accounts in its name at one or more United States banks that are qualified custodians under Rule 206(4)-2 under the Advisers Act, and uncertificated securities of private issuers are recorded on the issuer's books in the name of the Partnership. The Partnership does not engage a prime broker, does not maintain margin or securities lending accounts and shall not sell securities short.

### 7.4 Reports and Valuation

The General Partner shall deliver unaudited quarterly reports, including a schedule of Portfolio Investments at cost and fair value and a statement of each Partner's Capital Account, Management Fee and Fee Income offset, within sixty (60) days after each of the first three fiscal quarters, and audited annual financial statements and Schedules K-1 within one hundred twenty (120) days after each fiscal year. Portfolio Investments are valued quarterly at fair value under ASC Topic 820, and any permanent write-down or write-off shall be reported in the next quarterly report.

### 7.5 Legal Counsel

Pellow Ashdown LLP ("Fund Counsel") acts as counsel to the Partnership, the General Partner and the Manager in connection with the organization of the Partnership, the offering of Interests and its ongoing operations. Fund Counsel does not represent any Limited Partner or the Limited Partners as a group. Each Limited Partner acknowledges that it has had the opportunity to retain its own counsel and, to the extent permitted by applicable rules of professional conduct, waives any conflict arising from Fund Counsel's representation of the Partnership, the General Partner and the Manager.

## ARTICLE VIII. TRANSFERS

### 8.1 Restrictions on Transfer

No Limited Partner may sell, assign, pledge or otherwise transfer (a "Transfer") any part of its Interest without the prior written consent of the General Partner, which shall not be unreasonably withheld for a Transfer to an Affiliate. A Limited Partner proposing a Transfer shall give the General Partner not less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice. No Transfer shall be made if it would require registration under the Securities Act or the Investment Company Act of 1940, cause the Partnership to be a publicly traded partnership, or cause Partnership assets to constitute plan assets. A transferee becomes a substituted Limited Partner only with the consent of the General Partner and assumes the transferor's Unfunded Commitment to the extent transferred.

## ARTICLE IX. REMOVAL; EARLY TERMINATION OF THE INVESTMENT PERIOD

### 9.1 Removal for Cause

Within one hundred twenty (120) days after a final, non-appealable judicial determination that the General Partner, the Manager or a Key Person committed fraud, willful misconduct or a felony involving the Partnership, a Majority in Interest of the Limited Partners may remove the General Partner and admit a substitute general partner, whereupon the Investment Period terminates.

### 9.2 Termination Without Cause

At any time after the first (1st) anniversary of the Initial Closing Date, a Supermajority in Interest of the Limited Partners may terminate the Investment Period without cause by written notice to the General Partner, or elect to dissolve the Partnership under Section 10.1(c).

## ARTICLE X. DISSOLUTION AND LIQUIDATION

### 10.1 Events of Dissolution

The Partnership shall be dissolved upon the first to occur of (a) the expiration of the Term, as it may be extended under Section 2.3; (b) the bankruptcy or withdrawal of the General Partner, unless a Majority in Interest of the Limited Partners elects within ninety (90) days to continue the Partnership; (c) the election of a Supermajority in Interest of the Limited Partners; or (d) a decree of judicial dissolution under the Act.

### 10.2 Liquidation

Upon dissolution, the General Partner (or a liquidator appointed by a Majority in Interest of the Limited Partners) shall wind up the Partnership, pay or provide for its liabilities and distribute its remaining assets under Section 5.3, after giving effect to Section 5.5, within twenty-four (24) months unless the Advisory Committee consents to a longer period.

## ARTICLE XI. TAX AND REGULATORY MATTERS

### 11.2 Required Withdrawal
