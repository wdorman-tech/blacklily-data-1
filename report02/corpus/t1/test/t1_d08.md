## SECTION 1. SUMMARY OF PRINCIPAL TERMS

This summary is qualified by the more detailed information elsewhere in this Memorandum and by the Partnership Agreement.

### 1.1 The Fund

Tarrenfield Oakes Real Estate Partners III, L.P. is a Delaware limited partnership formed on October 21, 2025. The Fund is a closed-end fund: Capital Commitments are drawn down as Investments are made, and Interests are not redeemable. The Fund is a single, stand-alone vehicle and is not organized as part of a master-feeder structure. The General Partner may form alternative investment vehicles ("AIVs") for legal, tax or regulatory reasons, and the Fund expects to hold certain Investments through subsidiaries intended to qualify as real estate investment trusts ("Fund REITs"), organized as Maryland corporations or Delaware statutory trusts. The Fund will acquire, reposition and sell value-add multifamily, light industrial and grocery-anchored retail properties in the Mountain West and Sun Belt. See Section 3.

### 1.2 General Partner and Investment Manager

The general partner is Tarrenfield Oakes Fund III GP, L.L.C. (the "General Partner"). Tarrenfield Oakes Investment Management, LLC, an investment adviser registered with the SEC (the "Investment Manager"), manages the Fund's investments. The General Partner, the Investment Manager and their affiliates are the "Sponsor."

### 1.3 Offering Size and Closings

The Fund seeks Capital Commitments from Limited Partners of $500,000,000, subject to a hard cap of $650,000,000, in each case excluding the Sponsor Commitment. The initial closing (the date of which is the "Initial Closing Date") is expected in the second quarter of 2026. Subsequent closings may be held through the first anniversary of the Initial Closing Date, extendable by up to six months with Advisory Committee consent (the last such closing, the "Final Closing").

### 1.4 Minimum Commitment

The minimum Capital Commitment of each Limited Partner is $5,000,000. The General Partner may accept lesser amounts in its sole discretion.

### 1.5 Sponsor Commitment

The General Partner, the Investment Manager and their principals and affiliates will commit, in the aggregate, the greater of $7,500,000 or 2.0% of the aggregate Capital Commitments of the Limited Partners (the "Sponsor Commitment"), funded in cash pro rata with the Limited Partners and not through any fee waiver.

### 1.6 Investment Period

From the Initial Closing Date until the earliest of (a) the fourth anniversary of the Initial Closing Date, (b) the date the General Partner notifies the Limited Partners that substantially all Capital Commitments have been invested, committed or reserved, and (c) termination following a Key Person Event under Section 7.1 (the "Investment Period").

### 1.7 Term

The term of the Fund ends on the eighth anniversary of the Initial Closing Date, subject to two one-year extensions: the first at the discretion of the General Partner and the second with the consent of the Advisory Committee. See Section 7.4.

### 1.8 Management Fee

During the Investment Period, 1.50% per annum of Capital Commitments; thereafter, 1.50% per annum of Invested Equity. For Large Commitment Limited Partners, the rate is 1.25% per annum on the same bases. See Section 4.1.

### 1.9 Distributions and Carried Interest

Distributions attributable to each Limited Partner are made: first, 100% to that Limited Partner until it has received the return of its Capital Contributions; second, 100% to it until it has received a 9% per annum internal rate of return, compounded annually, on those Capital Contributions (the "Preferred Return"), which operates as a hard hurdle; third, 50% to it and 50% to the General Partner (the "Catch-Up") until the General Partner has received 20% of the profits distributed; and thereafter, 80% to it and 20% to the General Partner. See Section 5.

No redemption: Section 6.3. Transfers: Section 6.4. Key Person Event: Section 7.1. Side letters and most favored nation election: Section 7.5. Service providers: Section 9.

## SECTION 3. INVESTMENT STRATEGY AND LIMITATIONS

### 3.1 Strategy

The Fund targets properties whose income can be increased through capital improvement, re-tenanting, lease-up or better management. The Sponsor expects to deploy approximately 50% to 65% of Capital Commitments in multifamily, 20% to 35% in light industrial and up to 15% in grocery-anchored retail, in markets including Phoenix, Tucson, Denver, Salt Lake City, Boise, San Antonio, Austin and Raleigh, and to exit each Investment within three to five years.

### 3.2 Investment Limitations and Leverage

Without Advisory Committee consent, the Fund will not invest more than 20% of aggregate Capital Commitments in any single Investment, more than 30% in any single metropolitan area or more than 15% in ground-up development, invest outside the United States, or transact with a Prior Fund or other Sponsor client. Property-level indebtedness will not exceed 65% of the aggregate gross asset value of the Investments, or 75% of the cost of any single Investment, measured when incurred. Subscription Facility borrowings are governed by Section 6.1.

## SECTION 4. FEES AND EXPENSES

### 4.1 Management Fee

The Fund will pay the Investment Manager a management fee (the "Management Fee"), quarterly in advance, calculated for each Limited Partner:

(a) during the Investment Period, at 1.50% per annum of that Limited Partner's Capital Commitment; and

(b) after the Investment Period, at 1.50% per annum of that Limited Partner's share of Invested Equity as of the first day of the quarter.

For a Limited Partner whose Capital Commitment, aggregated with those of its affiliates, is $50,000,000 or more (a "Large Commitment Limited Partner"), the rate under each of clauses (a) and (b) is 1.25% per annum instead of 1.50% per annum. If the Sponsor begins to receive management fees from a Successor Fund before the Investment Period ends, clause (b) applies from the next quarter.

"Invested Equity" means the Capital Contributions used to fund the acquisition cost of Investments, including capital improvements and reserves funded from Capital Contributions, reduced by the portion attributable to Investments disposed of and by permanent write-downs determined by the General Partner and reviewed by the Advisory Committee. No Management Fee is charged on the Sponsor Commitment. A Limited Partner admitted after the Initial Closing Date bears the Management Fee from the Initial Closing Date, plus Equalization Interest under Section 6.2.

### 4.2 Fee Offset

The Management Fee is reduced by 100% of transaction, break-up, financing, monitoring and similar fees received by the Sponsor in connection with actual or proposed Investments ("Transaction Fees") and by 100% of any placement fees paid by the Fund. Excess offsets carry forward. The Sponsor charges no acquisition, disposition or financing fees.

### 4.3 Affiliated Service Fees

Tarrenfield Oakes Property Services, LLC, a Sponsor affiliate, may provide property management (expected fees of 2.5% to 3.5% of gross collected revenues) and construction management (up to 5.0% of project costs) at rates not exceeding market, as confirmed every two years by a consultant engaged by the Advisory Committee. Third-party leasing and sales brokers are retained on arm's-length terms. These "Affiliated Service Fees" are property-level expenses and do not reduce the Management Fee.

### 4.4 Organizational and Fund Expenses

The Fund bears organizational and offering expenses up to $1,500,000; the Investment Manager bears any excess and all placement fees. The Fund bears the costs of acquiring, financing, improving and disposing of Investments, borrowing costs, the fees of the Administrator and the Auditor, legal, appraisal, insurance and tax preparation expenses, and Advisory Committee expenses. The Investment Manager bears its own overhead and personnel costs.

## SECTION 5. DISTRIBUTIONS AND CARRIED INTEREST

### 5.1 Timing

The General Partner intends to distribute operating cash flow quarterly and net disposition or refinancing proceeds within 90 days after receipt, net of reserves. During the Investment Period, proceeds from an Investment held fewer than 24 months may be reinvested up to its cost.

### 5.2 Distribution Waterfall

Distributions are apportioned among the Partners by their interests in the Investment giving rise to them, and each Limited Partner's share is distributed in the following order:

(a) Return of Capital: 100% to the Limited Partner until it has received its aggregate Capital Contributions for all purposes, including Management Fees and Fund Expenses;

(b) Preferred Return: 100% to the Limited Partner until it has received the Preferred Return, an internal rate of return of 9% per annum, compounded annually, on its Capital Contributions;

(c) Catch-Up: 50% to the Limited Partner and 50% to the General Partner until the General Partner has received under this clause (c) 20% of the aggregate amounts distributed under clause (b) and this clause (c); and

(d) 80/20 Split: thereafter, 80% to the Limited Partner and 20% to the General Partner.

Distributions to the General Partner under clauses (c) and (d) are the "Carried Interest." The Carried Interest rate of 20% applies to every Limited Partner other than the Sponsor and its employees, whose Interests bear no Carried Interest.

### 5.3 Preferred Return; Hard Hurdle

The Preferred Return is a hard hurdle. It is computed on an internal rate of return basis from the date of each Capital Contribution and is satisfied only by cash distributed, not by unrealized appreciation. Nothing is distributed to the General Partner under clause (c) or (d) of Section 5.2 in respect of a Limited Partner until that Limited Partner has received all of its Capital Contributions and the full Preferred Return. Where the Fund borrows under a Subscription Facility in lieu of calling capital, the Preferred Return accrues from the date of the borrowing.

### 5.4 General Partner Clawback

The Carried Interest is determined on a cumulative, whole-of-fund basis under Section 5.2, and no high-water mark or loss carryforward applies to the Interests. Upon liquidation, if the General Partner has received Carried Interest exceeding the amount due under Section 5.2 on an aggregate basis, or any Limited Partner has not received its Capital Contributions and Preferred Return, the General Partner will return the excess, limited to the Carried Interest it received net of taxes at an assumed rate (the "General Partner Clawback"). Each recipient of Carried Interest, including each Key Person, severally guarantees its pro rata share. Tax distributions to the General Partner are advances against Carried Interest.

## SECTION 6. CAPITAL COMMITMENTS, LIQUIDITY AND TRANSFERS

### 6.1 Capital Calls; Subscription Facility

The General Partner will call Capital Commitments pro rata upon not less than 10 Business Days' prior written notice. After the Investment Period, capital may be called only to complete committed Investments, to fund follow-on investments (not exceeding 15% of aggregate Capital Commitments), and to pay Management Fees and Fund Expenses. The Fund may borrow under a credit facility secured by Unfunded Capital Commitments (a "Subscription Facility") if outstanding borrowings do not exceed 25% of aggregate Unfunded Capital Commitments and each borrowing is repaid within 270 days.

### 6.2 Subsequent Closings; Defaults

An investor admitted after the Initial Closing Date contributes its share of prior Capital Contributions plus interest at 6% per annum ("Equalization Interest"), which is paid to the earlier Partners and is not a Capital Contribution. If a Limited Partner fails to fund a capital call within five Business Days after notice of the failure, the General Partner may charge interest at 12% per annum, suspend its voting and distribution rights, reduce its capital account by up to 50% and force a sale of its Interest.

### 6.3 No Redemption or Withdrawal

The Fund is a closed-end fund. Limited Partners have no right to withdraw from the Fund or to redeem, in whole or in part, their Interests at any time, and the Partnership Agreement contains no redemption mechanism. Accordingly, no lock-up, redemption right, notice period or gate applies to the Interests. Liquidity is available only through distributions as Investments are realized and through a permitted transfer. The General Partner may require a Limited Partner to withdraw where its continued participation would cause a violation of law or cause the Fund's assets to be treated as "plan assets" under ERISA; that power confers no right on any Limited Partner.

### 6.4 Transfers

No Limited Partner may transfer any part of its Interest without the prior written consent of the General Partner, which may be withheld in its sole discretion except for transfers to affiliates assuming all obligations. A Limited Partner proposing a transfer must give at least 30 days' prior written notice. No transfer is permitted that would cause the Fund to be a publicly traded partnership, to register under the Investment Company Act or to hold "plan assets." Any secondary market is expected to be limited.

### 6.5 Subscription Procedures

Each investor must deliver a completed subscription agreement containing representations as to its status as an accredited investor and qualified purchaser, its tax status and the source of its funds, and must provide the information the Administrator requires under its anti-money laundering procedures. The General Partner may accept or reject any subscription in whole or in part.

## SECTION 7. GOVERNANCE

### 7.1 Key Person Event

The "Key Persons" are Garrett Vashon and Ines Kalloway. A "Key Person Event" occurs if, during the Investment Period, either Key Person ceases to devote substantially all of his or her business time to the Fund, the Prior Funds and the Investment Manager. Upon a Key Person Event, the Investment Period is automatically suspended, and the Fund may make only Investments already committed, follow-on investments and capital expenditures under approved business plans. The suspension ends if, within 180 days, Limited Partners holding a Majority in Interest vote to reinstate the Investment Period. Otherwise the Investment Period terminates and the Management Fee is thereafter calculated under Section 4.1(b). "Majority in Interest" means Limited Partners holding more than 50% of aggregate Limited Partner Capital Commitments, excluding defaulting Limited Partners and the Sponsor.

### 7.2 Advisory Committee

The General Partner will appoint an advisory committee of three to seven representatives of unaffiliated Limited Partners (the "Advisory Committee"). It consents to conflict transactions and Affiliated Service Fee arrangements, reviews valuations, may waive the limitations in Section 3.2, consents to the second term extension, and is consulted before any change of the Fund's independent auditor.

### 7.3 Removal of the General Partner

A Majority in Interest may remove the General Partner for Cause, in which case undistributed Carried Interest is forfeited. After the second anniversary of the Final Closing, Limited Partners holding at least 75% of Limited Partner Capital Commitments may remove the General Partner without Cause; the General Partner then retains Carried Interest only on existing Investments, reduced by 25 percent.

### 7.4 Term and Dissolution

The Fund will dissolve on the eighth anniversary of the Initial Closing Date unless dissolved earlier. The General Partner may extend the term for one additional year in its discretion, by notice at least 90 days before expiration, and for a second additional year with the consent of the Advisory Committee. In no event will the term extend beyond the tenth anniversary of the Initial Closing Date. Upon dissolution, the General Partner, or a liquidator appointed by a Majority in Interest, will wind up the Fund and distribute its assets under Section 5.2.

### 7.5 Side Letters; Most Favored Nation Election

The General Partner may enter into side letters with individual Limited Partners. Each Limited Partner whose Capital Commitment, aggregated with those of its affiliates, is $25,000,000 or more may elect to receive the benefit of any right granted in a side letter to another Limited Partner whose Capital Commitment is equal to or less than its own. Within 45 days after the Final Closing, the General Partner will deliver to each eligible Limited Partner a compendium of electable provisions, and the election must be made within 30 days after delivery. The election does not extend to: (a) rights reflecting legal, regulatory, tax or policy requirements specific to a Limited Partner; (b) Advisory Committee membership; (c) co-investment rights; (d) the 1.25% Management Fee rate for Large Commitment Limited Partners; and (e) rights granted to the Sponsor, its employees or its affiliates.

### 7.6 Exculpation and Indemnification

None of the General Partner, the Investment Manager, their affiliates, their respective members, officers and employees, or the members of the Advisory Committee (each, a "Covered Person") will be liable to the Fund or any Limited Partner for any act or omission in connection with the Fund, except for fraud, willful misconduct, gross negligence or a material breach of the Partnership Agreement. The Fund will indemnify each Covered Person against losses arising from the Fund's affairs, subject to the same exceptions, and may require Limited Partners to return distributions to fund that indemnity, limited to 25% of the distributions received by each Limited Partner.

## SECTION 10. CERTAIN RISK FACTORS

An investment in the Fund involves significant risks, including loss of the entire investment. The following is not complete.

### 10.1 Illiquidity

The Interests cannot be withdrawn or redeemed (Section 6.3), are subject to transfer restrictions and have no public market. A Limited Partner should expect to hold its Interest for the full term, including extensions.

### 10.2 Value-Add Execution

Returns depend on completing renovations on budget and on schedule and on achieving projected rents. Construction costs, labor shortages, permitting delays and contractor defaults may increase costs or delay stabilization, and rent growth may not materialize if competing supply increases or local employment weakens.

### 10.3 Leverage and Interest Rates

The Fund expects to use substantial property-level leverage, some at floating rates. Leverage magnifies losses. Rising rates increase debt service, may reduce values and may prevent refinancing at maturity. A lender may foreclose on a property whose cash flow does not cover debt service, eliminating the Fund's equity in it.

### 10.4 Concentration

The Fund will concentrate in a few property types and regional markets and may hold a limited number of Investments. Oversupply, migration changes or the loss of major local employers could affect several Investments at once.

### 10.5 Environmental, Physical and Insurance Risks

The Fund may incur liability for environmental conditions it did not cause. Target markets are exposed to extreme heat, wildfire, hail, flooding and water scarcity, and insurance may be unavailable or uneconomic for some risks.

### 10.6 Reliance on the Sponsor

Limited Partners cannot evaluate Investments in advance or participate in management. The loss of either Key Person could adversely affect the Fund, and Section 7.1 may not fully mitigate that effect.

### 10.7 Subscription Facility and Defaults

A Subscription Facility defers capital calls, which may increase reported internal rates of return while reducing multiples of invested capital, and its lender may call capital directly after a default. If a Limited Partner defaults, other Limited Partners may be required to fund additional amounts up to their Unfunded Capital Commitments.

### 10.8 Regulatory and Tax Changes

Changes in zoning, rent regulation, property tax assessment practices, tenant protection laws or federal tax law, including the rules governing real estate investment trusts, may reduce the value of Investments or the returns available to Limited Partners.

### 10.9 Limited Operating History

The Fund has a limited operating history. The Prior Funds invested in a different interest rate and construction cost environment, and their results are not necessarily indicative of the Fund's results.

## SECTION 11. CERTAIN TAX AND ERISA CONSIDERATIONS

The following is a general summary and not tax or legal advice.

### 11.1 Partnership Status

The Fund expects to be treated as a partnership, and not as a publicly traded partnership, for U.S. federal income tax purposes. Each Limited Partner must report its share of Fund income whether or not cash is distributed. The Fund will use commercially reasonable efforts to deliver Schedules K-1 within 120 days after each fiscal year end. Adjustments under the centralized partnership audit rules may be borne by current Partners.

### 11.2 Tax-Exempt and Non-U.S. Investors

Because the Fund uses leverage, part of its income is expected to be unrelated business taxable income to tax-exempt investors. The General Partner may hold Investments through Fund REITs, whose dividends generally are not such income, which may reduce after-tax returns to other Limited Partners. Non-U.S. Limited Partners will generally have effectively connected income, will be taxed on gains under the Foreign Investment in Real Property Tax Act of 1980, and must file U.S. returns.

### 11.3 ERISA

The General Partner intends to operate the Fund as a "real estate operating company" under 29 C.F.R. Section 2510.3-101 or, failing that, to limit "benefit plan investors" to less than 25% of each class of equity interests, so that the Fund's assets are not "plan assets." Plan fiduciaries should consider the liquidity implications of an investment that cannot be redeemed.

### 11.4 State and Local Taxes

Limited Partners may be required to file returns and pay tax in the states in which the Fund's properties are located. The Fund may pay composite or withholding taxes on behalf of Limited Partners, which are treated as distributions to them.
