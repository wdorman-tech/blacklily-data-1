## ARTICLE II. FORMATION, PURPOSE, TERM AND STRUCTURE

### 2.1 Formation; Name; Offices

The Partners hereby continue the Partnership as a limited partnership under the Delaware Revised Uniform Limited Partnership Act, 6 Del. C. Section 17-101 et seq. (the "Act"). The name of the Partnership is Fenlight Carrow Ventures II, L.P. The principal office of the Partnership is in Boulder, Colorado, and its registered office and registered agent in the State of Delaware are as stated in the Certificate. The initial limited partner, Delphine Wrexcombe, withdrew from the Partnership on the Initial Closing Date and received the return of her initial contribution.

### 2.2 Purpose

The purpose of the Partnership is to make, hold and dispose of privately negotiated equity and equity-linked investments in early-stage companies, principally at the seed and Series A stages, in enterprise software, developer infrastructure, applied machine learning and computational tools for the life sciences, and to engage in any lawful activity incidental to that purpose. The Partnership intends to qualify at all times as a "venture capital fund" within the meaning of Rule 203(l)-1 under the Advisers Act.

### 2.3 Term

The term of the Partnership shall continue until the tenth (10th) anniversary of the Initial Closing Date, unless the Partnership is dissolved earlier under Article XI; provided that the General Partner may extend the term for up to three (3) successive periods of one (1) year each, as follows: (a) the first such extension in its discretion, by written notice to the Limited Partners delivered not later than ninety (90) days before the then-scheduled expiration of the term; and (b) the second and the third such extensions only with the prior written consent of the Advisory Committee. Any extension beyond the third one-year extension requires an amendment under Section 12.2. During any extension period, the General Partner shall use commercially reasonable efforts to dispose of the remaining Portfolio Investments in an orderly manner.

### 2.4 Structure; Alternative Investment Vehicles

The Partnership is a closed-end investment vehicle. The Partners contribute capital from their Unfunded Commitments as and when called by the General Partner under Article III, and the Interests are not redeemable at the option of any Partner (Section 9.2). The Partnership is a stand-alone vehicle: it is not a feeder fund into, and does not invest through, any master fund, and no parallel fund shall be formed without the consent of the Advisory Committee. For legal, tax or regulatory reasons the General Partner may require Partners to invest through a separate vehicle (an "Alternative Investment Vehicle") on economically identical terms, aggregated with the Partnership for purposes of Articles V and VI.

## ARTICLE III. CAPITAL COMMITMENTS AND CAPITAL CONTRIBUTIONS

### 3.1 Admission; Register; Minimum Commitment

Each Limited Partner is admitted to the Partnership upon acceptance of its subscription agreement by the General Partner, which shall maintain a register of the name, notice address, Capital Commitment and Capital Contributions of each Partner (the "Register"). The minimum Capital Commitment of each Limited Partner, other than an Affiliated Limited Partner, is $1,000,000; provided that the General Partner may, in its sole discretion, accept Capital Commitments of lesser amounts. The aggregate Capital Commitments of all Partners shall not exceed $200,000,000.

### 3.2 General Partner Commitment

The General Partner, directly or through one or more of its Affiliates, shall make a Capital Commitment to the Partnership equal to not less than two percent (2.0%) of the aggregate Capital Commitments of all Partners, including the Capital Commitment of the General Partner, determined as of each Closing (the "GP Commitment"). The GP Commitment shall be funded in cash, pro rata with the Capital Contributions of the Limited Partners, and may not be satisfied through any waiver or reduction of the Management Fee. Capital Commitments of Affiliated Limited Partners, which the General Partner currently expects to total approximately $1,250,000 in the aggregate, are made by those Persons in their individual capacities and do not count toward the GP Commitment. No Management Fee or Carried Interest is borne in respect of the GP Commitment.

### 3.3 Subsequent Closings

(a) On or before the Final Closing Date, the General Partner may admit additional Limited Partners, or accept increased Capital Commitments, at one or more closings (each, a "Subsequent Closing" and, with the Initial Closing Date, a "Closing"), each such investor being a "Subsequent Limited Partner" to the extent of its new or increased Capital Commitment.

(b) Each Subsequent Limited Partner shall contribute at its Closing its pro rata share of all prior Capital Contributions, including Management Fees accrued from the Initial Closing Date, as if admitted on the Initial Closing Date.

(c) Each Subsequent Limited Partner shall also pay simple interest at seven percent (7%) per annum on the amount contributed under Section 3.3(b) from the dates the equalized contributions were due. That interest is not a Capital Contribution and is distributed to the Partners admitted at earlier Closings.

(d) Amounts contributed under Section 3.3(b) are distributed to the earlier Partners as a return of Capital Contributions, subject to recall under Section 3.5.

### 3.4 Capital Calls

The General Partner may call Capital Contributions by written notice (a "Drawdown Notice") delivered not less than ten (10) Business Days before the due date, stating the amount called, its purpose and wiring instructions. Capital Contributions are called pro rata in accordance with Unfunded Commitments, except as provided in Section 2.4 or Section 9.3, and no Partner is required to contribute more than its Unfunded Commitment. After the end of the Investment Period, Capital Contributions may be called only to complete Portfolio Investments committed to in writing before its end, for follow-on investments in existing Portfolio Companies, to pay Management Fees, Partnership Expenses and indemnification obligations, and to repay borrowings.

### 3.5 Recallable Distributions

The General Partner may restore to the Unfunded Commitments of the Partners, and call again, amounts distributed under Section 3.3(d), returns of capital from Portfolio Investments realized within eighteen (18) months after acquisition, and proceeds of bridge financings, provided that the aggregate amount restored shall not exceed twenty percent (20%) of the aggregate Capital Commitments of all Partners.

### 3.6 Default; Borrowing

A Partner that fails to fund a Capital Contribution within five (5) Business Days after notice of non-payment may be designated a "Defaulting Partner", shall pay interest at the lesser of twelve percent (12%) per annum and the maximum lawful rate, and may be required to forfeit up to fifty percent (50%) of its Capital Account or to sell its Interest. The Partnership may borrow against the Unfunded Commitments only to bridge Capital Contributions or pay Partnership Expenses, in an aggregate principal amount not exceeding the lesser of fifteen percent (15%) of aggregate Capital Commitments and the aggregate Unfunded Commitments, each borrowing to be repaid within ninety (90) days.

## ARTICLE IV. INVESTMENT PERIOD, KEY PERSONS AND INVESTMENT LIMITATIONS

### 4.1 Investment Period

The "Investment Period" commences on the Initial Closing Date and ends on the earliest of (a) the fifth (5th) anniversary of the Initial Closing Date, (b) the date on which the Management Company or any of its Affiliates first becomes entitled to a management fee from a Successor Fund, (c) its termination under Section 4.3(c), and (d) the date on which a Super Majority in Interest elects by written notice to terminate it, with or without reason.

### 4.2 Key Persons

The "Key Persons" are Delphine Wrexcombe and Tobias Quennell, together with any replacement Key Person approved under Section 4.3(c). During the Investment Period, each Key Person shall devote substantially all of his or her business time and attention to the affairs of the Partnership and the Affiliated Funds. Imogen Ravell, a Principal and a Venture Partner of the Management Company, is not a Key Person, and her death, incapacity, departure or reduced time commitment shall not constitute a Key Person Event.

### 4.3 Key Person Event; Suspension of the Investment Period

(a) A "Key Person Event" occurs if, during the Investment Period, either Key Person dies or becomes permanently incapacitated, ceases to devote substantially all of his or her business time and attention to the affairs of the Partnership and the Affiliated Funds, or ceases to be a managing member of the General Partner. The General Partner shall notify the Limited Partners and the Advisory Committee within five (5) Business Days.

(b) Upon a Key Person Event, the Investment Period is suspended automatically (the "Suspension Period"), and the Partnership shall make no new Portfolio Investment other than (i) investments committed to in writing before the Key Person Event and (ii) follow-on investments in existing Portfolio Companies not exceeding, in the aggregate, ten percent (10%) of aggregate Capital Commitments.

(c) The Suspension Period ends, and the Investment Period resumes, if within one hundred eighty (180) days after the notice under Section 4.3(a) a Majority in Interest approves the continuation of the Investment Period, with or without a replacement Key Person proposed by the General Partner. If no such approval is given within that period, the Investment Period terminates automatically and permanently at the end of the 180-day period.

(d) The Management Fee continues to accrue during any Suspension Period at the rate otherwise applicable under Section 5.1.

### 4.4 Investment Limitations

Without the consent of the Advisory Committee, the Partnership shall not invest (a) more than fifteen percent (15%) of aggregate Capital Commitments in any single Portfolio Company; (b) more than twenty-five percent (25%) of aggregate Capital Commitments in companies headquartered outside the United States and Canada; or (c) in any company in which an Affiliated Fund holds a security. Notwithstanding any consent, assets that are not "qualifying investments" under Rule 203(l)-1 (other than short-term holdings) shall not exceed twenty percent (20%) of aggregate Capital Contributions and Unfunded Commitments.

## ARTICLE V. MANAGEMENT FEE AND EXPENSES

### 5.1 Management Fee

(a) The Partnership shall pay to the Management Company an annual management fee (the "Management Fee"), payable semi-annually in advance on the first Business Day of each January and July, in an amount calculated as follows:

(i) for each of Fee Year 1 through Fee Year 5, inclusive, two and one-half percent (2.50%) per annum of the aggregate Capital Commitments of the Limited Partners, other than Affiliated Limited Partners; and

(ii) for each Fee Year commencing with Fee Year 6, whether or not the Investment Period has then ended, the annual rate applicable for the immediately preceding Fee Year less twenty-five basis points (0.25%), applied to the same aggregate Capital Commitments; provided that in no event shall the annual rate be less than one and one-half percent (1.50%) (the "Floor Rate"). Once reached, the Floor Rate applies for each remaining Fee Year of the term of the Partnership, including any extension under Section 2.3.

(b) Accordingly, the annual rate is 2.25% for Fee Year 6, 2.00% for Fee Year 7, 1.75% for Fee Year 8, and the Floor Rate of 1.50% for Fee Year 9 and each Fee Year thereafter. Because Fee Years begin on February 12, each installment applies the rate for each Fee Year to the days of that Fee Year falling within the relevant semi-annual period. The first installment is payable on the Initial Closing Date for the period ending June 30, 2026, and installments are adjusted after each Subsequent Closing.

(c) No Management Fee is charged in respect of the GP Commitment or the Capital Commitment of any Affiliated Limited Partner, and no Management Fee accrues for any period after the dissolution of the Partnership.

### 5.2 Management Fee Offset

The Management Fee for each semi-annual period is reduced, but not below zero, by one hundred percent (100%) of the Fee Income received during the preceding semi-annual period, any placement fees paid by the Partnership, and Organizational Expenses in excess of the cap in Section 5.3. Any unapplied reduction is carried forward to succeeding periods and, to the extent unapplied at dissolution, is paid to the Partnership in cash.

### 5.3 Expenses

The Partnership bears the expenses of its organization and the offering of Interests ("Organizational Expenses") up to $750,000; any excess, and all placement fees, are borne by the Management Company. The Partnership also bears its operating costs ("Partnership Expenses"), including expenses of evaluating, acquiring, holding and disposing of Portfolio Investments, fees of the Auditor, the Administrator and legal counsel, interest on borrowings, insurance, taxes, indemnification and winding up. The Management Company bears its own overhead, including compensation of the Principals, rent and its own regulatory compliance.

## ARTICLE VI. DISTRIBUTIONS AND ALLOCATIONS

### 6.1 Timing of Distributions

Cash proceeds from the disposition of Portfolio Investments, net of related expenses and reserves ("Net Proceeds"), shall be distributed within ninety (90) days after receipt, except amounts retained for recall under Section 3.5 or applied to repay borrowings. Current income is distributed at least annually.

### 6.2 Distribution Waterfall

Each distribution of Net Proceeds or other distributable cash shall first be apportioned among the Partners in proportion to their aggregate Capital Contributions. The amounts apportioned to the General Partner in respect of the GP Commitment and to each Affiliated Limited Partner are distributed to that Partner. The amount apportioned to each other Limited Partner is distributed between that Limited Partner and the General Partner in the following order of priority:

(a) Return of Capital: first, one hundred percent (100%) to that Limited Partner until its cumulative distributions under this Section 6.2(a) equal its aggregate Capital Contributions as of the date of the distribution, including Capital Contributions applied to unrealized Portfolio Investments, Management Fees, Organizational Expenses and Partnership Expenses;

(b) 80/20 Split: thereafter, until the Step-Up Threshold (determined for the Limited Partners collectively under Section 6.4) has been achieved, eighty percent (80%) to that Limited Partner and twenty percent (20%) to the General Partner; and

(c) 75/25 Split: thereafter, seventy-five percent (75%) to that Limited Partner and twenty-five percent (25%) to the General Partner.

Distributions to the General Partner under Sections 6.2(b) and 6.2(c) are referred to as "Carried Interest".

### 6.3 No Preferred Return

The Limited Partners are not entitled to any preferred return, hurdle rate or other priority return on their Capital Contributions. The General Partner's entitlement to Carried Interest under Section 6.2(b) arises as soon as a Limited Partner has received cumulative distributions equal to its aggregate Capital Contributions, and no catch-up distribution is made to the General Partner at any level of the order of priority in Section 6.2.

### 6.4 Step-Up in the Carried Interest Percentage

(a) The "Step-Up Threshold" is achieved on the first date on which the cumulative distributions to all Limited Partners other than Affiliated Limited Partners under Section 6.2, excluding amounts distributed under Section 3.3(c) or Section 3.3(d), equal or exceed three times (3.0x) the aggregate Capital Contributions of those Limited Partners, that is, three times their paid-in capital.

(b) The twenty-five percent (25%) Carried Interest percentage applies only to amounts distributed after the Step-Up Threshold is achieved. It does not apply retroactively to earlier distributions, and the General Partner is not entitled to any catch-up distribution to bring its cumulative Carried Interest to twenty-five percent (25%) of cumulative profits. If a single distribution causes the Step-Up Threshold to be achieved, the portion necessary to achieve it is distributed under Section 6.2(b) and the balance under Section 6.2(c).

(c) The Step-Up Threshold is measured at the time of each distribution. If, after it has been achieved, additional Capital Contributions cause cumulative distributions to fall below three times aggregate Capital Contributions, Section 6.2(b) again applies until the Step-Up Threshold is again achieved.

### 6.5 Tax Distributions

The General Partner may receive tax distributions, as advances against Carried Interest, sufficient to pay its members' assumed income tax on income allocated in respect of Carried Interest.

### 6.6 General Partner Clawback; No High-Water Mark

(a) Upon the final distribution under Section 11.2, if the General Partner has received cumulative Carried Interest in excess of the amount it would have received had all distributions been made at that time under Sections 6.2 and 6.4, the General Partner shall restore the excess to the Partnership for distribution to the Limited Partners, up to the Carried Interest it received net of taxes paid or payable by its members in respect of it. Each Key Person has delivered a several, and not joint, guaranty of this obligation in proportion to the Carried Interest received by that Key Person.

(b) Because Carried Interest is determined on a cumulative, whole-fund basis under Section 6.2 and is subject to restoration under Section 6.6(a), the Partnership does not maintain a high-water mark, loss carryforward account or similar mechanism, and no such mechanism applies to any Partner.

### 6.7 Distributions in Kind; Allocations

Before dissolution, securities may be distributed in kind only if listed on a national securities exchange and freely transferable, valued at their average closing price over the five (5) trading days before distribution. Net profit and net loss are allocated so that each Partner's Capital Account equals, as nearly as possible, the amount it would receive under Sections 6.2 and 6.4 on a hypothetical liquidation at book value, subject to Section 704(b) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended (the "Code").

## ARTICLE VII. MANAGEMENT AND THE ADVISORY COMMITTEE

### 7.1 Management; Advisory Committee

The General Partner has exclusive authority to manage the Partnership and may delegate investment advisory functions to the Management Company; the Limited Partners take no part in its control. The General Partner shall establish an advisory committee of three (3) to seven (7) representatives of Limited Partners that are not Affiliated Limited Partners (the "Advisory Committee"), which consents to conflict transactions, reviews valuations annually, considers extensions under Section 2.3(b) and waivers under Section 4.4, and owes no fiduciary duty to any Partner.

### 7.2 Successor Funds

No first closing of a Successor Fund shall occur before the earlier of the end of the Investment Period and the date on which at least seventy percent (70%) of aggregate Capital Commitments has been invested, committed or reserved. During the Investment Period, opportunities within the purpose of the Partnership are offered first to the Partnership.

### 7.3 Removal of the General Partner

A Majority in Interest may remove the General Partner for Cause, and a Super Majority in Interest may remove it without Cause, effective upon admission of a substitute general partner approved by the same vote. "Cause" means a final judicial determination of fraud, gross negligence or willful misconduct by the General Partner, the Management Company or a Key Person, a felony conviction of a Key Person, or a material breach of this Agreement uncured thirty (30) days after notice. A General Partner removed for Cause forfeits fifty percent (50%) of its undistributed Carried Interest.

### 7.4 Exculpation and Indemnification

The General Partner, the Management Company, the Principals, Advisory Committee members and their Affiliates are not liable to the Partnership or any Partner, and are indemnified by the Partnership, except for fraud, gross negligence, willful misconduct or material breach of this Agreement. A Limited Partner may be required to return distributions to fund indemnification, up to twenty-five percent (25%) of the distributions it has received.

## ARTICLE VIII. SERVICE PROVIDERS, REPORTS AND VALUATION

### 8.1 Independent Auditor

The General Partner has appointed Brisley Ashcombe LLP, independent certified public accountants, as auditor of the Partnership (the "Auditor"). The Auditor, which also audits the Prior Fund, shall audit the annual financial statements of the Partnership in accordance with auditing standards generally accepted in the United States. The General Partner may replace the Auditor upon prior written notice to the Advisory Committee.

### 8.2 Administrator

The Partnership has engaged Stavely Rook Fund Services, LLC (the "Administrator") to maintain its books and records, compute Capital Accounts, prepare Drawdown Notices and investor statements, and perform anti-money laundering procedures on behalf of the Partnership. The Administrator does not provide investment advice or independently value Portfolio Investments.

### 8.3 Legal Counsel

Quarrington Voss LLP acts as counsel to the Partnership, the General Partner and the Management Company in connection with the organization of the Partnership and the offering of Interests. Quarrington Voss LLP does not represent any Limited Partner, and each Limited Partner acknowledges that it has been advised to consult its own counsel.

### 8.4 Custody; No Prime Broker

Securities of privately held Portfolio Companies are generally uncertificated and recorded on the books of the issuer in the name of the Partnership, and cash and any listed securities are held with a bank or registered broker-dealer selected by the General Partner. The Partnership does not engage, and does not intend to engage, a prime broker, and it will not sell securities short or purchase securities on margin.

### 8.5 Reports; Valuation

The General Partner shall deliver unaudited quarterly financial statements within sixty (60) days after each of the first three fiscal quarters, and audited annual financial statements with a statement of each Partner's Capital Account, the Management Fee, offsets, Partnership Expenses and Carried Interest within one hundred twenty (120) days after each fiscal year. Portfolio Investments are valued at least quarterly at fair value under Accounting Standards Codification Topic 820.

## ARTICLE IX. TRANSFERS AND WITHDRAWALS

### 9.1 Restrictions on Transfer

No Limited Partner may transfer all or any part of its Interest without the prior written consent of the General Partner, which may be withheld in its sole discretion. No transfer is permitted that would cause the Partnership to be a publicly traded partnership under Section 7704 of the Code, require registration under the Investment Company Act of 1940, as amended (the Partnership relying on Section 3(c)(7) of that Act), cause Partnership assets to be "plan assets" under the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974, as amended ("ERISA"), or violate any securities law.

### 9.2 No Right of Withdrawal or Redemption

No Limited Partner has the right to withdraw from the Partnership, or to redeem, withdraw or require the Partnership to repurchase all or any part of its Interest or its Capital Account, at any time or for any reason, including the passage of any period of time. The Interests are not redeemable, and this Agreement does not provide for any redemption date, redemption notice period, lock-up period or limitation on redemptions of the kind found in the governing documents of open-end funds. A Limited Partner receives distributions only as provided in Article VI and Article XI.

### 9.3 Required Withdrawals; ERISA Matters

The General Partner may require a Limited Partner to withdraw, in whole or in part, upon not less than fifteen (15) days' prior written notice, if the General Partner determines on the advice of counsel that its continued participation would cause Partnership assets to be "plan assets" under ERISA, violate any law applicable to it or the Partnership, including the Bank Holding Company Act of 1956, as amended, or cause the Partnership to fail to qualify as a venture capital fund under Rule 203(l)-1. The withdrawing Limited Partner receives cash, securities in kind or a non-interest-bearing note payable from future distributions. A required withdrawal is effected solely at the election of the General Partner and confers no right of withdrawal or redemption on any Limited Partner. The General Partner intends to limit benefit plan investors to less than twenty-five percent (25%) of each class of equity interests or to operate the Partnership as a "venture capital operating company" under 29 C.F.R. Section 2510.3-101(d), and may excuse a benefit plan investor from any Portfolio Investment.

## ARTICLE X. SIDE LETTERS AND MOST FAVORED NATIONS

### 10.1 Side Letters

The General Partner may enter into side letters with one or more Limited Partners that establish rights under, or supplement or alter the terms of, this Agreement with respect to that Limited Partner, without the approval of any other Partner, but no side letter may adversely affect the rights of any other Limited Partner.

### 10.2 Most Favored Nation Election

(a) Within forty-five (45) days after the Final Closing Date, the General Partner shall deliver to each Limited Partner whose Capital Commitment, aggregated with the Capital Commitments of its Affiliates that are Limited Partners, is equal to or greater than $10,000,000 (an "MFN Eligible Limited Partner") a compendium of the provisions of all side letters entered into with other Limited Partners, identifying the Capital Commitment of each counterparty but not its identity.

(b) Each MFN Eligible Limited Partner may elect, by written notice to the General Partner within thirty (30) days after receipt of the compendium, to receive the benefit of any provision granted in a side letter to any other Limited Partner whose Capital Commitment is equal to or less than that of the electing Limited Partner, on the same terms and subject to the same conditions.

(c) The election in Section 10.2(b) does not extend to (i) any provision relating to the legal, regulatory, tax or public-disclosure status or policies of a particular Limited Partner, including provisions concerning public records laws, sovereign immunity and ERISA; (ii) the right to appoint a member of the Advisory Committee; (iii) co-investment rights; (iv) consents to transfers to Affiliates; or (v) any provision granted to an Affiliated Limited Partner. A Limited Partner that is not an MFN Eligible Limited Partner has no election under this Section 10.2.

## ARTICLE XI. DISSOLUTION AND LIQUIDATION

### 11.1 Events of Dissolution

The Partnership shall be dissolved upon the first to occur of (a) the expiration of its term under Section 2.3, as extended; (b) a determination by the General Partner, with Advisory Committee consent, after the disposition of all Portfolio Investments; (c) the election of a Super Majority in Interest; (d) the bankruptcy or withdrawal of the General Partner, unless within ninety (90) days a Majority in Interest continues the Partnership with a substitute general partner; or (e) judicial dissolution under Section 17-802 of the Act.

### 11.2 Winding Up and Final Distribution

Upon dissolution, the General Partner, or a liquidator appointed by a Majority in Interest, shall wind up the Partnership and apply its assets first to creditors, then to reasonable reserves, and then to the Partners under Sections 6.2 and 6.4, after which Section 6.6(a) is applied.
