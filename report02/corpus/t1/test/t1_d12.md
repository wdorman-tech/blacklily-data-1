## SECTION 3. INVESTMENT PROGRAMME

### 3.1 Objective and Strategy

The Fund seeks capital appreciation over a full commodity cycle with low correlation to equity and fixed income markets, and does not seek to track or outperform any commodity index. The Investment Manager trades a discretionary, fundamentally driven programme in exchange-traded futures, options on futures and cleared swaps on energy, metals and agricultural commodities. The core of the programme is relative value (calendar spreads, refining and crush margins, and location spreads), with outright positions where supply and demand balances diverge from market pricing. The Fund does not intend to make or take physical delivery, and physically settled positions are ordinarily rolled or closed before the first notice day.

### 3.2 Risk Management and Restrictions

Positions are sized by value at risk, scenario analysis and contract liquidity, and the margin-to-equity ratio is expected to range between 10% and 25%. The Fund will not: (a) commit more than 40% of Net Asset Value to initial margin and option premiums, measured when a position is established; (b) acquire physical commodities except where delivery is unavoidable, and then only pending disposal; (c) invest in other collective investment schemes, except government money market funds; or (d) borrow for investment purposes. Cash not required as margin is held in United States Treasury bills and government money market funds.

## SECTION 4. FEES AND EXPENSES

### 4.1 Management Fee

The Fund pays the Investment Manager a management fee (the "Management Fee") in respect of each Class, calculated as of the first Business Day of each calendar month and payable monthly in advance, at one-twelfth of 2.00% (a rate of 2.00% per annum) of the Net Asset Value of the Class A Shares and one-twelfth of 1.50% (a rate of 1.50% per annum) of the Net Asset Value of the Class B Shares, in each case determined as of the beginning of the month before deduction of the Management Fee then payable and before accrual of any Performance Fee. The Management Fee is prorated for any Share issued or redeemed other than at a month end. No Management Fee is charged on the Management Shares.

### 4.2 Performance Fee

As of the end of each Calculation Period, the Fund pays the Investment Manager a performance fee (the "Performance Fee") equal to 20% of the Net New Appreciation of each Class A Share and 15% of the Net New Appreciation of each Class B Share, calculated separately for each Series. "Calculation Period" means each calendar year ending 31 December, except that the first Calculation Period of a Series begins on its issue date and a Calculation Period ends for a redeemed Share on its Redemption Day. "Net New Appreciation" means the excess, if any, of the Net Asset Value per Share of the Series at the end of the Calculation Period (after the Management Fee and expenses, before the Performance Fee accrued for that period) over the High Water Mark. No hurdle rate, preferred return or index-relative threshold applies. The Performance Fee is accrued monthly, paid within 30 days after the end of each Calculation Period and not refundable.

### 4.3 High Water Mark

The "High Water Mark" of a Share is the greater of US$1,000 and the Net Asset Value per Share of its Series at the end of the most recent Calculation Period for which that Series paid a Performance Fee. The High Water Mark is never reset. A Series below its High Water Mark must recoup the shortfall in full before any further Performance Fee accrues, however many Calculation Periods that takes, so losses are carried forward indefinitely. Consolidation under Section 2.2 neither creates nor eliminates any loss carryforward.

### 4.4 Expenses, Waivers and Rebates

Organisational expenses of approximately US$285,000 are amortised over 60 months for Net Asset Value purposes, a departure from United States generally accepted accounting principles ("US GAAP") that may cause the Auditor to qualify its opinion. The Fund bears brokerage, clearing and exchange fees, interest, service provider fees, independent Directors' fees (US$25,000 per year each), insurance and CIMA fees. The Investment Manager may, by rebate, waive or reduce its fees for particular Shareholders, including its principals and employees, without affecting the fees borne by any other Shareholder.

## SECTION 6. REDEMPTIONS

### 6.1 Redemption Days and Notice

Subject to Section 6.2, a Shareholder may redeem some or all of its Shares as of the last Business Day of each calendar quarter (each a "Redemption Day") on not less than 90 calendar days' prior written notice to the Administrator (a "Redemption Notice"). A late Redemption Notice is treated as a request for the next Redemption Day. The Directors may waive the notice period generally or in any particular case. Shares are redeemed at the Net Asset Value per Share of the relevant Series as of the Redemption Day, after accrual of the Performance Fee and deduction of any early redemption charge. A partial redemption may not reduce a holding below US$500,000 of Class A Shares or US$5,000,000 of Class B Shares.

### 6.2 Lock-Up

Class A Shares are subject to a soft lock-up: a Class A Share may be redeemed as of a Redemption Day falling before the first anniversary of its issue only on payment of an early redemption charge of 3% of the redemption proceeds, retained by the Fund for the benefit of the remaining Shareholders. Class B Shares are subject to a hard lock-up: a Class B Share may not be redeemed as of any Redemption Day falling before the first anniversary of its issue. Each Series is treated separately, and Shares are redeemed first-in, first-out. The Directors may waive the early redemption charge, and may waive the Class B lock-up only where continued holding would be unlawful for the Shareholder.

### 6.3 Payment

Redemption proceeds are ordinarily paid in United States dollars within 30 days after the Redemption Day. On a redemption of 95% or more of a Shareholder's Shares, the Fund may hold back up to 5% of the proceeds until completion of the audit for the fiscal year that includes the Redemption Day and pay the balance, adjusted for any audit revision, without interest within 30 days thereafter. Proceeds are not paid in kind.

### 6.4 No Gate

The Fund does not impose any limit on the aggregate amount of redemptions that may be made as of any Redemption Day, whether at the level of the Fund or of an individual Shareholder, and Redemption Notices are not scaled back or deferred by reason of the aggregate amount requested. This Section does not restrict the power of suspension under Section 6.5 or the holdback under Section 6.3.

### 6.5 Suspension

The Directors may suspend the calculation of Net Asset Value and the issue and redemption of Shares while: (a) an exchange or clearing house material to the Fund's positions is closed or restricted; (b) daily price limits prevent the orderly liquidation of a material part of those positions; (c) the Fund's investments cannot be fairly valued; or (d) a resolution to wind up the Fund has been proposed. Redemptions resume as of the first Redemption Day after a suspension ends.

### 6.6 Compulsory Redemption and Transfers

The Directors may compulsorily redeem Shares on not less than 10 calendar days' prior written notice, or immediately where a holding is unlawful or the holder has ceased to be an Eligible Investor. Shares may be transferred only with the prior written consent of the Directors and only to an Eligible Investor.

## SECTION 10. CERTAIN RISK FACTORS

The following is not a complete list of the risks of an investment in the Fund.

### 10.1 Market and Trading Risks

**Volatility and leverage.** Commodity prices respond to supply and demand, weather, trade and monetary policy and political events. Low futures margins permit high leverage, and a small price movement may cause losses exceeding the margin posted.

**Daily limits.** Once a price has moved its daily limit, positions may be impossible to liquidate, possibly for several days.

**Position limits.** Federal and exchange speculative position limits aggregate accounts under common control and may require the Investment Manager to reduce or forgo positions for the Fund.

### 10.2 Reliance on Key Personnel

The Fund's performance depends to a significant degree on the skills of a small number of individuals, in particular Mr. Rennard. The loss of the services of Mr. Rennard or of other members of the investment committee could adversely affect the Fund.

### 10.3 Counterparty Risk

On the insolvency of the Clearing Broker, the Fund could share pro rata in any shortfall in customer segregated funds and suffer delay in recovering its assets. Assets held by the Prime Broker may be subject to its lien.

### 10.4 Risks Relating to the Shares

Shares may be redeemed only quarterly, on prior written notice, and subject to the lock-up, the early redemption charge, the holdback and suspension. The Performance Fee is charged on unrealised gains and may encourage greater risk-taking. The Directors may vary the terms of this Memorandum by supplement under Section 12.2.
