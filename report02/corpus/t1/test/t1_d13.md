## SECTION 2. AMENDED AND RESTATED AGREEMENT OF LIMITED PARTNERSHIP OF QUARRENDON VALE INFRASTRUCTURE SECONDARIES FUND II, L.P.

This AMENDED AND RESTATED AGREEMENT OF LIMITED PARTNERSHIP (this "Agreement") of Quarrendon Vale Infrastructure Secondaries Fund II, L.P. (the "Partnership") is entered into as of April 14, 2026 by Quarrendon Vale ISF II GP, L.L.C., a Delaware limited liability company, as general partner (the "General Partner"), Tamsin Ellery, as the withdrawing initial limited partner, and the Persons executing this Agreement as limited partners (the "Limited Partners" and, with the General Partner, the "Partners"). The parties amend and restate in its entirety the Agreement of Limited Partnership of the Partnership dated November 3, 2025 (the "Original Agreement") and agree as follows.

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

### 1.1 Defined Terms

"Acquisition Cost" means, for any Investment, the purchase price paid by the Partnership (including deferred purchase price, as and when paid), plus all amounts later funded by the Partnership in respect of it (including capital calls of the related Portfolio Fund) and capitalized transaction costs.

"Affiliated LP" means any Limited Partner that is an Affiliate of the General Partner, including any vehicle through which the Key Persons or employees of the Manager participate in the GP Commitment.

"Carried Interest" means distributions to the General Partner under Sections 6.2(c) and 6.2(d)(ii).

"Final Closing Date" means the date of the last Subsequent Closing, which shall occur not later than the first anniversary of the Initial Closing Date.

"Fund I" means Quarrendon Vale Infrastructure Secondaries Fund I, L.P.

"Initial Closing Date" means April 14, 2026.

"Key Persons" means Helena Strathdee, Marcus Vellacott and Priya Lathcombe, and any replacement approved under Section 8.3.

"Majority in Interest of the Limited Partners" means Limited Partners, other than Defaulting Partners and Affiliated LPs, whose Capital Commitments exceed 50% of the Capital Commitments of all such Limited Partners, and "Two-Thirds in Interest of the Limited Partners" has the correlative meaning at 66 2/3%.

"Net Invested Capital" means, as of any date, the aggregate Acquisition Cost of all Investments not Disposed of as of that date, reduced by the Acquisition Cost of any Investment, or portion of an Investment, that has been permanently written down, to the extent of the write-down.

"Portfolio Fund" means any fund, continuation vehicle or other collective investment vehicle in which the Partnership holds an interest.

"Preferred Return" means, for each Limited Partner, a return of 8% per annum, compounded annually, on the balance of its Capital Contributions outstanding from time to time, from the date each is due until it is returned under Section 6.2(a).

"Successor Fund" means any fund, other than a Parallel Fund, whose principal investment objective is substantially similar to that of the Partnership and which is sponsored by the Manager or an Affiliate of the Manager.

"Transaction Fees" means transaction, break-up, monitoring, directors' and similar fees received by the Manager, the General Partner or their Affiliates in connection with actual or proposed Investments.

"Unfunded Commitment" means a Partner's Capital Commitment less its aggregate Capital Contributions, increased by amounts recallable under Section 4.2.

Other capitalized terms have the meanings given where they first appear.

## ARTICLE II. ORGANIZATION

### 2.1 Formation, Name and Offices

The Partnership was formed on November 3, 2025 as a limited partnership under the Delaware Revised Uniform Limited Partnership Act (the "Act") under the name "Quarrendon Vale Infrastructure Secondaries Fund II, L.P." Its principal office is in Minneapolis, Minnesota, and its registered office in Delaware is as stated in its certificate of limited partnership.

### 2.2 Purpose; Closed-End Structure

The purpose of the Partnership is to make secondary acquisitions of interests in Portfolio Funds and sponsor-led vehicles holding infrastructure assets, to a limited extent to make primary commitments and co-investments, and to hold, hedge and dispose of those Investments. The Partnership is a closed-end fund. Limited Partners contribute capital only as called under Article IV, and Interests are not redeemable at the option of any Partner. The Partnership is not part of a master-feeder structure.

### 2.3 Term

The term of the Partnership shall continue until the twelfth (12th) anniversary of the Final Closing Date, unless the Partnership is sooner dissolved under Article XII. The General Partner may extend the term for up to two (2) successive periods of one (1) year each: the first such extension in its discretion, by notice to the Limited Partners given not less than sixty (60) days before the scheduled expiration, and the second only with the prior written consent of the Advisory Committee. The General Partner has no right to extend the term beyond the second such extension, and the Partnership shall not continue, other than to wind up under Section 12.2, after the fourteenth (14th) anniversary of the Final Closing Date.

### 2.4 Parallel Funds and Alternative Investment Vehicles

The General Partner may form one or more entities to accommodate the legal, tax or regulatory requirements of particular investors (each, a "Parallel Fund"). Each Parallel Fund shall invest alongside the Partnership pro rata to available commitments on terms substantially similar in all material respects to this Agreement. The General Partner may make any Investment through an alternative investment vehicle where advisable for tax, legal or regulatory reasons.

## ARTICLE III. PARTNERS AND CAPITAL COMMITMENTS

### 3.1 Minimum Capital Commitment

The minimum Capital Commitment of a Limited Partner is $10,000,000; provided that the General Partner may in its sole discretion accept Capital Commitments of lesser amounts, but not less than $5,000,000, and may aggregate the Capital Commitments of Affiliated investors for this purpose. Aggregate Capital Commitments to the Partnership and the Parallel Funds shall not exceed $2,000,000,000.

### 3.2 General Partner Commitment

The General Partner shall make, directly or through its Affiliates (including the Manager, the Key Persons and vehicles controlled by them), Capital Commitments to the Partnership and the Parallel Funds that are, in the aggregate, not less than two percent (2%) of the aggregate Capital Commitments of all partners of the Partnership and the Parallel Funds, determined as of the Final Closing Date (the "GP Commitment"). The GP Commitment shall be funded in cash, pro rata with the Limited Partners, and shall not be satisfied through any waiver or deferral of the Management Fee. Any portion made by an Affiliate other than the General Partner shall be held as the Interest of an Affiliated LP.

### 3.3 Subsequent Closings

The General Partner may admit additional Limited Partners, or accept increased Capital Commitments, at closings held after the Initial Closing Date and on or before the Final Closing Date (each, a "Subsequent Closing"). Each Partner so admitted shall contribute its pro rata share of all prior Capital Contributions, plus interest on that share at 6% per annum from the due dates of the prior contributions (the "Additional Amount"). The Additional Amount is not a Capital Contribution and shall be distributed to the previously admitted Partners.

## ARTICLE IV. CAPITAL CONTRIBUTIONS

### 4.1 Drawdowns

The General Partner shall call Capital Contributions pro rata to Unfunded Commitments by written drawdown notice delivered not less than ten (10) Business Days before the due date, stating the amount, due date and purpose of the call. After the Investment Period, Capital Contributions may be called only for (a) Investments committed to in writing before its end, (b) unfunded commitments of Portfolio Funds and deferred purchase price, (c) follow-on investments not exceeding 15% of aggregate Capital Commitments, and (d) the Management Fee, Partnership Expenses and repayment of borrowings.

### 4.2 Recallable Distributions

The General Partner may recall (a) distributions returning Capital Contributions used for an Investment Disposed of within eighteen (18) months after its acquisition, up to 20% of aggregate Capital Commitments, and (b) distributions later required to satisfy an indemnification obligation of the Partnership, for three (3) years after distribution.

### 4.3 Defaulting Partners

If a Limited Partner fails to pay a Capital Contribution when due and does not cure the failure within five (5) Business Days after notice, the General Partner may declare it a "Defaulting Partner" and may charge interest at 12% per annum on the unpaid amount, withhold its distributions, cause its Interest to be sold at 50% of its Capital Account, and bar it from future Investments, in addition to any remedy at law.

### 4.4 Borrowing

The Partnership may borrow, guarantee the obligations of its holding vehicles and pledge its Unfunded Commitments and Investments, provided that aggregate outstanding borrowings and guarantees shall not exceed 25% of aggregate Capital Commitments and each borrowing made in anticipation of Capital Contributions shall be repaid within 365 days. The Preferred Return accrues only from the due date of the Capital Contribution that repays a borrowing. The Partnership's credit facility is currently provided by Ostrander Keel Bank, N.A.

## ARTICLE V. CAPITAL ACCOUNTS AND ALLOCATIONS

### 5.1 Capital Accounts and Allocations

A capital account (a "Capital Account") shall be maintained for each Partner under Treasury Regulations Section 1.704-1(b)(2)(iv). Net income and loss for each fiscal year shall be allocated so that each Partner's Capital Account equals, as nearly as possible, the amount that would be distributed to it under Section 6.2 on a hypothetical liquidation at book value, subject to Section 704(c) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended (the "Code").

## ARTICLE VI. DISTRIBUTIONS

### 6.1 Timing of Distributions

The Partnership shall distribute net proceeds of Dispositions within ninety (90) days after receipt, and net current income, including operating distributions from Portfolio Funds, at least quarterly, in each case after reasonable reserves.

### 6.2 Distribution Waterfall

Distributable proceeds shall be apportioned among the Partners in proportion to their Capital Contributions in respect of the Investments giving rise to them. Amounts apportioned to the General Partner and the Affiliated LPs shall be distributed to them. Amounts apportioned to each other Limited Partner shall be distributed, on a cumulative whole-fund basis:

(a) Return of Capital: first, 100% to that Limited Partner until it has received its aggregate Capital Contributions for all purposes;

(b) Preferred Return: second, 100% to that Limited Partner until it has received its Preferred Return;

(c) Catch-Up: third, 100% to the General Partner until it has received under this Section 6.2(c) 12.5% of the sum of distributions to that Limited Partner under Section 6.2(b) and to the General Partner under this Section 6.2(c); and

(d) Final Split: thereafter, (i) 87.5% to that Limited Partner and (ii) 12.5% to the General Partner.

### 6.3 General Partner Clawback

Upon the final distribution under Section 12.2, if the General Partner has received Carried Interest in excess of its entitlement under Section 6.2 determined at that time, or any Limited Partner has not received its aggregate Capital Contributions plus its Preferred Return, the General Partner shall restore the greater of those amounts, but not more than the Carried Interest it received less taxes on it at the highest combined marginal rate applicable to an individual resident in Minneapolis, Minnesota. Each Key Person has delivered a several, not joint, guarantee of his or her pro rata share of this obligation.

### 6.4 Cumulative Determination

All determinations under Section 6.2 are made cumulatively over the life of the Partnership and across all Investments. No high water mark, loss carryforward or similar mechanism applies to the Carried Interest; Section 6.3 is the exclusive adjustment for Carried Interest distributed in excess of entitlement.

### 6.5 Tax Distributions; Distributions in Kind

The General Partner may receive distributions to pay taxes on income allocated to it in respect of the Carried Interest, treated as advances against future Carried Interest. The Partnership shall not distribute Portfolio Fund interests or other non-cash assets before dissolution without the consent of the Advisory Committee.

## ARTICLE VII. MANAGEMENT FEE AND EXPENSES

### 7.1 Management Fee

The Partnership shall pay the Manager, quarterly in advance, a management fee (the "Management Fee") with respect to each Limited Partner other than an Affiliated LP equal to (a) for each calendar quarter from the Initial Closing Date through the last day of the Investment Period, 0.25% (1.00% per annum) of that Limited Partner's Capital Commitment; and (b) for each calendar quarter thereafter, 0.1875% (0.75% per annum) of that Limited Partner's pro rata share of Net Invested Capital as of the first day of the quarter. The fee is prorated for partial quarters. A Partner admitted at a Subsequent Closing pays the Management Fee from the Initial Closing Date. No Management Fee shall be charged for any period after the twelfth anniversary of the Final Closing Date, including any extension under Section 2.3, or on the GP Commitment.

### 7.2 Management Fee Offset

The Management Fee for each quarter shall be reduced by (a) 100% of the Partnership's share of Transaction Fees received in the preceding quarter, (b) placement fees borne by the Partnership and (c) Organizational Expenses in excess of the cap in Section 7.3. Excess reductions carry forward, and any balance at dissolution is refunded to the Limited Partners.

### 7.3 Organizational and Partnership Expenses

The Partnership and the Parallel Funds shall bear formation and offering expenses ("Organizational Expenses") up to $2,500,000 in the aggregate. The Partnership shall bear its operating expenses ("Partnership Expenses"), including diligence and transfer costs for Investments, interest on borrowings, hedging costs, fees of the Administrator, the auditors and legal counsel, Advisory Committee expenses, insurance and extraordinary expenses. The Manager bears its own overhead and personnel costs.

## ARTICLE VIII. MANAGEMENT OF THE PARTNERSHIP

### 8.1 Authority of the General Partner; Manager

Management of the Partnership is vested exclusively in the General Partner. No Limited Partner shall take part in the control of the Partnership's business. The General Partner has delegated investment management authority to Quarrendon Vale Capital Partners, LLC (the "Manager") under an investment management agreement, and remains responsible for its performance.

### 8.2 Investment Period

The "Investment Period" begins on the Initial Closing Date and ends on the earliest of (a) the fourth (4th) anniversary of the Final Closing Date, (b) the date on which the General Partner notifies the Limited Partners that substantially all Capital Commitments have been invested, committed or reserved, (c) termination under Section 8.3 and (d) the date on which the Manager or an Affiliate first charges a management fee to a Successor Fund.

### 8.3 Key Person Event

If at any time during the Investment Period fewer than two (2) of the Key Persons devote substantially all of their business time to the affairs of the Partnership, the Parallel Funds, Fund I and the Manager (a "Key Person Event"), (a) the General Partner shall notify the Limited Partners within ten (10) Business Days; (b) the Investment Period shall be automatically suspended, and the Partnership shall make no new Investment other than one committed to in writing before the Key Person Event; and (c) the suspension shall end if, within one hundred eighty (180) days after the Key Person Event, Two-Thirds in Interest of the Limited Partners approve replacement Key Persons proposed by the General Partner or otherwise elect to reinstate the Investment Period. Absent such approval or election, the Investment Period terminates at the end of that period.

### 8.4 Investment Limitations; Successor Funds

Without Advisory Committee consent, the Partnership shall not invest more than 20% of aggregate Capital Commitments in any single Portfolio Fund, more than 10% in primary commitments, or anything in Fund I or another fund sponsored by the Manager. The Partnership shall not sell short, buy on margin or use derivatives other than for bona fide hedging. Until the earlier of the end of the Investment Period and the investment, commitment or reservation of 75% of aggregate Capital Commitments, the Manager and its Affiliates shall not hold a first closing of a Successor Fund. Opportunities suitable for the Partnership and Fund I are allocated under the Manager's written allocation policy.

## ARTICLE IX. ADVISORY COMMITTEE; EXCULPATION

### 9.1 Advisory Committee

The General Partner shall appoint an advisory committee (the "Advisory Committee") of three (3) to seven (7) representatives of Limited Partners that are not Affiliates of the Manager. The Advisory Committee shall (a) review, and where required consent to, conflicts of interest, including cross-transactions with Fund I; (b) review valuations and approve departures from the valuation policy; (c) consider consents requested under Sections 6.5 and 8.4; and (d) consider the second extension of the term under Section 2.3. Members owe no fiduciary duty to the Partnership or any Partner.

### 9.2 Exculpation and Indemnification

None of the General Partner, the Manager, the Key Persons or their Affiliates, members, officers and employees (each, a "Covered Person") is liable to the Partnership or any Partner for any act or omission, and each shall be indemnified by the Partnership, except for fraud, gross negligence, willful misconduct, material breach of this Agreement or knowing violation of law.

## ARTICLE X. WITHDRAWAL AND REDEMPTION

### 10.1 No Withdrawal or Redemption

No Limited Partner shall have the right to withdraw from the Partnership, or to withdraw or redeem all or any part of its Interest or Capital Account, before the dissolution and winding up of the Partnership. The Partnership has no redemption dates, lock-up period, redemption notice requirement or gate, and none shall be implied from any provision of this Agreement governing Transfers.

### 10.2 Required Withdrawal

The General Partner may require a Limited Partner to withdraw, in whole or in part, on not less than ten (10) days' prior written notice, if it determines on advice of counsel that the Limited Partner's continued participation would cause the Partnership's assets to be "plan assets" under ERISA, violate applicable law or cause a material adverse regulatory or tax consequence to the Partnership. The General Partner may settle a required withdrawal in cash, by promissory note or by arranging a Transfer at a price equal to the Capital Account. This Section 10.2 confers no right on any Limited Partner.

## ARTICLE XI. TRANSFERS

### 11.1 Consent and Notice

No Limited Partner may sell, assign, pledge or otherwise transfer any part of its Interest (a "Transfer") without the prior written consent of the General Partner, which may be withheld in its sole discretion, except that consent shall not be unreasonably withheld for a Transfer to an Affiliate that assumes the transferor's Unfunded Commitment. A Limited Partner proposing a Transfer shall give the General Partner not less than forty-five (45) days' prior written notice. Permitted Transfers take effect on the first day of the following calendar quarter, at the cost of the transferor and transferee.

### 11.2 Tax and Regulatory Conditions

The General Partner shall not consent to any Transfer that would (a) cause the Partnership to be a publicly traded partnership under Section 7704 of the Code, and to that end, other than Transfers described in Treasury Regulations Section 1.7704-1(e), Transfers in any taxable year shall not exceed 2% of the total interests in Partnership capital or profits, consistent with Treasury Regulations Section 1.7704-1(j); (b) require registration of the Partnership under the Investment Company Act of 1940; or (c) cause Benefit Plan Investors to hold 25% or more of any class of Interests.

## ARTICLE XII. DISSOLUTION AND LIQUIDATION

### 12.1 Events of Dissolution

The Partnership shall be dissolved upon the first of (a) expiration of its term under Section 2.3, as extended in accordance with that Section; (b) the election of the General Partner with the consent of a Majority in Interest of the Limited Partners; (c) the withdrawal, removal or bankruptcy of the General Partner, unless Two-Thirds in Interest of the Limited Partners admit a successor within ninety (90) days; (d) the Disposition of all Investments after the Investment Period; or (e) judicial dissolution under Section 17-802 of the Act.

### 12.2 Winding Up

Upon dissolution, the General Partner, or a liquidator appointed by a Majority in Interest of the Limited Partners, shall wind up the Partnership and use commercially reasonable efforts to Dispose of all Investments within two (2) years. Proceeds shall be applied to creditors, then to reserves, and then to the Partners under Section 6.2. No Management Fee accrues during winding up.

## ARTICLE XIII. BOOKS, REPORTS AND SERVICE PROVIDERS

### 13.1 Books, Valuation and Reports

The Partnership shall keep its books in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles, applying ASC 946, on a fiscal year ending December 31, and shall value Investments at fair value under ASC 820, ordinarily by reference to the most recent net asset value reported by each Portfolio Fund sponsor, adjusted for subsequent cash flows and known events. The General Partner shall deliver to each Limited Partner unaudited quarterly reports within sixty (60) days after each of the first three fiscal quarters, audited annual financial statements within one hundred twenty (120) days after fiscal year-end, and Schedules K-1 within one hundred eighty (180) days after fiscal year-end.

### 13.2 Independent Auditors

The financial statements of the Partnership shall be audited annually by an independent certified public accounting firm selected by the General Partner. The General Partner has appointed Merriwick Sowerby LLP as the independent auditors of the Partnership, commencing with the fiscal year ending December 31, 2026, and shall notify the Limited Partners of any change of auditors within thirty (30) days.

### 13.3 Administrator

The General Partner has engaged Orrelby Fund Services (US) LLC (the "Administrator") to maintain the register of Partners and the Capital Accounts, process drawdowns and distributions, perform anti-money laundering procedures and prepare Partner statements.

### 13.4 Custody; No Prime Broker

The Partnership does not engage, and does not intend to engage, a prime broker. Its interests in Portfolio Funds are uncertificated and recorded in its name, or that of an alternative investment vehicle, on the books of the Portfolio Funds, and its cash is held in its name at one or more U.S. banks, in compliance with Rule 206(4)-2 under the Investment Advisers Act of 1940. The Partnership may enter into currency forwards under ISDA master agreements; its hedging counterparty is currently Ostrander Keel Bank, N.A., which acts only in that capacity and as lender under Section 4.4.

## ARTICLE XIV. SIDE LETTERS

### 14.1 Side Letters

The General Partner may enter into a side letter with any Limited Partner that establishes rights under, or supplements or alters, this Agreement as to that Limited Partner, without the approval of any other Partner.

### 14.2 Most Favored Nations Election

Within thirty (30) days after the Final Closing Date, the General Partner shall furnish to each Limited Partner whose Capital Commitment, aggregated with those of its Affiliates and accounts under common investment control, equals or exceeds $50,000,000 a compilation of all side letter provisions granted to any other Limited Partner whose Capital Commitment is equal to or less than its own. Each such Limited Partner may elect, within thirty (30) days after receipt, the benefit of any such provision, other than provisions (a) relating to the tax, legal, regulatory or policy status of another Limited Partner, (b) granting an Advisory Committee seat, (c) granting co-investment rights, (d) consenting to Transfers to Affiliates or (e) granted in consideration of admission at the Initial Closing Date. A Limited Partner whose Capital Commitment is less than $50,000,000 has no election under this Section 14.2.

## ARTICLE XV. MISCELLANEOUS

In the event of any conflict or inconsistency between this Agreement and the Summary of Principal Terms or any other offering material, this Agreement shall control.

### 15.2 Amendments

The General Partner may amend this Agreement without consent to reflect admissions and withdrawals, cure ambiguities or implement an agreement under Section 14.1.

### 15.3 Legal Counsel

Section 2510.3-101 as modified by Section 3(42) of ERISA. The Partnership shall be classified as a partnership for U.S. federal income tax purposes, and the General Partner is its "partnership representative" under Section 6223 of the Code. The General Partner shall use commercially reasonable efforts to hold through blocker corporations any Investment expected to generate material unrelated business taxable income or effectively connected income.

### 15.5 Governing Law; Jurisdiction
