This Amended and Restated Limited Partnership Agreement (this "Agreement") of Tamberlow Capital Partners III, L.P., a Delaware limited partnership (the "Partnership"), is made as of January 26, 2026 by Tamberlow Associates III, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, as general partner (the "General Partner"), and the Persons admitted as limited partners under this Agreement (the "Limited Partners" and, with the General Partner, the "Partners"), and amends and restates in its entirety the Limited Partnership Agreement of the Partnership dated as of April 7, 2025.

## ARTICLE I. ORGANIZATION AND DEFINITIONS

### 1.1 Definitions

In this Agreement:

"Affiliate" means, as to any Person, any Person controlling, controlled by or under common control with it, but no Portfolio Company is an Affiliate of the General Partner.

"Business Day" means any day on which banks in New York, New York are open.

"Commitment" means the amount a Partner has agreed to contribute, as shown in the Schedule of Partners, and "Capital Contribution" means cash contributed in respect of a Commitment.

"Commitment Period" means the period from the Initial Closing Date to the earliest of (a) the fifth anniversary of the Initial Closing Date, (b) its termination under Section 5.5 or Section 7.2, and (c) the date the Manager or an Affiliate first becomes entitled to a management fee from a Successor Fund.

"Initial Closing Date" means the date Limited Partners unaffiliated with the General Partner are first admitted.

"Majority in Interest" and "Supermajority in Interest" mean Limited Partners (other than Defaulting Partners and Affiliates of the General Partner) holding more than 50%, or at least 66 2/3%, respectively, of the Commitments of all such Limited Partners.

"Manager" means Tamberlow Management, L.P., a registered investment adviser.

"Net Invested Capital" means Capital Contributions applied to the cost of Investments not yet disposed of, less permanent write-downs.

"Person" means any individual or entity.

"Portfolio Company" means any Person in which the Partnership holds an equity, equity-related or debt investment (an "Investment").

"Unfunded Commitment" means a Partner's Commitment less its Capital Contributions, plus amounts restored to it under this Agreement.

### 1.2 Name, Office and Purpose

The name of the Partnership is Tamberlow Capital Partners III, L.P., and its principal office is at 212 Lowry Crest Avenue, Suite 600, Minneapolis, Minnesota 55401. The purpose of the Partnership is to make, hold and dispose of privately negotiated control and significant minority investments in middle-market businesses located principally in the United States and Canada, and to engage in any lawful activity incidental thereto, with all powers of a limited partnership under the Delaware Revised Uniform Limited Partnership Act (the "Act").

### 1.3 Term

The Partnership shall continue until the tenth anniversary of the Initial Closing Date, subject to extension by the General Partner for two successive one-year periods, the first in its discretion and the second with the consent of the Advisory Committee. The Partnership shall dissolve earlier upon (a) the disposition of all Investments, (b) the removal, bankruptcy or withdrawal of the General Partner unless a successor is admitted under Section 7.2, (c) the election of a Majority in Interest after a Key Person Event not cured under Section 5.5, or (d) judicial dissolution under the Act. The General Partner shall wind up the Partnership within two years after dissolution and distribute the proceeds under Section 3.1.

## ARTICLE II. CAPITAL CONTRIBUTIONS

### 2.1 Commitments and Closings

Each Partner shall contribute cash to the Partnership up to its Commitment, as set forth in the Schedule of Partners maintained by the General Partner and updated through January 26, 2026. The General Partner may admit additional Limited Partners, or accept increased Commitments, at subsequent closings held within twelve months after the Initial Closing Date, provided that aggregate Commitments shall not exceed $1,750,000,000. Each such Limited Partner shall contribute its pro rata share of prior Capital Contributions plus interest at 8% per annum, which interest is paid to earlier-admitted Partners and does not reduce its Unfunded Commitment. The General Partner and its Affiliates shall maintain Commitments of at least 2% of aggregate Commitments, funded in cash.

### 2.2 Capital Calls

The General Partner may call Capital Contributions pro rata in accordance with Unfunded Commitments by written notice (a "Capital Call Notice") delivered at least ten Business Days before the due date. Each Capital Call Notice shall state the amount called, the due date, the purpose of the call and any portion attributable to the Management Fee or Partnership Expenses. After the Commitment Period, capital may be called only for the Management Fee, Partnership Expenses and indemnification obligations, for Investments committed to in writing during the Commitment Period, and for follow-on Investments not exceeding 15% of aggregate Commitments. Capital not applied to its stated purpose within 90 days shall be returned and restored to Unfunded Commitments.

### 2.3 Defaulting Partners

A Limited Partner that fails to pay a Capital Contribution within ten Business Days after written notice of the failure is a "Defaulting Partner." In addition to any other remedy, the General Partner may (a) charge interest on the unpaid amount at the prime rate plus 5% per annum, (b) suspend its rights to distributions and to vote, (c) sell its interest at a price the General Partner determines, which may be below fair value, and (d) cause it to forfeit up to 50% of its capital account for reallocation to the other Partners.

### 2.4 Subscription Credit Facility

The Partnership may borrow under revolving credit facilities with unaffiliated lenders secured by the Unfunded Commitments and the right to call Capital Contributions (each, a "Subscription Facility"), provided that (a) borrowings outstanding under all Subscription Facilities shall not exceed the lesser of 25% of aggregate Commitments and aggregate Unfunded Commitments, and (b) each borrowing shall be repaid within 364 days. Each Limited Partner shall, on request, confirm to a lender its obligation to fund Capital Contributions without setoff or defense. For purposes of Section 3.1(b), a Capital Contribution applied to repay a borrowing is treated as made on the date of the borrowing, and each quarterly report under Section 8.1 shall state the net internal rate of return with and without Subscription Facilities.

## ARTICLE III. DISTRIBUTIONS

### 3.1 Distribution Waterfall

Net proceeds from the disposition of, and current income from, each Investment shall be apportioned among the Partners in proportion to their Capital Contributions for that Investment, and the share of each Limited Partner shall be distributed as follows:

(a) first, 100% to the Limited Partner until it has received its Capital Contributions for all realized Investments and its share of the Management Fee and Partnership Expenses;

(b) second, 100% to the Limited Partner until it has received a preferred return of 8% per annum, compounded annually, on the amounts in clause (a);

(c) third, 80% to the General Partner and 20% to the Limited Partner until the General Partner has received 20% of the aggregate amounts distributed under clause (b) and this clause (c); and

(d) thereafter, 80% to the Limited Partner and 20% to the General Partner.

Distributions shall be made within 90 days after receipt, subject to reasonable reserves.

### 3.2 General Partner Clawback

If, upon the final distribution in liquidation, the General Partner has received distributions under Section 3.1(c) and Section 3.1(d) exceeding what it would have received had all distributions been made on an aggregate basis (the "Clawback Amount"), the General Partner shall restore the Clawback Amount to the Partnership within 90 days, but not in excess of such distributions net of income taxes thereon, computed at the highest combined marginal rate applicable to an individual resident in New York, New York. Each principal of the General Partner who received carried interest shall severally, and not jointly, guarantee its pro rata share of the Clawback Amount.

## ARTICLE IV. FEES AND EXPENSES

### 4.1 Management Fee

The Partnership shall pay the Manager a management fee (the "Management Fee") quarterly in advance, equal to one-fourth of (a) during the Commitment Period, 2.00% of the aggregate Commitments of the Limited Partners other than Affiliates of the General Partner, and (b) thereafter, 1.25% of the Net Invested Capital attributable to those Limited Partners on the first day of the quarter, in each case prorated daily for partial quarters. The Management Fee is reduced as provided in Section 4.2. The Management Fee for any quarter after the Commitment Period shall not exceed that for the last full quarter of the Commitment Period, and no Management Fee accrues after dissolution.

### 4.2 Fee Offset

The Management Fee for any quarter shall be reduced by 100% of the share of the Limited Partners in all transaction, monitoring, directors', break-up and similar fees received by the Manager, the General Partner or their Affiliates from any Portfolio Company or prospective Portfolio Company ("Portfolio Fees"), net of related unreimbursed expenses. To the extent the reduction for a quarter exceeds the Management Fee for that quarter, the excess shall be carried forward, and any excess not applied by dissolution shall be refunded to the Limited Partners. Fees paid by a Portfolio Company to operating advisers who are not employees of the Manager are not Portfolio Fees if disclosed in the reports under Section 8.1.

### 4.3 Organizational and Partnership Expenses

The Partnership shall bear the expenses of its organization and the offering of interests up to $2,000,000, and any excess shall reduce the Management Fee. The Partnership shall also bear the costs of its operations ("Partnership Expenses"), including expenses of evaluating, acquiring, holding and disposing of Investments, including unconsummated transactions; interest on and fees of borrowings; fees of Pellorin Fund Administration, LLC, as administrator, Ferrand Oakes LLP, as auditor, and legal counsel; costs of reports to Partners and of Advisory Committee meetings; insurance premiums; taxes; litigation and indemnification expenses; and winding-up expenses. Partnership Expenses also include the allocable share of the Partnership in the regulatory compliance costs of the Manager and the General Partner, including Form PF and Form ADV filings and responses to regulatory examinations. The Manager shall bear its own ordinary overhead, including the compensation of its personnel and its office rent.

## ARTICLE V. MANAGEMENT

### 5.1 Authority of the General Partner

The management and control of the Partnership are vested exclusively in the General Partner, which may delegate investment advisory functions to the Manager. No Limited Partner shall take part in the management or control of the Partnership or have authority to bind it, and service on the Advisory Committee is not participation in control for purposes of the Act. Subject to this Agreement, the General Partner may on behalf of the Partnership acquire, hold and dispose of Investments, borrow money, guarantee obligations of Portfolio Companies, engage agents and advisers, and commence, defend and settle litigation.

### 5.2 Advisory Committee

The General Partner shall establish an advisory committee (the "Advisory Committee") of three to seven representatives of Limited Partners unaffiliated with the General Partner, selected by the General Partner. The Advisory Committee shall meet at least annually and shall consult with the General Partner on conflicts of interest, valuations, waivers of the investment limitations in Section 5.6 and other matters referred to it under this Agreement. Its approval requires a majority of its members, excluding any member whose Limited Partner is interested in the matter. Members owe no fiduciary duty to any Partner and are Covered Persons under Section 6.1.

### 5.3 Conflicts and Co-Investment

The General Partner shall not cause the Partnership to (a) buy any asset from, or sell any asset to, the General Partner, the Manager or any of their Affiliates, including any other fund managed by the Manager, (b) invest in a Portfolio Company in which another fund managed by the Manager holds an investment, other than on the same terms and at the same time, or (c) enter any other transaction in which the General Partner or its Affiliates have a material conflict of interest (each, an "Affiliated Transaction"), unless the Affiliated Transaction has been approved in advance by the General Partner as fair and reasonable to the Partnership, with written notice to the Advisory Committee. Co-investors offered opportunities by the General Partner shall invest and exit on terms no more favorable than those of the Partnership.

### 5.4 Successor Funds

Until the earlier of the end of the Commitment Period and the date on which at least 75% of aggregate Commitments has been invested, committed or reserved for follow-on Investments and Partnership Expenses, none of the General Partner, the Manager or their Affiliates shall hold a first closing of any pooled vehicle with investment objectives substantially similar to those of the Partnership (a "Successor Fund") without the consent of a Majority in Interest. This restriction does not apply to co-investment vehicles, to vehicles formed to hold interests in Portfolio Companies, or to funds investing principally outside North America.

### 5.5 Key Person Event

A "Key Person Event" occurs if, during the Commitment Period, any two of Callum Ashgrove, Marisol Tennant and Dmitri Halvorsen (the "Key Persons") cease to devote substantially all of their business time to the Partnership, its Portfolio Companies and prior funds managed by the Manager. The General Partner shall notify the Limited Partners within ten Business Days after a Key Person Event. Upon a Key Person Event, the General Partner shall suspend the Commitment Period, and during any suspension the Partnership shall make no new Investments. Any suspension ends if, within 180 days, the Advisory Committee approves a replacement Key Person proposed by the General Partner; otherwise the Commitment Period terminates unless a Majority in Interest elects to continue it.

### 5.6 Investment Limitations

Without the consent of the Advisory Committee, the Partnership shall not (a) invest more than 20% of aggregate Commitments in any single Portfolio Company and its Affiliates, measured at the time of investment; (b) invest more than 25% of aggregate Commitments in businesses headquartered outside the United States and Canada; (c) acquire publicly traded securities, other than in a transaction intended to delist the issuer; (d) make any Investment opposed by the board of directors of the target; (e) invest in any pooled vehicle that charges a management fee or carried interest; or (f) invest in any business principally engaged in owning real property or producing oil and gas.

### 5.7 Continuation Vehicle Transactions

Notwithstanding Section 5.3, the Partnership may sell one or more Investments to a vehicle managed by the Manager or its Affiliates and capitalized in part by third-party investors (a "Continuation Vehicle") only if (a) the Advisory Committee approves the sale in advance, (b) the price is set through a competitive process run by an independent intermediary and supported by a fairness opinion, and (c) each Limited Partner may elect, on at least 20 Business Days' notice, to receive its share of the proceeds in cash or to roll its interest into the Continuation Vehicle on the same economic terms. The General Partner shall disclose to the Advisory Committee all fees and carried interest payable by the Continuation Vehicle, and the Continuation Vehicle shall bear all expenses of the sale.

## ARTICLE VI. EXCULPATION AND INDEMNIFICATION

### 6.1 Exculpation and Indemnification

No Covered Person shall be liable to the Partnership or any Partner for any act or omission in good faith in connection with the Partnership, except to the extent of its fraud, gross negligence, willful misconduct, material breach of this Agreement or knowing violation of law ("Disabling Conduct"). "Covered Person" means the General Partner, the Manager, their Affiliates and personnel, and the members of the Advisory Committee. The Partnership shall indemnify each Covered Person against all losses, claims, damages, liabilities and expenses, including reasonable legal fees, arising from any proceeding relating to the Partnership, except to the extent resulting from its Disabling Conduct. Indemnification shall not extend to any proceeding initiated by a Covered Person against the Partnership or another Covered Person, other than a proceeding to enforce its rights under this Section 6.1. No Partner shall be required to return distributions for indemnification in excess of 25% of the distributions it has received, or any distribution after the third anniversary of its receipt.

## ARTICLE VII. TRANSFERS AND REMOVAL

### 7.1 Transfers by Limited Partners

No Limited Partner may sell, assign, pledge or otherwise transfer any part of its interest without the prior written consent of the General Partner, which may be withheld in its sole discretion, except that consent to a transfer to an Affiliate of the transferor shall not be unreasonably withheld. No transfer shall be permitted that would cause the Partnership to be a publicly traded partnership for tax purposes, require the Partnership to register under the Investment Company Act of 1940, cause the assets of the Partnership to be "plan assets" under ERISA, or violate any securities law. A transferee becomes a substituted Limited Partner only by executing a joinder satisfactory to the General Partner.

### 7.2 Removal of the General Partner

A Supermajority in Interest may remove the General Partner for Cause. "Cause" means a final, non-appealable judicial determination that the General Partner, the Manager or any Key Person committed fraud, willful misconduct or gross negligence in connection with the Partnership. A Majority in Interest may appoint a successor general partner, and the removed General Partner retains its share of distributions under Section 3.1(c) and Section 3.1(d) only for Investments disposed of before removal. At any time after the second anniversary of the Initial Closing Date, Limited Partners holding at least 75% of the Commitments counted in determining a Majority in Interest may terminate the Commitment Period without cause, and the Management Fee shall thereafter be calculated under Section 4.1(b).

## ARTICLE VIII. GENERAL PROVISIONS

### 8.1 Reports to Partners

The General Partner shall deliver to each Partner (a) within 120 days after the end of each fiscal year, audited financial statements of the Partnership prepared in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles and a statement of its capital account; (b) within 60 days after the end of each of the first three fiscal quarters, unaudited financial statements and a report on each Investment, its fair value, and the Management Fee, Portfolio Fees and Partnership Expenses for the quarter; and (c) as soon as practicable after the end of each fiscal year, its Schedule K-1. Investments are valued at fair value under the valuation policy of the Manager, which the Advisory Committee reviews annually.

### 8.2 Confidentiality

Each Limited Partner shall keep confidential all information received from the Partnership, the General Partner or the Manager concerning the Partnership, its Investments or any Portfolio Company ("Confidential Information"), except disclosure (a) to its officers, employees, auditors and advisers bound by duties of confidentiality, (b) as required by law, regulation or legal process, after prompt notice to the General Partner where permitted, (c) of information that becomes public other than through a breach of this Section 8.2, or (d) with the prior written consent of the General Partner. A Limited Partner subject to public records laws may disclose its Commitment, Capital Contributions, distributions and net internal rate of return.

### 8.3 Amendments; Governing Law

This Agreement may be amended with the written consent of the General Partner and a Majority in Interest, but no amendment may, without the consent of each Partner adversely affected, (a) increase its Commitment, (b) reduce its share of distributions under Section 3.1 or increase its obligation to return distributions under Section 6.1, or (c) amend this Section 8.3. The General Partner may make amendments required by law without the consent of any Limited Partner.
