This Limited Partnership Agreement (this "Agreement") of Kestrow Halden Senior Credit Fund II, L.P. (the "Partnership") is made by Kestrow Halden Credit GP II, LLC, as general partner (the "General Partner"), and the Persons admitted as limited partners (the "Limited Partners" and, with the General Partner, the "Partners").

## ARTICLE I. FORMATION AND PURPOSE

### 1.1 Formation, Name and Purpose

The Partnership was formed under the Delaware Revised Uniform Limited Partnership Act (the "Delaware Act") on January 22, 2025 and has its principal office in Charlotte, North Carolina. Its purpose is to originate, acquire, hold, restructure and dispose of senior secured and unitranche loans and other debt instruments of middle-market companies based principally in the United States and Canada, and to engage in related activities. The General Partner has engaged Kestrow Halden Capital Management, LP (the "Investment Manager") to manage the investments of the Partnership and may delegate to it any power under this Agreement, remaining responsible for all matters so delegated.

## ARTICLE II. DEFINITIONS

### 2.1 Defined Terms

Capitalized terms not otherwise defined have the following meanings. "Affiliate" means any Person controlling, controlled by or under common control with another Person, but no Obligor is an Affiliate of the General Partner. "Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or New York bank holiday. "Capital Contribution" means cash contributed by a Partner under Article III, including amounts deemed contributed under Section 3.4. "Code" means the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended. "Distributable Proceeds" means cash received in respect of Investments, net of Partnership Expenses, reasonable reserves and amounts retained under Section 3.4. "ERISA" means the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974, as amended. "Final Closing Date" means the date of the last Subsequent Closing. "Initial Closing Date" means the first date on which unaffiliated Persons are admitted as Limited Partners. "Invested Capital" means Capital Contributions used to fund the cost of Investments not repaid, sold or written off. "Investment" means any loan, note, participation or other instrument held by the Partnership. "Investment Period" means the period from the Initial Closing Date until the earlier of (a) the third anniversary of the Initial Closing Date and (b) its termination under Section 5.2. "Majority in Interest" means Limited Partners, other than Defaulting Limited Partners and Affiliates of the General Partner, holding more than fifty percent (50%) of the Commitments of all such Limited Partners. "Obligor" means any borrower, issuer or guarantor of an Investment. "Unfunded Commitment" means a Partner's Commitment less its Capital Contributions, plus amounts restored under Section 3.4. "Valuation Policy" means the valuation policy of the Investment Manager, as amended from time to time.

## ARTICLE III. CAPITAL COMMITMENTS AND CONTRIBUTIONS

### 3.1 Commitments and Subsequent Closings

Each Partner shall make Capital Contributions up to the amount set forth in the records of the Partnership as its commitment (its "Commitment"). The minimum Commitment of a Limited Partner is $5,000,000, subject to waiver by the General Partner, whose own Commitment shall be at least one percent (1%) of aggregate Commitments. Aggregate Commitments shall not exceed $1,250,000,000. The General Partner may admit additional Limited Partners, or accept increased Commitments, at closings held within twelve (12) months after the Initial Closing Date (each, a "Subsequent Closing"). Each Limited Partner participating in a Subsequent Closing shall contribute its pro rata share of prior Capital Contributions plus interest at six percent (6%) per annum from the original due dates, which interest is paid to the Partners admitted earlier and is not a distribution under Section 4.1.

### 3.2 Capital Calls

The General Partner shall call Capital Contributions by written notice (a "Drawdown Notice") delivered at least ten (10) Business Days before the due date, stating the amount and purpose of the call and each Limited Partner's share. Calls shall be made pro rata in accordance with Unfunded Commitments. After the Investment Period, Capital Contributions may be called only to (a) pay Partnership Expenses, Management Fees and indebtedness of the Partnership, (b) complete Investments committed to in writing during the Investment Period, and (c) fund follow-on Investments in existing Obligors not exceeding fifteen percent (15%) of aggregate Commitments.

### 3.3 Defaulting Limited Partners

A Limited Partner that fails to pay a Capital Contribution within five (5) Business Days after written notice of the failure is a "Defaulting Limited Partner." The General Partner may (a) charge interest on the unpaid amount at the lesser of twelve percent (12%) per annum and the highest lawful rate, (b) reduce the capital account of the Defaulting Limited Partner by fifty percent (50%) and reallocate the reduction to the other Partners, (c) require it to sell its interest to a Person designated by the General Partner at the lesser of fifty percent (50%) of its capital account and fair market value, and (d) exclude it from future Investments. A Defaulting Limited Partner has no voting or consent rights while its default continues.

### 3.4 Reinvestment of Proceeds

During the Investment Period, the General Partner may cause the Partnership to retain and reinvest, rather than distribute, (a) Distributable Proceeds representing the return of the cost of an Investment and (b) amounts equal to Capital Contributions used to pay Management Fees and Partnership Expenses (together, "Recycled Amounts"). After the Investment Period, Recycled Amounts may be retained only for the purposes described in Section 3.2(b) and (c). Recycled Amounts are treated as distributed under Section 4.1 and recontributed, and the Unfunded Commitment of each Partner is restored by its share of them; provided that the aggregate cost of all Investments made by the Partnership, including Investments made with Recycled Amounts, shall not at any time exceed one hundred twenty-five percent (125%) of aggregate Commitments.

## ARTICLE IV. DISTRIBUTIONS

### 4.1 Distributions

Distributable Proceeds from current income shall be distributed within forty-five (45) days after each quarter end, and those from repayments and dispositions within ninety (90) days after receipt, subject to Section 3.4. Distributable Proceeds shall be apportioned among the Partners in proportion to their Capital Contributions, and the share of each Limited Partner shall be distributed: (a) first, to it until it has received its aggregate Capital Contributions; (b) second, to it until it has received a preferred return of six percent (6%) per annum, compounded annually, on its unreturned Capital Contributions; (c) third, to the General Partner until it has received ten percent (10%) of the sum of the amounts distributed under clause (b) and this clause (c); and (d) thereafter, 90% to such Limited Partner and 10% to the General Partner (amounts under clauses (c) and (d), "Carried Interest"). On final liquidation the General Partner shall restore any Carried Interest received in excess of the amount due on an aggregate basis, net of taxes paid or payable on it. Any amount withheld pursuant to Section 4.2 shall be treated as distributed to the Partner in respect of which it was withheld.

### 4.2 Tax Withholding

The General Partner may withhold from any distribution or allocation to a Partner, and pay to the relevant taxing authority, any amount required to be withheld under the Code or other applicable law, including Sections 1441, 1446 and 1471 through 1474 of the Code. A Partner in respect of which the Partnership pays an amount exceeding its current distributions shall contribute the excess within ten (10) Business Days after demand, without reduction of its Unfunded Commitment. Each Partner shall indemnify the Partnership against taxes, interest and penalties imposed in respect of its share of income or its status, which obligation survives the dissolution of the Partnership.

### 4.3 Distributions in Kind

Before its dissolution, the Partnership shall not distribute any asset in kind other than freely tradable securities listed on a national securities exchange. Any Limited Partner may, within ten (10) Business Days after notice of a proposed distribution in kind, require the General Partner to use commercially reasonable efforts to sell the securities otherwise distributable to it and remit the net cash proceeds. Assets distributed in kind are valued under the Valuation Policy.

## ARTICLE V. MANAGEMENT AND FEES

### 5.1 Investment Limitations

The General Partner shall not cause the Partnership to: (a) invest in any single Obligor and its Affiliates an aggregate amount exceeding ten percent (10%) of aggregate Commitments; (b) invest more than twenty percent (20%) of aggregate Commitments in second lien loans, last-out unitranche tranches and unsecured debt, taken together; (c) invest more than fifteen percent (15%) of aggregate Commitments in Obligors based outside the United States and Canada; (d) hold equity securities, other than warrants and equity received in a restructuring or alongside a loan, costing more than five percent (5%) of aggregate Commitments; or (e) invest in a portfolio company of another fund managed by the Investment Manager or its Affiliates, or trade assets with such a fund, without the consent of the Advisory Committee. Each limitation is measured at the time of investment on the basis of cost, including any unfunded revolving or delayed draw commitment, and is not breached by later changes in value. The Advisory Committee may waive any limitation in this Section.

### 5.2 Key Person Event

A "Key Person Event" occurs if, during the Investment Period, fewer than two (2) of Daniel Orlesby, Priya Vantongeren and Colm Dunstreve (the "Key Persons") devote substantially all of their business time to the Investment Manager and the funds it manages. Upon a Key Person Event, of which the General Partner shall notify the Limited Partners within five (5) Business Days, the Investment Period is automatically suspended. If replacement Key Persons approved by the Advisory Committee are not in place within one hundred eighty (180) days, the Investment Period terminates unless a Majority in Interest elects to reinstate it.

### 5.3 Management Fee

The Partnership shall pay the Investment Manager a management fee (the "Management Fee") quarterly in advance at an annual rate of one percent (1.00%) of Invested Capital as of the first day of each quarter. Closing fees, original issue discount, amendment fees and prepayment premiums on Investments belong to the Partnership. Agency, monitoring, structuring and similar fees paid by any Obligor to the Investment Manager or its Affiliates shall reduce the next Management Fee payment by one hundred percent (100%) of such fees, with any excess carried forward.

### 5.4 Partnership Expenses

The Partnership shall bear its organizational and offering expenses up to $1,750,000, any excess being borne by the General Partner. The Partnership shall also bear all expenses of its operations ("Partnership Expenses"), including unreimbursed costs of originating, monitoring, restructuring and disposing of Investments, loan administration, custody and fund administrator fees, audit and tax preparation, borrowing costs, Advisory Committee expenses, insurance, indemnification, litigation and broken deal expenses. Partnership Expenses shall not include the ordinary overhead of the General Partner and the Investment Manager, including rent, office equipment and the compensation and benefits of their personnel, which shall be paid by the Investment Manager out of the Management Fee.

## ARTICLE VI. LIMITED PARTNERS AND ADVISORY COMMITTEE

### 6.1 Limited Liability of Limited Partners

No Limited Partner shall be liable for obligations of the Partnership beyond its Commitment, its share of undistributed Partnership assets and any obligation to return distributions under Section 10.2 or the Delaware Act. No Limited Partner shall take part in the management or control of the business of the Partnership, and no Limited Partner shall have any right or authority to act for or bind the Partnership.

### 6.2 Advisory Committee

The General Partner shall establish an advisory committee (the "Advisory Committee") of three (3) to seven (7) representatives of Limited Partners unaffiliated with the General Partner. The Advisory Committee shall meet at least annually and acts by majority vote at a meeting or by written consent. It shall (a) approve or disapprove any transaction presenting a conflict of interest between the General Partner or its Affiliates and the Partnership, (b) approve any departure from the Valuation Policy, and (c) grant any other consent reserved to it by this Agreement. Its members owe no fiduciary duty to any Partner.

## ARTICLE VII. TRANSFERS AND REMOVAL

### 7.1 Transfers by Limited Partners

No Limited Partner may sell, assign, pledge or otherwise transfer any part of its interest without the prior written consent of the General Partner, which may be withheld in its sole discretion but shall not be unreasonably withheld for a transfer to an Affiliate. No transfer is permitted if it would cause the Partnership to be a publicly traded partnership under Section 7704 of the Code, cause its assets to be "plan assets" under ERISA or require its registration under the Investment Company Act of 1940. A transferee becomes a substituted Limited Partner only upon signing an instrument of accession to this Agreement.

### 7.2 Removal of the General Partner

The General Partner may be removed for Cause by written election of Limited Partners, other than Defaulting Limited Partners and Affiliates of the General Partner, holding at least sixty-six and two-thirds percent (66 2/3%) of the Commitments of all such Limited Partners, and a Majority in Interest may admit a successor general partner. "Cause" means fraud, willful misconduct or a felony conviction of the General Partner, the Investment Manager or any Key Person, or an uncured material breach of this Agreement by the General Partner, in each case as determined by a final court judgment. A removed General Partner retains fifty percent (50%) of the Carried Interest attributable to Investments made before its removal.

## ARTICLE VIII. REPORTS AND CONFIDENTIALITY

### 8.1 Reports to Partners

Within sixty (60) days after each of the first three quarters of each year, the General Partner shall deliver to each Limited Partner unaudited financial statements, a capital account statement and a schedule of Investments showing cost, fair value under the Valuation Policy and Recycled Amounts retained. Within one hundred twenty (120) days after each fiscal year, it shall deliver financial statements audited by Brannock Vey LLP or another independent accounting firm. The General Partner may also furnish, within forty-five (45) days after each quarter end, a schedule setting out in reasonable detail the Management Fees, Partnership Expenses, fee offsets and Carried Interest paid or accrued during the quarter.

### 8.2 Confidentiality

Each Limited Partner shall keep confidential all information concerning the Partnership, the Investment Manager, any Obligor or any Investment received in connection with its interest ("Confidential Information"), except for disclosure (a) to its officers, employees and professional advisers bound by duties of confidentiality, (b) as required by law, legal process or any public records statute, after notice to the General Partner where permitted, and (c) of information that becomes public other than through a breach of this Section. The General Partner may withhold Confidential Information from any Limited Partner that it reasonably believes may be required to disclose it under a public records statute.

## ARTICLE IX. TAX MATTERS

### 9.1 Partnership Representative and Tax Information

The General Partner is the "partnership representative" of the Partnership under Section 6223 of the Code, may appoint a designated individual and may make any election available to the Partnership, including under Section 6226 of the Code, keeping the Limited Partners reasonably informed of any material tax audit. The General Partner shall use commercially reasonable efforts to deliver to each Partner a Schedule K-1 and such other information as is reasonbly required for its income tax returns within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each fiscal year.

## ARTICLE X. TERM, DISSOLUTION AND GENERAL PROVISIONS

### 10.1 Term and Dissolution

The Partnership shall continue until the seventh (7th) anniversary of the Final Closing Date, provided that the General Partner may extend the term for up to two (2) consecutive periods of one (1) year each, the first in its discretion and the second with the consent of the Advisory Committee. The Partnership shall dissolve earlier upon (a) the realization of all Investments after the Investment Period, (b) the election of the General Partner with the consent of a Majority in Interest, (c) the removal of the General Partner unless a successor is admitted under Section 7.2, or (d) a decree of judicial dissolution. The liquidator may distribute assets in cash or in kind and shall pay creditors before distributing the balance under Section 4.1.

### 10.2 Exculpation and Indemnification

None of the General Partner, the Investment Manager, their Affiliates and their respective officers and employees, or the members of the Advisory Committee (each, a "Covered Person") shall be liable to the Partnership or any Partner for any act or omission in connection with the Partnership, except for fraud, gross negligence, willful misconduct or material breach of this Agreement. The Partnership shall indemnify each Covered Person on the same basis. Partners may be required to return distributions to fund indemnification, but not more than twenty-five percent (25%) of a Partner's Commitment or any distribution received more than two (2) years earlier.

### 10.3 Anti-Money Laundering and Privacy

Each Limited Partner represents that its Capital Contributions are not derived from unlawful activity and that neither it nor any beneficial owner is a sanctioned Person under United States law, and shall provide such information as the General Partner reasonably requests to verify its identity and source of funds. The General Partner may suspend distributions to, or compel the withdrawal of, a Limited Partner where anti-money laundering or sanctions laws so require. Personal information of Limited Partners shall be handled in accordance with the privacy notice of the Partnership dated February 3, 2025.

### 10.4 Governing Law and Miscellaneous

This Agreement is governed by Delaware law, and each Partner submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts located in Delaware and waives trial by jury. The General Partner may, without the consent of any other Partner, enter into side letters with Limited Partners that alter or supplement this Agreement as to them. This Agreement may be amended by the General Partner with the consent of a Majority in Interest, but an amendment increasing a Partner's Commitment requires its consent. This Agreement may be executed in any number of counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument.
