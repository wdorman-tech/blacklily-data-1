## SECTION 1. SCOPE AND DEFINITIONS

### 1.1 Scope

This Schedule forms part of each Investment Advisory Agreement between Tennick Arlow Wealth Partners, LLC (the "Firm") and its clients. If a client's signed Investment Advisory Agreement specifies a different fee, the Agreement controls.

### 1.2 Definitions

"Billable Assets" means all assets in accounts managed by the Firm, including cash and cash equivalents, other than assets the Firm reports on but does not manage. "Billing Value" means the value of Billable Assets as reported by the custodian or, for assets the custodian does not price and for Level 3 assets such as private fund interests, as determined under the Firm's Valuation Policy, including accrued interest. "Household" has the meaning given in Section 2.2.

## SECTION 2. WEALTH MANAGEMENT FEE

### 2.1 Standard Tiered Schedule

| Household Billable Assets | Annual rate |
|---|---|
| First $1,000,000 | 1.00% |
| Next $2,000,000 (over $1,000,000 up to $3,000,000) | 0.80% |
| Next $2,000,000 (over $3,000,000 up to $5,000,000) | 0.65% |
| Next $5,000,000 (over $5,000,000 up to $10,000,000) | 0.50% |
| Over $10,000,000 | 0.35% |

Each rate applies only to the Billable Assets within its tier. The fee includes portfolio management, financial planning and the coordination services described in the Firm's Form ADV Part 2A.

### 2.2 Household Aggregation

A Household consists of the client, the client's spouse or domestic partner, their dependent children, and trusts, foundations and entities of which one or more of them is the grantor, primary beneficiary or controlling owner. The Billable Assets of all accounts in a Household are aggregated to determine the applicable tiers, and the resulting fee is allocated among the accounts in proportion to their Billing Values unless the client directs otherwise.

### 2.3 Minimum Relationship and Minimum Fee

The minimum household relationship for new clients is $1,000,000 of Billable Assets, and the minimum annual fee is $10,000, billed as $2,500 per quarter. Households that became clients before July 1, 2025 remain subject to the $750,000 minimum relationship in effect when they engaged the Firm. The Managing Partner may waive or reduce either minimum in writing.

## SECTION 3. FIXED INCOME MANDATES

### 3.1 Schedule

Standalone Tax-Managed Municipal and Taxable Fixed Income portfolios are charged 0.40% annually on the first $5,000,000 and 0.30% on Billable Assets above $5,000,000. The minimum Fixed Income Mandate is $2,000,000.

### 3.2 No Aggregation

Fixed Income Mandate assets are billed under Section 3.1 only and are not included in the Household total used to apply Section 2.1.

## SECTION 4. FINANCIAL PLANNING AND CONSULTING

### 4.1 Standalone Planning

Standalone financial planning engagements are charged a fixed fee between $5,000 and $25,000, stated in the engagement letter. Half of the fee is due on signing and the balance on delivery of the written plan.

### 4.2 Hourly Consulting

Hourly consulting is charged at $400 per hour, billed monthly in arrears.

## SECTION 5. BILLING

### 5.1 Frequency and Basis

Wealth management and Fixed Income Mandate fees are billed quarterly in advance, based on the Billing Value of each account on the last business day of the preceding calendar quarter. The quarterly fee is one quarter of the annual fee calculated under Section 2 or Section 3.

### 5.2 Payment

Unless the client elects to be invoiced, fees are deducted from the account designated by the client under the authority granted in the Investment Advisory Agreement. Invoiced fees are payable within 30 days of the invoice date.

### 5.3 Contributions and Withdrawals

A contribution or withdrawal of $100,000 or more in a single transaction during a quarter results in a pro rata charge or credit, based on the number of days remaining in the quarter, on the next quarterly invoice. Smaller contributions and withdrawals are not adjusted.

### 5.4 New Accounts

The fee for the first partial quarter is calculated on the Billing Value at the date of funding, prorated for the days remaining in the quarter, and billed with the first full quarter.

## SECTION 6. TERMINATION AND REFUNDS

### 6.1 Notice

Either party may terminate the Investment Advisory Agreement upon 30 days' written notice to the other party.

### 6.2 Refund of Prepaid Fees

On termination, the unearned portion of the prepaid fee is refunded pro rata based on the number of days remaining in the quarter after the effective date of termination. Refunds are paid within 15 business days of the effective date of termination. No termination fee is charged by the Firm.

### 6.3 Five-Business-Day Cancellation

A client who terminates within five business days of signing the Investment Advisory Agreement receives a full refund of any fees paid.

## SECTION 7. FEES NOT INCLUDED

### 7.1 Third-Party Costs

The fees in this Schedule do not include custodian charges, transaction charges, dealer markups, wire and transfer fees, or the management fees and expenses of mutual funds, exchange-traded funds and private funds, all of which are borne by the client.
