## SECTION 1. PURPOSE AND EFFECT

On August 12, 2026, the Management Committee approved the amendments described in this memorandum. Each amendment identifies the document and section it amends and supersedes the provision it identifies. The affected documents are the Compliance Policies and Procedures Manual (Version 7.0, effective January 15, 2026), the Valuation Policy and Procedures (effective February 10, 2026), the Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Plan (Version 5.2, revised May 4, 2026), the Schedule of Advisory Fees (effective April 1, 2026) and the Form ADV Part 2A brochure dated March 27, 2026.

The affected documents have not yet been reissued and continue to show the prior provisions. Where this memorandum conflicts with an earlier document, this memorandum controls. Unless a section below states otherwise, each amendment is effective on the date of this memorandum.

## SECTION 2. PERSONAL TRADING: MINIMUM HOLDING PERIOD

**Amends:** Compliance Manual, Section 4.5 (Minimum Holding Period).

The minimum holding period for Reportable Securities acquired in a Covered Account is increased from 30 calendar days to 60 calendar days. This change supersedes the 30-day period stated in Section 4.5 of the Compliance Manual. The 60-day period applies to every Reportable Security acquired on or after the date of this memorandum; securities acquired before that date remain subject to the 30-day period. The disgorgement and hardship exception provisions of Section 4.5 are unchanged. Compliance's review of 2025 personal trading identified repeated short-term trading in securities also held in the Core Equity strategy, and the Management Committee concluded that a longer holding period better separates personal trading from model activity.

## SECTION 3. VALUATION: APPROVAL OF PRICE OVERRIDES

**Amends:** Valuation Policy, Section 4.2 (Price Overrides).

Any override of a primary source price used for billing or client reporting must now be approved in writing by both the Chief Financial Officer and the Chief Compliance Officer before it is applied. This change supersedes the requirement in Section 4.2 of the Valuation Policy that overrides be approved in advance by the Director of Operations. The Director of Operations continues to prepare each override request and to maintain the Override Log, and overrides continue to be reported to the Valuation Committee at its next meeting. Because the Operations department also calculates client fees, the Management Committee concluded that override approval should sit with officers who do not perform the fee calculation.

## SECTION 4. BUSINESS CONTINUITY: TIER 1 RECOVERY TIME OBJECTIVE

**Amends:** Business Continuity Plan, Section 3.1 (Recovery Time Objectives).

The recovery time objective for Tier 1 systems is shortened from 24 hours to 8 hours from the declaration of a Significant Business Disruption. This change supersedes the 24-hour objective stated in Section 3.1 of the Business Continuity Plan. The change is supported by the amendment to the Firm's agreement with Norcastle Managed Services, LLC executed on August 5, 2026, under which Norcastle maintains a warm standby environment for the order management system. The recovery time objectives for Tier 2 and Tier 3 systems are unchanged.

## SECTION 5. FEE SCHEDULE: TOP BREAKPOINT

**Amends:** Schedule of Advisory Fees, Section 2.1 (Standard Tiered Schedule), and Form ADV Part 2A, Item 5.1 (Wealth Management Fee Schedule).

The 0.35% rate now applies to household assets above $7,500,000, rather than above $10,000,000, and the 0.50% tier now covers household assets above $5,000,000 up to $7,500,000. This change supersedes the corresponding tiers in Section 2.1 of the Schedule of Advisory Fees and in Item 5.1 of the brochure. The rates and breakpoints of the first three tiers are unchanged. The change is effective for the billing period that began July 1, 2026. Because third-quarter fees were billed in advance under the prior schedule, the difference for affected households will be credited on the invoice for the quarter beginning October 1, 2026. The Chief Compliance Officer will file an other-than-annual amendment to the brochure, and affected clients will be notified by letter.

## SECTION 6. ELECTRONIC COMMUNICATIONS ARCHIVING VENDOR

**Amends:** Compliance Manual, Section 14.3 (Archiving), and Business Continuity Plan, Section 5.2 (Critical Vendor List).

Effective August 3, 2026, Sennacott Retention Systems, LLC replaced Marrowgate Archiving, Inc. as the Firm's electronic communications archiving vendor. This change supersedes the references to Marrowgate Archiving, Inc. in Section 14.3 of the Compliance Manual and Section 5.2 of the Business Continuity Plan. All communications captured by Marrowgate before that date have been migrated to Sennacott, and the Chief Compliance Officer has verified the completeness of the migration by sample testing. Marrowgate will provide read-only access to its copy of the archive until its contract terminates on September 30, 2026. The retention period and the review procedures in Sections 14.3 and 14.4 of the Compliance Manual are unchanged.

## SECTION 7. BEST EXECUTION COMMITTEE: REVIEW FREQUENCY

**Amends:** Compliance Manual, Section 10.2 (Best Execution Committee).

The Best Execution Committee will meet quarterly rather than semi-annually, beginning with its review of the third quarter of 2026. This change supersedes the semi-annual frequency stated in Section 10.2 of the Compliance Manual. The Committee's membership and the scope of its review are unchanged.

The affected documents will be reissued to reflect these amendments in connection with the next annual compliance review.
