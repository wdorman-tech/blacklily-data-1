## SECTION 1. SCOPE

### 1.1 Application

This Fee Schedule sets out the management fees, incentive allocation, carried interest, minimum investments and redemption charges applicable to Sallowmere Partners Fund, LP (the "Flagship Fund"), Sallowmere Partners Offshore Fund, Ltd. (the "Offshore Fund") and Sallowmere Co-Investment Fund I, LP (the "Co-Investment Fund"). It summarizes the Offering Documents of each Fund, which control in the event of any inconsistency. Fees for a particular investor may differ under a side letter.

## SECTION 2. MANAGEMENT FEES

### 2.1 Rates by Class

| Class | Eligibility | Annual management fee |
|---|---|---|
| Class A | All investors other than those listed below | 2.00% |
| Class A (Large Investor Rate) | Aggregate capital of $50 million or more, as described in Section 2.2 | 1.50% |
| Class F | Founders' class, closed to new investment since December 31, 2018 | 1.25% |
| Class E | Principals and employees of the Adviser and their family members | None |

### 2.2 Large Investor Rate

The Large Investor Rate applies to the entire Class A capital account of an investor whose aggregate capital in the Flagship Fund and the Offshore Fund, together with the capital of its affiliates, is $50 million or more as of the first day of a calendar quarter. Eligibility is determined by the Administrator at the start of each quarter, and an investor that falls below the threshold as a result of redemptions reverts to the standard Class A rate from the following quarter. Market depreciation alone does not cause an investor to lose the Large Investor Rate.

### 2.3 Billing Frequency and Basis

Management fees are calculated and payable quarterly in advance as of the first business day of each calendar quarter, based on each capital account (or each Offshore Fund shareholder's look-through share) as of that date, before accrual of any incentive allocation. Fees are charged once, at the Flagship Fund level, and the Offshore Fund pays no separate management fee.

### 2.4 Proration

Subscriptions are accepted monthly. A subscription accepted as of the first day of a month other than the first month of a quarter bears a management fee pro-rated for the remaining months of that quarter, payable on the subscription date.

## SECTION 3. INCENTIVE ALLOCATION

### 3.1 Rate

The general partner of the Flagship Fund receives an incentive allocation equal to 20% of the net profits allocated to each Class A capital account and 15% of the net profits allocated to each Class F capital account. Class E capital accounts bear no incentive allocation.

### 3.2 High Water Mark and Timing

The incentive allocation is subject to a high water mark with a loss carryforward, and there is no hurdle rate. It is determined as of December 31 of each year and as of the effective date of any redemption.

## SECTION 4. CO-INVESTMENT FUND

### 4.1 Management Fee

The Co-Investment Fund pays no management fee.

### 4.2 Carried Interest

The general partner of the Co-Investment Fund receives carried interest equal to 10% of net realized profits, distributed only after investors have received distributions equal to 100% of their capital contributions and their share of expenses. No carried interest is paid on unrealized gains.

### 4.3 Expenses

The Co-Investment Fund bears its administration, audit, tax and legal expenses. Organizational expenses were borne by the Co-Investment Fund up to $150,000, with any excess borne by the Adviser.

## SECTION 5. REDEMPTIONS, TERMINATION AND REFUNDS

### 5.1 Redemption Terms

Interests in the Flagship Fund and shares of the Offshore Fund may be redeemed as of the last business day of any calendar quarter on at least 65 days' prior written notice. Each Fund may limit aggregate redemptions as of any quarter-end to 20% of its net asset value. Ninety percent of redemption proceeds are ordinarily paid within 30 days after the redemption date, and a holdback of 10% applies to a full redemption until completion of the annual audit. Investors in the Co-Investment Fund have no redemption rights.

### 5.2 Early Redemption Fee

A redemption effective within 12 months after an investor's initial subscription is subject to an early redemption fee of 3% of the amount redeemed. The fee is retained by the Fund for the benefit of remaining investors and is not paid to the Adviser. The fee does not apply to Class E interests.

### 5.3 Termination of Investment Management Agreements

Each investment management agreement between the Adviser and a Fund may be terminated by either party on 90 days' prior written notice.

### 5.4 Refunds

If an investment management agreement terminates during a quarter, or if the general partner permits a redemption as of a date other than a quarter-end, the unearned portion of the prepaid management fee is refunded pro rata within 30 days.

## SECTION 6. MINIMUM INVESTMENTS

### 6.1 Minimums

The minimum initial investment in each of the Flagship Fund and the Offshore Fund is $1,000,000, and the minimum additional investment is $250,000. The minimum commitment to the Co-Investment Fund was $5,000,000. The general partner of each Fund, or the board of directors of the Offshore Fund, may accept lesser amounts in its discretion.
