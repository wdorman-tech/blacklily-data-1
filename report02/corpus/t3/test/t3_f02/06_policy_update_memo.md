## SECTION 1. PURPOSE, APPROVAL AND EFFECTIVE DATE

### 1.1 Purpose and Approval

This memorandum is issued under Section 1.3 of the Compliance Manual. Following the mid-year review of the compliance program conducted with Ferrisburg Regulatory Consulting LLC and the Management Committee's review of the Adviser's technology vendors, the Management Committee approved the amendments set out in Section 2 at its meeting on August 20, 2026.

### 1.2 Effective Date and Supersession

Each amendment in Section 2 is effective September 1, 2026, except the amendment in Section 2.6, which is effective for fee periods beginning on or after October 1, 2026. ## SECTION 2. AMENDMENTS

### 2.1 Compliance Manual Section 4.5 (Minimum Holding Period)

The minimum holding period in Section 4.5 of the Compliance Manual is extended from 30 calendar days to 60 calendar days. Access persons must hold any position in a reportable security acquired in a covered account for at least 60 calendar days from the date of acquisition, and may not close a short position within 60 calendar days of opening it. Positions acquired before September 1, 2026 remain subject to the 30-day period. The disgorgement and hardship exception provisions of Section 4.5 are unchanged. This amendment supersedes Section 4.5 of the Compliance Manual.

### 2.2 Compliance Manual Section 4.6 (Quarterly Transaction Reports)

Quarterly transaction reports must be submitted within 15 calendar days after the end of each calendar quarter, rather than within 30 calendar days. The first report subject to the new deadline is the report for the quarter ending September 30, 2026, which is due by October 15, 2026. The deadlines for initial and annual holdings reports are unchanged. This amendment supersedes the quarterly reporting deadline in Section 4.6 of the Compliance Manual.

### 2.3 Compliance Manual Section 5.2 (Gifts Received)

The limit on gifts that supervised persons may accept from any person or entity that does business or seeks to do business with the Adviser or the Funds is reduced from $250 to $100 per source per calendar year. Gifts received on or after September 1, 2026 count against the reduced limit together with gifts received from the same source earlier in 2026. The limits on gifts given in Section 5.3 and on business entertainment in Section 5.4 are unchanged. This amendment supersedes Section 5.2 of the Compliance Manual.

### 2.4 Valuation Policy Section 2.3 (Meetings)

The Valuation Committee will meet monthly, within ten business days after the end of each calendar month, rather than quarterly, so that the Committee reviews Level 3 values, overrides and stale price reports within each monthly net asset value cycle. The first monthly meeting will address the September 30, 2026 month-end. The provisions for ad hoc meetings in Section 2.3, and the quorum and voting requirements in Section 2.2, are unchanged. This amendment supersedes Section 2.3 of the Valuation Policy.

### 2.5 Business Continuity Plan Section 5.2 (IT Managed Service Provider)

Effective September 1, 2026, Anselby Managed IT LLC replaces Oskerby Technology Group LLC as the Adviser's IT managed service provider, on completion of the transition that began in July 2026. Anselby Managed IT LLC assumes all services described in Section 5.2 of the Business Continuity Plan, including help desk support, endpoint management, network monitoring, administration of the cloud environment and backup platform, and the 24-hour security operations function, and its service agreement commits to a 30-minute response to any incident the Adviser classifies as critical. The backup platform, backup schedule and storage locations described in Section 6 of the Business Continuity Plan are unchanged. The reference to Oskerby Technology Group LLC in Section 11.4 of the Compliance Manual, and references to the IT managed service provider elsewhere in the Business Continuity Plan, are to be read as references to Anselby Managed IT LLC. This amendment supersedes Section 5.2 of the Business Continuity Plan.

### 2.6 Fee Schedule Section 2.2 (Large Investor Rate)

The aggregate capital threshold for the Large Investor Rate is reduced from $50 million to $25 million. For fee periods beginning on or after October 1, 2026, the Large Investor Rate of 1.50% per annum applies to the entire Class A capital account of an investor whose aggregate capital in the Flagship Fund and the Offshore Fund, together with the capital of its affiliates, is $25 million or more as of the first day of a calendar quarter. The rate and the remaining provisions of Section 2.2 are unchanged. The general partner of the Flagship Fund and the board of directors of the Offshore Fund have approved corresponding amendments to the offering documents of those Funds, and investors will be notified as described in Section 3.1. This amendment supersedes Section 2.2 of the Fee Schedule and the corresponding eligibility entry in the table in Section 2.1.
