# Schedule of Fees

**Quenby Holt Family Office, LLC and Quenby Holt Trust Company**

## SECTION 1. SCOPE

### 1.1 Applicability

This Schedule sets out the fees charged by Quenby Holt Family Office, LLC (the "Firm") under its investment management agreements and by Quenby Holt Trust Company ("QHTC") for trustee services. It forms part of each agreement. Where an agreement provides otherwise, the agreement controls.

### 1.2 Definitions

"Family Relationship" means all accounts of a client family aggregated under a single investment management agreement. "Reporting-only assets" means assets included in consolidated reporting over which the Firm has no management authority.

## SECTION 2. ADVISORY FEE

### 2.1 Tiered Rates

The annual advisory fee is calculated on the aggregate value of Family Relationship assets under management, applying each rate only to the portion of assets within its tier:

| Family Relationship assets | Annual rate |
|---|---|
| First $25,000,000 | 0.80% |
| Above $25,000,000 to $50,000,000 | 0.60% |
| Above $50,000,000 to $100,000,000 | 0.45% |
| Above $100,000,000 to $250,000,000 | 0.30% |
| Above $250,000,000 | 0.20% |

The first breakpoint occurs at $25,000,000. Family Relationship assets in excess of $250,000,000 are charged at 0.20% per year.

### 2.2 Illustration

A Family Relationship with $80,000,000 of assets under management pays an annual advisory fee of $485,000, calculated as $200,000 on the first $25,000,000, $150,000 on the next $25,000,000 and $135,000 on the remaining $30,000,000, an effective rate of approximately 0.61%.

### 2.3 Reporting-Only Assets

Reporting-only assets are charged at an annual rate of 0.05% of their reported value. They are not aggregated with assets under management for purposes of determining the tier rates in Section 2.1.

### 2.4 Fee Base

The fee base includes all assets under management in the Family Relationship, including cash and cash equivalents and assets of trusts for which QHTC serves as trustee. Interests in private funds, co-investments and other private investments are included at their values determined under the Firm's Valuation Policy. Unfunded capital commitments are excluded from the fee base. Margin balances and securities-based loans are not deducted.

## SECTION 3. TRUST COMPANY FEES

### 3.1 Full Trustee

Where Quenby Holt Trust Company serves as full trustee, it charges an annual fee of 0.10% of trust assets, subject to a minimum of $10,000 per trust per year.

### 3.2 Directed Trustee

Where QHTC serves as directed trustee, acting at the direction of an investment adviser or distribution adviser named in the trust instrument, it charges a flat annual fee of $6,000 per trust.

### 3.3 Billing of Trust Company Fees

Trust Company fees are calculated on the value of trust assets on the last business day of each calendar quarter, billed quarterly in arrears by QHTC and charged to the trust account. Extraordinary services, including trust decanting, migration of situs, litigation and administration of non-marketable assets outside the Firm's management, are charged at QHTC's hourly rates disclosed at the time of engagement.

## SECTION 4. MINIMUMS AND SUPPLEMENTAL SERVICES

### 4.1 Minimum Annual Fee

The minimum annual advisory fee is $150,000 per Family Relationship. The minimum does not include Trust Company fees or supplemental service fees.

### 4.2 Supplemental Services

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| Individual income tax return (federal and one state) | $4,500 per return |
| Each additional state return | $750 |
| Fiduciary or partnership return | $3,500 per return |
| Private foundation return (Form 990-PF) | $5,000 per return |
| Bill payment and household administration | Included for Family Relationships above $50,000,000; otherwise $2,000 per month |

### 4.3 Negotiated Arrangements

Any fee arrangement that departs from this Schedule, including a waiver or reduction of the minimum annual fee, requires the written approval of the Managing Partner and is documented in the client file. The Chief Compliance Officer maintains a log of all non-standard fee arrangements.

## SECTION 5. BILLING, TERMINATION AND REFUNDS

### 5.1 Billing Frequency and Basis

The advisory fee is billed quarterly in advance, at one-quarter of the annual fee, based on the aggregate market value of the Family Relationship on the last business day of the preceding calendar quarter. The fee for a partial first quarter is prorated by the number of days remaining in the quarter. Additions or withdrawals exceeding $2,000,000 in a single account during a quarter result in a prorated adjustment on the next invoice.

### 5.2 Payment

Fees are deducted from the custodial accounts designated in the client's written authorization or, at the client's election, invoiced and payable within 30 days of the invoice date.

### 5.3 Termination and Refunds

Either party may terminate the investment management agreement on 30 days' written notice. Fees are prorated to the effective date of termination, and unearned fees paid in advance are refunded to the client on a pro rata basis within 30 days after the effective date of termination. A client may terminate within five business days after signing the agreement and receive a full refund. No termination fee is charged.
