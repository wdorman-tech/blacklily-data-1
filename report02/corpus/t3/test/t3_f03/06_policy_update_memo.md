## SECTION 1. PURPOSE AND EFFECT

This memorandum, issued by the Chief Compliance Officer on September 8, 2026, records six amendments to the Firm's policies and procedures. The amendments in Sections 2 through 6 were approved by the Management Committee on September 3, 2026, following the mid-year compliance review. The amendment in Section 7 was approved by the Management Committee on June 9, 2026 and is recorded here so that all amendments since the last restatement appear in one place. The documents affected are the Compliance Manual (Version 2026.1, effective January 15, 2026), the Valuation Policy (Version 4.0, adopted February 10, 2026), the Business Continuity Plan (revised April 21, 2026) and the Schedule of Fees (effective January 1, 2026). Those documents are not being reissued at this time. Each amendment below supersedes the cited provision to the extent inconsistent with it, and this memorandum controls until the affected documents are restated. Unless otherwise stated, each amendment is effective on the date of this memorandum.

## SECTION 2. PERSONAL TRADING DE MINIMIS EXEMPTION

**Amends:** Compliance Manual, Section 4.3 (De Minimis Exemption).

The aggregate amount below which transactions in an issuer with a market capitalization of at least $10 billion are exempt from pre-clearance is reduced from $25,000 to $10,000 in any 30 calendar day period. All other conditions of Section 4.3 are unchanged. Transactions above $10,000 in the aggregate in any 30 calendar day period require pre-clearance under Section 4.2. This amendment supersedes Compliance Manual Section 4.3.

## SECTION 3. QUARTERLY TRANSACTION REPORT DEADLINE

**Amends:** Compliance Manual, Section 4.6 (Holdings and Transaction Reports).

Quarterly transaction reports, and confirmations of the completeness of electronic data feeds, must be submitted no later than 15 days after the end of each calendar quarter, rather than 30 days. The amendment applies beginning with reports for the quarter ending September 30, 2026, which are due October 15, 2026. The deadlines for initial and annual holdings reports are unchanged. This amendment supersedes the corresponding deadline in Compliance Manual Section 4.6.

## SECTION 4. VALUATION COMMITTEE MEETING FREQUENCY

**Amends:** Valuation Policy, Section 2.3 (Meetings).

The Valuation Committee will meet monthly, within 10 business days after the end of each calendar month, rather than quarterly. The change reflects the growth in private investments held by client families and the monthly valuation reports now delivered to families through the reporting portal. At each monthly meeting the Committee will approve valuations of Level 3 positions as of the month end and review the reports described in Sections 5 through 7 of the Valuation Policy. The first monthly meeting will be held within 10 business days after September 30, 2026. Special meetings may still be called as provided in Section 2.3. This amendment supersedes the meeting frequency stated in Valuation Policy Section 2.3.

## SECTION 5. APPROVAL OF PRICE OVERRIDES

**Amends:** Valuation Policy, Section 5.3 (Overrides).

Any override of a price supplied by a pricing source must be approved in advance, in writing, by two voting members of the Valuation Committee, one of whom must be the Chief Financial Officer. The Chief Investment Officer may no longer approve overrides, because the Chief Investment Officer is responsible for the investment decisions whose results the valuations measure. The requirement for a written recommendation from the Controller, and the override log in Section 5.4, are unchanged. This amendment supersedes the approval requirement in Valuation Policy Section 5.3.

## SECTION 6. OFFSITE DATA BACKUP VENDOR

**Amends:** Business Continuity Plan, Section 5.1 (Data Backup) and Section 5.2 (Critical Vendors).

Effective August 31, 2026, following completion of the data migration, Kelvedon Cloud Recovery, Inc. replaced Ferncastle Data Vault, Inc. as the Firm's offsite data backup provider. Backup frequency (hourly incremental and weekly full backups) and the 90-day immutability period are unchanged. Backup copies are now replicated to Kelvedon facilities in Kansas City, Missouri and Denver, Colorado. Ferncastle will retain read-only copies of prior backups through September 30, 2026, after which it will destroy them and certify the destruction in writing. Kelvedon Cloud Recovery, Inc. replaces Ferncastle Data Vault, Inc. in the critical vendor list. This amendment supersedes Business Continuity Plan Sections 5.1 and 5.2 to the extent they name Ferncastle Data Vault, Inc. or its facilities.

## SECTION 7. FEE SCHEDULE BREAKPOINT

**Amends:** Schedule of Fees, Section 2.1 (Tiered Rates) and Section 2.2 (Illustration), and the corresponding table in Item 5.1 of the Firm's Form ADV Part 2A brochure dated March 27, 2026.

The first breakpoint is lowered from $25,000,000 to $20,000,000. The 0.80% rate now applies to the first $20,000,000 of Family Relationship assets, and the 0.60% rate applies to Family Relationship assets above $20,000,000 up to $50,000,000. The rates and breakpoints for assets above $50,000,000 are unchanged. The change was approved by the Management Committee on June 9, 2026, communicated to clients by letter dated June 12, 2026, and is effective for fees billed for the quarter beginning July 1, 2026. Under the amended schedule, a Family Relationship with $80,000,000 of assets under management pays an annual advisory fee of $475,000 rather than $485,000. The minimum annual fee of $150,000 is unchanged. The Firm has reflected this change in an interim amendment to its brochure. This amendment supersedes Schedule of Fees Sections 2.1 and 2.2 and brochure Item 5.1 to the extent inconsistent.
