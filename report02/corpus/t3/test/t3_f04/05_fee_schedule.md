## SECTION 1. GENERAL TERMS

### 1.1 Scope and Effective Date

This Fee Schedule sets out the standard management fees of Wrenholt Pryce Asset Management LLC (the "Firm") for separate accounts and for Wrenholt Pryce Long Duration Credit Fund, L.P. (the "Commingled Fund"), effective January 1, 2026. The fees payable by an existing client are those stated in its investment management agreement ("IMA"), which controls over this schedule.

### 1.2 Negotiated Fees

Fees are negotiable. The Firm may agree to fees that differ from this schedule based on account size, guideline complexity, reporting requirements and the overall relationship. Where a client's IMA contains a most-favored-nation provision, the Chief Compliance Officer reviews each new fee arrangement in the same strategy to determine whether it must be offered to that client.

## SECTION 2. SEPARATE ACCOUNT FEE SCHEDULES

Rates are annual percentages of account assets and are applied separately to each tier. An account's fee is the sum of the fees calculated for each tier.

### 2.1 Core Fixed Income

| Account assets | Annual fee rate |
|---|---|
| First $100 million | 0.20% |
| Next $150 million | 0.15% |
| Next $250 million | 0.12% |
| Over $500 million | 0.10% |

### 2.2 Long Duration Credit

| Account assets | Annual fee rate |
|---|---|
| First $100 million | 0.30% |
| Next $200 million | 0.25% |
| Over $300 million | 0.20% |

### 2.3 Insurance Core

| Account assets | Annual fee rate |
|---|---|
| First $250 million | 0.14% |
| Next $250 million | 0.10% |
| Over $500 million | 0.07% |

Insurance Core fees include monthly statutory reporting support. Custom regulatory reporting requested by a client may be subject to an additional negotiated fee.

### 2.4 Non-Discretionary Accounts

Fees for non-discretionary accounts are negotiated individually and generally range from 0.05% to 0.08% per annum of account assets.

## SECTION 3. COMMINGLED FUND

### 3.1 Management Fee

The Commingled Fund pays the Firm a management fee at an annual rate of 0.28% of the Fund's net asset value, accrued monthly and payable quarterly in arrears. The Fund pays no performance fee or performance allocation. The management fee is waived on interests held by Firm employees.

### 3.2 Fund Expenses

The Fund bears its operating expenses, including administration, custody, audit, tax preparation and legal expenses, up to 0.05% per annum of its average net asset value. The Firm bears operating expenses in excess of that cap.

### 3.3 Subscriptions and Redemptions

Subscriptions are accepted as of the first business day of each month. The minimum initial investment is $10 million and the minimum additional investment is $1 million, subject to waiver by the general partner. Investors may redeem as of the last business day of any month on at least 15 business days' prior written notice. No redemption fee is charged.

## SECTION 4. BILLING

### 4.1 Frequency and Basis

Separate account fees are billed quarterly in arrears based on the average of the three month-end market values of the account during the quarter, including accrued interest. Market values are determined under the Firm's Valuation Policy and Procedures and reconciled to custodian records.

### 4.2 Invoices and Payment

The Firm delivers invoices within 15 business days after the end of each calendar quarter. Payment is due within 30 days after the invoice date. The Firm does not deduct fees directly from separate accounts.

### 4.3 Aggregation

At the client's request, accounts of the same plan sponsor, or of insurance companies under common control, are aggregated for purposes of applying the tiers in Section 2. The aggregate fee is allocated among the accounts in proportion to their assets.

## SECTION 5. MINIMUMS

### 5.1 Minimum Account Size

The minimum account size for a separate account is $100 million. The Firm may accept a smaller account in its discretion.

### 5.2 Minimum Annual Fee

Each separate account is subject to a minimum annual fee of $150,000, billed in equal quarterly amounts and prorated for any partial year.

## SECTION 6. TERMINATION AND REFUNDS

### 6.1 Termination

Either party may terminate an IMA on 30 days' prior written notice unless the IMA provides otherwise. No termination fee is charged.

### 6.2 Final Fees and Refunds

Fees accrue through the termination date and are prorated for the final partial quarter based on the number of days the account was under management. A client that has agreed in writing to pay fees quarterly in advance receives a prorated refund of any unearned fees within 30 days after the termination date.
