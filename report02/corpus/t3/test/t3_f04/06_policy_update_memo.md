## SECTION 2. AMENDMENTS TO THE COMPLIANCE MANUAL

### 2.1 Minimum Holding Period

This Section 2.1 amends Section 4.4 (Holding Period) of the Compliance Manual. Effective September 1, 2026, covered securities must be held for at least 60 calendar days from the date of acquisition. The 60-day period supersedes the 30-day period stated in Section 4.4. The exclusion for exchange-traded funds that track broad market indices, the last-in, first-out convention, the CCO's authority to grant hardship exceptions and the disgorgement provision are unchanged.

### 2.2 Gifts Received

This Section 2.2 amends Section 5.2 (Gifts Received) of the Compliance Manual. Effective September 1, 2026, supervised persons may not accept gifts from any single business contact with an aggregate value exceeding $100 per calendar year. The $100 limit supersedes the $250 limit stated in Section 5.2. Gifts received from a business contact earlier in 2026 count toward the new limit for the remainder of the calendar year. The entertainment provisions of Section 5.3, including the requirement of prior CCO approval for entertainment exceeding $500 per person per event, and the limits on gifts given in Section 5.4 are unchanged.

## SECTION 3. AMENDMENTS TO THE VALUATION POLICY

### 3.1 Stale Price Threshold

This Section 3.1 amends Section 6.1 (Stale Price Threshold) of the Valuation Policy and Procedures. Effective September 1, 2026, a security whose price from its assigned source has not changed for three consecutive business days is flagged as stale. The three-day threshold supersedes the five-day threshold stated in Section 6.1. The escalation of securities unchanged for 20 consecutive business days to the Valuation Committee under Section 6.2 is unchanged.

### 3.2 Price Override Approval

This Section 3.2 amends Section 5.3 (Overrides) of the Valuation Policy and Procedures. Effective September 1, 2026, a price override must be approved in writing by both the Chief Financial Officer and the Chief Risk Officer before it is applied. Approval by the Chief Financial Officer alone, as stated in Section 5.3, is no longer sufficient. If either officer is unavailable, the override may be applied only with the approval of the Valuation Committee at a special meeting at which a quorum is present. The reporting and lapse provisions of Section 5.3 are unchanged.

## SECTION 4. AMENDMENT TO THE BUSINESS CONTINUITY PLAN

### 4.1 Recovery Time Objective for Trading Systems

This Section 4.1 amends Section 3.1 (Recovery Time and Recovery Point Objectives) of the Business Continuity Plan. Pelloran Systems completed the migration of the Firm's hosted order management system to active operation in two regions in June 2026. Effective September 1, 2026, the recovery time objective for order management, trading platforms and market data is two hours from the declaration of a business disruption. The two-hour objective supersedes the four-hour objective stated in Section 3.1. The recovery point objective of 15 minutes and the objectives for all other systems are unchanged. The full test of the Plan scheduled for the fourth quarter of 2026 will measure performance against the new objective.
