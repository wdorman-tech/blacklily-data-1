## Slide 7. Key Risks

- Investors can lose all or most of their investment. A failed or repriced transaction can cost far more than the expected gain.
- Short losses are theoretically unlimited, and leverage magnifies losses and can force sales at unfavorable prices.
- Restructured securities may trade thinly, and some positions are valued by the General Partner using models.
- The incentive allocation may encourage risk-taking; the Fund and the managed accounts share opportunities under a written allocation policy.
- The Fund depends on its two founders and on the solvency of its prime brokers.
