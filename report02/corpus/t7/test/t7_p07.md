### How We Invest

Selbrook Aldine has managed one strategy since July 2013: 30 to 40 U.S. companies with market capitalizations of $300 million to $6 billion at purchase, bought at a discount to our estimate of normalized earnings power and tangible book value. We are an independent, eleven-person firm in Omaha, Nebraska, managing $1.9 billion in the strategy for foundations, endowments, retirement plans and high-net-worth individuals through separately managed accounts.

### The Record in Context

Over the ten calendar years from 2016 through 2025, the composite outperformed its comparison index in every single year, net of fees.

The strategy was built for periods when investors price smaller companies for the worst. Measured from the market low on March 23, 2020 through December 31, 2021, the composite returned 131.4% net of fees. That is the stretch we point to when clients ask what the discipline is for.

### Where the Last Twelve Months Came From

For the twelve months ended June 30, 2026, the composite returned 23.5% net of fees (24.8% gross of fees), against 15.5% for the index. The five largest contributors to the composite's return were a regional bank holding company (+3.1 percentage points), an aftermarket truck-parts distributor (+2.6), a specialty chemicals producer (+2.2), a title insurance underwriter (+1.9) and a short-line railroad operator (+1.7). The five largest detractors were a home-furnishings retailer (-1.4), a contract driller (-0.8), a hospital operator (-0.5), a trucking company (-0.4) and a homebuilder (-0.3). Every holding is measured the same way, before fees, because fees are charged at the account level. The contribution of every holding over the period is available on request.

### What to Keep in Mind

Small-cap stocks are generally more volatile and less liquid than large-cap stocks, and a 30- to 40-stock portfolio is moved more by any single holding than a diversified index is. Value investing can trail other approaches, and the broad market, for extended periods. The composite has had losing years, including 2015, 2018 and 2022, and investors can lose money in the strategy.
