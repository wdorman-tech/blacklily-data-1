### Slide 10. Principal Risks

- **Deal risk.** Transactions may fail or be repriced; one failed deal can cost several times its expected spread.
- **Leverage and short selling.** Leverage magnifies losses; short losses are theoretically unlimited.
- **Correlation in stress.** Positions can fall together, and with equities, as in March 2020.
- **Liquidity, concentration and counterparties.** Some positions are hard to sell, a few can drive results, and the Fund depends on its prime broker.
- **Key person and withdrawals.** The Fund depends on Mr. Haske; withdrawals may be gated or suspended. See "Certain Risk Factors" in the PPM.
