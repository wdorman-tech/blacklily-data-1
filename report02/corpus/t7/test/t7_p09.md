### Strategy Overview

The Core Plus Fixed Income Strategy (the "Strategy") invests primarily in U.S. dollar-denominated investment-grade bonds, and may hold up to 20% in below-investment-grade corporate debt and up to 10% in emerging-market debt that is dollar-denominated or hedged to the dollar. Duration is kept within 1.5 years of the Index. The Strategy is offered through separately managed accounts with a $25 million minimum.

### Investment Process

- **Top-down.** A five-member investment committee sets duration, yield-curve and sector ranges monthly.
- **Bottom-up.** Nine credit analysts rate every corporate issuer before purchase. Non-government issuers are limited to 2% of the portfolio (3% if rated A or better).
- **Risk.** Tracking error is managed within 1.0% to 2.5% against the Index and reviewed daily by a risk team independent of the portfolio managers.

### Return Objective

Target: the Strategy seeks to outperform the Index by 0.75% to 1.25% per year, net of fees, over rolling five-year periods.

**How the target was set.** The range is the Investment Manager's estimate of the excess return the Strategy's tracking-error budget can reasonably support. It assumes (1) excess return from sector allocation, security selection and out-of-index holdings in proportions similar to the composite's history; (2) a management fee of 0.30% per year, the highest in the standard schedule; (3) no material change in the team or process; and (4) an Index broadly similar in composition to today's. The composite's net excess return was 0.8% per year for the five years ended June 30, 2026, and has fallen below 0.75% in some rolling five-year periods since inception.

**Limitations.** The target is hypothetical. It is not the result of actual trading, is not a projection of any account's return, and may not be achieved. It was developed with knowledge of the composite's past results. Actual results depend on rate, credit and liquidity conditions that may differ from those assumed, and may fall below the target, or be negative, over any period. The composite trailed the Index, net of fees, in 3 of the 14 full calendar years since inception.

### Principal Risks

- **Interest rates.** Bond prices fall when rates rise; a six-year duration makes the portfolio more rate-sensitive than shorter-duration portfolios.
- **Credit and high yield.** Issuers may be downgraded or default; below-investment-grade debt can fall sharply in recessions.
- **Emerging markets.** Political, legal and convertibility risk; hedges may be incomplete or costly.
- **Securitized and liquidity.** Securitized bonds may prepay or extend unexpectedly, and some bonds sell only at a discount in stressed markets.
- **Derivatives.** Treasury futures, interest-rate swaps and credit default swap indices used to manage exposure can lose more than the amount invested and create counterparty risk.
- **Manager risk.** Security and sector decisions may cause the Strategy to trail the Index, and accounts can lose principal.
