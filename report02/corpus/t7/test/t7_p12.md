If you are within ten years of retirement, or already there, the questions change. It is no longer only how much you have saved. It is which account you draw from first, when to claim Social Security, how to keep Medicare surcharges and taxes from quietly eating into your income, and what happens to it all when you are gone. We built this two-and-a-half-hour workshop around those questions, and we would like you to join us, with a guest, for dinner and a working session.

## What we will cover

### 1. Sequencing withdrawals

The order in which you draw from taxable, tax-deferred and Roth accounts affects how much tax you pay over a retirement, and the right order differs from one household to the next. We will walk through a worked example and the tradeoffs involved, including the years when the conventional order is the wrong one.

### 2. Social Security timing

Claiming at 62, at full retirement age or at 70: what you gain and give up with each choice, how spousal and survivor benefits change the math, and why the break-even age is only one input.

### 3. Roth conversions and Medicare surcharges

How partial conversions in the years between retirement and required minimum distributions can lower lifetime taxes, and how the same conversions can push income over the Medicare IRMAA thresholds two years later if they are not sized carefully.

### 4. Our Retirement Income Model

Our Retirement Income Model, applied to market returns for every 30-year period from 1970 through 2025, would have supported a 6.5% annual withdrawal, raised each year for inflation, without ever running out of money. Dana will show how the model turns a household's savings, pensions and Social Security into a first-year spending figure.

### 5. Private credit for retirees

Learn how our Private Credit Income sleeve can pay a monthly distribution, smooth out the swings on your statement and add a source of return that does not move with the stock market.

### 6. Estate basics with our guest

Estate planning attorney Nadia Quade of Hesper Quade PLLC will cover beneficiary designations, revocable trusts, and the documents every retiree should review after a move, a marriage or a death in the family. Hesper Quade PLLC is not affiliated with Ollerby Strand, and neither firm pays the other for referrals.
