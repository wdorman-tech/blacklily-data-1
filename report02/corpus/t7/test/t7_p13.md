## Strategy overview

The U.S. Small-Mid Cap Core Equity strategy invests in 55 to 70 U.S. companies with market capitalizations between $1 billion and $25 billion at purchase. The team looks for businesses that earn a return on invested capital above their cost of capital, carry balance sheets the team judges able to fund operations through a recession without issuing new equity, and trade below the team's estimate of intrinsic value. Offered as a separately managed account with a $1 million minimum, it has been managed since its October 1, 2011 inception by a three-person team led by Portfolio Manager Everett Corrigall.

## Fees

Standard annual fee schedule: 0.85% on the first $5 million, 0.70% on the next $10 million and 0.60% on assets above $15 million, billed quarterly in arrears. Custody is provided by the client's chosen custodian, which charges its own fees.

## Principal risks

- **Market risk.** The portfolio's value will fluctuate and can fall sharply in a broad sell-off. The composite's largest peak-to-trough decline since inception, measured net of fees, was 34.8%, from January through March 2020.
- **Small and mid-cap risk.** Smaller companies can be less liquid, more volatile and more dependent on credit than large companies.
- **Style risk.** The emphasis on profitability and valuation can cause the strategy to lag the index for extended periods when lower-quality or faster-growing companies lead.
- **Concentration risk.** With 55 to 70 holdings and sector weights of up to 30%, results can differ materially from the index, and a single holding or sector can have an outsized effect.
