## What to expect from this strategy

A dividend value approach has real limitations, and we would rather you hear them from us.

- **It can trail for long stretches.** When growth stocks lead, as they did for much of the last decade, a portfolio of dividend-paying value companies tends to lag.
- **It is concentrated.** Thirty to forty holdings, with meaningful weights in financials, energy, utilities and consumer staples, mean results can differ sharply from the index in either direction.
- **Dividends can be cut.** A company that has paid for decades can reduce or suspend its dividend, and its share price usually falls when it does.
- **It is sensitive to interest rates.** Utilities and other steady dividend payers often decline when rates rise.
- **It can lose money.** Dividend-paying companies fall in broad sell-offs too, sometimes sharply, and an account can be worth less than was invested.
