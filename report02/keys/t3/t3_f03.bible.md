## Compliance manual facts (Version 2026.1, effective January 15, 2026)

- Pre-clearance via Tollard; approvals by CCO or Compliance Manager; CCO's trades by CFO; valid only for the business day granted.
- De minimis: issuers with market cap at least $10 billion; aggregate not more than $25,000 per issuer in any 30 calendar days (ORIGINAL 4.3) -> memo $10,000.
- IPOs prohibited; limited offerings need CCO pre-approval. 30-calendar-day holding period. Blackout 3 business days before/after Core Equity trade.
- Initial holdings within 10 days; annual holdings by February 14 (as of Dec 31); quarterly transaction reports within 30 days (ORIGINAL 4.6) -> memo 15 days.
- Gifts: $250 per source or recipient per calendar year without CCO prior written approval; entertainment over $500 per person per event needs CCO pre-approval; cash prohibited.
- Political contributions: all contributions by any Supervised Person to state/local officials, candidates, parties, PACs pre-cleared by CCO; de minimis $350 / $150 still require pre-clearance; 2-year look-back (6 months non-solicitors).
- Client-Affiliated Issuer List (officer, director or 10% holder); CCO prior approval for client and Reportable Account trades; family member's own trades need written confirmation of open window or Rule 10b5-1 plan.
- Marketing: CCO approval (Compliance Manager for non-performance pieces); no client testimonials.
- Allocation of limited private opportunities: Investment Committee determines eligibility then pro rata by shortfall vs IPS private markets target; minimum $250,000 per family; CCO reviews before commitments submitted; employees only after families' demand satisfied.
- Trade Oversight Committee semi-annual (January and July).
- Cyber: MFA; quarterly access reviews; Northgale 24/7; Pellow Stack MSP; critical patches within 14 days; quarterly vulnerability scans; quarterly phishing simulations; annual penetration test by Ostler Vane; incident: ISO notifies CCO within one hour; Northgale containment/forensics; outside counsel named in BCP; S-P notice within 30 days; service providers notify within 72 hours; callback verification; dual approval for disbursements over $100,000.
- Records: seven years, first two in office; Arcwell WORM.
- E-comms: lexicon-flagged review weekly; random sample at least 50 messages monthly; off-channel message forwarded within one business day.
- Annual review: CCO with Hethersett; report to Management Committee no later than April 30.
- Training: new hires within 30 days; annual training in fourth quarter; annual attestation by January 31.
