## DDQ design notes

- The memo makes six amendments; five are tested by the five `supersession` questions (q02 holding period, q08 Core breakpoint, q14 stale threshold, q21 RTO, q29 override approver).
- The sixth amendment (memo 2.2, gifts received $250 -> $100) is tested inside q25, which is labelled `multi_part` because it also needs the unchanged CM 5.3 entertainment approver. q25 is the memo-sourced answerable item that also involves supersession: its source document is the memo and it carries a `STALE_250` reject. Per-trap statistics count it in the `multi_part` bucket, so the supersession bucket has five items, not six; a stale answer on q25 still grades as STALE.
