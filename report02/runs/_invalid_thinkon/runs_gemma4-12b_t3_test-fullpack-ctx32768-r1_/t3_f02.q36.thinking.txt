    *   Looking at `03_valuation_policy.md`:
        *   Section 6 is "STALE PRICE MONITORING".
        *   Section 6.1 "Stale Price Reports" describes the process:
            *   "A price that is unchanged for three consecutive business days is flagged and investigated by the Controller."
            *   "A price that is unchanged for five consecutive business days is escalated to the Chief Financial Officer."
            *   "A price that is unchanged for ten consecutive business days may not continue to be used without a fair value determination by the Committee."
