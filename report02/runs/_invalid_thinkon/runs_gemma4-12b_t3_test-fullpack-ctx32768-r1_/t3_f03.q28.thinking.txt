*   Question: How does the firm control trading in securities of public companies at which a member of a client family is an officer, director or 10% holder?
    *   Key terms: "public companies", "member of a client family", "officer", "director", "10% holder", "control trading".

    *   *02_compliance_manual.md*: Section 7.3 (Client-Affiliated Issuers) specifically addresses this.
        *   "The CCO maintains a Client-Affiliated Issuer List of public companies of which a member of a client family is an officer, director or beneficial owner of 10% or more of any class of equity securities."
        *   "No transaction in a security of an issuer on the Client-Affiliated Issuer List may be effected for any client account or Reportable Account without the prior approval of the CCO."
        *   "For a transaction in the affiliated family member's own accounts, the CCO will also require written confirmation from the family member, or the issuer's general counsel, that the transaction is permitted under the issuer's trading window or is made under a Rule 10b5-1 plan, and that any required Rule 144 or Section 16 filings will be made."

    *   The document says: "No transaction in a security of an issuer on the Client-Affiliated Issuer List may be effected for any client account or Reportable Account without the prior approval of the CCO."
    *   It also mentions requirements for the "affiliated family member's own accounts".

    *   Answer: No transaction in a security of an issuer on the Client-Affiliated Issuer List may be effected for any client account or Reportable Account without the prior approval of the CCO. For a transaction in the affiliated family member's own accounts, the CCO will also require written confirmation from the family member, or the issuer's general counsel, that the transaction is permitted under the issuer's trading window or is made under a Rule 10b5-1 plan, and that any required Rule 144 or Section 16 filings will be made.
