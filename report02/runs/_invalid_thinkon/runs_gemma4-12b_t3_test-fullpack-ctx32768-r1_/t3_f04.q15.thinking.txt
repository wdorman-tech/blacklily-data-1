    *   Section 6.1 (Scope): "the Firm treats every employee as a covered associate for purposes of this Section."
    *   Section 6.2 (Pre-Clearance and Limits):
        *   "Every employee must obtain the CCO's written pre-clearance before making any political contribution, regardless of amount, to an incumbent, candidate or successful candidate for state or local office (including a candidate for federal office who currently holds a state or local office), a state or local political party, or a political action committee."
        *   "The CCO will approve a contribution to an official or candidate only if it does not exceed $350 per election for an official or candidate for whom the employee is entitled to vote, or $150 per election for any other official or candidate. Primary and general elections are separate elections. A contribution by a spouse or household member made at the employee's direction is treated as a contribution by the employee."
    *   Section 6.3 (Pre-Employment Review): "Each candidate for employment must disclose all political contributions made during the two years before the anticipated start date, and the CCO reviews the disclosure before an offer becomes final."
    *   Section 6.4 (Solicitation, Coordination and Placement Agents): "Employees may not solicit or coordinate contributions to an official of a government entity, or payments to a political party of a state or locality, where the Firm provides or seeks to provide advisory services. The Firm does not compensate placement agents or other third parties to solicit government entities."

    *   The question asks for "restrictions on political contributions by employees".
    *   The core restrictions are:
        1.  Pre-clearance from the CCO for any contribution to specific entities (incumbents, candidates, parties, PACs).
        2.  Limits: $350 per election for those the employee can vote for, $150 for others.
        3.  Primary and general elections are separate.
        4.  Spouse/household member contributions at employee's direction count as employee's.
        5.  Pre-employment disclosure of the last 2 years.
        6.  Prohibition on soliciting or coordinating contributions.
