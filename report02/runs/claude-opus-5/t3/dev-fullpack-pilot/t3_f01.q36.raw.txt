{"answer": "The firm does not currently advise any government entity, but it applies political contribution controls under Rule 206(4)-5 to all Supervised Persons. Pre-clearance: every Supervised Person must obtain CCO pre-clearance before making any political contribution, regardless of amount, to a state or local official, a candidate for state or local office, a state or local political party, or a political action committee. This also covers contributions by a spouse or domestic partner from jointly held funds. Supervised Persons may not solicit or coordinate contributions, and the CCO records each request and decision. New-hire look-back: before an offer is extended, the CCO reviews the candidate's contributions for the two years before the hiring date if the candidate will solicit clients or supervise those who do, and for the six months before the hiring date for all other candidates. The CCO keeps contribution records as required by Rule 204-2(a)(18).
