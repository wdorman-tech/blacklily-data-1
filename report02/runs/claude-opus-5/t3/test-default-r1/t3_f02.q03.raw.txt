Access persons must hold any position in a reportable security acquired in a covered account for at least 60 calendar days from the date of acquisition, and may not close a short position within 60 calendar days of opening it. Positions acquired before September 1, 2026 remain subject to the 30-day period. The disgorgement and hardship exception provisions of Section 4.5 are unchanged.
