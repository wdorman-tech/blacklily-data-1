VALUATION COMMITTEE > 2.1 Composition; 2.2 Quorum and Voting", "quote": "The Valuation Committee (the \"Committee\") has four voting members: the Chief Financial Officer, Oren Pellisier, who chairs the Committee; the President and Chief Operating Officer, Mireille Dansby; the Head of Risk, Ingrid Vallancey; and the Controller, Lydia Marchbanks, who also acts as secretary. The General Counsel and Chief Compliance Officer attends every meeting as a non-voting member. The Chief Investment Officer and members of the investment team may attend to provide information about an investment, but may not vote and are excused before any vote is taken. ... A quorum consists of three voting members, one of whom must be the Chief Financial Officer or the Chief Operating Officer. Determinations are made by majority vote of the voting members present."}
