TRADE AGGREGATION AND ALLOCATION > 9.4 Limited Private Investment Opportunities","quote":"Private funds with limited capacity and co-investment opportunities are allocated as follows. The Head of Private Investments presents the opportunity to the Investment Committee, which first determines which Family Relationships are eligible, taking into account investor qualification status, suitability, liquidity, concentration and the IPS. Where aggregate eligible demand exceeds available capacity, the Investment Committee allocates the opportunity among eligible Family Relationships pro rata in proportion to each Family Relationship's shortfall relative to the private markets target allocation in its Investment Policy Statement, subject to a minimum allocation of $250,000 per Family Relationship. The Head of Private Investments documents the eligibility determination and the allocation, and the CCO reviews each allocation before commitments are submitted. Supervised Persons may participate only after the demand of all eligible Family Relationships has been satisfied."}
