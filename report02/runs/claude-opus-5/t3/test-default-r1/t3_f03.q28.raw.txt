For a transaction in the affiliated family member's own accounts, the CCO will also require written confirmation from the family member, or the issuer's general counsel, that the transaction is permitted under the issuer's trading window or is made under a Rule 10b5-1 plan, and that any required Rule 144 or Section 16 filings will be made."}
