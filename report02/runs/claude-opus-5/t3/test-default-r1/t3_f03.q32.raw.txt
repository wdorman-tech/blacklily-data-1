APPROVAL OF PRICE OVERRIDES","quote":"**Amends:** Valuation Policy, Section 5.3 (Overrides). Any override of a price supplied by a pricing source must be approved in advance, in writing, by two voting members of the Valuation Committee, one of whom must be the Chief Financial Officer. The Chief Investment Officer may no longer approve overrides, because the Chief Investment Officer is responsible for the investment decisions whose results the valuations measure. The requirement for a written recommendation from the Controller, and the override log in Section 5.4, are unchanged. This amendment supersedes the approval requirement in Valuation Policy Section 5.3."}
