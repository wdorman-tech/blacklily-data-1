AMENDMENTS TO THE VALUATION POLICY > 3.2 Price Override Approval","quote":"This Section 3.2 amends Section 5.3 (Overrides) of the Valuation Policy and Procedures. Effective September 1, 2026, a price override must be approved in writing by both the Chief Financial Officer and the Chief Risk Officer before it is applied. Approval by the Chief Financial Officer alone, as stated in Section 5.3, is no longer sufficient. If either officer is unavailable, the override may be applied only with the approval of the Valuation Committee at a special meeting at which a quorum is present."}
