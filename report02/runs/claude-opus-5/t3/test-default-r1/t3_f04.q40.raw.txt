Each review covers the preceding calendar year and considers compliance matters that arose, changes in the Firm's business and changes in applicable law. The CCO delivers a written report on the review to the Management Committee by March 31. ... Every second year, Hessendale Compliance Advisors, an outside compliance consulting firm, performs an independent assessment of the compliance program as part of the annual review, including sample reviews of personal trading, allocation, marketing and pay-to-play records. The most recent independent assessment was performed as part of the review of calendar year 2024 and was completed in the first quarter of 2025."}
