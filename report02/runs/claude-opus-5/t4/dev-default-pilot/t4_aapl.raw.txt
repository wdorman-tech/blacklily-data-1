**Apple reported record June-quarter revenue of $109.4 billion, up 16 percent year over year, and diluted EPS of $2.02, up 29 percent, with both gross margin and EPS lifted by tariff refunds.**

## Results
- Net sales for fiscal Q3 2026 (ended June 27, 2026) were $109,417 million, against $94,036 million in the year-ago quarter. The release calls this a June-quarter record.
- Reported gross margin was 50.1 percent. This includes a favorable impact of approximately 2 percentage points from tariff refunds. The release presents no non-GAAP or adjusted figures.
- Diluted EPS was $2.02 (year-ago quarter: $1.57), including a $0.11 favorable impact from tariff refunds. Net income was $29,789 million (year-ago quarter: $23,434 million).
- Operating income was $35,695 million (year-ago quarter: $28,202 million). R&D expense was $11,729 million (year-ago quarter: $8,866 million).

## Segments and drivers
- iPhone, Mac and Services each set June-quarter records, and the CEO said each grew by double digits:
  - iPhone: $54,252 million (year-ago quarter: $44,582 million)
  - Mac: $10,352 million (year-ago quarter: $8,046 million)
  - Services: $30,739 million (year-ago quarter: $27,423 million)
- iPad was the only category below the year-ago quarter, at $6,191 million against $6,581 million.
- The CEO cited double-digit growth in every geographic segment. Greater China was $18,816 million (year-ago quarter: $15,369 million) and Europe was $29,395 million (year-ago quarter: $24,014 million).
- Management cited an all-time-high installed base of active devices and the WWDC26 introduction of the all-new Siri AI. The release gives no figures for either.

## Outlook
- The release provides no guidance or outlook for revenue, margins or earnings for any future period.

## Capital and balance sheet
- For the nine months ended June 27, 2026 (prior-year nine months in parentheses):
  - Cash generated by operating activities: $116,996 million ($81,754 million)
  - Share repurchases: $62,094 million ($70,579 million)
  - Dividends and dividend equivalents: $11,778 million ($11,559 million)
- At June 27, 2026 (September 27, 2025 in parentheses):
  - Cash and cash equivalents: $39,544 million
  - Commercial paper: $1,997 million ($7,979 million)
  - Non-current term debt: $71,340 million ($78,328 million)
- The board declared a cash dividend of $0.27 per share, payable August 13, 2026, to shareholders of record on August 10, 2026.

## Watch items
- **Tariff refunds:** The release gives their effect on gross margin and EPS but not their dollar amount, their timing, or whether more are expected. How repeatable are this quarter's gross margin and EPS?
- **Balance sheet shifts:** Two balance sheet items changed sharply, and the release explains neither:
  - Inventories were $11,092 million at June 27, 2026, against $5,718 million at September 27, 2025.
  - Net intangible assets were $20,342 million, against $11,093 million at September 27, 2025.
- **Disclosure gaps:** The CFO cites a June-quarter record for operating cash flow, but the release shows cash flows only for the nine-month period. The committee should ask for the quarterly figure and for the reasons iPad sales were lower.
