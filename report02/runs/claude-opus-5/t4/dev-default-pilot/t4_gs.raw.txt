**Goldman Sachs' second-quarter 2026 net revenues rose 39% year over year to $20.34 billion, led by Global Banking & Markets, and diluted EPS was $20.98; the quarterly dividend was raised to $5.00 per share.**

## Results
- Net revenues were $20.34 billion, up 39% from 2Q25 and 18% from 1Q26. Net earnings were $6.63 billion.
- Diluted EPS was $20.98, compared with $10.91 in 2Q25 and $17.55 in 1Q26.
- Annualized ROE was 23.5% (21.7% for the first half of 2026). Book value per share rose 1.8% in the quarter to $367.67.
- Operating expenses were $11.67 billion, up 26% from 2Q25, mainly on higher compensation and transaction-based expenses. The efficiency ratio was 58.8% for the first half of 2026 (62.0% for the first half of 2025).
- Provision for credit losses was $102 million, compared with $384 million in 2Q25 and $315 million in 1Q26.

## Segments and drivers
- Global Banking & Markets net revenues were $15.52 billion, up 53% from 2Q25. Within that, Equities was $7.42 billion (up 72%), FICC $4.59 billion (up 32%) and investment banking fees $3.40 billion (up 55%).
- Asset & Wealth Management net revenues were $4.60 billion, up 20% from 2Q25. Higher management and other fees and higher private equity investment gains drove the increase. Private banking and lending fell because of lower net interest margin on Marcus deposits.
- Platform Solutions net revenues fell 64% from 2Q25 to $221 million. The decline was primarily due to net markdowns on the Apple Card loan portfolio, which was transferred to held for sale in 4Q25.
- Assets under supervision (preliminary) were $4,041 billion, with $230 billion of total net inflows. Long-term net inflows included $31 billion from the Innovator acquisition and $15 billion of outflows from the TFI disposition.

## Outlook
- The release gives no financial guidance or outlook. It states only that the investment banking fees backlog increased compared with both the end of 1Q26 and the end of 2025.

## Capital and balance sheet
- The quarterly dividend rises to $5.00 from $4.50 per share, payable September 29, 2026. In 2Q26 the firm returned $5.36 billion to common shareholders, including $4.00 billion of repurchases (4.1 million shares at an average $984.57).
- The following are preliminary estimates. The standardized CET1 ratio was 12.9% at June 30, 2026, compared with 12.5% at March 31, 2026. The supplementary leverage ratio was 4.3%, compared with 4.7%. Global core liquid assets averaged $555 billion in 2Q26, compared with $494 billion in 1Q26.

## Watch items
- Tax benefits from employee share-based awards reduced first-half 2026 taxes by about $965 million. That added roughly $3.15 to diluted EPS and 1.7 points to annualized ROE for that period. How repeatable is this?
- What further net markdowns could arise on the held-for-sale Apple Card portfolio?
- The supplementary leverage ratio fell while total assets rose to $2,128 billion from $2,060 billion. Capital and balance sheet figures are preliminary and may be revised in the 10-Q.
