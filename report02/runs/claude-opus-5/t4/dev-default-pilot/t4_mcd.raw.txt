**McDonald's second-quarter 2026 diluted EPS rose 6% to $3.32, with global comparable sales up 1.3% and negative U.S. comparable guest counts.**

## Results
- Global comparable sales rose 1.3%, versus 3.8% in Q2 2025. Comparable sales exclude currency translation.
- Revenues were $7,099 million, up 4% (2% constant currency). Systemwide sales, which include franchisee sales not recorded as revenue, rose 5% (4% constant currency) to $37 billion.
- Operating income was $3,338 million, up 3% (2% constant currency). It reflected $52 million of pre-tax charges ($43 million in Q2 2025), primarily restructuring under Accelerating the Organization. On a non-GAAP basis excluding both years' charges, operating income rose 4% (2% constant currency).
- Net income was $2,362 million, up 5% (4% constant currency). Diluted EPS was $3.32, up 6% (5% constant currency). Non-GAAP diluted EPS was $3.38 versus $3.19 in Q2 2025, up 6% (5% constant currency).

## Segments and drivers
- U.S. comparable sales rose 0.8% (2.5% in Q2 2025). Growth came from higher average check, including favorable product mix, partly offset by negative comparable guest counts.
- International Operated Markets rose 1.5%, led by Germany, Australia and the U.K., partly offset by France. International Developmental Licensed Markets rose 1.9%, led by Japan, partly offset by negative comparable sales in China.
- Excluding charges, results were primarily driven by higher sales-driven Franchised margins and higher Other operating income, partly offset by higher SG&A. Reported "Other" SG&A rose 19% to $706 million. Other operating (income) expense, net was $37 million of income, versus $29 million of expense in Q2 2025.
- Across 70 markets, Systemwide sales to loyalty members for the trailing twelve months rose over 20% to $40 billion. Active loyalty users (active in the last 90 days) rose 13% to nearly 220 million at quarter-end.

## Outlook
- The release provides no guidance or outlook.
- The company appointed Skye Anderson President of McDonald's USA. The CEO cited "an opportunity to raise the bar in the U.S." but gave no targets.

## Capital and balance sheet
- The release contains no balance sheet, cash flow, dividend or buyback figures. It defines free cash flow but does not report it.
- Diluted weighted average shares were 711.1 million, versus 717.6 million in Q2 2025 (down 1%). Interest expense rose 5% to $409 million.

## Watch items
- U.S. comparable guest counts were negative. What is Skye Anderson's plan for McDonald's USA, and on what timetable?
- How much of the 19% rise in "Other" SG&A reflects restructuring ($52 million of charges in the quarter) versus ongoing spending?
- Net income rose 5% and pre-tax income 3%, while the tax provision fell 6% to $574 million. What drove the lower provision, and will it persist?
