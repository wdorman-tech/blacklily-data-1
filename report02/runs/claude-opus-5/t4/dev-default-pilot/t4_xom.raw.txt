**ExxonMobil reported second-quarter 2026 GAAP earnings of $14.5 billion ($3.48 per diluted share), up from $4,183 million in 1Q26, with adjusted (non-GAAP) earnings of $14.7 billion and free cash flow (non-GAAP) of $17.2 billion.**

## Results
- Adjusted earnings (non-GAAP) were $14,680 million, or $3.52 per diluted share, compared with $8,772 million ($2.09) in 1Q26.
- GAAP results included $2,638 million of identified items: impairments of $1,079 million and other items of $1,559 million. The other items include $1,365 million of financial reserve additions and $194 million of Middle East impacts. GAAP results also gained $2,483 million from favorable estimated timing effects.
- Cash flow from operating activities was $23.6 billion.
- First half 2026 (YTD): GAAP earnings were $18,708 million, compared with $14,795 million in YTD 2025. Adjusted (non-GAAP) earnings were $23,452 million, compared with $14,555 million.

## Segments and drivers
- Upstream: GAAP earnings were $7,927 million and adjusted (non-GAAP) earnings $9,189 million. The company cites record Permian production above 1.8 Moebd, partly offset by Middle East disruptions. Total production was 4,514 koebd, compared with 4,594 koebd in 1Q26.
- Energy Products: 2Q26 GAAP earnings of $5,465 million (1Q26: $1,262 million loss) included $2,546 million of favorable timing effects and $884 million of non-U.S. impairments. Adjusted (non-GAAP) earnings were $4,099 million, compared with $2,799 million. Cited drivers are U.S. Gulf Coast utilization and record second-quarter diesel production.
- Chemical Products: GAAP earnings were $1,131 million (adjusted, non-GAAP: $1,214 million), up from $110 million in 1Q26. Specialty Products: GAAP earnings were $956 million (adjusted, non-GAAP: $969 million), up from $651 million.
- Estimated cumulative structural cost savings (non-GAAP) reached $16.3 billion versus 2019, including $1.2 billion in the first half of 2026.

## Outlook
- The release gives no full-year earnings, production or capital expenditure guidance.
- Stated plans:
  - The fifth Guyana FPSO has set sail, with startup on plan for 4Q26, adding 250 Kbd of capacity.
  - Permian production is consistent with a planned 9% CAGR through 2030.
  - 2026 planned investments are described only as 20% higher than the nearest IOC.

## Capital and balance sheet
- 2Q26 shareholder distributions were $9.4 billion: $4.3 billion of dividends and $5.1 billion of share repurchases. A third-quarter dividend of $1.03 per share was declared, payable September 10, 2026.
- Cash capital expenditures (non-GAAP) were $6,787 million in 2Q26 and $13.0 billion YTD 2026.
- Management says the balance sheet was strengthened, but the release gives no debt or cash-balance figures.

## Watch items
- The release does not explain the $1,365 million financial reserve addition. Upstream U.S. also carried $1,183 million of "other" identified items. What do these cover?
- Timing effects moved from $3,883 million unfavorable in 1Q26 to $2,483 million favorable in 2Q26 (YTD 2026: $1,400 million unfavorable). The release expects such impacts to unwind in later periods, so GAAP results may stay volatile, especially in Energy Products.
- YTD 2026 identified items include $900 million of Middle East impacts, and the production record claim excludes Middle East volumes. Monitor production and the 4Q26 Guyana startup.
