**Alphabet reported Q2 2026 revenues of $119.8 billion, up 24%, with Google Cloud growing 82% and net income available to common stockholders of $112.1 billion, the large majority of which came from a $99.0 billion net gain on equity securities.**

## Results
- Revenues were $119,796 million, up 24% year over year, and up 23% on a non-GAAP constant currency basis. Total TAC was $16,179 million; employees numbered 198,933 at quarter end.
- Income from operations was $40,770 million, up 30%, with operating margin of 34%, which the release says expanded by 2 percentage points.
- Other income (expense), net was $97,983 million, including a net gain on equity securities of $99,031 million. The release states this gain increased the tax provision by $21.9 billion, net income by $77.1 billion, and diluted EPS by $6.26.
- Net income available to common stockholders was $112,107 million, up 298%; diluted EPS was $9.11, up 294%. Year-to-date revenues were $229,692 million and year-to-date diluted EPS was $14.24.

## Segments and drivers
- Google Services revenues were $94,540 million, up 15%, with Google Search & other up 17% to $63,271 million, subscriptions/platforms/devices up 15% to $12,911 million, and YouTube ads up 13% to $11,055 million. Google Network was $7,303 million versus $7,354 million a year ago. Services operating income was $39,544 million.
- Google Cloud revenues rose 82% to $24,768 million, which management attributes to GCP growth across enterprise AI Solutions, enterprise AI Infrastructure and core GCP services; segment operating income was $8,814 million.
- Other Bets revenues were $382 million with an operating loss of $1,799 million. Alphabet-level activities, described as primarily shared AI R&D, were a $5,789 million charge versus $3,372 million a year ago.
- By geography, U.S. revenues grew 32% as reported to $60,846 million; EMEA grew 15% as reported and 12% in constant currency (non-GAAP).

## Outlook
- The release contains no revenue, margin, capital expenditure or EPS guidance for any future period. Do not infer one; management commentary is limited to results and product metrics.

## Capital and balance sheet
- Cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities totaled $242,474 million at June 30, 2026, against long-term debt of $98,165 million. Q2 capital expenditures were $44,924 million and operating cash flow was $39,069 million; non-GAAP free cash flow was negative $5,855 million for the quarter and $53,273 million on a trailing-twelve-month basis.
- Alphabet raised $49.6 billion in net proceeds from June equity and mandatory convertible preferred issuance, issued senior unsecured notes for $20.3 billion net, and established a $40.0 billion ATM program under which no shares had been sold as of June 30, 2026.
- The Board declared a $0.22 per share common dividend and $12.15 per share on the Series A and Series B mandatory convertible preferred. Repurchases of stock were $0 in the quarter and year to date.

## Watch items
- Funding mix: ask why $69.9 billion of combined equity and debt proceeds was raised alongside zero buybacks, and what capital expenditure run-rate the $40.0 billion unused ATM implies.
- Earnings quality: the release warns that equity-security fluctuations "could significantly contribute to the volatility of OI&E in future periods." Strip the $6.26 EPS contribution when assessing run-rate.
- Working capital and deal activity: inventory rose to $9,991 million from $2,439 million at year end (Cloud product revenue is primarily TPU systems), and year-to-date acquisitions net of cash were $33,697 million with goodwill up to $57,828 million.
