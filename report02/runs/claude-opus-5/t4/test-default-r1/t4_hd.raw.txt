**Home Depot delivered second-quarter fiscal 2026 sales of $47.9 billion, up 5.7%, with comparable sales up 1.7%, and reaffirmed its full-year fiscal 2026 guidance.**

## Results
- Net sales for the quarter were $47,861 million, an increase of $2.6 billion, or 5.7%, from the second quarter of fiscal 2025. Comparable sales rose 1.7%; U.S. comparable sales rose 1.3%.
- Gross profit was $16,115 million, up 6.5%. Selling, general and administrative expense was $8,424 million, up 8.5%, and total operating expenses rose 8.2%. Operating income was $6,839 million, up 4.3%; operating margin was 14.3% versus 14.5% a year ago.
- Net earnings were $4,766 million and diluted EPS was $4.79, versus $4,551 million and $4.58 in the prior-year quarter.
- Adjusted diluted EPS (non-GAAP, excluding acquired intangible asset amortization and related tax effects) was $4.92 versus $4.68. Adjusted operating income (non-GAAP) was $7,017 million and adjusted operating margin (non-GAAP) was 14.7%, versus 14.8%.
- For the six months ended August 2, 2026, net sales were $89,626 million (up 5.3%), comparable sales rose 1.2%, diluted EPS was $8.09 and adjusted diluted EPS (non-GAAP) was $8.35.

## Segments and drivers
- The release provides no segment-level results. Comparable customer transactions declined 1.0% while comparable average ticket rose 2.8%; average ticket was $92.50 and customer transactions were 443.2 million, down 0.8%. These transaction and ticket measures exclude HD Supply and SRS.
- CFO Richard McPhail said results "exceeded our expectations" with "broad based demand across the business as customers continued to engage in smaller projects."
- Acquired intangible asset amortization was $178 million in the quarter, of which $125 million related to SRS Distribution.
- The company ended the quarter with 2,364 retail stores, over 1,340 SRS locations and over 470,000 associates.

## Outlook
- Fiscal 2026 guidance was reaffirmed: total sales growth of approximately 2.5% to 4.5%; comparable sales growth of approximately flat to 2.0%; approximately 15 new stores; gross margin approximately 33.1%; operating margin approximately 12.4% to 12.6%; adjusted operating margin (non-GAAP) approximately 12.8% to 13.0%; effective tax rate approximately 24.3%; net interest expense approximately $2.3 billion; capital expenditures approximately 2.5% of total sales.
- Full-year diluted EPS is guided to grow approximately flat to 4.0% from $14.23 in fiscal 2025; adjusted diluted EPS (non-GAAP) approximately flat to 4.0% from $14.69 in fiscal 2025.
- Guidance includes IEEPA tariff refunds, which are expected to partially offset unplanned fuel, energy and other product input costs throughout the fiscal year.

## Capital and balance sheet
- At August 2, 2026: cash and equivalents $2,085 million; merchandise inventories $26,847 million (versus $24,843 million a year earlier); short-term debt $4,248 million; current installments of long-term debt $4,697 million; long-term debt excluding current installments $43,951 million; total stockholders' equity $16,617 million.
- For the six months, operating cash flow was $11,422 million (versus $8,968 million), capital expenditures $1,724 million, payments for businesses acquired, net $1,333 million, long-term debt repayments $3,040 million and cash dividends $4,643 million.

## Watch items
- Comparable transactions fell 1.0% while ticket rose 2.8%. Ask management how much of the ticket gain reflects price versus mix, and whether the "smaller projects" demand described by the CFO is sustaining unit volumes.
- Guidance explicitly relies on IEEPA tariff refunds to partially offset unplanned fuel, energy and product input costs. Ask about the timing and certainty of those refunds and what happens to margin guidance without them.
- Six-month comparable sales of 1.2% sit inside the full-year flat-to-2.0% range; inventories are above prior-year levels and the six-month financing section shows dividends but no share repurchase line item. Monitor second-half comps, inventory productivity and any resumption of repurchases, which the forward-looking statements flag as subject to continuation or suspension.
