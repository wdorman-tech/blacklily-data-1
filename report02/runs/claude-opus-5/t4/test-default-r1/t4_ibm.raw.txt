**IBM reported second-quarter 2026 revenue of $17.2 billion, up 1 percent, with GAAP pre-tax income down 5 percent, while management raised its full-year constant-currency revenue growth expectation to four-to-five percent.**

## Results
- Revenue of $17.2 billion ($17,162 million), up 1 percent. Gross profit $9.9 billion; gross profit margin 57.7 percent, down 1.0 points.
- GAAP pre-tax income $2.5 billion, down 5 percent; pre-tax margin 14.4 percent, down 0.9 points. Net income $2.2 billion, down 1 percent. Diluted EPS from continuing operations $2.27, versus $2.31 in the prior-year quarter.
- Operating (non-GAAP) results: gross profit $10.2 billion at a 59.4 percent margin; pre-tax income $3.3 billion, up 3 percent, at a 19.2 percent margin (up 0.3 points); net income $2.8 billion, up 5 percent; diluted EPS $2.93, up 5 percent.
- Adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP) of $4.8 billion, margin 27.8 percent, up 0.2 points.
- GAAP effective tax rate 12.6 percent versus 15.5 percent a year ago (operating non-GAAP: 15.1 percent versus 17.0 percent). R&D expense $2,311 million versus $2,097 million; SG&A $4,981 million versus $5,027 million.

## Segments and drivers
- Software revenue $7.8 billion, up 5 percent: Red Hat +11 percent, Data +19 percent (+18 percent constant currency), Automation +4 percent (+3 percent cc), Transaction Processing −8 percent (−9 percent cc). Segment profit margin 32.2 percent versus 31.1 percent.
- Consulting revenue $5.3 billion, flat (+1 percent cc), with Strategy and Technology and Intelligent Operations both flat (+1 percent cc). Segment profit margin 12.1 percent versus 10.6 percent.
- Infrastructure revenue $3.8 billion, down 7 percent: Hybrid Infrastructure −10 percent, with IBM Z −42 percent and Distributed Infrastructure +37 percent; Infrastructure Support −1 percent. Segment profit margin 21.8 percent versus 23.3 percent. The release says Power and Storage grew at a record pace, with an order backlog of nearly $500 million.
- Financing revenue $0.2 billion, up 12 percent (+11 percent cc). The CFO cited "revenue headwinds late in the second quarter."

## Outlook
- Full-year 2026: the company now expects constant-currency revenue growth of four-to-five percent; at current FX rates, currency is expected to be neutral to growth for the year.
- Full-year 2026: continues to expect free cash flow (non-GAAP) to increase by about $1 billion year-over-year, and now expects improved pre-tax income margin expansion.

## Capital and balance sheet
- Q2 net cash from operating activities $2.6 billion, up $0.9 billion year to year; free cash flow (non-GAAP) $2.5 billion, down $0.3 billion. First six months: operating cash flow $7.8 billion (up $1.7 billion); free cash flow $4.8 billion, flat.
- Ended the quarter with $8.2 billion of cash, restricted cash and marketable securities, down $6.3 billion from year-end 2025; $10.5 billion invested in acquisitions this year; debt of $62.0 billion (including $13.0 billion IBM Financing debt), up $0.7 billion year to date.
- Returned $1.6 billion in Q2 dividends; declared a quarterly dividend of $1.69 per share, record date August 10, payable September 10, 2026.

## Watch items
- IBM Z fell 42 percent while Distributed Infrastructure rose 37 percent: ask about mainframe cycle timing and the conversion and durability of the nearly $500 million Power/Storage backlog.
- The CFO referenced unspecified "revenue headwinds late in the second quarter": ask which segments and geographies were affected and whether they persist, given the raised constant-currency growth range.
- Cash fell $6.3 billion from year-end after $10.5 billion of acquisitions, and Q2 free cash flow declined $0.3 billion: ask what delivers the full-year ~$1 billion free cash flow increase, and how much of GAAP earnings reflects the lower 12.6 percent tax rate.
