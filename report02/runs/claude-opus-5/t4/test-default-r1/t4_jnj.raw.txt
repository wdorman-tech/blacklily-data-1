**Johnson & Johnson reported 6.6% Q2 2026 sales growth to $25.3 billion and raised full-year 2026 sales and adjusted EPS guidance, while reported net earnings and diluted EPS were essentially flat to slightly lower year over year.**

## Results
- Reported Q2 sales were $25,310 million versus $23,743 million, up 6.6%. Operational sales growth (non-GAAP, excluding translational currency) was 5.6%, and adjusted operational sales growth (non-GAAP, excluding net acquisitions/divestitures and translational currency) was 5.7%. Currency contributed 1.0 point worldwide.
- Reported net earnings were $5,534 million versus $5,537 million (-0.1%); reported diluted EPS was $2.27 versus $2.29 (-0.9%).
- Adjusted net earnings (non-GAAP, excluding intangible amortization and special items) were $7,081 million versus $6,699 million, up 5.7%; adjusted diluted EPS (non-GAAP) was $2.90 versus $2.77, up 4.7%.
- Free cash flow (non-GAAP) was approximately $8,700 million, which the release footnotes as second-quarter year-to-date 2026 and estimated as of July 15, 2026, against $6,214 million in the prior-year column.
- By region, U.S. sales were $14,533 million (+7.3% reported; 7.3% operational, non-GAAP) and International sales were $10,777 million (+5.7% reported; 3.4% operational, non-GAAP, with 2.3 points of currency).

## Segments and drivers
- Innovative Medicine sales were $16,384 million, +7.8% reported and +6.8% operational (non-GAAP), with divestitures a 10 basis point drag. Drivers cited: DARZALEX, CARVYKTI, TECVAYLI and RYBREVANT/LAZCLUZE in Oncology; TREMFYA and Other Immunology; SPRAVATO and CAPLYTA in Neuroscience.
- Offsets were STELARA — an approximate 760 basis point impact — plus REMICADE, IMBRUVICA and ZYTIGA.
- MedTech sales were $8,926 million, +4.5% reported and +3.6% operational (non-GAAP), with net acquisitions and divestitures a 10 basis point drag. Drivers: wound closure and biosurgery in Surgery; electrophysiology and Shockwave in Cardiovascular; contact lenses in Vision; trauma in Orthopaedics.

## Outlook
- Full-year 2026 estimated reported sales guidance is $100.8B–$101.4B, midpoint $101.1B (7.3% growth at midpoint), raised from $100.3B–$101.3B in April 2026.
- Full-year 2026 adjusted EPS guidance (non-GAAP) was raised $0.13 to a $11.68 midpoint (range $11.60–$11.75); adjusted operational EPS guidance (non-GAAP) was raised $0.18 to a $11.58 midpoint. The company states it does not provide forward-looking GAAP measures, citing inability to predict legal proceedings and other items.
- The reported-sales guidance uses an illustrative Euro average rate of $1.15, versus $1.17 in April 2026.

## Capital and balance sheet
- The release discloses free cash flow (non-GAAP) only; it provides no balance sheet, debt, dividend or share repurchase detail, and notes the comparable GAAP operating cash flow will appear in subsequent SEC filings.
- Announced capital deployment includes more than $1 billion invested in U.S. Vision manufacturing in Jacksonville, the agreement to acquire Firefly Bio, and a DePuy Synthes agreement to acquire RF tracking technology.

## Watch items
- Ask how long the STELARA headwind (~760 basis points to Innovative Medicine operational growth) persists, and what offsets management assumes in the raised guidance.
- Reported EPS fell while adjusted EPS rose; request the size and nature of the amortization and special items bridging $2.27 to $2.90.
- The forward-looking statements cite the planned separation of the Orthopaedics business and the inability to predict legal outcomes; monitor both, including the talc disclosure reference in the release.
