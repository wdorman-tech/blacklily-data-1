**JPMorganChase reported second-quarter 2026 net income of $21.2 billion ($7.70 per diluted share) on reported revenue of $57.3 billion, with $5.6 billion of significant items — a Visa-related net gain and equity investment gains — lifting results the Firm also presents on an excluding-significant-items basis.**

## Results
- Reported net revenue was $57.3 billion, up 28%; managed net revenue (non-GAAP) was $58.0 billion, up 27%, or up 15% excluding significant items (non-GAAP). Net income was $21.2 billion, up 41%, and diluted EPS was $7.70, up 47%.
- The Firm states net income excluding significant items (non-GAAP) of $16.9 billion, or $6.14 per share, up 13%. The significant items were a $4.6 billion net gain related to Visa shares (Corporate) and $1.0 billion of gains on certain equity investments ($763 million Corporate, $263 million CIB).
- ROE was 24%; ROTCE (non-GAAP) was 29%, or 23% excluding significant items (non-GAAP).
- Noninterest expense was $27.3 billion, up 15%, on higher compensation, brokerage, marketing, technology and occupancy. Reported overhead ratio was 48%; managed overhead ratio (non-GAAP) was 47%.
- Credit costs were $2.5 billion, with $2.4 billion of net charge-offs (down $44 million) and a $149 million net reserve build, primarily in Wholesale, versus a $439 million build a year ago.

## Segments and drivers
- CIB: net revenue $24.9 billion, up 27%; net income $9.7 billion, up 46%; ROE 22%. Investment Banking fees were $3.3 billion, up 30%. Equity Markets revenue was $6.0 billion, up 86%; Fixed Income Markets $6.1 billion, up 6%.
- CCB: net revenue $20.3 billion, up 8%; net income $5.3 billion, up 3%; ROE 34%. Card Services & Auto revenue rose 12%; expense rose 13%. Card Services net charge-off rate was 3.34%.
- AWM: net revenue $6.9 billion, up 19%; net income $2.0 billion, up 33%; ROE 48%. AUM was $5.1 trillion, up 18%; client assets $7.7 trillion, up 19%.
- Corporate: net revenue $6.0 billion, up $4.5 billion, but down $805 million excluding significant items (non-GAAP); net income down $1.5 billion on that basis, partly reflecting absence of a prior-year $774 million tax benefit.

## Outlook
- The release provides no financial guidance or outlook for future periods.
- Management commentary only: Dimon said "market sentiment remains constructive for continued activity" in investment banking, while citing geopolitical tensions, sticky inflation, fiscal deficits and elevated asset prices as risks.

## Capital and balance sheet
- CET1 capital of $303 billion; estimated Standardized ratio 14.1%, Advanced 14.2%; SLR 5.5%; estimated TLAC $590 billion on estimated Standardized RWA of $2.1 trillion.
- Distributions: $4.0 billion common dividend ($1.50 per share) and $6.2 billion of net common repurchases; net payout ratio of 73% for the last twelve months.
- Book value per share $133.01, up 9%; tangible book value per share (non-GAAP) $113.35, up 10%. Total assets $5.0 trillion at June 30, 2026.

## Watch items
- Durability of the Markets result: Equity Markets revenue rose 86% on "strong performance across products and regions," and Dimon attributed results to "a particularly favorable environment." Ask what run-rate management assumes absent elevated client activity.
- Expense trajectory: noninterest expense grew 15% while revenue excluding significant items grew 15% (both non-GAAP for revenue). Drivers include front-office headcount growth and revenue-related compensation — ask how much is variable.
- Consumer credit and rate sensitivity: CCB net charge-offs rose $70 million, predominantly Card Services, with reserves flat; separately, Corporate net interest income fell $667 million on lower rates. Both merit monitoring.
