**Meta's Q2 2026 revenue rose 28% to $60.80 billion, but a 55% increase in costs — including $2.40 billion of legal charges and $1.18 billion of severance — drove operating income down 8% and free cash flow to $784 million.**

## Results
- Revenue was $60.80 billion, up 28% year over year. On a constant currency basis (non-GAAP), revenue was $60,116 million, a 27% increase.
- Total costs and expenses were $42.03 billion, up 55%, including $2.40 billion of charges related to legal proceedings and $1.18 billion of severance tied to the May 2026 headcount reduction.
- Income from operations was $18,775 million, down 8%; operating margin was 31%, versus 43% in the prior-year quarter.
- Net income was $15,848 million, down 14%, and diluted EPS was $6.18 versus $7.14. The effective tax rate was 16%, versus 11% a year ago.
- Cash flow from operating activities was $31.86 billion; free cash flow (non-GAAP) was $784 million. Capital expenditures, including principal payments on finance leases, were $31.08 billion.

## Segments and drivers
- Family of Apps revenue was $60,370 million, of which advertising was $59,363 million (up 27%; up 26% excluding foreign exchange effect, non-GAAP) and other revenue $1,007 million. FoA income from operations was $23,394 million versus $24,971 million.
- Reality Labs revenue was $431 million with a loss from operations of $4,619 million, versus a $4,530 million loss a year ago.
- Ad impressions across the Family of Apps rose 14% and average price per ad rose 12%. Family daily active people averaged 3.60 billion in June 2026, up 3%.
- R&D expense was $21,656 million versus $12,942 million; G&A was $5,609 million versus $2,663 million and includes the $2.40 billion legal charge.

## Outlook
- Q3 2026 revenue guidance is $61–64 billion, assuming foreign currency is an approximately 1% headwind to year-over-year growth.
- Full year 2026 total expenses are guided to $165–169 billion (lower end raised for the legal charges); full year 2026 capex, including finance lease principal payments, is $130–145 billion, narrowed from $125–145 billion. Management continues to expect 2026 operating income above 2025 operating income.
- The tax rate for the remaining quarters of 2026 is expected at 15–17%, up from a prior 13–16% outlook.

## Capital and balance sheet
- Cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities were $90.26 billion and long-term debt was $83.66 billion at June 30, 2026, versus $58,744 million of long-term debt at December 31, 2025; net proceeds from long-term debt issuance in the quarter were $24,910 million.
- Dividend and dividend equivalent payments were $1.35 billion; the cash flow statement shows no Class A repurchases in the quarter, versus $10,167 million in Q2 2025.
- Property and equipment, net was $225,724 million, versus $176,400 million at December 31, 2025.

## Watch items
- Free cash flow fell to $784 million from $8,549 million a year ago while full year capex is guided to $130–145 billion — ask how management intends to fund the gap beyond the $24.9 billion debt raise, and whether repurchases remain suspended.
- The CFO commentary states youth-related trials are scheduled this year in the U.S. and "may ultimately result in a material loss," on top of the $2.40 billion charge already taken; monitor sizing and timing.
- Headcount was 75,472 and includes roughly 8,000 employees from the May reduction who will largely leave the count by the end of Q3 2026 — ask what expense run-rate relief is embedded in the $165–169 billion full year guide.
