**Morgan Stanley reported second-quarter 2026 net revenues of $21.3 billion, diluted EPS of $3.46 and ROTCE (non-GAAP) of 26.6%, with record results led by Equity and a record $148 billion of Wealth Management net new assets.**

## Results
- Net revenues were $21,348 million versus $16,792 million a year ago; pre-tax income was $7,348 million versus $4,622 million. The release describes firm net revenues and pre-tax income as records (footnoted as records for a reported quarterly period excluding DVA and reflecting the current reporting structure).
- Net income applicable to Morgan Stanley was $5,581 million, or $3.46 per diluted share, versus $3,539 million, or $2.13, a year ago.
- Return on equity was 20.7% and return on tangible common equity was 26.6% (non-GAAP), versus 13.9% and 18.2% a year ago.
- Compensation expense was $8,187 million and non-compensation expenses $5,715 million; the expense efficiency ratio was 65% versus 71% a year ago. The release highlights the same 65% ratio for the first half of the year.
- Provision for credit losses was $98 million versus $196 million. The effective tax rate was 23.1% versus 22.7%.

## Segments and drivers
- Institutional Securities net revenues were $11,040 million (record, per the release) versus $7,643 million, with pre-tax income of $4.3 billion versus $2.1 billion. Investment Banking was $2,437 million (up 58%), Equity $6,300 million (up 69%, with notable strength in Asia) and Fixed Income $2,455 million (up 13%). Other was negative $152 million versus positive $202 million, on higher mark-to-market losses on corporate loans including hedges.
- Wealth Management net revenues were $8,856 million (up 14%), pre-tax income $2.7 billion and pre-tax margin 30.5%. Net new assets were $148.1 billion versus $59.2 billion; the release states just over half represented inflows related to IPOs of certain Workplace-channel clients. Fee-based client assets were $3,022 billion; loans $195.7 billion.
- Investment Management net revenues were $1,646 million (up 6%), pre-tax income $404 million versus $323 million, AUM $2,004 billion, long-term net flows $7.5 billion versus $12.2 billion.

## Outlook
- The release provides no guidance, outlook or financial targets for any future period.

## Capital and balance sheet
- Standardized CET1 was 14.8% (15.0% a year ago); Advanced CET1 16.2%; Tier 1 leverage 6.0% and SLR 4.9%, versus 6.8% and 5.5%. Ratios are stated as estimates subject to change in the Form 10-Q.
- Book value per share was $67.80; tangible book value per share was $53.18 (non-GAAP).
- The Firm repurchased $1,500 million of stock at an average price of $197.64; the Board reauthorized a repurchase program of up to $20 billion beginning in 3Q26 and declared a $1.15 quarterly dividend, an increase of 15 cents.

## Watch items
- Durability of Wealth Management flows: net new assets more than doubled year over year, yet the release attributes just over half to client IPO inflows, and fee-based asset flows fell to $39.1 billion from $42.8 billion.
- Credit: the provision reflects portfolio growth plus individual assessments for certain corporate and commercial real estate loans, alongside mark-to-market losses on corporate loans in ISG Other.
- Capital trajectory: Standardized CET1, Tier 1 leverage and SLR all stand below year-ago levels while a $20 billion repurchase authorization begins next quarter.
