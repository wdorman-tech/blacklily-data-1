**Microsoft closed FY2026 with $90.0 billion of quarterly revenue, up 18%, as Intelligent Cloud grew 32% and Azure grew 43%, while More Personal Computing declined 4%.**

## Results
- Q4 revenue was $90.0 billion, up 18% (up 17% in constant currency); operating income was $40.6 billion, up 18%.
- Q4 net income was $35.8 billion, up 31% on a GAAP basis, and $35.3 billion, up 22%, on a non-GAAP basis. Diluted EPS was $4.81, up 32% GAAP, and $4.74, up 23%, non-GAAP. The non-GAAP measures exclude the impact from investments in OpenAI, which increased net income by $480 million and diluted EPS by $0.07 in the quarter.
- Management states that discrete items produced a $0.27 diluted EPS benefit versus its April 29, 2026 guidance, including a $3.2 billion gain on its Anthropic investment and lower-than-expected Voluntary Retirement Program expenses, partly offset by severance and XBOX impairment charges. Adjusting for these, the company says it exceeded expectations on revenue, operating income and diluted EPS.
- Full-year FY2026 (twelve months ended June 30, 2026): revenue $331.8 billion, up 18% (up 16% in constant currency); operating income $155.2 billion, up 21%; GAAP diluted EPS $17.95, up 32%; non-GAAP diluted EPS up 22%.

## Segments and drivers
- Intelligent Cloud revenue was $39.3 billion, up 32% (up 31% constant currency), with segment operating income of $15,955 million versus $12,140 million; Azure and other cloud services grew 43%. Microsoft Cloud revenue was $59.3 billion, up 27%.
- Productivity and Business Processes revenue was $37.8 billion, up 14%. Microsoft 365 Commercial cloud grew 14% reported, or 16% adjusted for a prior-year comparable that benefited from 2 points of in-period revenue recognition; LinkedIn +12%, Dynamics 365 +13%.
- More Personal Computing revenue was $12.9 billion, down 4% (down 5% constant currency), with operating income of $2,748 million versus $3,190 million; Windows OEM and Devices fell 7% and XBOX content and services fell 10%, while Search ex-TAC rose 10%.
- Commercial remaining performance obligation increased 84% to $678 billion. Management cites full-year Azure revenue surpassing $100 billion and over 30 million Microsoft 365 Copilot paid seats.

## Outlook
- The release contains no guidance figures; it states only that forward-looking guidance will be provided on the earnings call and webcast.

## Capital and balance sheet
- Q4 operating cash flow was $55,441 million; additions to property and equipment were $35,802 million in the quarter and $115,948 million for FY2026, versus $17,079 million and $64,551 million in the prior-year periods.
- Cash, equivalents and short-term investments were $76,843 million at June 30, 2026, versus $94,565 million a year earlier; net property and equipment was $313,076 million versus $204,966 million.
- Microsoft returned $10.2 billion via dividends and repurchases in Q4 FY2026.

## Watch items
- Capital intensity: full-year PP&E additions of $115,948 million against a declining cash and short-term investment balance — ask about the FY2027 spending path and funding mix.
- Earnings quality: other income (expense), net swung to $3,444 million from $(1,707) million, and the OpenAI adjustment moved from a $1,575 million drag to a $480 million benefit year over year. How much reported EPS growth is mark-to-market?
- RPO of $678 billion, up 84%: request duration and expected revenue recognition timing, plus the durability of 43% Azure growth against the 4% More Personal Computing decline and XBOX impairment.
