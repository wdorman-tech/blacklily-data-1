**NVIDIA reported second-quarter fiscal 2027 revenue of $96.2 billion, up 106% year over year, and guided third-quarter revenue to $108.0 billion while assuming no Data Center compute revenue from China.**

## Results
- Revenue was $96,221 million, up 18% sequentially and up 106% year over year. Six-month (first-half fiscal 2027) revenue was $177,837 million.
- GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins were both 75.0%, versus 72.4% (GAAP) and 72.5% (non-GAAP) a year ago. GAAP operating income was $63,734 million, up 124% year over year; non-GAAP operating income was $63,956 million.
- GAAP net income was $59,688 million (up 2% sequentially, 126% year over year) and GAAP diluted EPS was $2.46. Non-GAAP net income was $53,954 million (up 18% sequentially) and non-GAAP diluted EPS was $2.22.
- GAAP other income, net was $7,773 million and included $7,771 million of gains from equity securities, net, which the non-GAAP reconciliation excludes — the main reason GAAP and non-GAAP results diverge this quarter.
- GAAP operating expenses were $8,408 million (up 55% year over year); non-GAAP operating expenses were $8,232 million. Beginning in Q1 FY27, non-GAAP measures no longer exclude stock-based compensation (historicals restated); stock-based compensation was $2,027 million. Operating cash flow was $24,077 million (Q1 FY27: $50,344 million); free cash flow (non-GAAP) was $21,341 million.

## Segments and drivers
- Data Center revenue was $89.0 billion, up 18% sequentially and 117% year over year. The Vera Rubin platform is ramping into full production with racks running at CoreWeave, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, Oracle Cloud Infrastructure and Nebius.
- Edge Computing revenue was $7.2 billion, up 13% sequentially and 27% year over year, with RTX Spark (with Microsoft), DGX Station for Windows and DRIVE Hyperion cited.
- NVIDIA announced partnerships with Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs and KKR to establish compute financing platforms intended to mobilize over $500 billion of third-party capital over time, subject to definitive agreements.
- Sovereign activity cited includes Korea (SK Telecom, NAVER, Brookfield), Japan's national AI infrastructure, and a record 35 new AI HPC supercomputers in development across Europe.

## Outlook
- Q3 FY27 guidance: revenue of $108.0 billion, plus or minus 2%, assuming no Data Center compute revenue from China; GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins of 74.0%, plus or minus 50 basis points.
- Q3 FY27 operating expenses guided to approximately $9.2 billion (GAAP) and $9.0 billion (non-GAAP). Full-year FY27 GAAP and non-GAAP tax rates are expected at 16.0%–18.0%, excluding discrete items.

## Capital and balance sheet
- Approximately $26.0 billion was returned in the quarter via repurchases and dividends; approximately $99.0 billion remained under authorization. The next quarterly dividend is $0.25 per share, payable October 1, 2026 to holders of record September 10, 2026.
- Versus January 25, 2026: cash and equivalents $22,443 million (from $10,605 million); accounts receivable $63,059 million (from $38,466 million); inventories $31,575 million (from $21,403 million); long-term debt $32,366 million (from $7,469 million). Debt issuance proceeds, net of costs, were $24,896 million in the quarter.
- Non-marketable securities were $51,157 million (from $22,251 million); marketable equity securities were $42,783 million (from $12,886 million).

## Watch items
- Guidance excludes China Data Center compute revenue entirely — ask what conditions would change that assumption and whether any China revenue is embedded in current results.
- Receivables and inventories both rose materially since January 25, 2026, while quarterly operating cash flow fell sequentially from $50,344 million to $24,077 million; ask management to reconcile working capital build with the demand commentary.
- Guided gross margin of 74.0% sits below the 75.0% reported this quarter, and equity-securities gains drove GAAP-only earnings; monitor both the margin trajectory and the growing equity and non-marketable securities positions, plus execution risk on the $500 billion financing platforms that remain subject to definitive agreements.
