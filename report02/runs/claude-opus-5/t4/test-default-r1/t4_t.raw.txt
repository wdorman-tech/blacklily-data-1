**AT&T reported second-quarter 2026 revenues of $31.6 billion, up 2.3% year over year, with record combined fiber and fixed wireless net adds, while reiterating all consolidated full-year 2026 and multi-year guidance and raising the pace of 2026 buybacks to approximately $10 billion.**

## Results
- Revenues were $31.6 billion, versus $30.8 billion a year ago, up 2.3%. Operating expenses were $24.5 billion, versus $24.3 billion.
- Operating income was $7.0 billion, versus $6.5 billion. Adjusted operating income (non-GAAP) was $7.5 billion, versus $6.5 billion.
- Diluted EPS from continuing operations was $0.66, versus $0.62. Adjusted EPS (non-GAAP) was $0.65, versus $0.54. Income from continuing operations was $5.0 billion, up 3.6%; adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP) was $12.3 billion, up 5.2%.
- Cash from operating activities from continuing operations was $10.8 billion, versus $9.8 billion. Capital expenditures were $5.7 billion, versus $4.9 billion; capital investment (non-GAAP) was $6.1 billion, versus $5.1 billion. Free cash flow (non-GAAP) was $4.7 billion, versus $4.4 billion.

## Segments and drivers
- Advanced Connectivity revenues were $28,615 million, up 4.1%, with service revenue of $23,478 million, up 5.1%. Advanced home internet revenue rose 27.3% to $2,926 million; wireless service revenue rose 3.3%. Segment operating income was $7,345 million, up 20.3%; segment EBITDA (non-GAAP) was $12,032 million, up 8.0%.
- Subscriber metrics: 646,000 internet net adds (367,000 fiber, 279,000 fixed wireless), 432,000 postpaid phone net adds, postpaid phone churn of 0.86%, and over 1 million Advanced Connectivity customers added. Fiber locations reached 38.6 million; 42.5% of advanced home internet households also took AT&T wireless.
- Legacy revenues fell 25.9% to $1,632 million; operating income and EBITDA (non-GAAP) were $523 million, down $436 million.
- Latin America revenues rose 16.1% to $1,224 million, driven by favorable FX and postpaid growth; operating income was $38 million, down $8 million; EBITDA (non-GAAP) was $227 million, up $26 million.

## Outlook
- 2026 guidance: adjusted EPS (non-GAAP) of $2.25–$2.35; adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP) growth of 3%–4%; free cash flow (non-GAAP) of $18 billion+; Advanced Connectivity service revenue growth of 5%+.
- 2026–2028 guidance: low-single-digit annual service revenue growth, capital investment (non-GAAP) of $23–$24 billion annually, free cash flow (non-GAAP) of $19 billion+ in 2027 and $21 billion+ in 2028, and $45 billion+ returned to shareholders.

## Capital and balance sheet
- Total debt was $144.0 billion; net debt (non-GAAP) was $126.4 billion, with cash and equivalents of $17.6 billion.
- The company returned $4.1 billion to shareholders in the quarter, including approximately $2.2 billion of repurchases under the 2024 authorization, and plans to maintain the $1.11 annualized dividend per share.

## Watch items
- Operating expenses included an asset abandonment charge tied to "reprioritization of the Company's spectrum strategy." Ask what was abandoned and whether further spectrum-related charges are expected.
- Reported EPS included a $0.05 tax benefit and a $0.03 abandonment charge (per the company's reconciliation); monitor the durability of the tax item.
- The acquired Lumen fiber network subsidiary is held-for-sale in discontinued operations pending sale of a controlling interest to an equity partner; and the 2.5x leverage target is framed as roughly three years after the EchoStar transaction closes. Track both transactions.
