**Visa's fiscal Q3 2026 delivered net revenue of $11.6 billion, up 14% (13% constant-dollar), with GAAP EPS of $2.97 and non-GAAP EPS of $3.32, alongside double-digit growth across all four key business drivers.**

## Results
- Net revenue was $11.633 billion, an increase of 14% (13% on a constant-dollar basis), which the release attributes to growth in payments volume, cross-border volume and processed transactions.
- GAAP net income was $5.628 billion (+7%) and GAAP diluted class A EPS was $2.97 (+10%; approximately 9% on a constant-dollar basis). Non-GAAP net income was $6.296 billion (+8%) and non-GAAP diluted EPS was $3.32 (+11%; approximately 11% constant-dollar).
- GAAP results include special items of $563 million in severance costs, a $237 million MDL litigation provision and an $18 million deferred tax benefit, plus $9 million of net equity investment losses and $78 million of acquired-intangible amortization and acquisition-related costs. Prior-year results included a $615 million litigation provision, $35 million of equity losses and $73 million of amortization/acquisition costs.
- GAAP operating expenses were $4.756 billion, up 19%, primarily driven by higher personnel expense; non-GAAP operating expenses rose 17%, primarily on marketing and personnel. The GAAP effective tax rate was 17.6%; the non-GAAP effective tax rate was 18.4%.

## Segments and drivers
- Constant-dollar drivers for the quarter: payments volume +10%, cross-border volume excluding intra-Europe +12%, total cross-border volume +13%, processed transactions +10% (71.7 billion). On a nominal basis: +11%, +14%, +15% and +10%, respectively.
- Revenue lines: service revenue $4.922 billion (+14%), data processing $6.042 billion (+17%), international transaction $3.853 billion (+6%), other revenue $1.496 billion (+45%). Client incentives were $4.680 billion, up 18%.
- Service revenue is recognized on the prior quarter's payments volume; payments volume for the three months ended March 31, 2026 rose 9% constant-dollar.

## Outlook
- The release provides no financial guidance or outlook for any future period. It contains only a forward-looking-statements disclaimer and risk factors; no revenue, EPS or volume targets are given.

## Capital and balance sheet
- Share repurchases and dividends totaled $6.2 billion in the quarter; Visa repurchased approximately 14.5 million class A shares at an average cost of $330.71 for $4.9 billion, with $28.4 billion of authorization remaining at June 30, 2026. A $0.670 per-share quarterly dividend was declared July 28, 2026.
- Cash, cash equivalents and investment securities were $13.9 billion at June 30, 2026. Accrued litigation was $1.274 billion versus $3.033 billion at September 30, 2025; litigation escrow restricted cash was $888 million versus $2.990 billion.
- On June 25, 2026, Visa deposited $250 million into the litigation escrow, reducing as-converted class B-1, B-2 and B-3 share counts at a volume-weighted average price of $333.42. The class B-1/B-2 exchange offer settled May 12, 2026.

## Watch items
- International transaction revenue grew 6% while total cross-border volume rose 13% constant-dollar (15% nominal) — ask management to explain the gap and its durability.
- Interest expense was $194 million this quarter versus $39 million in the prior-year quarter; the balance sheet shows long-term debt of $20.862 billion versus $19.602 billion at September 30, 2025.
- Other revenue rose 45% and client incentives rose 18% against 14% net revenue growth; probe the mix and sustainability, and whether the $563 million severance charge implies a lower run-rate personnel expense.
