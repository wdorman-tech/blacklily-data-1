Thank you for your continued confidence in us. Enclosed you will find your performance report for the second quarter of 2026, covering April 1 through June 30. This letter summarizes how your Balanced Growth 70/30 portfolio performed, the adjustments we made and a reduction in your advisory fee.

For the quarter, your household portfolio returned -1.42% net of fees (-1.19% gross of fees, meaning before advisory fees are deducted). Our comparison index returned -1.87% over the same period. That index blends 70% global stocks and 30% US bonds. For the year to date, your portfolio has returned 2.36% net of fees. Since the strategy began on March 1, 2019, it has returned an annualized 7.91% net of fees. Your household assets stood at $3,184,620 on June 30, 2026.

We made three adjustments to your portfolio:

- In May, we reduced US large-company growth stocks to 19% of the portfolio from 24%, taking gains after their rise in the first quarter.
- We used those proceeds to increase stocks of companies in developed markets outside the US to 17% from 13%.
- We shortened the duration of your bond holdings to 5.1 years from 6.0. Duration measures how sensitive bonds are to changes in interest rates, and a shorter duration means less sensitivity. Cash made up 3.4% of the portfolio at quarter end.

I am also pleased to share that, following our annual review of household pricing, your advisory fee is being reduced. Effective October 1, 2026, the annual rate on the first $2,000,000 of your household assets will decrease from 1.00% to 0.85%. The annual rate on assets above $2,000,000 remains 0.70%, unchanged. Because fees are billed quarterly in advance, you will first see the new rate on your fourth-quarter invoice.

Past performance is not indicative of future results, and returns shown net of fees reflect the deduction of advisory fees actually billed but not of any taxes you may owe.
