This letter reports on the Tax-Managed US Index Portfolio held in the trust account for the second quarter of 2026, the period from April 1 through June 30, 2026.

For the quarter, the portfolio returned **-2.71% net of fees** (the gross-of-fees return, before the advisory fee is deducted, was -2.63%). During the April selloff we harvested $86,400 of realized losses, bringing the year-to-date total to $112,900 with no net gains realized. Separately, we have set aside $140,000 of low-basis lots to fund the family's planned December gift to its donor-advised fund.

**Tracking against the index**

Estimated (ex-ante) tracking error against the index stood at 0.62%, within the 1.00% ceiling set by the account's investment policy.

**Change to advisory fees**

Effective **November 1, 2026**, the annual advisory fee on the first $5,000,000 of trust assets will **increase from 0.30% to 0.40%**. The rate on assets above $5,000,000 remains unchanged at 0.20% annually. This increase reflects the cost of the lot-level tax overlay applied to the portfolio and of the expanded realized gain and loss reporting we now provide. The trust's advisory agreement requires 90 days' written notice of any fee change, and this letter serves as that notice.
