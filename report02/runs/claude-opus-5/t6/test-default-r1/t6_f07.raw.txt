Here is our report on your Private Credit Sleeve for the third quarter of 2026, covering July 1 through September 30.

**Performance**

The sleeve returned 1.94% for the quarter, net of fees. The gross-of-fees return, before the Corriveth sleeve fee, was 2.26%. For comparison, the Senior Secured Direct Lending Index returned 2.12% in the quarter on a preliminary basis. Year to date, the sleeve has returned 5.71% net of fees, and since we began the strategy on April 1, 2023, it has returned 9.38% annualized, net of fees. The sleeve was valued at $1,874,560 on September 30. These figures are based on valuation marks provided by the underlying managers.

**What happened in the portfolio**

Loans on non-accrual — that is, loans where the borrower has stopped making the payments we expect — represented 1.3% of the sleeve at quarter-end, up from 0.7% at June 30. One software borrower restructured its loan in August. We are watching this closely and will keep you informed.

The portfolio remains conservatively positioned in the capital structure. First-lien senior secured loans, which sit at the front of the line for repayment, make up 94% of the sleeve.

Hamish, on the capital-call schedule: we funded a $150,000 capital call to Direct Lending Fund III on September 12. Of your $500,000 commitment to that fund, $210,000 remains unfunded and may be called in future quarters.

**A reduction in your fee**

Following our annual review of pricing across our alternative investment sleeves, we are lowering the fee on Private Credit Sleeve assets from 1.25% annually to 1.10% annually, effective January 1, 2027. The fee continues to be billed quarterly in arrears on quarter-end net asset value. This reduction applies to all clients in the sleeve.

Private credit investments are illiquid, are valued using estimates that may not reflect the price at which a loan could actually be sold, and may lose value.
