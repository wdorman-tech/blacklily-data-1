I am writing with the first quarterly report of 2026 for the Delacourt-Ambrose Charitable Remainder Unitrust, covering January 1 through March 31, 2026.

For the quarter, the trust returned 2.2% net of fees. (The gross-of-fees return, before the advisory fee is deducted, was 2.4%.) The comparison index — 55% global equity and 45% US aggregate bonds, matching the trust's balanced allocation — returned 1.8% over the same period. Year to date through March 31, the net-of-fees return was also 2.2%, and since the trust's inception on October 2, 2017, the annualized return net of fees stands at 6.8%. These figures are time-weighted and measured before unitrust payments.

Three items from the quarter are worth noting.

First, the trust made its first 2026 unitrust payment of $31,250 on March 31. This represents one of four equal installments of the 5% unitrust amount, calculated on the trust's January 2 valuation of $2,500,000.

Second, we raised cash and Treasury bills to 6.5% of trust assets. This sets aside the money for the June and September payments in advance, so that those distributions can be made without selling equity or other risk assets to raise cash.

Third, we reduced the trust's high-yield bond position to 3.5% from 7.5%. The extra yield these bonds offered over safer bonds had narrowed to its slimmest margin since 2021, and we felt the additional compensation no longer justified the additional risk.

Unitrust payments are set by the trust instrument as a fixed percentage of the trust's net assets as revalued each year, will rise or fall with the value of the trust, and are not a measure of investment performance.

I am also pleased to tell you about a reduction in your advisory fee. Effective June 1, 2026, our annual rate on the first $3,000,000 of trust assets falls from 1.00% to 0.90%. The rate on assets above $3,000,000 is unchanged at 0.70% and is not currently reached. The change will be prorated on your second-quarter invoice. This brings trust accounts into line with our standard individual fee schedule.
