I am writing with an update on your Preferred and Hybrid Income sleeve, which holds about a third of your rollover IRA. The remainder stays invested in our balanced model and is covered separately.

**How the sleeve performed**

For the second quarter — April 1 through June 30, 2026 — the sleeve returned 1.6% net of fees (1.8% gross of fees, before the advisory fee is deducted). For comparison, the US preferred securities index, which covers both investment grade and high yield issues, returned 1.1% over the same three months. Year to date through June 30, the sleeve returned 3.9% net of fees. Since the sleeve began on August 8, 2022, it has returned 5.2% a year, annualized and net of fees.

**What we did and why**

At June 30 the sleeve's current yield was 6.4%, which is the income it is producing at today's prices.

We sold the two bank preferreds whose interest rate was scheduled to switch from fixed to floating, and whose first call dates fall in November 2026. A call date is simply the first day the company that issued the security is allowed to buy it back from you, usually at a set price. When that day is close, the price of the security has little room to rise, so we chose to move on. We reinvested the $186,000 of proceeds into $25-par utility preferreds.

We also reduced the sleeve's duration to worst from 5.0 years to 4.2 years. In plain terms, that makes the sleeve somewhat less sensitive to movements in interest rates.

**A lower fee for you**

After moving to a lower-cost fixed income trading platform, we have repriced our income sleeves. The annual advisory fee on your Preferred and Hybrid Income sleeve falls from 0.70% to 0.55%, effective July 1, 2026. You will see it first on your third-quarter invoice. The fee on the balanced portion of your IRA is unchanged.
