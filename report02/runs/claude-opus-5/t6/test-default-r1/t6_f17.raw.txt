This letter covers the Real Assets sleeve of your joint taxable account x6190 for the third quarter of 2026, July 1 through September 30.

**Performance**

The sleeve returned 2.9% net of fees for the quarter (3.1% gross of fees). Its comparison index — the real assets policy blend of 40% global listed infrastructure, 30% global REITs, 20% US TIPS and 10% broad commodities — returned 3.4% over the same period. Year to date through September 30, the sleeve returned 6.1% net of fees. Over the trailing twelve months through September 30, it returned 8.7% net of fees.

**What changed in the sleeve**

We raised listed infrastructure to 44% of the sleeve from 38%, with the additions mostly in towers and natural gas pipelines. We bought a new 2034 TIPS position at a 1.95% real yield. And we cut commodities to 5% of the sleeve following the gold rally; the policy range for commodities is 4% to 12%, so the sleeve remains inside its mandate.

**A change to the fee on this sleeve**

The advisory fee on the Real Assets sleeve will rise from 0.60% per year to 0.75% per year, effective December 1, 2026. Fees on your core equity and bond holdings are unchanged.

The reason is a change in how the sleeve is built. TIPS and commodity exposure moves out of ETFs and into directly held TIPS and exchange-traded futures run by our firm. As those ETFs are sold, the weighted fund expenses inside the sleeve fall from 0.14% to 0.03%. That reduction offsets most of the fee increase.

Section 7 of your advisory agreement requires 30 days' written notice of a fee change. This letter serves as that notice.

Real asset investments, including commodities, real estate securities and inflation-linked bonds, can be affected by changes in interest rates, inflation, supply and demand, and regulation, and may not keep pace with inflation in every period.
