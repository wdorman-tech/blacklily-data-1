Here is your quarterly update on Mateo's North Carolina UTMA custodial account (x2266), which follows our Education Glide Path, Moderate track. This letter covers April 1 through June 30, 2026.

**How the account performed**

The account returned 3.7% net of fees for the quarter. Before the advisory fee was deducted, the gross-of-fees return was 3.9%. **What we changed**

On June 1 we took the account's annual glide path step, lowering the equity target from 75% to 70%. As part of that step we moved $14,000 out of stocks and into Treasury bonds maturing in 2031, earmarked for Mateo's first year of college costs. After the June rebalance, cash stands at 2.5% of the account.

Nils, you asked in May whether 70% in stocks is too much with roughly five years until college. It is a fair question, and the glide path is built to answer it. The equity target steps down automatically every year, so the account holds progressively less in stocks as Mateo gets closer to enrolling. Just as important, the money intended for that first tuition bill is no longer riding on the stock market at all — it now sits in the 2031 Treasuries, with a maturity date chosen to line up with when you will need it.

**A change to your fee**

Because you asked in May to have Mateo's account grouped with your own accounts for breakpoint purposes — a request we approved in June — the annual advisory fee on this account drops from 1.00% to 0.80%. The new household rate takes effect October 1, 2026, and will first appear on your Q4 invoice.

Assets in a custodial account under the Uniform Transfers to Minors Act are the irrevocable property of the minor, pass to the minor's control at the age set by state law, and may reduce eligibility for need-based financial aid.
