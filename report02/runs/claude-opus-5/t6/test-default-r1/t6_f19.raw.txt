I am writing with the quarterly report for the Foundation's portfolio covering January 1 through March 31, 2026.

The portfolio returned **-3.1% net of fees** for the first quarter. The same figure gross of fees, that is, before the advisory fee was deducted, was -2.9%. For comparison, the policy portfolio index — 60% global equity, 25% US aggregate bond, 15% hedge fund composite — returned -3.5% over the quarter. Year to date through March 31, the portfolio returned -3.1% net of fees. Over the trailing five years through March 31, the annualized return was 6.6% net of fees.

Three points on how the portfolio was managed during the quarter. First, grants of $212,500 were paid in the first quarter, funded entirely from the short-term reserve account. No equity holdings were sold during the March market decline, which means the portfolio's long-term investments were left undisturbed. Grantmaking is on pace to meet the 5% minimum payout requirement. Second, fixed income finished the quarter at 27.5% of assets, above the 25% target, after we added Treasury bonds during the rise in yields. Third, global equity stood at 57.8% following the decline. That sits inside the five-percentage-point band around the 60% target, so no rebalancing was triggered.

I also want to tell you about a change to your fee schedule, which follows our annual fee review of the foundation and endowment schedule. The annual rate on Foundation assets above $10,000,000 will fall from 0.60% to 0.45%. The rate on the first $10,000,000 is unchanged at 0.75%. The new rate takes effect May 1, 2026, and will be prorated on your second-quarter invoice.
