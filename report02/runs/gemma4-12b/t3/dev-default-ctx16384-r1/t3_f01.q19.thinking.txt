        *   "A trade error is any error in the placement, execution or just allocation of a trade, including a trade for the wrong account, security, side or quantity, or a trade that violates a client's restrictions. Trade errors are corrected promptly at no cost to the client. The Firm reimburses the client for any loss, and any gain remains in the client's account. The Head of Trading records every trade error in the Trade Error Log, and the CCO reviews each entry. Any trade error resulting in a loss or gain exceeding $5,000 is reported to the Management Committee at its next meeting."
