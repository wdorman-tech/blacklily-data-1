  "quote": "The minimum holding period in Section 4.5 of the Compliance Manual is extended from 30 calendar days to 60 calendar days. Access persons must hold any position in a reportable security acquired in a covered account for at least 60 calendar days from the date of acquisition, and may not close a short position within 60 calendar days of opening it. Positions acquired before September 1, 2026 remain subject to the 30-day period."
