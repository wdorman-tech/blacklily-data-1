  "quote": "Limited partners of the Flagship Fund and shareholders of the Offshore Fund may redeem as of the last business day of any calendar quarter on at least 65 days' prior written notice. Redemptions effective within 12 months after an investor's initial subscription are subject to an early redemption fee of 3% of the amount redeemed, which is retained by the relevant Fund and not paid to the Adviser. A full redemption is subject to a holdback of 10% of the proceeds pending completion of the Fund's annual audit, and 90% of redemption proceeds are ordinarily paid within 30 days after the redemption date. Each of the Flagship Fund and the Offshore Fund may limit aggregate redemptions as of any quarter-end to 20% of its net asset value."
