  "quote": "Privately placed notes are valued by a matrix method. The Firm determines a spread over the interpolated U.S. Treasury curve equal to the spread of comparable public securities of the same issuer or, where none exist, of comparable issuers with a similar internal rating, sector and maturity, plus an illiquidity premium, plus adjustments for structural features such as financial covenants, make-whole provisions, security and subordination. The note's scheduled cash flows are discounted at the resulting yield. The illiquidity premium is set by the Committee quarterly and currently ranges from 15 to 40 basis points depending on issue size and structure."
