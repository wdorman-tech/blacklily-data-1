**Alphabet reported a 24% increase in consolidated revenues to $119.8 billion for the second quarter of 2026.**

## Results
*   Consolidated Alphabet revenues reached $119.8 billion for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, representing a 24% increase (or 23% in constant currency).
*   Consolidated Alphabet operating income increased 30% to $40.8 billion, with the operating margin expanding by 2 percentage points to 34%.
*   Net income available to common stockholders increased 298% to $112.1 billion, with diluted net income per common share increasing 294% to $9.11.
*   Other income for the quarter included a net gain of $98.0 billion, primarily from net unrealized gains on equity securities.

## Segments and drivers
*   Google Services revenues grew 15% to $94.5 billion, supported by 17% growth in Google Search & other, 15% in Google subscriptions, platforms, and devices, and 13% in YouTube ads.
*   Google Cloud revenues increased 82% to $24.8 billion, driven by demand for enterprise AI solutions, enterprise AI infrastructure, and core Google Cloud Platform (GCP) services.
*   The company reported that nearly 90% of the Fortune 100 are using Gemini Enterprise.

## Outlook
*   The release provides no specific guidance or outlook for future periods.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   In June 2026, the company issued a combination of Class A stock, Class C stock, and mandatory convertible preferred stock for aggregate net proceeds of $49.6 billion for general corporate purposes, including capital expenditures for AI infrastructure.
*   The company issued senior unsecured notes in the second quarter of 2026 for net proceeds of $20.3 billion.
*   Total cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities were $242.5 billion as of June 30, 2026.

## Watch items
*   The impact of the $98.0 billion net gain in "Other income" on future earnings, given the release notes that fluctuations in the value of these investments can significantly contribute to the volatility of other income.
*   The sustainability of the 82% growth rate in Google Cloud revenue as it scales across enterprise AI infrastructure and solutions.
