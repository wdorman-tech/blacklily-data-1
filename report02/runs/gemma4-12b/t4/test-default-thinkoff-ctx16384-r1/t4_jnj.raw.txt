**Johnson & Johnson reports Q2 2026 results and raises 2026 outlook.**

## Results
*   Reported sales for the second quarter of 2026 were $25.3 billion, representing a 6.6% growth over the prior year.
*   Reported earnings per share (EPS) for the second quarter of 2026 were $2.27.
*   Non-GAAP adjusted operational sales growth for the second quarter of 2026 was 5.7%.
*   Non-GAAP adjusted earnings per share (EPS) for the second quarter of 2026 were $2.90.
*   Non-GAAP free cash flow for the second quarter of 2026 was approximately $8.7 billion.

## Segments and drivers
*   Innovative Medicine: Reported sales were $16.384 billion (7.8% growth). Growth was driven by DARZALEX, CARVYKTI, TECVAYLI, and RYBREVANT/LAZCLUZE in Oncology; TREMFYA and other products in Immunology; and SPRAVATO and CAPLYTA in Neuroscience. Growth was partially offset by STELARA, REMICADE, IMBRUVICA, and ZYTIGA.
*   MedTech: Reported sales were $8.926 billion (4.5% growth). Growth was driven by wound closure and biosurgery products in Surgery; electrophysiology products and Shockwave in Cardiovascular; contact lenses in Vision; and trauma in Orthopaedics.

## Outlook
*   The company raised 2026 guidance for estimated reported sales to a range of $100.8 billion to $101.4 billion (midpoint of $101.1 billion, or 7.3% at the midpoint).
*   The company raised 2026 guidance for non-GAAP adjusted earnings per share (EPS) to a range of $11.60 to $11.75 (midpoint of $11.68, or 8.2% at the midpoint).
*   The company raised 2026 guidance for non-GAAP adjusted operational earnings per share (EPS) to a range of $11.50 to $11.65 (midpoint of $11.58, or 7.3% at the midpoint).

## Capital and balance sheet
*   The company reported non-GAAP free cash flow of approximately $8.7 billion for the second quarter of 2026.
*   The company announced an investment of more than $1 billion to strengthen U.S. Vision manufacturing in Jacksonville, Florida.

## Watch items
*   The extent to which the "partial offsets" in the Innovative Medicine segment (specifically STELARA and REMICADE) might impact long-term growth targets.
*   The impact of the acquisition of Firefly Bio, Inc. on the expansion of the oncology pipeline.
