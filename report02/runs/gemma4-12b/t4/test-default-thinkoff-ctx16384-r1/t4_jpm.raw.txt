**JPMorgan Chase & Co. reports second-quarter 2026 net income of $21.2 billion, with net income excluding significant items of $16.9 billion.**

## Results
*   Reported net income was $21.2 billion ($7.70 per share) for the second quarter of 2026, while net income excluding significant items was $16.9 billion ($6.14 per share).
*   Reported revenue was $57.3 billion; managed revenue (non-GAAP) was $58.0 billion.
*   Noninterest expense was $27.3 billion, resulting in a reported overhead ratio of 48% and a managed overhead ratio (non-GAAP) of 47%.
*   The provision for credit losses was $2.5 billion, with $2.4 billion in net charge-offs and a $149 million net reserve build.

## Segments and drivers
*   **CIB:** Net income was $9.7 billion (up 46% YoY). Markets revenue grew 35% YoY, with Equity Markets growing 86% YoY. Investment Banking fees rose 30% YoY.
*   **CCB:** Net income was $5.3 billion (up 3% YoY). Card Services net revenue grew 12% YoY, and active mobile customers increased 6% YoY.
*   **AWM:** Net income was $2.0 billion (up 33% YoY). Assets under management (AUM) reached $5.1 trillion, up 18% YoY.

## Outlook
*   The release provides no specific guidance or outlook for future periods.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   The firm reported a ROTCE of 29% and a ROTCE excluding significant items (non-GAAP) of 23%.
*   Capital distributions included a $4.0 billion common dividend and $6.2 billion of common stock net repurchases.
*   The Standardized CET1 Capital Ratio was 14.1% and the Advanced CET1 Capital Ratio was 14.2%.
*   Book value per share was $133.01 (up 9% YoY); tangible book value per share (non-GAAP) was $113.35 (up 10% YoY).

## Watch items
*   The impact of "significant items" on core performance, specifically the $4.6 billion net gain related to Visa shares and $1.0 billion of gains on certain equity investments.
*   The drivers of noninterest expense, which increased 15% this quarter, primarily due to higher compensation, technology, and marketing costs.
