**Visa reports fiscal third quarter 2026 results with net revenue of $11.6 billion.**

## Results
*   Net revenue for the fiscal third quarter was $11.6 billion, representing a 14% increase over the prior year and a 13% increase on a constant-dollar basis.
*   GAAP net income for the fiscal third quarter was $5.6 billion (or $2.97 per share), while non-GAAP net income was $6.3 billion (or $3.32 per share).
*   GAAP earnings per share increased 10% over the prior year, while non-GAAP earnings per share increased 11% over the prior year.
*   GAAP operating expenses for the fiscal third quarter were $4.8 billion, a 19% increase over the prior year.

## Segments and drivers
*   Growth in net revenue was driven by year-over-year increases in payments volume, cross-border volume, and processed transactions.
*   Processed transactions for the three months ended June 30, 2026, reached 71.8 billion, a 10% increase over the prior year.
*   Cross-border volume excluding intra-Europe increased 12% on a constant-dollar basis, while total cross-border volume increased 13% on a constant-dollar basis.
*   Data processing revenue rose 17% over the prior year to $6.0 billion, and other revenue rose 45% over the prior year to $1.5 billion.

## Outlook
*   The release provides no specific guidance or outlook for future periods.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   Cash, cash equivalents, and investment securities were $13.9 billion as of June 30, 2026.
*   The company repurchased approximately 14.5 million shares of class A common stock at an average cost of $330.71 per share for $4.9 billion during the three months ended June 30, 2026.
*   The board of directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.670 per share of class A common stock payable on September 1, 2026.

## Watch items
*   The committee should note the significant variance between GAAP operating expenses ($4.8 billion) and non-GAAP operating expenses ($3.878 billion) for the third quarter, which includes the exclusion of severance costs and litigation provisions.
*   The committee should monitor the impact of personnel expenses, which were a primary driver for the 19% increase in GAAP operating expenses during the fiscal third quarter.
