**The Home Depot reports $47.9 billion in second-quarter fiscal 2026 sales and reaffirms its full-year guidance.**

## Results
*   Reported sales for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 were $47.9 billion, an increase of $2.6 billion (or 5.7%) from the second quarter of fiscal 2025.
*   Net earnings for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 were $4.8 billion, or $4.79 per diluted share, compared to $4.6 billion, or $4.58 per diluted share, in the same period of fiscal 2025.
*   Adjusted diluted earnings per share (non-GAAP) for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 were $4.92, compared to $4.68 in the same period of fiscal 2025.
*   Comparable sales for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 increased 1.7%, with U.S. comparable sales increasing 1.3%.

## Segments and drivers
*   Management noted broad-based demand across the business as customers engaged in smaller projects.
*   Comparable average ticket increased 2.8% for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 compared to 1.4% in the second quarter of fiscal 2025.
*   Comparable customer transactions decreased 1.0% for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 compared to a 0.4% decrease in the second quarter of fiscal 2025.

## Outlook
*   The Company reaffirms fiscal 2026 guidance, including total sales growth of approximately 2.5% to 4.5% and comparable sales growth of approximately flat to 2.0%.
*   Gross margin is projected at approximately 33.1%, with an operating margin of approximately 12.4% to 12.6% and an adjusted operating margin (non-GAAP) of approximately 12.8% to 13.0%.
*   Diluted earnings-per-share are expected to grow approximately flat to 4.0% from $14.23 in fiscal 2025; adjusted diluted earnings-per-share (non-GAAP) are expected to grow approximately flat to 4.0% from $14.69 in fiscal 2025.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   Capital expenditures are projected to be approximately 2.5% of total sales for fiscal 2026.
*   Net interest expense for fiscal 2026 is projected to be approximately $2.3 billion.
*   As of the end of the second quarter, the company operated 2,364 retail stores and over 1,340 SRS locations.

## Watch items
*   The impact of IEEPA tariff refunds on offsetting unplanned fuel, energy, and other product input costs throughout the fiscal year.
*   The variance between reported operating margin (12.4% to 12.6%) and adjusted operating margin (12.8% to 13.0%) due to the exclusion of acquired intangible asset amortization.
