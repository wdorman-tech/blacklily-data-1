**Morgan Stanley reported record net revenues of $21.3 billion and record EPS of $3.46 for the second quarter of 2026.**

## Results
*   Net revenues reached $21.3 billion for the second quarter of 2026, compared to $16.8 billion in the same period a year ago.
*   Net income applicable to Morgan Stanley was $5.6 billion, or $3.46 per diluted share, compared to $3.5 billion, or $2.13 per diluted share, for the second quarter of 2025.
*   The Firm reported a Return on Tangible Common Equity (a non-GAAP measure) of 26.6% for the second quarter of 2026.
*   The expense efficiency ratio was 65% for the first half of the year.

## Segments and drivers
*   Institutional Securities reported record net revenues of $11.0 billion, with Equity net revenues reaching $6.3 billion and Investment Banking net revenues of $2.4 billion.
*   Wealth Management reported record net revenues of $8.9 billion and added $148 billion in net new assets during the quarter.
*   Investment Management reported net revenues of $1.6 billion, with asset management and related fees of $1.5 billion.

## Outlook
*   The release provides no specific guidance or outlook for future periods.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   The Firm repurchased $1.5 billion of its outstanding common stock during the second quarter of 2026.
*   The Board of Directors reauthorized a multi-year common equity share repurchase program of up to $20 billion beginning in the third quarter of 2026.
*   The Standardized Common Equity Tier 1 capital ratio was 14.8% as of the report date.

## Watch items
*   The impact of the "Other" category in Institutional Securities, which saw a decrease in net revenues to $(152) million in the second quarter of 2026 compared to $202 million in the second quarter of 2025.
*   The transition of Wealth Management deferred cash-based compensation (DCP) awards to derivative instruments starting in the first quarter of 2026, which changed how the firm reports these items.
