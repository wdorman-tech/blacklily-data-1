**Chevron reports $12.1 billion in reported earnings for the second quarter of 2026, supported by record U.S. production and robust downstream margins.**

## Results
*   Reported earnings of $12.1 billion ($6.11 per share - diluted) for the second quarter of 2026.
*   Adjusted earnings of $12.0 billion ($6.06 per share - diluted) for the second quarter of 2026.
*   Return on capital employed (ROCE) of 21 percent for the second quarter of 2026.
*   Cash flow from operations (CFFO) of $22.6 billion for the second quarter of 2026.
*   Adjusted free cash flow of $15.4 billion for the second quarter of 2026.

## Segments and drivers
*   **Upstream:** U.S. upstream earnings were $3.541 billion, while international upstream earnings were $4.641 billion; international upstream production was up 292,000 barrels per day from the year-ago period.
*   **Downstream:** U.S. downstream earnings were $2.411 billion; international downstream earnings were $2.457 billion, which included an asset sale gain of $230 million.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** The company achieved $3 billion in annual run-rate structural cost reductions and $1.5 billion in annual run-rate synergies related to the Hess Corporation acquisition.

## Outlook
*   The release provides no specific guidance or outlook figures for future periods.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   Total debt was reduced by a record $8.4 billion in the second quarter of 2026.
*   Capital expenditures (Capex) for the second quarter of 2026 were $4.5 billion.
*   The debt-to-CFFO ratio was 0.8x for the second quarter of 2026.

## Watch items
*   The impact of the 20-year power purchase agreement with Microsoft for the West Texas data center on long-term power infrastructure requirements.
*   The sustainability of the $3 billion in annual run-rate structural cost reductions and the $1.5 billion in Hess-related synergies.
*   The effect of the "supply disruptions from the Middle East conflict" on international downstream production and sales volumes.
