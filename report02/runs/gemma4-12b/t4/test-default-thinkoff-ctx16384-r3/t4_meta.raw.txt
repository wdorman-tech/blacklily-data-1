**Meta Reports Second Quarter 2026 Results with Revenue Growth of 28% Year-over-Year.**

## Results
*   Revenue for the three months ended June 30, 2026, was $60.80 billion, compared to $47.516 billion in the same period of 2025.
*   Costs and expenses for the three months ended June 30, 2026, were $42.03 billion, which included $2.40 billion of charges related to legal proceedings and $1.18 billion of severance expenses.
*   Net income for the three months ended June 30, 2026, was $15.848 billion, compared to $18.337 billion in the same period of 2025.
*   Diluted earnings per share (EPS) for the three months ended June 30, 2026, was 6.18, compared to 7.14 in the same period of 2025.

## Segments and drivers
*   Family of Apps (FoA) segment revenue was $60.370 billion for the three months ended June 30, 2026, with an operating income of $23.394 billion.
*   Reality Labs (RL) segment revenue was $431 million for the three months ended June 30, 2026, with an operating loss of $4.619 billion.
*   Ad impressions delivered across the Family of Apps increased by 14% year-over-year, while average price per ad increased by 12% year-over-year.

## Outlook
*   Management expects third quarter 2026 total revenue to be in the range of $61-64 billion.
*   Full year 2026 total expenses are expected to be in the range of $165-169 billion.
*   Capital expenditures, including principal payments on finance leases, are anticipated to be in the range of $130-145 billion for the full year 2026.
*   The expected tax rate for the remaining quarters of 2026 is between 15-17%.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities were $90.26 billion as of June 30, 2026.
*   Free cash flow (non-GAAP) for the three months ended June 30, 2026, was $784 million.
*   Long-term debt was $83.66 billion as of June 30, 2026.

## Watch items
*   The impact of the $2.40 billion in legal proceeding charges on the revised full-year expense outlook.
*   The potential for material losses from ongoing youth-related trials in the U.S. and other markets.
