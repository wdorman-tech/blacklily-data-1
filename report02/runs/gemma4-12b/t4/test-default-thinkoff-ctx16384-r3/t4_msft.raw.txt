**Microsoft reports strong fourth-quarter results driven by Cloud and AI growth.**

## Results
*   Revenue for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, was $90.0 billion, an increase of 18% (17% in constant currency) compared to the same period last year.
*   Operating income was $40.6 billion, an increase of 18% over the prior year.
*   Net income was $35.8 billion on a GAAP basis (up 31%) and $35.3 billion on a non-GAAP basis (up 22%).
*   Diluted earnings per share were $4.81 on a GAAP basis (up 32%) and $4.74 on a non-GAAP basis (up 23%).
*   Full-year revenue for the period ended June 30, 2026, was $331.8 billion, up 18% (16% in constant currency).

## Segments and drivers
*   Microsoft Cloud revenue reached $59.3 billion, up 27% year-over-year, while Azure and other cloud services revenue increased 43%.
*   Productivity and Business Processes revenue was $37.8 billion (up 14%), with Microsoft 365 Commercial cloud revenue increasing 14% on a reported basis.
*   Intelligent Cloud revenue was $39.3 billion, up 32% (31% in constant currency).
*   More Personal Computing revenue was $12.9 billion, a decrease of 4% (down 5% in constant currency).

## Outlook
*   The company will provide forward-looking guidance during the earnings conference call and webcast.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   The company returned $10.2 billion to shareholders through dividends and share repurchases in the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2026.
*   Total assets as of June 30, 2026, were $758.3 billion.
*   Total cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments were $76.8 billion as of June 30, 2026.

## Watch items
*   The committee should monitor the impact of OpenAI investments, which resulted in a $480 million increase in net income and $0.07 increase in diluted earnings per share for the fourth quarter on a non-GAAP basis.
*   The committee should note the $3.2 billion gain from the investment in Anthropic and lower-than-expected expenses related to the Voluntary Retirement Program, which contributed to a $0.27 benefit on diluted earnings per share compared to prior guidance.
