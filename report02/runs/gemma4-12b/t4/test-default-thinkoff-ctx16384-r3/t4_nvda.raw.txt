**NVIDIA reports revenue of $96.2 billion for the second quarter of fiscal 2027, reflecting a 106% increase from the prior year.**

## Results
*   Revenue for the second quarter of fiscal 2027 was $96.2 billion, up 18% from the previous quarter and 106% from a year ago.
*   GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins for the quarter were both 75.0%.
*   GAAP and non-GAAP earnings per diluted share were $2.46 and $2.22, respectively.
*   Net income for the quarter was $59,688 (GAAP) and $53,954 (non-GAAP).

## Segments and drivers
*   Data Center revenue reached $89.0 billion, up 18% from the previous quarter and 117% from a year ago.
*   Edge Computing revenue was $7.2 billion, up 13% from the previous quarter and 27% from a year ago.
*   The company reported the Vera Rubin platform is in full production and the Groq 3 LPX is in full production.

## Outlook
*   Revenue for the third quarter of fiscal 2027 is expected to be $108.0 billion, plus or minus 2%, with no Data Center compute revenue from China assumed in this outlook.
*   GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins for the third quarter of fiscal 2027 are expected to be 74.0%, plus or minus 50 basis points.
*   GAAP and non-GAAP operating expenses for the third quarter of fiscal 2027 are expected to be approximately $9.2 billion and $9.0 billion, respectively.
*   For the full year fiscal 2027, the company expects GAAP and non-GAAP tax rates to be between 16.0% and 18.0%.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   The company returned approximately $26.0 billion to shareholders in the form of shares repurchased and cash dividends during the second quarter of fiscal 2027.
*   As of the end of the second quarter, approximately $99.0 billion remained under the share repurchase authorization.
*   Cash and cash equivalents at the end of the second quarter were $22,443 million.

## Watch items
*   The committee should monitor the impact of the Vera Rubin platform and the transition to the Blackwell platform as they move into full production.
*   The committee should monitor the exclusion of Data Center compute revenue from China in the third-quarter outlook.
