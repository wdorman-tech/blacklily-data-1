**AT&T reports strong second-quarter results driven by investment-led strategy and growth in Advanced Connectivity.**

## Results
*   Revenues totaled $31.6 billion for the second quarter, up 2.3% from the year-ago quarter.
*   Diluted EPS from continuing operations was $0.66, compared to $0.62 in the year-ago quarter; adjusted EPS was $0.65, compared to $0.54 in the year-ago quarter.
*   Operating income was $7.0 billion; adjusted operating income was $7.5 billion.
*   Income from continuing operations was $5.0 billion, up 3.6% year over year; adjusted EBITDA was $12.3 billion, up 5.2% year over year.
*   Free cash flow was $4.7 billion, compared to $4.4 billion in the year-ago quarter.

## Segments and drivers
*   Advanced Connectivity service revenue grew 5.1% year over year to $23.5 billion, with operating income increasing 20.3% year over year and EBITDA growing 8.0%.
*   Advanced Connectivity saw 646,000 total consumer and business internet net adds (367,000 fiber and 279,000 fixed wireless) and 432,000 postpaid phone net adds.
*   Legacy revenue declined 25.9% year over year as the company continues to decommission its copper-based network.
*   Latin America revenue increased 16.1% year over year, primarily due to favorable foreign exchange rates and postpaid wireless subscriber growth.

## Outlook
*   The company reiterates its plan to return $45 billion+ to shareholders during 2026-2028 through dividends and share repurchases.
*   For 2026, the company projects adjusted EPS of $2.25 to $2.35 with a double-digit 3-year CAGR through 2028.
*   The company expects free cash flow of $18 billion+ in 2026, $19 billion+ in 2027, and $21 billion+ in 2028.
*   The company expects its net debt-to-adjusted EBITDA ratio to return to a target in the 2.5x range within approximately three years following the EchoStar transaction.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   The company is accelerating the pace of planned share repurchases in 2026 to approximately $10 billion.
*   Capital investment was $6.1 billion in the second quarter of 2026, compared to $5.1 billion in the year-ago quarter.
*   Total debt was $144.0 billion at the end of the second quarter, with net debt of $126.4 billion.

## Watch items
*   Monitor the pace of the copper-based network decommissioning, which is expected to make Legacy EBITDA turn negative after 2027.
*   Track the progress toward the goal of reaching over 40 million total fiber locations by the end of 2026.
