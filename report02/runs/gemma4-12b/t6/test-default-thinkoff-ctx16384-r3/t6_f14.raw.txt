I am writing to provide you with the quarterly update for the Delacourt-Ambrose Charitable Remainder Unitrust for the period of January 1 through March 31, 2026.

During the first quarter of 2026, the portfolio achieved a return of 2.2% net of fees (2.4% gross of fees). This performance outperformed the comparison index of 1.8% for the same period. Since the inception of the account on October 2, 2017, the annualized return net of fees has been 6.8%. The year-to-date return through March 31, 2026, net of fees, stands at 2.2%.

Regarding the trust's specific activities this quarter, the first 2026 unitrust payment of $31,250 was made on March 31. This represents 5% of the $2,500,000 January 2 valuation, paid in four equal installments. To manage liquidity, we increased the allocation of cash and Treasury bills to 6.5% of trust assets. This move allows us to pre-fund the June and September payments without the need to sell risk assets. Additionally, we trimmed the high-yield bond position to 3.5% from 7.5%, as spreads have reached levels near their narrowest since 2021.

I also want to inform you of an upcoming change to the advisory fee structure. For the first $3,000,000 of trust assets, the annual rate will decrease from 1.00% to 0.90%. This change will take effect on June 1, 2026, and will be prorated on your Q2 invoice. The rate for assets exceeding $3,000,000 remains unchanged at 0.70% per year.

Unitrust payments are set by the trust instrument as a fixed percentage of the trust's net assets as revalued each year, will rise or fall with the value of the trust, and are not a measure of investment performance.
