**Apple reports record June quarter revenue and earnings per share**

## Results
* Total quarterly revenue for the period ended June 27, 2026, was $109.4 billion, representing a 16 percent increase year over year.
* Diluted earnings per share for the quarter ended June 27, 2026, was $2.02, an increase of 29 percent year over year; this figure included a favorable impact of $0.11 from tariff refunds.
* The company reported a gross margin of 50.1 percent for the quarter, which included a favorable impact of approximately 2 percentage points from tariff refunds.
* Net income for the three months ended June 27, 2026, was $29,789 million.

## Segments and drivers
* Revenue for iPhone, Mac, and Services reached new June quarter records, with the company reporting double-digit revenue growth across these categories.
* iPhone net sales for the quarter ended June 27, 2026, were $54,252 million, compared to $44,582 million in the prior year period.
* Services revenue for the quarter ended June 27, 2026, was $30,739 million, compared to $27,423 million in the prior year period.
* The company achieved double-digit revenue growth in every geographic segment.

## Outlook
* The release provides no guidance or outlook for future periods.

## Capital and balance sheet
* The Board of Directors declared a cash dividend of $0.27 per share, payable on August 13, 2026, to shareholders of record as of the close of business on August 10, 2026.
* Cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash and cash equivalents ended the quarter at $39,544 million, compared to $35,934 million as of September 27, 2025.
* Total assets as of June 27, 2026, were $383,266 million.

## Watch items
* Monitor the impact of tariff refunds on margins and earnings, as the release notes these refunds contributed 2 percentage points to gross margin and $0.11 to diluted EPS.
* Track the performance of the installed base of active devices, which the company stated reached a new all-time high across all major product categories and geographic segments.
* Evaluate the impact of software and AI innovations, such as the introduction of the all-new Siri AI at WWDC26, on future product performance.
