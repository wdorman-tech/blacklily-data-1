**Chevron reports $12.1 billion in second quarter 2026 earnings and record U.S. production.**

## Results
*   Reported earnings for the second quarter 2026 were $12.1 billion ($6.11 per share - diluted).
*   Adjusted earnings for the second quarter 2026 were $12.0 billion ($6.06 per share - diluted).
*   The company achieved a return on capital employed (ROCE) of 21.4 percent for the second quarter 2026.
*   Cash flow from operations (CFFO) for the second quarter 2026 was $22.6 billion.
*   The company achieved its $3 billion annual run-rate structural cost reduction target six months early.

## Segments and drivers
*   **Upstream:** U.S. upstream earnings were $3,541 million for the second quarter 2026, while international upstream earnings were $4,641 million. Net oil-equivalent production for the second quarter 2026 was 4,070 MBOED, a 20 percent increase from the second quarter 2025.
*   **Downstream:** U.S. downstream earnings were $2,411 million for the second quarter 2026, supported by record crude unit throughput of 1.07 million barrels per day at U.S. refineries. International downstream earnings were $2,457 million for the second quarter 2026.

## Outlook
*   The company aims to reduce structural costs by $3-4 billion by the end of 2026.
*   The company expects to close a transaction to sell its 50 percent interest in the Singapore Refining Company and other downstream assets in Singapore, Vietnam, Australia, Indonesia, the Philippines, and Malaysia in 2027.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   Total debt was reduced by $8.4 billion during the second quarter 2026.
*   Capital expenditures (Capex) for the second quarter 2026 were $4.5 billion.
*   The Board of Directors declared a quarterly dividend of $1.78 per share, payable September 10, 2026.

## Watch items
*   **Synergy realization:** Monitor the progress of the $1.5 billion annual run-rate synergies related to the Hess Corporation acquisition, which the company stated were delivered within one year of closing.
*   **Production volatility:** Monitor the impact of Middle East conflict-related supply disruptions, which caused a 10 percent decrease in refinery crude unit inputs for international downstream and curtailments in the Partitioned Zone between Saudi Arabia and Kuwait.
*   **Cost targets:** Monitor the achievement of the remaining structural cost reduction target of up to $4 billion by the end of 2026.
