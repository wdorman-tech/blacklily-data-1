**Alphabet reports $119.8 billion in revenue for the second quarter of 2026, representing 24% year-over-year growth.**

## Results
* Consolidated Alphabet revenues for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, were $119.8 billion, a 24% increase year-over-year and a 23% increase in non-GAAP constant currency.
* Net income available to common stockholders increased 298% to $112.1 billion for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.
* Diluted net income per common share increased 294% to $9.11 for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.
* Consolidated operating income increased 30% to $40.8 billion, with operating margin expanding by 2 percentage points to 34%.
* Other income, net, reflected a net gain of $98.0 billion for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, primarily due to net unrealized gains on equity securities.

## Segments and drivers
* Google Services revenues increased 15% to $94.5 billion, driven by 17% growth in Google Search & other, 15% in Google subscriptions, platforms, and devices, and 13% in YouTube ads.
* Google Cloud revenues increased 82% to $24.8 billion, led by increases in Google Cloud Platform (GCP) across enterprise AI Solutions, enterprise AI Infrastructure, and core GCP services.
* Other Bets revenues were $382 million for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, with an operating loss of $1.8 billion.

## Outlook
* The release provides no guidance or outlook.

## Capital and balance sheet
* In June 2026, the company issued a combination of Class A stock, Class C stock, and mandatory convertible preferred stock for aggregate net proceeds of $49.6 billion for general corporate purposes, including AI infrastructure and global compute.
* The company entered into an equity distribution agreement to sell up to $40.0 billion of Class A and Class C stock through an At-the-Market Program, with proceeds primarily intended to meet tax obligations associated with employee equity grants.
* In the second quarter of 2026, the company issued senior unsecured notes for net proceeds of $20.3 billion for general corporate purposes.

## Watch items
* The impact of the $99.0 billion net gain on equity securities, which the release notes increased net income and diluted net income per common share by $77.1 billion and $6.26, respectively.
* The volatility of other income (expense), net, as the release states fluctuations in the value of investments may significantly contribute to volatility in future periods.
* The execution of the At-the-Market Program, noting that as of June 30, 2026, the company had not sold any shares under this program.
