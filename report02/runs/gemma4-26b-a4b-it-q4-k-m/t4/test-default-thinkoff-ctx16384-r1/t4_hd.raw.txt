**The Home Depot reports second quarter fiscal 2026 sales of $47.9 billion, an increase of $2.6 billion from the second quarter of fiscal 2025.**

## Results
* Net sales for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 were $47.861 billion, compared to $45.277 billion in the second quarter of fiscal 20

* Net earnings for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 were $4.766 billion, or $4.79 per diluted share, compared to $4.551 billion, or $4.58 per diluted share, in the same period of fiscal 2025.
* Adjusted(1) diluted earnings per share for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 were $4.92, compared with adjusted(1) diluted earnings per share of $4.68 in the same period of fiscal 2025.
* Comparable sales for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 increased 1.7%, while comparable sales in the U.S. increased 1.3%.

## Segments and drivers
* The company noted broad based demand across the business as customers continued to engage in smaller projects.
* Comparable average ticket for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 was $92.50, compared to $90.01 in the second quarter of fiscal 2025.
* Customer transactions (excluding results from HD Supply or SRS) for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 were 443.2 million, a decrease from 446.8 million in the second quarter of fiscal 2025.

## Outlook
* The company reaffirmed its fiscal 2026 guidance, which includes total sales growth of approximately 2.5% to 4.5% and comparable sales growth of approximately flat to 2.0%.
* Fiscal 2026 guidance for adjusted(1) operating margin is approximately 12.8% to 13.0%.
* The company expects to add approximately 15 new stores during fiscal 2026.

## Capital and balance sheet
* Capital expenditures for the six months ended August 2, 2026, were $(1,724) million.
* Cash and cash equivalents at the end of the second quarter of fiscal 2026 were $2,085 million, compared to $2,804 million at the end of the second quarter of fiscal 2025.
* Long-term debt, excluding current installments, was $43,951 million as of August 2, 2026.

## Watch items
* The impact of IEEPA tariff refunds, which are expected to partially offset unplanned fuel, energy, and other product input costs throughout fiscal 2026.
* The impact of acquired intangible asset amortization on margins, as the fiscal 2026 adjusted(1) operating margin guidance excludes an expected approximately 40 basis point impact from this expense.
* The trajectory of net interest expense, which is guided to be approximately $2.3 billion for fiscal 2026.
