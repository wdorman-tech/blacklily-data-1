**IBM reports second-quarter 2026 results with updated full-year constant currency revenue growth expectations.**

## Results
* Second-quarter 2026 GAAP revenue was $17.2 billion, compared to $17.0 billion in the second quarter of 2025.
* Second-quarter 2026 GAAP net income was $2.2 billion, compared to $2.2 billion in the second quarter of 20．
* The company reported second-quarter 2026 operating (non-GAAP) earnings per share of $2.93, compared to $2.80 in the second quarter of 2025.
* Second-quarter 2026 free cash flow was $2.5 billion, a decrease from $2.8 billion in the second quarter of 2025.

## Segments and drivers
* Software revenue grew 5 percent in the second quarter of 2026, driven by an 11 percent increase in Hybrid Cloud (Red Hat) and a 19 percent increase in Data.
* Consulting revenue was flat in the second quarter of 2026, though it grew 1 percent at constant currency.
* Infrastructure revenue declined 7 percent in the second quarter of 2026, with IBM Z revenue decreasing 42 percent.
* Financing revenue increased 12 percent in the second quarter of 2026.

## Outlook
* For the full year 2026, the company now expects constant currency revenue growth in the range of four-to-five percent.
* The company continues to expect full-year 2026 free cash flow to increase by about $1 billion year-over-year.
* The company expects improved pre-tax income margin expansion for the full year 2026.

## Capital and balance sheet
* IBM ended the second quarter of 2026 with $8.2 billion of cash, restricted cash, and marketable securities, which was a decrease of $6.3 billion from year-end 2025.
* Total debt, including IBM Financing debt of $13.0 billion, was $62.0 billion, an increase of $0.7 billion year to date.
* The company invested $10.5 billion in acquisitions during the first six months of 2026.

## Watch items
* Monitor the impact of revenue headwinds experienced late in the second quarter of 2026 on future performance.
* Track the progress of the $10 billion investment in quantum computing planned over the next five years.
* Evaluate the ability to maintain the expected full-year 2026 constant currency revenue growth of four-to-five percent given that currency is expected to be neutral to growth at current exchange rates.
