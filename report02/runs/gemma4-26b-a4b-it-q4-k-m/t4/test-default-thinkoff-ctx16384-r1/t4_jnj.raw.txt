**Johnson & Johnson raises 2026 outlook following Q2 2026 sales growth of 6.6%**

## Results
* Q2 2026 reported sales were $25.3 billion, representing 6.6% growth.
* Q2 2026 reported earnings per share (EPS) was $2.27.
* Q2 2026 adjusted EPS* was $2.90.
* Q2 2026 adjusted net earnings* were $7,081 million.

## Segments and drivers
* **Innovative Medicine:** Q2 2026 reported sales were $16,384 million (7.8% growth). Worldwide operational sales* grew 6.8%, driven by DARZALEX, CARVYKTI, TECVAYLI, and RYBREVANT/LAZCLUZE in Oncology, and TREMFYA and Other Immunology in Immunology. Growth was partially offset by STELARA (an approximate 760 basis points impact) and REMICADE in Immunology, and IMBRUVICA and ZYTIGA in Oncology.
* **MedTech:** Q2 2026 reported sales were $8,926 million (4.5% growth). Worldwide operational sales* grew 3.6%, driven by wound closure and biosurgery products in Surgery, electroelectrophysiology and Shockwave in Cardiovascular, contact lenses in Vision, and trauma in Orthopaedics.

## Outlook
* The company increased 2026 guidance for estimated reported sales to a midpoint of $101.1 billion (7.3%).
* The company increased 2026 guidance for adjusted EPS* to a midpoint of $11.68 (8.2%).
* The company increased 2026 guidance for adjusted operational EPS* to a midpoint of $11.58 (7.3%).

## Capital and balance sheet
* Q2 2026 free cash flow was approximately $8,700 million.

## Watch items
* **Impact of Divestitures:** Monitor the impact of divestitures on Innovative Medicine, which negatively impacted worldwide operational sales* growth by 10 basis points in Q2 2026.
* **Legal and Uncertainty Risks:** Monitor the impact of legal proceedings and unusual gains/losses, as the company stated it is unable to predict the ultimate outcome of these items with reasonable certainty.
* **Currency Fluctuations:** Monitor the impact of translational currency, which contributed 1.0% to worldwide sales growth in Q2 2026.
