**JPMorgan Chase reports second-quarter 2026 net income of $21.2 billion**

## Results
* Reported net income for the second quarter of 2026 was $21.2 billion ($7.70 per share), while net income excluding significant items was $16.9 billion ($6.14 per share).
* Reported revenue was $57.3 billion, with managed revenue of $58.0 billion.
* The firm reported an expense of $27.3 billion, with a reported overhead ratio of 4 and a managed overhead ratio of 47%.
* Credit costs were $2.5 billion, consisting of $2.4 billion of net charge-offs and a $149 million net reserve build.
* Average loans were up 10% YoY and 2% QoQ, while average deposits were up 7% YoY and 3% QoQ.

## Segments and drivers
* **Consumer & Community Banking (CCB):** Net revenue was $20.3 billion, up 8%. Card Services & Auto net revenue was $7.8 billion, up 12%, driven by higher Card Services net interest income and higher auto operating lease income.
* **Commercial & Investment Bank (CIB):** Net revenue was $24.9 billion, up 27%. Investment Banking fees were $3.3 billion, up 30%, while Markets revenue was $12.1 billion, up 35%, driven by Equity Markets revenue of $6.0 billion, up 86%.
* **Asset & Wealth Management (AWM):** Net revenue was $6.9 billion, up 19%. Assets under management were $5.1 trillion, up 18% YoY.

## Outlook
* The release provides no guidance or outlook.

## Capital and balance sheet
* The firm reported a Basel III common equity Tier 1 capital ratio of 14.1% (Standardized) and 14.2% (Advanced).
* Total Loss-Absorbing Capacity was $590 billion.
* The firm executed common stock net repurchases of $6.2 billion and a common dividend of $4.0 billion ($1.50 per share).

## Watch items
* **Impact of significant items:** Monitor the impact of the $4.6 billion net gain related to Visa shares and $1.0 billion of gains on certain equity investments on future earnings volatility.
* **Credit trends:** Monitor the drivers of the $2.2 billion net charge-offs in CCB, which were predominantly driven by Card Services.
