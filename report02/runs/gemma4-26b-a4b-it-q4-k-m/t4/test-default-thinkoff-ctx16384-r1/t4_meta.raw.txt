**Meta reports second quarter 2026 revenue of $60.8 billion, a 28% increase year-over-year.**

## Results
* Revenue for the three months ended June 30, 2026, was $60,801 million, compared to $47,516 million in the three months ended June 30, 2025.
* Net income for the three months ended June 30, 2026, was $15,848 million, a 14% decrease from $18,337 million in the three months ended June 30, 2025.
* Diluted earnings per share (EPS) for the three months ended June 30, 2026, was $6.18, compared to $7.14 in the three months ended June 30, 2025.
* Total costs and expenses for the three months ended June 30, 2026, were $42,026 million, an increase of 55% year-over-year.

## Segments and drivers
* Family of Apps (FoA) revenue for the three months ended June 30, 2026, was $60,370 million, compared to $47,146 million for the same period in 2025.
* Reality Labs (RL) reported an operating loss of $4,619 million for the three months ended June 30, 2026, compared to an operating loss of $4,530 million for the three months ended June 30, 2025.
* Family daily active people (DAP) averaged 3.60 billion for June 2026, a 3% increase year-over-year.
* Ad impressions across the Family of Apps increased by 14% year-over-year.

## Outlook
* The company expects third quarter 2026 total revenue to be in the range of $61-64 billion.
* The company expects full year 2026 total expenses to be in the range of $165-169 billion.
* The company anticipates 2026 capital expenditures, including principal payments on finance leases, will be in the range of $130-145 billion.

## Capital and balance sheet
* Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities totaled $90.26 billion as of June 30, 2026.
* Long-term debt was $83.66 billion as of June 30, 2026.
* Free cash flow (non-GAAP) for the three months ended June 30, 2026, was $784 million.
