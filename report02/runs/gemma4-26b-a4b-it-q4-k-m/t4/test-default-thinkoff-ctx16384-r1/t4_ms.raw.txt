**Morgan Stanley reports record second quarter 2026 net revenues of $21.3 billion and EPS of $3.46.**

## Results
* The Firm reported net revenues of $21.3 billion for the second quarter ended June 30, 2026, compared with $16.8 billion for the same period a year ago.
* Net income applicable to Morgan Stanley was $5.6 billion, or $3.46 per diluted share, compared with $3.5 billion, or $2.13 per diluted share, for the same period a year ago.
* The Firm reported a return on average tangible common equity (a non-GAAP financial measure) of 26.6% for the second quarter ended June 30, 2026, compared with 18.2% for the same period a year ago.
* The expense efficiency ratio for the first half of the year was 65%.

## Segments and drivers
* **Institutional Securities:** Reported net revenues of $11.0 billion for the second quarter ended June 30, 2026, compared with $7.6 billion a year ago. Drivers included Equity net revenues of $6.3 billion and Investment Banking net revenues of $2.4 billion.
* **Wealth Management:** Reported net revenues of $8.9 billion for the second and quarterly ended June 30, 2026, compared with $7.8 billion a year ago. The segment added $148 billion in net new assets for the quarter.
* **Investment Management:** Reported net revenues of $1.6 billion for the second quarter ended June 30, 2026, compared with $1.5 billion a year ago, driven by higher average AUM.

## Outlook
* The Board of Directors reauthorized a multi-year common equity share repurchase program of up to $20 billion, without a set expiration date, beginning in the third quarter of 2026.
* The Firm provides no guidance or outlook in the release.

## Capital and balance sheet
* The Standardized Approach CET1 capital ratio was 14.8% as of July 15, 2026.
* The Firm repurchased $1.5 billion of its outstanding common stock during the second quarter of 2026.
* The Board of Directors declared a $1.15 quarterly dividend per share, an increase of 15 cents, payable on August 14, 2026.

## Watch items
* **Credit Risk:** Monitor the drivers behind the provision for credit losses, which was $98 million for the second quarter ended June 30, 2026, following a $196 million provision for the same period a year ago.
* **Expense Management:** Monitor non-compensation expenses, which increased to $5.7 billion for the second quarter ended June 30, 2026, from $4.8 billion in the second quarter of 2025.
* **Asset Flows:** Monitor long-term net flows in Investment Management, which were $7.5 billion for the second quarter ended June 30, 2026, compared to $12.2 billion for the same period a year ago.
