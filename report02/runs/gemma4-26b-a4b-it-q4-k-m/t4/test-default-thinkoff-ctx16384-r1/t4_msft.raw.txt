**Microsoft reports $90.0 billion in revenue for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, driven by cloud and AI strength.**

## Results
* Revenue for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, was $90.0 billion, an 18% increase compared to the corresponding period of last fiscal year (up 17% in constant currency).
* GAAP net income for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, was $35.8 billion, an increase of 31% (non-GAAP net income was $35.3 billion, an increase of 22%).
* GAAP diluted earnings per share for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, was $4.81, an increase of 32% (non-GAAP diluted earnings per share was $4.74, an increase of 23%).
* For the full fiscal year ended June 30, 2026, revenue was $331.8 billion, an 18% increase (up 16% in constant currency).

## Segments and drivers
* Microsoft Cloud revenue for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, was $59.3 billion, an increase of 27% year-over-year.
* Intelligent Cloud revenue for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, was $39.3 billion, an increase of 32% (up 31% in constant currency), with Azure and other cloud services revenue increasing 43%.
* Productivity and Business Processes revenue for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, was $37.8 billion, an increase of 14%.
* More Personal Computing revenue for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, was $12.9 billion, a decrease of 4% (down 5% in constant currency).

## Outlook
* The company will provide forward-looking guidance in connection with its earnings conference call and webcast.

## Capital and balance sheet
* Microsoft returned $10.2 billion to shareholders in the form of dividends and share repurchable in the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2026.
* Total cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments as of June 30, 2026, were $76.8 billion.

## Watch items
* The impact of discrete items on quarterly earnings, including a $3.2 billion gain from an investment in Anthropic and lower-than-expected expenses related to the Voluntary Retirement Program.
* The performance of the More Personal Computing segment, specifically the 10% decrease in XBOX content and services revenue for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.
* The scale of ongoing infrastructure investment, as additions to property and equipment for the twelve months ended June 30, 2026, were $115.9 billion.
