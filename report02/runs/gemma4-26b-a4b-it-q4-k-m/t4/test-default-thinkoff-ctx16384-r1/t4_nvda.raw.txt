**NVIDIA reports $96.2 billion in revenue for the second quarter of fiscal 2027, representing 106% growth from a year ago.**

## Results
* Revenue for the second quarter ended July 26, 2026, was $96.2 billion, an increase of 18% from the previous quarter and 106% from a year ago.
* GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins for the quarter were both 75.0%.
* GAAP diluted earnings per share were $2.46, while non-GAAP diluted earnings per share were $2.22.
* GAAP net income for the quarter was $59,688 million, compared to $26,422 million for the second quarter of fiscal 2026.

## Segments and drivers
* Data Center revenue reached $89.0 billion for the second quarter, up 117% from a year ago and 18% from the previous quarter.
* Edge Computing revenue for the second quarter was $7.2 billion, up 27% from a year ago and 13% from the previous quarter.
* The company announced the NVIDIA Vera Rubin platform is entering full production, with racks running at partners including Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, and Oracle Cloud Infrastructure.

## Outlook
* For the third quarter of fiscal 2027, NVIDIA expects revenue to be $108.0 billion, plus or minus 2%, noting that the company is not assuming any Data Center compute revenue from China in this outlook.
* The company expects GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins for the third quarter of fiscal 2027 to be 74.0%, plus or minus 50 basis points.
* For the full year fiscal 2027, NVIDIA expects GAAP and non-GAAP tax rates to be between 16.0% and 18.0%.

## Capital and balance sheet
* NVIDIA returned approximately $26.0 billion to shareholders during the second quarter of fiscal 2027 through cash dividends and share repurchases.
* As of the end of the second quarter, the company has approximately $99.0 billion remaining under its share repurchase authorization.
* The company will pay its next quarterly cash dividend of $0.25 per share on October 1, 2026, to shareholders of record as of September 10, 2026.

## Watch items
* Monitor the impact of the exclusion of China Data Center compute revenue on the third quarter revenue guidance of $108.0 billion.
* Track the execution of the NVIDIA Vera Rubin platform as it moves into full production.
* Observe the progress of strategic partnerships intended to mobilize over $500 billion of third-party capital for AI infrastructure buildout.
