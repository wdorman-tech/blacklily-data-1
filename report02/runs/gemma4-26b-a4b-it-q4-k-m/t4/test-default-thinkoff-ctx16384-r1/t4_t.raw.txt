**AT&T reports second-quarter 2026 results driven by Advanced Connectivity growth and increased share repurchases.**

## Results
* Revenues totaled $31.6 billion for the second quarter, an increase of 2.3% from the year-ago quarter.
* Diluted EPS from continuing operations was $0.66, compared to $0.62 in the year-ago quarter, while adjusted EPS* was $0.65, compared to $0.54 in the year-ago quarter.
* Adjusted operating income* was $7.5 billion, compared to $6.5 billion in the year-ago quarter.
* Adjusted EBITDA* was $12.3 billion, up 5.2% year over year.
* Free cash flow* was $4.7 billion, compared to $4.4 billion in the year-ago quarter.

## Segments and drivers
* Advanced Connectivity service revenue grew 5.1% year over year, with the segment adding 646,000 total consumer and business internet net adds (367,000 fiber and 279,000 fixed wireless).
* The Advanced Connectivity segment reported an operating income of $7.3 billion and an adjusted EBITDA* of $12.0 billion.
* Legacy segment revenues declined 25.9% year over year, with operating income of $523 million and an adjusted EBITDA* of $523 million.
* Latin America segment revenues increased 16.1% year over and reported an adjusted EBITDA* of $227 million.

## Outlook
* For the 2026-2028 period, the Company expects annual capital investment* in the $23 billion to $24 billion range.
* The Company projects annual free cash flow* of $18 billion+ in 2026, $19 billion+ in 2027, and $21 billion+ in 2028.
* The Company maintains a long-term outlook for 2026-2028 that includes annual service revenue growth in the low-single-digit range.

## Capital and balance sheet
* The Company is accelerating the pace of planned share repurchases in 2026 to approximately $10 billion.
* Total debt was $144.0 billion at the end of the second quarter, with net debt* of $126.4 billion.
* The Company plans to return $45 billion+ to shareholders during 2026-2028 through dividends and share repurchases.

## Watch items
* Monitor the progress of the fiber network expansion, as the Company aims to reach over 40 million total fiber locations by the end of 2026.
* Track the impact of the decommissioning of the copper-based network, as the Company expects legacy service revenue decline of 20%+ in 2026.
* Observe the achievement of the net debt-to-adjusted EBITDA* target of approximately 2.5x, which is expected within roughly three years following the closing of the transaction with EchoStar.
