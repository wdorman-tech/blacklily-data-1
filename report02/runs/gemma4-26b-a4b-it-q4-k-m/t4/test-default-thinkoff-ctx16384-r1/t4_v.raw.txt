**Visa reports fiscal third quarter 2026 net revenue increase of 14%**

## Results
* GAAP net income for the fiscal third quarter ended June 30, 2026, was $5.6 billion, or $2.97 per share, representing a 7% increase in net income and a 10% increase in earnings per share over the prior year.
* Non-GAAP net income for the fiscal third quarter ended June 3 and 2026 was $6.3 billion, or $3.32 per share, representing an 8% increase in net income and an 11% increase in earnings per share over the prior year.
* Net revenue for the fiscal third quarter ended June 30, 2026, was $11.6 billion, an increase of 14% year-over-year and a 13% increase on a constant-dollar basis.
* GAAP operating expenses for the fiscal third quarter ended June 30, 2026, were $4.8 billion, a 19% increase over the prior year.

## Segments and drivers
* Payments volume for the three months ended June 30, 2026, increased 10% over the prior year on a constant-dollar basis.
* Total cross-border volume on a constant-dollar basis increased 13% over the prior year for the three months ended June 30, 2026.
* Processed transactions for the three months ended June 30, 2026, were 71.7 billion, a 10% increase over the prior year.
* Data processing revenue rose 17% over the prior year to $6.0 billion for the fiscal third quarter ended June 30, 2026.

## Outlook
* The release provides no guidance or outlook.

## Capital and balance sheet
* Cash, cash equivalents and investment securities were $13.9 billion at June 30, 2026.
* During the three months ended June 30, 2026, Visa repurchased approximately 14.5 million shares of class A common stock for $4.9 billion.
* The board of directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.670 per share of class A common stock, payable on September 1, 2026.
