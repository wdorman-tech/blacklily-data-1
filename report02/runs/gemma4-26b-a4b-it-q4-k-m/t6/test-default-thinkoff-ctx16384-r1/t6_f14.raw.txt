I am pleased to provide you with the quarterly report for the Delacourt-Ambrose Charitable Remainder Unitrust for the period ending March 31, 2026.

During the first quarter of 2026, the trust’s income-oriented balanced strategy returned 2.2% net of fees (2.4% gross of fees). This performance compares favorably to the return of 1.8% for the comparison index, which consists of 55% global equity and 45% US aggregate bond indices. The year-to-date return through March 31, 2026, is 2.2% net of fees, and the annualized return since the strategy's inception on October 2, 2017, is 6.8% net of fees.

Regarding trust distributions, the first 2026 unitrust payment of $31,250 was made on March 31. This represents the first of four equal installments based on 5% of the $2,500,000 trust value recorded on January 2.

In terms of portfolio positioning, we have increased our holdings in cash and Treasury bills to 6.5% of the trust assets. This adjustment was made to pre-fund the upcoming June and September payments without the need to sell risk assets. Additionally, we have trimmed our allocation to high-yield bonds from 7.5% to 3.5%, as spreads are currently near their narrowest levels since 2021.

I also want to inform you of an upcoming change to our advisory fee structure. To align trust accounts with the firm's standard individual schedule, the fee for the first $3,000,000 of trust assets will change from 1.00% per year to 0.90% per year. This new rate will take effect on June 1, 2026, and will be applied on a prorated basis on your Q2 invoice.

Unitrust payments are set by the trust instrument as a fixed percentage of the trust's net assets as revalued each year, will rise or fall with the value of the trust, and are not a measure of investment performance.
