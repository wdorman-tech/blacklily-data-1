**Apple reports record revenue and EPS for the June quarter.**

## Results
* Total quarterly revenue was $109.4 billion, up 16 percent year over year.
* Diluted earnings per share was $2.02, up 29 percent year over year.
* Company gross margin was 50.1 percent.
* Net income for the three months ended June 27, 2026, was $29.789 billion.
* For the nine months ended June 27, 2026, net income was $101.464 billion.

## Segments and drivers
* Quarterly revenue records were set for iPhone ($54.252 billion), Mac ($10.352 billion), and Services ($30.739 billion).
* Revenue growth was double-digit across iPhone, Mac, Services, and every geographic segment.
* The company's gross margin included a favorable impact of approximately 2 percentage points from tariff refunds.
* Diluted earnings per share included a favorable impact of $0.11 from tariff refunds.

## Outlook
* The release provides no guidance or outlook.

## Capital and balance sheet
* The board of directors declared a cash dividend of $0.27 per share.
* Total assets as of June 27, 2026, were $383.266 billion, compared to $359.241 billion as of September 27, 2025.
* Total liabilities as of June 27, 2026, were $275.746 billion, compared to $285.508 billion as of September 27, 2025.
* Cash and cash equivalents as of June 27, 2026, were $39.544 billion.

## Watch items
* Monitor the impact of the "all-new Siri AI" and other software innovations introduced at WWDC26 on future product cycles.
* Evaluate the sustainability of margins given the favorable impact of tariff refunds on both gross margin and EPS.
* Track the trend of inventories, which rose to $11.092 billion as of June 27, 2026, from $5.718 billion as of September 27, 2025.
