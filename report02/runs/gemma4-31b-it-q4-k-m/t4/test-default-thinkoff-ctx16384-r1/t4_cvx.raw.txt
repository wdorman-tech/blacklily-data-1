**Chevron reports second quarter 2026 earnings of $12.1 billion.**

## Results
* Reported earnings for second quarter 2026 were $12.1 billion, or $6.11 per share (diluted).
* Adjusted earnings for second quarter 2026 were $12.0 billion, or $6.06 per share (diluted).
* Cash flow from operations (CFFO) for second quarter 2026 was $22.6 billion.
* Return on capital employed (ROCE) for second quarter 2026 was 21.4 percent.
* Year to date 2026 reported earnings were $14.282 billion.

## Segments and drivers
* Upstream earnings for second quarter 2026 were $8.182 billion, driven by higher sales volumes and liquids realizations.
* Downstream earnings for second quarter 2026 were $4.868 billion, with U.S. downstream earnings benefiting from higher margins on refined product sales.
* Worldwide production increased 20 percent from second quarter 2025, attributed to growth in the Permian Basin and Gulf of America, and contributions from legacy Hess assets.
* U.S. refinery crude unit throughput reached a record 1.07 million barrels per day, with capacity utilization of more than 97 percent.

## Outlook
* The company aims to reduce structural costs by $3-4 billion by the end of 2026.
* A transaction to sell the company's 50 percent interest in the Singapore Refining Company and other downstream assets in Singapore, Vietnam, Australia, Indonesia, the Philippines and Malaysia is expected to close in 2027.
* The release provides no other guidance or outlook.

## Capital and balance sheet
* Total debt was reduced by $8.4 billion in the second quarter.
* Capital expenditures (Capex) for second quarter 2026 were $4.5 billion.
* The Board of Directors declared a quarterly dividend of $1.78 per share.
* Net debt-to-CFFO ratio for second quarter 2026 was 0.6x (non-GAAP).

## Watch items
* Monitor the progress of the structural cost reduction program, as the company has captured $3 billion in annual run-rate savings to date.
* Track the integration of Hess Corporation, noting that $1.5 billion of annual run-rate synergies have been delivered.
* Observe the impact of the Middle East conflict, which the release states caused curtailments in the Partitioned Zone and supply disruptions affecting international downstream refinery crude unit inputs and refined product sales.
