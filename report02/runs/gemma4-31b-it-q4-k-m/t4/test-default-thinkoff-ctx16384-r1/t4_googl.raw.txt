**Alphabet reports second quarter 2026 revenues of $119.8 billion.**

## Results
* Consolidated revenues increased 24% to $119.8 billion, or 23% in constant currency (non-GAAP).
* Operating income increased 30% to $40,770 million, with operating margin expanding by 2 percentage points to 34%.
* Net income available to common stockholders increased 298% to $112,107 million, and diluted EPS increased 294% to $9.11.
* Other income reflected a net gain of $97,983 million, primarily due to net unrealized gains on equity securities.

## Segments and drivers
* Google Services revenues increased 15% to $94,540 million, driven by growth in Google Search & other (17%), Google subscriptions, platforms, and devices (15%), and YouTube ads (13%).
* Google Cloud revenues increased 82% to $24,768 million, led by core GCP services and an increase in Google Cloud Platform (GCP) across enterprise AI Infrastructure and enterprise AI Solutions.
* Other Bets revenues were $382 million, with an operating loss of $1,799 million.
* Alphabet-level activities, which primarily reflect expenses related to shared AI research and development, resulted in an operating loss of $5,789 million.

## Outlook
The release provides no guidance or outlook.

## Capital and balance sheet
* In June 2026, the company raised $49.6 billion in aggregate net proceeds from the issuance of Class A, Class C, and mandatory convertible preferred stock for general corporate purposes, including scaling AI infrastructure and global compute.
* The company issued senior unsecured notes in the second quarter of 2026 for net proceeds of $20.3 billion.
* Total cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities were $242,474 million as of June 30, 2026, compared to $126,843 million as of December 31, 2025.
* In July 2026, the Board declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.22 per share on Class A, Class B, and Class C stock.

## Watch items
* Monitor the volatility of Other income (expense), net, as the release notes that fluctuations in the value of investments may significantly contribute to volatility in future periods.
* Track the utilization of the At-the-Market Program (ATM Program), as the company entered into an agreement to sell up to $40.0 billion of Class A and Class C stock but had not sold any shares as of June 30, 2026.
* Observe the impact of AI investments on the balance sheet, specifically the $44,924 million spent on purchases of property and equipment during the quarter.
