**The Home Depot reaffirms its fiscal 2026 guidance following second quarter sales of $47.9 billion.**

## Results
* Net sales for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 were $47.9 billion, an increase of $2.6 billion from the second quarter of fiscal 2025.
* Comparable sales for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 increased 1.7%, with U.S. comparable sales increasing 1.3%.
* Net earnings for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 were $4.8 billion, or $4.79 per diluted share.
* Adjusted (non-GAAP) diluted earnings per share for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 were $4.92.

## Segments and drivers
* Management attributed results to broad based demand as customers continued to engage in smaller projects.
* Comparable average ticket for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 increased 2.8% to $92.50, while comparable customer transactions decreased 1.0%.
* As of the end of the second quarter, the company operated 2,364 retail stores and over 1,340 SRS locations.

## Outlook
The company reaffirmed its fiscal 2026 guidance, which includes:
* Total sales growth of approximately 2.5% to 4.5% and comparable sales growth of approximately flat to 2.0%.
* Gross margin of approximately 33.1% and operating margin of approximately 12.4% to 12.6%.
* Adjusted (non-GAAP) operating margin of approximately 12.8% to 13.0%.
* Diluted earnings-per-share growth of approximately flat to 4.0% from $14.23 in fiscal 2025, and adjusted (non-GAAP) diluted earnings-per-share growth of approximately flat to 4.0% from $14.69 in fiscal 2025.
* Approximately 15 new stores and capital expenditures of approximately 2.5% of total sales.

## Capital and balance sheet
* As of August 2, 2026, total assets were $109.384 billion and total liabilities were $92.767 billion.
* Cash and cash equivalents at the end of the second quarter were $2.085 billion.
* For the six months ended August 2, 2026, the company paid $4.643 billion in cash dividends and spent $1.724 billion on capital expenditures.

## Watch items
* Monitor the impact of IEEPA tariff refunds, which are expected to partially offset unplanned fuel, energy, and other product input costs throughout the fiscal year.
* Track the ability to recognize anticipated benefits from completed or pending acquisitions, specifically SRS and GMS.
* Observe the trend in comparable customer transactions, which decreased 1.0% in the second quarter and 1.2% for the six months ended August 2, 2026.
