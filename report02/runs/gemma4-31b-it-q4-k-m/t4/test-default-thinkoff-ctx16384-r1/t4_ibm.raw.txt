**IBM reports second-quarter 2026 results and updates full-year revenue expectations.**

## Results
* Total revenue for the second quarter was $17.2 billion.
* GAAP net income for the second quarter was $2.2 billion, with diluted earnings per share of $2.27.
* Operating (non-GAAP) pre-tax income for the second quarter was $3.3 billion, with diluted earnings per share of $2.93.
* Adjusted EBITDA for the second quarter was $4.8 billion, representing an adjusted EBITDA margin of 27.8%.

## Segments and drivers
* Software revenue was $7.8 billion, up 5 percent, driven by growth in Data (up 19 percent) and Hybrid Cloud (Red Hat) (up 11 percent), while Transaction Processing declined 8 percent.
* Consulting revenue was $5.3 billion, which was flat.
* Infrastructure revenue was $3.8 billion, down 7 percent; this included a 37 percent increase in Distributed Infrastructure and a 42 percent decrease in IBM Z.
* Financing revenue was $0.2 billion, up 12 percent.

## Outlook
* For the full year 2026, the company expects constant currency revenue growth in the range of four-to-five percent.
* The company expects full-year free cash flow to increase by about $1 billion year-over-year.
* The company expects improved pre-tax income margin expansion for the full year.
* At current foreign exchange rates, currency is expected to be neutral to growth for the full year.

## Capital and balance sheet
* IBM ended the second quarter with $8.2 billion of cash, restricted cash and marketable securities, a decrease of $6.3 billion from year-end 2025.
* Total debt, including IBM Financing debt of $13.0 billion, was $62.0 billion at the end of the second quarter.
* The company invested $10.5 billion in acquisitions during the first six months of the year.
* Free cash flow for the second quarter was $2.5 billion, and the company returned $1.6 billion to shareholders in dividends during the period.

## Watch items
* Monitor the impact of revenue headwinds mentioned by the CFO that occurred late in the second quarter.
* Track the progress of the $10 billion investment in quantum computing over the next five years and the goal to deliver a large-scale fault-tolerant quantum computer by 2029.
* Observe the effectiveness of the expanded sales coverage and the deployment of Forward Deployed Engineers in capturing AI-driven growth opportunities.
