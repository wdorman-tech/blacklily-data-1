**Johnson & Johnson reports Q2 2026 sales of $25.3 billion and raises full-year 2026 guidance.**

## Results
* Second-quarter reported sales were $25,310 million, representing growth of 6.6%.
* Reported earnings per share (EPS) for the second quarter were $2.27.
* Non-GAAP operational sales growth for the second quarter was 5.6%, while non-GAAP adjusted operational growth was 5.7%.
* Non-GAAP adjusted EPS for the second quarter was $2.90, and non-GAAP adjusted net earnings were $7,081 million.

## Segments and drivers
* Innovative Medicine reported second-quarter sales of $16,384 million, with non-GAAP operational sales growth of 6.8%. Growth was driven by DARZALEX, CARVYKTI, TECVAYLI, and RYBREVANT/LAZCLUZE in Oncology; TREMFYA and Other Immunology in Immunology; and SPRAVATO and CAPLYTA in Neuroscience.
* MedTech reported second-quarter sales of $8,926 million, with non-GAAP operational sales growth of 3.6%. Growth was driven by wound closure and biosurgery products in Surgery; electrophysiology products and Shockwave in Cardiovascular; contact lenses in Vision; and trauma in Orthopaedics.
* Regional performance for the second quarter showed reported sales of $14,533 million in the U.S. and $10,777 million internationally.

## Outlook
* For the full year 2026, the company increased estimated reported sales guidance to $101.1 billion at the midpoint.
* Full-year 2026 non-GAAP adjusted EPS guidance was increased by $0.13 to $11.68 at the midpoint.
* Full-year 2026 non-GAAP adjusted operational EPS guidance was increased by $0.18 to $11.58 at the midpoint.

## Capital and balance sheet
* Non-GAAP free cash flow for the second quarter YTD 2026 is estimated at approximately $8,700 million.
* The company invested more than $1 billion to strengthen U.S. Vision Manufacturing in Jacksonville, Florida.

## Watch items
* Monitor the impact of STELARA and REMICADE in Immunology, as well as IMBRUVICA and ZYTIGA in Oncology, which partially offset growth in Innovative Medicine.
* Track the integration and pipeline contributions from the acquisition of Firefly Bio, Inc. and the acquisition of Miniature Radiofrequency Tracking Technology.
* Observe the progress of the planned separation of the Company's Orthopaedics business.
