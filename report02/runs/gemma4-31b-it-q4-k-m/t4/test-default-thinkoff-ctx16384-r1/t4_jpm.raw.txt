**JPMorgan Chase & Co. reports second-quarter 2026 net income of $21.2 billion.**

## Results
* Reported net income was $21.2 billion ($7.70 per share), while net income excluding significant items (non-GAAP) was $16.9 billion ($6.14 per share).
* Reported revenue was $57.3 billion, and managed revenue (non-GAAP) was $58.0 billion.
* Expense was $27.3 billion, resulting in a reported overhead ratio of 48% and a managed overhead ratio (non-GAAP) of 47%.
* Credit costs were $2.5 billion, consisting of $2.4 billion of net charge-offs and a $149 million net reserve build.
* Reported Return on Equity (ROE) was 24% and Return on Tangible Common Equity (ROTCE, non-GAAP) was 29%, while ROTCE excluding significant items (non-GAAP) was 23%.

## Segments and drivers
* **Commercial & Investment Bank (CIB):** Net income was $9.7 billion. Markets revenue rose 35% YoY, with Equity Markets up 86% and Fixed Income Markets up 6%. Investment Banking fees increased 30% YoY.
* **Consumer & Community Banking (CCB):** Net income was $5.3 billion. Revenue rose 8%, with Card Services & Auto net revenue up 12% YoY, predominantly driven by higher net interest income on higher revolving balances.
* **Asset & Wealth Management (AWM):** Net income was $2.0 billion. Assets under management (AUM) were $5.1 trillion, up 18% YoY.

## Outlook
The release provides no guidance or outlook.

## Capital and balance sheet
* The firm reported a Common dividend of $4.0 billion ($1.50 per share) and $6.2 billion of common stock net repurchases.
* Basel III common equity Tier 1 capital was $303 billion, with a Standardized ratio of 14.1% and an Advanced ratio of 14.2%.
* Cash and marketable securities were $1.5 trillion (estimated), and average loans were $1.5 trillion.
* Tangible book value per share (non-GAAP) was $113.35, up 10% YoY.

## Watch items
* Monitor the impact of "tectonic" risks cited by the CEO, including geopolitical tensions, wars, sticky inflation, large global fiscal deficits, and elevated asset prices.
* Track the Card Services net charge-off rate, which was 3.34% for the quarter.
* Observe the trend of net interest income in Corporate, which decreased by $667 million this quarter predominantly due to lower rates.
