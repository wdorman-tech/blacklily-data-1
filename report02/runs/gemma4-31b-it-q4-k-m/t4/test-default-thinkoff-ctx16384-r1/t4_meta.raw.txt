**Meta Platforms reports second quarter 2026 revenue growth of 28% alongside increased costs and expenses.**

## Results
* Revenue for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, was $60.80 billion, compared to $47.52 billion in the prior year period.
* Net income was $15.85 billion, a decrease from $18.34 billion in the second quarter of 2025.
* Diluted earnings per share (EPS) was $6.18, compared to $7.14 in the second quarter of 2025.
* Total costs and expenses were $42.03 billion, which includes $2.40 billion of charges related to legal proceedings and $1.18 billion of severance expenses from the May 2026 headcount reduction.
* Free cash flow (non-GAAP) was $784 million.

## Segments and drivers
* Family of Apps revenue was $60.37 billion, while Reality Labs revenue was $431 million.
* Reality Labs reported an operating loss of $4.62 billion for the quarter.
* Ad impressions across the Family of Apps increased 14% year-over-year, and the average price per ad increased 12% year-over-year.
* Family daily active people (DAP) averaged 3.60 billion for June 2026.

## Outlook
* Third quarter 2026 total revenue is expected to be in the range of $61-64 billion.
* Full year 2026 total expenses are expected to be in the range of $165-169 billion.
* 2026 capital expenditures, including principal payments on finance leases, are anticipated to be in the range of $130-145 billion.
* The tax rate for the remaining quarters of 2026 is expected to be between 15-17%.
* The company expects to deliver operating income this year that is above 2025 operating income.

## Capital and balance sheet
* Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities were $90.26 billion as of June 30, 2026.
* Long-term debt was $83.66 billion as of June 30, 2026.
* Capital expenditures, including principal payments on finance leases, were $31.08 billion for the quarter.
* Dividend and dividend equivalent payments were $1.35 billion.

## Watch items
* The impact of youth-related trials scheduled for this year in the U.S., which the company notes may result in a material loss.
* The status of the May 2026 headcount reduction, as approximately 8,000 employees impacted are still included in the reported headcount of 75,472.
* The effect of foreign currency, which is assumed to be an approximately 1% headwind to year-over-year total revenue growth in the third quarter.
