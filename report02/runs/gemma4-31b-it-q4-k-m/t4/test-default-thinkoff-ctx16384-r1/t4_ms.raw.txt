**Morgan Stanley reports record second quarter 2026 net revenues of $21.3 billion and EPS of $3.46.**

## Results
* Net revenues were $21.3 billion for the second quarter ended June 30, 2026, compared with $16.8 billion a year ago.
* Net income applicable to Morgan Stanley was $5.6 billion, or $3.46 per diluted share, compared with $3.5 billion, or $2.13 per diluted share, for the same period a year ago.
* The Firm reported a return on tangible common equity (non-GAAP) of 26.6%.
* The expense efficiency ratio was 65% for the first half of the year.
* Pre-tax income for the quarter was $7.3 billion.

## Segments and drivers
* Institutional Securities reported record net revenues of $11.0 billion, with Equity net revenues reaching $6.3 billion and Investment Banking net revenues at $2.4 billion.
* Wealth Management net revenues were $8.9 billion, supported by net new assets of $148 billion and a pre-tax margin of 30.5%.
* Investment Management reported net revenues of $1.6 billion, with assets under management (AUM) of $2.0 trillion.
* Institutional Securities' Equity performance was noted as having notable strength in Asia.

## Outlook
* The Board of Directors reauthorized a multi-year common equity share repurchase program of up to $20 billion beginning in the third quarter of 2026.
* The release provides no other guidance or outlook.

## Capital and balance sheet
* The Standardized Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) capital ratio was 14.8%.
* Tangible book value per share (non-GAAP) was $53.18, compared with $47.25 a year ago.
* The Firm repurchased $1.5 billion of its outstanding common stock during the quarter.
* The Board declared a quarterly dividend of $1.15 per share.

## Watch items
* Monitor the impact of the "workforce management action" from the first quarter of 2026, which impacted approximately 2% of the global workforce and resulted in $178 million in severance costs.
* Track the performance of corporate and commercial real estate loans, as these contributed to the quarter's provision for credit losses.
* Observe the sustainability of Wealth Management's net new assets, as just over half of the $148 billion represented inflows related to IPOs of certain clients in the Workplace channel.
