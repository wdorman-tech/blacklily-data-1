**Microsoft Cloud and AI strength fuels fourth quarter results.**

## Results
* Revenue for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, was $90.0 billion, an increase of 18% (17% in constant currency).
* Operating income for the quarter was $40.6 billion, an increase of 18%.
* Net income for the quarter was $35.8 billion on a GAAP basis (up 31%) and $35.3 billion on a non-GAAP basis (up 22%).
* Diluted earnings per share for the quarter was $4.81 on a GAAP basis (up 32%) and $4.74 on a non-GAAP basis (up 23%).
* For the full fiscal year 2026, revenue was $331.8 billion (up 18%) and net income was $133.7 billion on a GAAP basis (up 31%).

## Segments and drivers
* Intelligent Cloud revenue was $39.3 billion (up 32% reported, 31% in constant currency), with Azure and other cloud services revenue increasing 43%.
* Productivity and Business Processes revenue was $37.8 billion (up 14%), driven by Microsoft 365 Consumer cloud revenue (up 24% reported, 22% in constant currency) and LinkedIn revenue (up 12% reported, 10% in constant currency).
* More Personal Computing revenue decreased 4% (down 5% in constant currency) to $12.9 billion, impacted by a 10% decrease in XBOX content and services revenue and a 7% decrease in Windows OEM and Devices revenue.
* Microsoft Cloud revenue for the quarter was $59.3 billion, an increase of 27%.

## Outlook
* Microsoft will provide forward-looking guidance in connection with this quarterly earnings announcement on its earnings conference call and webcast.

## Capital and balance sheet
* Total assets as of June 30, 2026, were $758.376 billion, compared to $619.003 billion as of June 30, 2025.
* Total cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments were $76.843 billion as of June 30, 2026.
* The company returned $10.2 billion to shareholders via dividends and share repurchases in the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2026.

## Watch items
* Monitor the impact of investments in OpenAI, which resulted in a $4.963 billion increase to full-year 2026 net income.
* Evaluate the sustainability of AI adoption, noting that Microsoft 365 Copilot has reached over 30 million paid seats.
* Track the recovery of the More Personal Computing segment, specifically the declines in XBOX content and services and Windows OEM and Devices.
