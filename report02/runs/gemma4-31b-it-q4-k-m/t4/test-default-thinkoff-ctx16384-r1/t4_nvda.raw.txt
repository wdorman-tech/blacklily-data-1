**NVIDIA reports second quarter fiscal 2027 revenue of $96.2 billion, up 106% from a year ago.**

## Results
* Revenue for the second quarter ended July 26, 2026, was $96.2 billion, an increase of 18% from the previous quarter and 106% from a year ago.
* GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins were both 75.0% for the quarter.
* GAAP net income was $59.688 billion, while non-GAAP net income was $53.954 billion.
* GAAP diluted earnings per share were $2.46 and non-GAAP diluted earnings per share were $2.22.

## Segments and drivers
* Data Center revenue was $89.0 billion, up 117% from a year ago and 18% from the previous quarter.
* Edge Computing revenue was $7.2 billion, up 27% from a year ago and 13% from the previous quarter.
* The company announced the Vera Rubin platform is ramping into full production and unveiled NVIDIA Vera, the first CPU built for AI agents.
* NVIDIA established strategic partnerships with Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs, and KKR to mobilize over $500 billion of third-party capital for AI infrastructure buildout, subject to definitive agreements.

## Outlook
* For the third quarter of fiscal 2027, revenue is expected to be $108.0 billion, plus or minus 2%, with the company assuming no Data Center compute revenue from China.
* GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins for the third quarter of fiscal 2027 are expected to be 74.0%, plus or minus 50 basis points.
* GAAP and non-GAAP operating expenses for the third quarter of fiscal 2027 are expected to be approximately $9.2 billion and $9.0 billion, respectively.
* For the full year fiscal 2027, GAAP and non-GAAP tax rates are expected to be between 16.0% and 18.0%, excluding discrete items and material changes to the tax environment.

## Capital and balance sheet
* NVIDIA returned approximately $26.0 billion to shareholders through cash dividends and shares repurchased during the second quarter of fiscal 2027.
* As of the end of the second quarter, the company had approximately $99.0 billion remaining under its share repurchase authorization.
* Total assets were $320.272 billion and total liabilities were $91.288 billion as of July 26, 2026.

## Watch items
* Monitor the impact of the exclusion of Data Center compute revenue from China in the third quarter outlook.
* Track the progress of the Vera Rubin platform production and its deployment across partners such as Google Cloud and Microsoft Azure.
