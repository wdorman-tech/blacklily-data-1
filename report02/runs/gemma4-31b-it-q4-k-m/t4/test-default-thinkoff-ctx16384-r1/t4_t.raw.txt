**AT&T reports second-quarter 2026 results characterized by growth in Advanced Connectivity and reiterated long-term guidance.**

## Results
* Revenues totaled $31.6 billion, up 2.3% from the year-ago quarter.
* Diluted EPS from continuing operations was $0.66, compared to $0.62 in the year-ago quarter; adjusted EPS was $0.65, compared to $0.54 in the year-ago quarter.
* Operating income was $7.0 billion, while adjusted operating income was $7.5 billion.
* Adjusted EBITDA was $12.3 billion, up 5.2% year over year.
* Free cash flow (non-GAAP) was $4.7 billion, compared to $4.4 billion in the year-ago quarter.

## Segments and drivers
* Advanced Connectivity service revenue was $23.5 billion, up 5.1% year over year, with EBITDA (non-GAAP) of $12.0 billion, up 8.0%.
* The company added over 1 million Advanced Connectivity customers, including 646,000 total consumer and business internet net adds (367,000 fiber and 279,000 fixed wireless) and 432,000 postpaid phone net adds.
* Legacy segment revenues declined 25.9% year over year to $1.6 billion, driven by lower demand as the company decommissions its copper-based network.
* Latin America segment revenues increased 16.1% year over year to $1.2 billion, primarily driven by postpaid wireless subscriber growth and favorable foreign exchange rates.

## Outlook
* For full-year 2026, the company expects adjusted EPS of $2.25 to $2.35 and adjusted EBITDA growth in the 3% to 4% range.
* Long-term guidance for 2026-2028 includes annual capital investment (non-GAAP) in the $23 billion to $24 billion range and service revenue growth in the low-single-digit range annually.
* Free cash flow (non-GAAP) is projected at $18 billion+ in 2026, $19 billion+ in 2027, and $21 billion+ in 2028.

## Capital and balance sheet
* Total debt was $144.0 billion and net debt (non-GAAP) was $126.4 billion at the end of the second quarter.
* The company returned $4.1 billion to shareholders in the second quarter, including approximately $2.2 billion in common share repurchases.
* AT&T plans to return $45 billion+ to shareholders during 2026-2028, including approximately $10 billion in share repurchases during 2026.

## Watch items
* Monitor the progress of the copper-based network decommissioning, as Legacy EBITDA (non-GAAP) is expected to turn negative after 2027.
* Track the net debt-to-adjusted EBITDA ratio (non-GAAP), which the company expects to return to the 2.5x range within approximately three years following the closing of its transaction with EchoStar.
