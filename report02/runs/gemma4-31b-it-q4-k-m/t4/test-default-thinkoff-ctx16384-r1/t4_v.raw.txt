**Visa reports 14% net revenue growth for the fiscal third quarter of 2026.**

## Results
* Net revenue was $11.6 billion, an increase of 14% (or 13% on a constant-dollar basis).
* GAAP net income was $5.6 billion, or $2.97 per share.
* Non-GAAP net income was $6.3 billion, or $3.32 per share.
* GAAP operating expenses were $4.8 billion, a 19% increase over the prior year.
* Non-GAAP operating expenses increased 17% over the prior year.

## Segments and drivers
* Total processed transactions for the three months ended June 30, 2026, were 71.7 billion, a 10% increase over the prior year.
* Payments volume for the three months ended June 30, 2026, increased 10% over the prior year on a constant-dollar basis.
* Total cross-border volume on a constant-dollar basis increased 13% over the prior year, while cross-border volume excluding transactions within Europe increased 12% on a constant-dollar basis.
* Revenue by category for the quarter: Data processing revenue was $6.0 billion, service revenue was $4.9 billion, international transaction revenue was $3.9 billion, and other revenue was $1.5 billion.

## Outlook
* The release provides no guidance or outlook.

## Capital and balance sheet
* Cash, cash equivalents and investment securities were $13.9 billion at June 30, 2026.
* During the three months ended June 30, 2026, Visa repurchased approximately 14.5 million shares of class A common stock for $4.9 billion.
* The board of directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.670 per share of class A common stock on July 28, 2026.
* Remaining authorized funds for share repurchases were $28.4 billion as of June 30, 2026.

## Watch items
* Monitor the impact of litigation, as the company recorded a $237 million litigation provision for the quarter and deposited $250 million into its litigation escrow account on June 25, 2026.
* Track the trend of operating expenses, specifically personnel expenses, which were cited as a primary driver of the 19% increase in GAAP operating expenses.
* Observe the effects of the exchange offer for class B-1 and B-2 common stock settled on May 12, 2026.
