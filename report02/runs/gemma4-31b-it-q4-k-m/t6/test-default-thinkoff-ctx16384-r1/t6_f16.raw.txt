I am pleased to provide you with the second quarter update for the Preferred and Hybrid Income sleeve of your rollover IRA.

During the quarter, I made a few adjustments to your holdings to better manage "call risk," which is the possibility that an issuer may choose to buy back their securities before they mature. Specifically, I sold two fixed-to-floating bank preferreds that had first call dates in November 2026. I reinvested the $186,000 in proceeds into $25-par utility preferreds. These changes helped reduce the duration to worst from 5.0 years to 4.2 years.
