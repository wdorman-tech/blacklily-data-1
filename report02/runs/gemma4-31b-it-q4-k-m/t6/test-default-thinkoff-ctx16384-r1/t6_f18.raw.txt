Dear Adaora and Nils,

I am pleased to provide the second quarter report for Mateo’s North Carolina UTMA custodial account.

For the period from April 1 through June 30, 2026, the account earned a return of 3.7% net of fees (3.9% gross of fees). For further context, the comparison index for the age-based policy blend returned 4.1% during the same period. Year to date through June 30, the return is 5.3% net of fees, and the annualized return since inception on September 16, 2019, is 8.2% net of fees.

Regarding your question about whether a 70% equity allocation is too high with five years remaining until college, we have addressed this through the Education Glide Path strategy. On June 1, we took the annual glide path step, which reduced the equity target from 75% to 70%. As part of this process, we moved $14,000 from equities into Treasuries maturing in 2031 to earmark those funds for Mateo's first-year college costs. Following this June rebalance, the account's cash position is 2.5%.

I also have an update regarding your advisory fees. Following your request in May, which was approved in June, we have aggregated the UTMA account with your other accounts for breakpoint purposes. As a result, the fee rate for the UTMA account will decrease from the standalone rate of 1.00% per year to a household rate of 0.80% per year. This change takes effect on October 1, 2026, and will be reflected on the Q4 invoice.

Assets in a custodial account under the Uniform Transfers to Minors Act are the irrevocable property of the minor, pass to the minor's control at the age set by state law, and may reduce eligibility for need-based financial aid.
