  "quote": "All supervised persons, whether or not covered associates, must obtain the prior written approval of the CCO before making any political contribution to a candidate for or holder of federal, state or local office, a political party, or a political action committee, and before engaging in any fundraising activity. The CCO will approve contributions by covered associates only within the following limits: up to **$350 per election** to an official for whom the covered associate is entitled to vote, and up to **$150 per election** to an official for whom the covered associate is not entitled to vote. Contributions by a spouse or household member that are directed or funded by a supervised person are treated as contributions of the supervised person."
