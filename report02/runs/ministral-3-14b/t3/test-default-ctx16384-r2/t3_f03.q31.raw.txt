  "quote": "The Firm votes proxies for securities held in discretionary accounts, other than securities held in sleeves managed by third-party managers, unless the client has retained voting authority in writing. The Firm has engaged Stannard Proxy Services, Inc. to provide proxy research, vote recommendations under guidelines adopted by the Firm, and vote execution and recordkeeping. Third-party managers vote proxies for their sleeves under their own policies, which the Firm reviews in due diligence. Where the Firm has a conflict of interest in a vote, for example because the issuer appears on the Firm's Client-Affiliated Issuer List, the Firm votes in accordance with the recommendation of Stannard Proxy Services, Inc. without exercising discretion, and the Chief Compliance Officer documents the conflict. A family may direct the vote of securities held in its own accounts. Clients may obtain the Firm's proxy voting policies and their voting records from the Chief Compliance Officer."
