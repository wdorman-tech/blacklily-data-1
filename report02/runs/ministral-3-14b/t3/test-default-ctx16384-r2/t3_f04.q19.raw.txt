  "quote": "The Firm may effect a cross trade between two client accounts, in which one account sells a security to another, only when the portfolio manager has determined that the transaction is in the best interest of both accounts and consistent with each account's guidelines; the Chief Compliance Officer or the Deputy Chief Compliance Officer has approved the transaction in advance; and the transaction is executed at the current evaluated price supplied by the Firm's independent primary pricing service. Neither the Firm nor any affiliate receives a commission, markup or other compensation in connection with a cross trade. Cross trades are not effected for any account whose IMA or governing law prohibits them.\n\nThe Firm does not engage in principal transactions, in which the Firm or an affiliate buys a security from or sells a security to a client for its own account, and it does not effect agency cross transactions, because it has no affiliated broker-dealer."
