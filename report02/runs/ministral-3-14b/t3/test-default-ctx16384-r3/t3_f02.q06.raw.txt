  "quote": "As of December 31 of each year, and as of the effective date of any redemption, Sallowmere Partners GP, LLC receives an incentive allocation from each Class A capital account in the Flagship Fund equal to 20% of the net profits allocated to that capital account for the period, and from each Class F capital account equal to 15% of such net profits. Class E capital accounts bear no incentive allocation. The incentive allocation is subject to a high water mark: no incentive allocation is made from a capital account until prior net losses allocated to it have been recovered. There is no hurdle rate. For the Offshore Fund's investment, the incentive allocation is calculated on a look-through basis and allocated once, at the Flagship Fund level. The Adviser manages the Flagship Fund and the Co-Investment Fund side by side. The Flagship Fund pays a management fee and an annual incentive allocation, while the Co-Investment Fund pays no management fee and a lower carried interest on realized profits only."
