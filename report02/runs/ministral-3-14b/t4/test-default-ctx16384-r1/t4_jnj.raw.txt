**Johnson & Johnson Q2 2026: Strong Growth, Raised Guidance, and Pipeline Momentum**

## **Results**
- Reported sales of **$25.3 billion** in Q2 2026, up **6.6%** year-over-year, with **operational sales growth of 5.6%** and **adjusted operational sales growth of 5.7%**.
- Reported earnings per share (EPS) of **$2.27**, down **0.9%** from $2.29 in Q2 2025, while **adjusted EPS** rose **4.7%** to **$2.90** (excluding intangible amortization and special items).
- Free cash flow for Q2 2026 (year-to-date) was approximately **$8.7 billion**, up from **$6.2 billion** in Q2 2025.
- Net earnings of **$5.5 billion** in Q2 2026, essentially flat year-over-year (down **0.1%**).
- The company raised **2026 full-year guidance**, with **reported sales** now expected to reach **$101.1 billion** (midpoint, **7.3% growth**), and **adjusted EPS** guidance increased by **$0.13** to **$11.68** (midpoint, **8.2% growth**).

## **Segments and Drivers**
- **Innovative Medicine** delivered **7.8% reported sales growth**, driven by **DARZALEX, CARVYKTI, TECVAYLI, RYBREVANT/LAZCLUZE** (Oncology), **TREMFYA** (Immunology), and **SPRAVATO, CAPLYTA** (Neuroscience), offset partially by **STELARA** and **IMBRUVICA**.
- **MedTech** grew **4.5%**, led by **wound closure, biosurgery (Surgery), electrophysiology, Shockwave (Cardiovascular), contact lenses (Vision), and trauma (Orthopaedics)**.
- **U.S. sales** rose **7.3%** (operational), while **international sales** grew **5.7%** (reported), with **adjusted operational growth of 7.4%** in the U.S. and **3.5%** internationally.
- Key regulatory approvals included **TREMFYA** (psoriatic arthritis joint damage inhibition), **CAPLYTA** (schizophrenia relapse prevention), and **Dual Energy THERMOCOOL SMARTTOUCH SF** (cardiology platform).

## **Outlook**
- **2026 full-year guidance** now projects **operational sales of $100.6 billion** (midpoint, **6.8% growth**) and **adjusted operational EPS of $11.58** (midpoint, **7.3% growth**).
- No GAAP guidance provided for 2026 due to uncertainty in legal proceedings, acquisitions, and special items.
- Pipeline advancements include **RYBREVANT FASPRO** (head/neck cancer), **TECVAYLI + DARZALEX FASPRO** (multiple myeloma), and **OTTAVA** (upper abdominal procedures).

## **Capital and Balance Sheet**
- Free cash flow for Q2 2026 (YTD) was **~$8.7 billion**, reflecting strong operational cash generation.
- No specific debt or capital allocation details provided in the release.
- Recent investments include **$1 billion in U.S. vision manufacturing** and the **acquisition of Firefly Bio** to expand oncology pipeline.

## **Watch Items**
- **Pipeline execution risk**: The company’s guidance assumes continued success in **TECVAYLI, RYBREVANT, and IMAAVY**, but regulatory or commercial hurdles could impact growth.
- **Geographic mix**: International growth (**3.5% adjusted operational**) lags the U.S. (**7.4%**), raising questions about sustainability outside core markets.
- **Guidance assumptions**: The absence of GAAP guidance suggests volatility in **legal, acquisition, or special-item costs**—committee should monitor if these materialize.
