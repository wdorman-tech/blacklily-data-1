**JPMorgan Chase Reports $21.2B Net Income in Q2 2026, Driven by Record Revenue and Elevated Market Activity**

## **Results**
- **Net income** of **$21.2 billion** ($7.70 per share), up **41%** YoY, including **$4.6 billion** in net gains related to Visa shares and **$1.0 billion** in gains on equity investments.
- **Managed revenue** of **$58.0 billion**, up **27%** YoY, with **net interest income** of **$25.6 billion** (+10% YoY) and **noninterest revenue** of **$32.4 billion** (+45% YoY).
- **Return on common equity (ROE)** of **24%**, **return on tangible common equity (ROTCE)** of **29%**, and **ROTCE excluding significant items** of **23%**.
- **Credit costs** of **$2.5 billion**, with **net charge-offs** of **$2.4 billion** and a **$149 million** net reserve build.
- **Average loans** up **10% YoY** to **$1.5 trillion**, **average deposits** up **7% YoY** to **$1.5 trillion**, and **cash and marketable securities** of **$1.5 trillion**.

## **Segments and Drivers**
- **Consumer & Community Banking (CCB)** net income of **$5.3 billion**, up **3% YoY**, with **Card Services & Auto revenue** up **12%** YoY due to higher revolving balances and auto lease income.
- **Commercial & Investment Bank (CIB)** net income of **$9.7 billion**, up **46% YoY**, with **Markets revenue** up **35% YoY** (Equity Markets +86%) and **Investment Banking fees** up **30% YoY** to the highest level since 2021.
- **Asset & Wealth Management (AWM)** net income of **$2.0 billion**, up **33% YoY**, with **assets under management (AUM)** of **$5.1 trillion** (+18% YoY) and **$50 billion** in long-term AUM net inflows.
- **Corporate** net income of **$4.2 billion**, up **$2.5 billion** YoY, driven by **$4.5 billion** in noninterest revenue (excluding significant items), including **$4.6 billion** in Visa-related gains.

## **Outlook**
- No explicit guidance provided in the release.
- CEO Jamie Dimon noted **"a particularly favorable environment with elevated market activity"** but highlighted **"tectonic risks"** including geopolitical tensions, inflation, and fiscal deficits.
- Emphasized **"rigorous execution, years of consistent investment, and thoughtful capital deployment"** as key drivers of performance.

## **Capital and Balance Sheet**
- **Basel III CET1 capital ratios** of **14.1% (standardized)** and **14.2% (advanced)**, with **total loss-absorbing capacity** of **$590 billion** and **standardized RWA** of **$2.1 trillion**.
- **Book value per share** of **$133.01** (+9% YoY), **tangible book value per share** of **$113.35** (+10% YoY).
- **Capital distributions** included a **$4.0 billion** common dividend and **$6.2 billion** in common stock repurchases, with a **73% net payout ratio** over the last twelve months.

## **Watch Items**
- **Credit trends**: Net charge-offs in **Card Services** and **Wholesale** warrant monitoring, particularly as **net reserve builds** remain elevated.
- **Market volatility**: **Equity Markets revenue** surged **86% YoY**, but **Fixed Income Markets** grew only **6%**, suggesting sensitivity to client activity and macroeconomic conditions.
- **Deposit and loan growth**: **Average deposits** (+7% YoY) and **loans** (+10% YoY) are strong, but **net interest income growth** (+4% excluding Markets) reflects **lower rates**, requiring scrutiny of pricing power.
