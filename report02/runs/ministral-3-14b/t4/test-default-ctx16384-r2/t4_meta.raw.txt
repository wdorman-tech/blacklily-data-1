**Meta Q2 2026: Revenue Growth Amid Rising Costs and AI Investments**

## **Results**
- Revenue rose **28% YoY** to **$60.80 billion** (reported) and **$60.12 billion** on a **constant-currency basis**, driven by a **14% YoY increase in ad impressions** and a **12% YoY rise in average price per ad**.
- Costs and expenses surged **55% YoY** to **$42.03 billion**, including **$2.40 billion in legal charges** and **$1.18 billion in severance** from May 2026 headcount reductions.
- Operating income declined **8% YoY** to **$18.78 billion**, with an operating margin of **31%** (down from **43%** in Q2 2025).
- Net income fell **14% YoY** to **$15.85 billion**, while diluted EPS dropped **13% YoY** to **$6.18**.
- Free cash flow was **$784 million** (non-GAAP), down from **$8.55 billion** in Q2 2025.

## **Segments and Drivers**
- **Family of Apps (FoA)** revenue grew **28% YoY** to **$60.37 billion**, with advertising revenue at **$59.36 billion** (up **27% YoY**).
- **Reality Labs** revenue increased **16% YoY** to **$431 million**, though it remains unprofitable, with an operating loss of **$4.62 billion**.
- **Family daily active people (DAP)** reached **3.60 billion**, up **3% YoY**.
- **Research and development (R&D) costs** rose **67% YoY** to **$21.66 billion**, reflecting AI and immersive technology investments.

## **Outlook**
- **Q3 2026 revenue guidance** of **$61–64 billion**, assuming a **1% FX headwind**.
- **Full-year 2026 expenses** now expected in the range of **$165–169 billion**, up from prior guidance, due to legal charges.
- **Capital expenditures** for 2026 revised to **$130–145 billion**, with no change to the expectation of **operating income above 2025 levels**.

## **Capital and Balance Sheet**
- **Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities** totaled **$90.26 billion** as of June 30, 2026.
- **Long-term debt** stood at **$83.66 billion**, up from **$58.74 billion** at year-end 2025.
- **Dividend and dividend equivalent payments** were **$1.35 billion** for the quarter.

## **Watch Items**
- **Legal and regulatory risks**: The company faces ongoing scrutiny on youth-related issues, with pending trials that could result in material losses.
- **Expense growth vs. revenue growth**: Costs rose **55% YoY** while revenue grew **28%**, raising questions about sustainability of operating margins.
- **Reality Labs profitability**: Despite revenue growth, the segment remains deeply unprofitable, with no indication of near-term improvement.
