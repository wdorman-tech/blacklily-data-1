**Morgan Stanley Q2 2026: Record Revenues, Strong Segment Growth, and Capital Accretion**

## **Results**
- Net revenues rose to **$21.3 billion** (vs. **$16.8 billion** in Q2 2025), marking a record quarterly figure.
- Net income applicable to Morgan Stanley was **$5.6 billion**, or **$3.46 per diluted share** (vs. **$3.5 billion**, or **$2.13 per diluted share** in Q2 2025).
- Pre-tax income reached **$7.3 billion**, up from **$4.6 billion** in Q2 2025.
- The **expense efficiency ratio** improved to **65%** (vs. **71%** in Q2 2025).
- **Return on tangible common equity (ROTCE)** was **26.6%** (vs. **18.2%** in Q2 2025).

## **Segments and Drivers**
- **Institutional Securities** generated **$11.0 billion** in net revenues (vs. **$7.6 billion** in Q2 2025), driven by:
  - **Equity revenues of $6.3 billion** (up **69%** YoY), reflecting strong client engagement and market conditions.
  - **Investment Banking revenues of $2.4 billion** (up **58%** YoY), with gains in advisory, equity underwriting, and fixed income underwriting.
- **Wealth Management** reported **$8.9 billion** in net revenues (vs. **$7.8 billion** in Q2 2025), with:
  - **$148 billion in net new assets**, including **$39.1 billion in fee-based asset flows**.
  - A **pre-tax margin of 30.5%**, supported by asset management fees, transactional revenues, and net interest income.
- **Investment Management** recorded **$1.6 billion** in net revenues (vs. **$1.55 billion** in Q2 2025), with **$7.5 billion in long-term net flows**.

## **Outlook**
- No forward-looking guidance was provided in the release.
- The Board reauthorized a **$20 billion share repurchase program** beginning in Q3 2026.
- The **Standardized Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) capital ratio** was **14.8%** (vs. **15.0%** in Q2 2025).

## **Capital and Balance Sheet**
- **Book value per share** increased to **$67.80** (vs. **$61.59** in Q2 2025).
- **Tangible book value per share** rose to **$53.18** (vs. **$47.25** in Q2 2025).
- The firm repurchased **$1.5 billion** of common stock in Q2 2026.

## **Watch Items**
- **Credit losses and lending exposure**: The **provision for credit losses** was **$98 million** (vs. **$196 million** in Q2 2025), but the release notes growth in corporate loans and commercial real estate assessments.
- **Wealth Management asset flows**: While net new assets were a record **$148 billion**, long-term net flows in Investment Management were **$7.5 billion** (down from **$12.2 billion** in Q2 2025), raising questions about sustainability.
- **Compensation and expense trends**: Compensation expense rose **$997 million YoY** (to **$8.2 billion**), while non-compensation expenses increased **$931 million** (to **$5.7 billion**), warranting monitoring of cost discipline amid revenue growth.
