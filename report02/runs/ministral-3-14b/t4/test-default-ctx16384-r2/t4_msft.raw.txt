**Microsoft Cloud and AI Growth Drives Record Q4 Revenue of $90.0B**

## **Results**
- Revenue of **$90.0 billion** (up **18%** year-over-year, **17%** in constant currency).
- Operating income of **$40.6 billion** (up **18%**).
- GAAP net income of **$35.8 billion** (up **31%**), non-GAAP net income of **$35.3 billion** (up **22%**).
- GAAP diluted EPS of **$4.81** (up **32%**), non-GAAP diluted EPS of **$4.74** (up **23%**).
- Non-GAAP figures exclude OpenAI investment impacts.

## **Segments and Drivers**
- **Microsoft Cloud revenue** of **$59.3 billion** (up **27%**), with **Azure and other cloud services** growing **43%**.
- **Productivity and Business Processes** revenue of **$37.8 billion** (up **14%**), including **Microsoft 365 Commercial cloud** (up **14%**) and **Microsoft 365 Consumer cloud** (up **24%**).
- **Intelligent Cloud** revenue of **$39.3 billion** (up **32%**), driven by **Azure** and **Dynamics 365** (up **13%**).
- **More Personal Computing** revenue of **$12.9 billion** (down **4%**), with **Windows OEM and Devices** (down **7%**) and **XBOX content and services** (down **10%**).

## **Outlook**
- No forward-looking guidance provided in the release; management will discuss outlook during the earnings call.

## **Capital and Balance Sheet**
- Returned **$10.2 billion** to shareholders via dividends and share repurchases in Q4.
- Cash and cash equivalents decreased to **$20.9 billion** from **$30.2 billion** at the end of Q3.
- Total assets increased to **$758.4 billion** from **$619.0 billion** year-over-year.

## **Watch Items**
- **OpenAI investment impact**: Non-GAAP adjustments highlight volatility from OpenAI-related gains/losses (e.g., **$3.2 billion gain from Anthropic** in Q4).
- **Cloud and AI momentum**: Azure revenue surpassed **$100 billion** for the first time, and **Microsoft 365 Copilot** reached **30 million paid seats**—key metrics for future growth.
- **Segmental performance divergence**: While **Cloud and Productivity** segments grew strongly, **More Personal Computing** declined, warranting further analysis of underlying trends.
