**Visa Reports Strong Q3 2026 Growth in Payments Volume and Revenue**

## **Results**
- **Net revenue** of **$11.6 billion**, up **14%** year-over-year (13% on a constant-dollar basis).
- **GAAP net income** of **$5.6 billion** ($2.97 per share), a **7%** increase; **non-GAAP net income** of **$6.3 billion** ($3.32 per share), up **8%**.
- **Payments volume** rose **10%** (constant-dollar), with **cross-border volume** (excluding intra-Europe) up **12%** and **total cross-border volume** up **13%**.
- **Processed transactions** reached **71.7 billion**, a **10%** increase.
- **Share repurchases and dividends** totaled **$6.2 billion** in the quarter.

## **Segments and Drivers**
- **Service revenue** (recognized based on prior-quarter payments volume) grew **14%** to **$4.9 billion**.
- **Data processing revenue** increased **17%** to **$6.0 billion**, while **international transaction revenue** rose **6%** to **$3.9 billion**.
- **Other revenue** surged **45%** to **$1.5 billion**, and **client incentives** climbed **18%** to **$4.7 billion**.
- **GAAP operating expenses** rose **19%** to **$4.8 billion**, primarily due to personnel costs; **non-GAAP operating expenses** increased **17%**.

## **Outlook**
- No forward guidance provided in the release.

## **Capital and Balance Sheet**
- **Cash, cash equivalents, and investment securities** totaled **$13.9 billion** as of June 30, 2026.
- **$28.4 billion** remaining authorized for share repurchases.
- **$250 million** deposited into litigation escrow in June 2026, reducing share count equivalent to repurchases at an average price of **$333.42**.

## **Watch Items**
- **Litigation provisions**: Q3 included **$237 million** for interchange multidistrict litigation (MDL) and **$9 million** in equity investment losses, compared to **$615 million** in prior-year litigation provisions.
- **Operating expense growth**: **19% GAAP increase** (17% non-GAAP) driven by personnel and marketing costs—monitor whether this trend persists and impacts margins.
- **Cross-border volume strength**: **12% growth** in cross-border (excluding intra-Europe) and **13% total**—track geopolitical or regulatory shifts that could affect this segment.
