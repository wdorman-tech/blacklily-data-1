**Chevron Reports Record Q2 2026 Earnings of $12.1B, Driven by Production Growth and Cost Discipline**

## **Results**
- Reported earnings of **$12.1 billion** ($6.11 per share, diluted) for Q2 2026, including a **$230 million asset sale gain** and **$86 million pension settlement costs**; foreign currency effects reduced earnings by **$49 million**.
- **Adjusted earnings** were **$12.0 billion** ($6.06 per share, diluted), excluding non-operational items.
- **Cash flow from operations** reached **$22.6 billion**, with **adjusted free cash flow** of **$15.4 billion** and **free cash flow** of **$18.1 billion**.
- **Return on capital employed (ROCE)** was **21.4%** for Q2 2026, up from **6.2%** in Q2 2025.
- **Total debt was reduced by $8.4 billion** in Q2 2026, improving the **debt-to-CFFO ratio to 0.8x** (from 1.2x in Q2 2025).

## **Segments and Drivers**
- **Upstream earnings** of **$8.18 billion** (vs. **$2.73 billion** in Q2 2025), driven by **20% YoY production growth** (4.07 MBOED vs. 3.396 MBOED), including contributions from **legacy Hess assets** and **Permian Basin/Gulf of Mexico growth**.
- **U.S. upstream earnings** rose to **$3.54 billion** (vs. **$1.42 billion** in Q2 2025), with **liquids production at 1.49 MBD** (up from 1.22 MBD) and **liquids realization at $70.80/BBL** (vs. $47.77/BBL).
- **Downstream earnings** of **$4.87 billion** (vs. **$737 million** in Q2 2025), with **U.S. refinery crude throughput at a record 1.07 MBD** (97% utilization) and **higher refined product margins**.
- **International downstream earnings** improved to **$2.46 billion** (vs. **$333 million** in Q2 2025), though **refinery inputs declined 10% YoY** due to **Middle East conflict disruptions**.

## **Outlook**
- No forward guidance provided in the release.
- **Structural cost reductions** of **$3 billion** achieved **six months ahead of schedule**, with **$1.5 billion in Hess-related synergies** delivered **ahead of target**.
- **20-year power agreement** signed with Microsoft for **2.67 GW of behind-the-meter power** in West Texas.

## **Capital and Balance Sheet**
- **Capital expenditures** of **$4.5 billion** (vs. **$3.7 billion** in Q2 2025), with **Hess-related spend** partially offset by **lower Permian Basin capex**.
- **Net debt-to-CFFO ratio** improved to **0.6x** (from 1.0x in Q2 2025).
- **Dividend declared** at **$1.78 per share**, payable September 10, 2026.

## **Watch Items**
- **Geopolitical risks** impacting **Middle East production and refining operations**—monitor curtailments in the **Partitioned Zone (Saudi Arabia/Kuwait)** and **Iraq developments**.
- **Hess integration progress**—track **synergy realization** and **cost savings** against the **$3–4 billion target by year-end 2026**.
- **Working capital fluctuations**—Q2 2026 saw a **$2.9 billion increase**, contrasting with **$1.7 billion decrease in Q1 2026**; assess sustainability of cash flow trends.
