**Alphabet Q2 2026: Revenue Growth Accelerates Amid AI-Driven Expansion**

## **Results**
- **Revenues** rose **24% YoY** to **$119.8 billion** (23% in constant currency), marking the **12th consecutive quarter of double-digit growth**.
- **Google Services revenues** increased **15% YoY** to **$94.5 billion**, driven by **17% growth in Search & other**, **15% in subscriptions, platforms, and devices**, and **13% in YouTube ads**.
- **Google Cloud revenues** surged **82% YoY** to **$24.8 billion**, led by AI infrastructure and enterprise solutions.
- **Operating income** grew **30% YoY** to **$40.8 billion**, with an **operating margin expansion** of **2 percentage points to 34%**.
- **Net income available to common stockholders** increased **298% YoY** to **$112.1 billion**, with **EPS rising 294% to $9.11**, primarily due to a **$98.0 billion net gain on equity securities**.

## **Segments and Drivers**
- **Google Services** accounted for **$94.5 billion** in revenue, with **Search & other** (17% growth) and **YouTube ads** (13% growth) as key contributors.
- **Google Cloud** saw **82% YoY revenue growth**, with **Gemini Enterprise adoption** reaching **90% of Fortune 100 companies** and **Gemini models processing 22 billion API tokens per minute**.
- **YouTube** attracted **1.7 billion unique viewers** for FIFA World Cup 2026 content, highlighting its role as a global event platform.
- **AI-driven features** (e.g., Gemini App with **950 million monthly active users**) and **security solutions** (e.g., **Gemini 3.5 Flash Cyber**) contributed to growth in enterprise and consumer segments.

## **Outlook**
- No formal guidance provided in the release.
- CEO Sundar Pichai emphasized **AI investments as a key driver**, with **enterprise AI solutions** and **infrastructure scaling** as focal points.
- **No mention of revenue or earnings expectations** for future periods.

## **Capital and Balance Sheet**
- **Cash and cash equivalents** increased to **$55.9 billion** (up from **$30.7 billion** at year-end 2025).
- **Equity capital raise** of **$49.6 billion** in June 2026, with proceeds allocated to **AI infrastructure and global compute scaling**.
- **Senior unsecured notes issuance** of **$20.3 billion** in Q2 2026 for general corporate purposes.

## **Watch Items**
- **Equity securities volatility**: The **$98.0 billion net gain** in Q2 2026 (vs. **$2.7 billion in Q2 2025**) significantly impacted net income—future fluctuations could materially affect earnings.
- **Google Cloud growth trajectory**: While **82% YoY growth** is strong, the committee should monitor **sustainability of enterprise AI demand** and **competitive pressures** in cloud infrastructure.
- **AI R&D investments**: Alphabet-level activities (e.g., **shared AI research costs**) are not allocated to segments—clarity on **cost efficiency** and **ROI** of AI spending will be critical.
