**The Home Depot Reports Q2 FY2026 Results; Reaffirms Full-Year Guidance**

## **Results**
- **Net sales** of **$47.9 billion** for Q2 FY2026, up **$2.6 billion (5.7%)** year-over-year.
- **Net earnings** of **$4.8 billion**, or **$4.79 per diluted share**, compared with **$4.6 billion ($4.58 per diluted share)** in Q2 FY2025.
- **Adjusted diluted earnings per share** of **$4.92**, up from **$4.68** in Q2 FY2025.
- **Comparable sales** increased **1.7%**, with U.S. comparable sales up **1.3%**.
- **Gross profit** of **$16.1 billion**, a **6.5% increase** from **$15.1 billion** in Q2 FY2025.

## **Segments and Drivers**
- **Customer transactions** declined **0.8%**, while **average ticket** rose **2.8%** in Q2 FY2026.
- **Operating income** of **$6.8 billion**, up **4.3%** from **$6.55 billion** in Q2 FY2025.
- **Adjusted operating income** of **$7.0 billion**, a **4.8% increase** from **$6.7 billion** in Q2 FY2025.
- **Net interest expense** of **$524 million**, down **4.7%** from **$550 million** in Q2 FY2025.

## **Outlook**
- **Fiscal 2026 guidance reaffirmed**, including:
  - **Total sales growth** of **2.5% to 4.5%**.
  - **Comparable sales growth** of **flat to 2.0%**.
  - **Adjusted diluted EPS growth** of **flat to 4.0%** from **$14.69** in FY2025.
- **No new store guidance provided beyond the previously stated ~15 new stores for FY2026.**
- **No outlook on share repurchases or dividend policy.**

## **Capital and Balance Sheet**
- **Cash and cash equivalents** of **$2.1 billion** as of Q2 FY2026, down from **$2.8 billion** at Q2 FY2025.
- **Total liabilities** of **$92.8 billion**, up from **$89.4 billion** in Q2 FY2025.
- **Capital expenditures** of **$1.7 billion** for the first six months of FY2026, consistent with prior period.

## **Watch Items**
- **Customer transaction trends**: Q2 saw a **0.8% decline** in transactions, while average ticket rose **2.8%**—monitor whether this shift reflects structural demand changes or temporary factors.
- **Inventory levels**: Merchandise inventories increased to **$26.8 billion** from **$24.8 billion**—assess whether this aligns with sales trends or signals potential overstocking.
- **Interest expense stability**: Net interest expense declined **4.7%**, but long-term debt remains high (**$43.9 billion**); track debt refinancing or cost fluctuations.
