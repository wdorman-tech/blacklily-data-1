**IBM Reports Q2 2026 Results with Updated Full-Year Guidance**

## **Results**
- **Revenue**: $17.2B (GAAP), up 1% year-over-year; **Software** grew 5% ($7.8B), **Consulting** was flat ($5.3B), **Infrastructure** declined 7% ($3.8B), and **Financing** rose 12% ($186M).
- **Profitability**: GAAP pre-tax income of $2.5B (14.4% margin), down 5% YoY; **operating (non-GAAP) pre-tax income** of $3.3B (19.2% margin), up 3% YoY.
- **Cash Flow**: Free cash flow of $2.5B (down $0.3B YoY); net cash from operations of $2.6B (up $0.9B YoY).
- **Dividend**: $1.69/share declared (August 10 record date), maintaining consecutive quarterly dividends since 1916.
- **Acquisitions**: $10.5B invested in acquisitions year-to-date.

## **Segments and Drivers**
- **Software strength**: Hybrid Cloud (Red Hat) +11%, Data +19% (18% constant currency), Automation +4% (3% constant currency); **Transaction Processing** declined 8% (9% constant currency).
- **Infrastructure mixed**: Distributed Infrastructure +37% (backlog of ~$500M), but **IBM Z** fell 42% and Hybrid Infrastructure declined 10%.
- **Consulting flat**: Strategy & Technology and Intelligent Operations both up 1% constant currency.
- **Financing growth**: +12% YoY ($186M), driven by client demand.

## **Outlook**
- **Full-year guidance**: Constant-currency revenue growth of **4–5%**, with currency expected to be neutral; **free cash flow** to increase by ~$1B YoY.
- **Margin expansion**: Pre-tax income margin improvement expected for the full year.
- **No specific segment or product guidance** provided beyond existing trends.

## **Capital and Balance Sheet**
- **Cash position**: $8.2B in cash, restricted cash, and marketable securities (down $6.3B from year-end 2025).
- **Debt**: Total debt of $62.0B (up $0.7B YTD), including $13.0B in IBM Financing debt.
- **Shareholder returns**: $1.6B returned via dividends in Q2.

## **Watch Items**
- **IBM Z decline**: 42% drop in revenue—does this reflect structural shift or cyclical weakness?
- **Distributed Infrastructure backlog**: $500M order book—can execution sustain growth amid broader infrastructure softness?
- **Quantum and AI investments**: $10B+ quantum commitment and Lightwell adoption—will these drive long-term revenue or remain R&D-heavy?
