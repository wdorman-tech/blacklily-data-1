**NVIDIA Reports Record Q2 FY2027 Revenue of $96.2B, Up 106% YoY**

## **Results**
- Revenue of **$96.2 billion** (up **106% YoY**, **18% QoQ**).
- Data Center revenue of **$89.0 billion** (up **117% YoY**, **18% QoQ**).
- GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins both **75.0%** (up **2.6 pts YoY**).
- GAAP net income of **$59.7 billion** (up **126% YoY**, **2% QoQ**).
- Returned **$26.0 billion** to shareholders via repurchases and dividends.

## **Segments and Drivers**
- **Data Center** drove **92.5% of revenue** ($89.0B), with **Vera Rubin** ramping into full production at partners (CoreWeave, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, Oracle Cloud).
- **Edge Computing** revenue of **$7.2 billion** (up **27% YoY**, **13% QoQ**).
- **New product introductions**: NVIDIA Vera CPU, Groq 3 LPX inference accelerator, and **NVIDIA DSX** platform for AI factory design.
- **Strategic partnerships** with Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, and others to mobilize **$500B+** in third-party capital for AI infrastructure.

## **Outlook**
- **Q3 FY2027 revenue guidance**: **$108.0 billion** (±2%), excluding China Data Center compute.
- **GAAP/non-GAAP gross margins** expected at **74.0%** (±50 bps).
- **GAAP/non-GAAP operating expenses** projected at **$9.2B/$9.0B**, respectively.

## **Capital and Balance Sheet**
- **$99.0 billion** remaining under share repurchase authorization.
- **$22.4 billion** in cash and cash equivalents (up from **$10.6B** prior quarter).
- **$32.4 billion** in long-term debt (up from **$7.5B** prior quarter).

## **Watch Items**
- **China Data Center exclusion**: Guidance assumes **no revenue from China**—monitor potential reintegration risks.
- **Operating expense growth**: **$8.4B QoQ increase** (10% QoQ) raises scrutiny on sustainability amid guidance for **$9.2B in Q3**.
- **Tax rate volatility**: FY2027 guidance range (**16.0–18.0%**) excludes discrete items—track actuals for material deviations.
