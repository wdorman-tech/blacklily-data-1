**AT&T Q2 2026: Growth in Advanced Connectivity Drives Revenue and Profitability**

## **Results**
- **Revenues** totaled **$31.6 billion**, up **2.3%** year-over-year (YoY), driven by **Advanced Connectivity** growth.
- **Adjusted EPS*** was **$0.65**, up from **$0.54** YoY; **diluted EPS** was **$0.66**, up from **$0.62** YoY.
- **Adjusted EBITDA*** was **$12.3 billion**, up **5.2%** YoY; **operating income** was **$7.0 billion**, up from **$6.5 billion** YoY.
- **Free cash flow*** was **$4.7 billion**, up from **$4.4 billion** YoY.
- **Capital expenditures** were **$5.7 billion**; **capital investment*** was **$6.1 billion**.

## **Segments and Drivers**
- **Advanced Connectivity** revenues grew **5.1%** YoY to **$23.5 billion**, with **$2.9 billion** in **Advanced Home Internet** revenue (up **27.3%** YoY).
- **Net adds** totaled **1.0 million Advanced Connectivity customers**, including **367,000 fiber** and **279,000 fixed wireless** subscribers.
- **Legacy segment** revenues declined **25.9%** YoY to **$1.6 billion**, reflecting continued copper network decommissioning.
- **Latin America** revenues rose **16.1%** YoY to **$1.2 billion**, driven by foreign exchange and wireless subscriber growth.

## **Outlook**
- **Full-year 2026 guidance** remains unchanged, with **adjusted EBITDA*** growth of **3%–4%** and **adjusted EPS*** of **$2.25–$2.35**.
- **Capital investment*** expected at **$23–$24 billion annually** through 2028.
- **$45 billion+** in shareholder returns (dividends + repurchases) planned for **2026–2028**, including **$10 billion in repurchases in 2026**.

## **Capital and Balance Sheet**
- **Net debt** was **$126.4 billion** at June 30, 2026.
- **$4.1 billion** returned to shareholders in Q2, including **$2.2 billion in share repurchases**.
- **Free cash flow*** of **$4.7 billion** in Q2, with **$18 billion+** projected for 2026.

## **Watch Items**
- **Legacy segment profitability**: EBITDA* is expected to turn negative after 2027 as copper network costs decline, but timing depends on regulatory approvals.
- **Fiber expansion progress**: AT&T remains on track to reach **40 million fiber locations by year-end 2026** and **60 million by 2030**, but execution risks persist.
- **Capital allocation**: Accelerated share repurchases (**$10 billion in 2026**) may impact liquidity if free cash flow falls short of projections.
