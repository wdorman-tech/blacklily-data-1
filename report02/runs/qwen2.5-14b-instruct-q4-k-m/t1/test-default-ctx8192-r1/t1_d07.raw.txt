{
  "section": "6.1 Quarterly Repurchase Offers",
  "content": "Limited Partners have no right to redeem or withdraw from the Fund. The General Partner currently intends, but is not obligated, to cause the Fund to offer to repurchase Interests quarterly, as of the last Business Day of each calendar quarter (each, a 'Repurchase Date'). The Fund sends each Limited Partner written notice of each offer (a 'Repurchase Offer Notice') not less than 20 Business Days before the Notice Deadline. The General Partner may decline to make an offer for any quarter if it determines in good faith that doing so is in the best interests of the Fund."
}
