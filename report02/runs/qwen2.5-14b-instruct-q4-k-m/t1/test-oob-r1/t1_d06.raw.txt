Based on the provided documents, the key points regarding the designation of Advisory Committee members and the Most Favored Nation (MFN) election are as follows:

### Advisory Committee Member Designation
- **Right to Designate:** The General Partner may grant the right to designate a member of the Advisory Committee, but this right is not included in the Most Favored Nation (MFN) election process.
- **Exclusion from MFN:** According to Section 12.2(b)(ii) of the Agreement, the right to designate a member of, or an observer to, the Advisory Committee is explicitly excluded from the MFN election process.

### Most Favored Nation Election
- **Eligibility:** MFN Eligible Partners are those whose Capital Commitment (aggregated with those of their Affiliates and of other investors advised by the same discretionary investment adviser) is equal to or greater than $25,000,000.
- **Election Process:** Within 30 days after receiving a copy or summary of each Side Letter entered into with other Limited Partners or Parallel Fund partners, an MFN Eligible Partner may elect to obtain the benefit of any right granted in those Side Letters to an investor whose Capital Commitment is equal to or less than that of the MFN Eligible Partner.
- **Exclusions from MFN Election:** The MFN election does not extend to:
  - Rights arising from the legal, tax, regulatory, or policy status or jurisdiction of the investor.
  - The right to designate a member of, or an observer to, the Advisory Committee.
  - Rights relating to co-investment opportunities.
  - Consents to Transfers to Affiliates or to the manner of delivery of notices and reports.
  - Any right granted to a GP-Affiliated Limited Partner.

### Summary
- **Designation Right:** The General Partner retains the right to designate Advisory Committee members, and this right is not subject to the MFN election process.
- **MFN Scope:** The MFN election process allows eligible partners to obtain benefits from Side Letters but excludes specific rights, including the right to designate Advisory Committee members.

These provisions ensure that the General Partner maintains control over the composition of the Advisory Committee while providing certain benefits to larger investors through the MFN process.
