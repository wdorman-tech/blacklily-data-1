### Summary of Key Points from the Brackenholt Senior Lending Fund, LP Partnership Agreement

#### 1. **Introduction and Purpose**
- **Fund Structure:** The Brackenholt Senior Lending Fund, LP is a Delaware limited partnership formed to engage in the business of making senior secured loans to middle-market companies.
- **Investment Strategy:** The Fund aims to generate income and capital appreciation through a diversified portfolio of senior secured loans.
- **Parties Involved:** The General Partner is Brackenholt Credit GP, LLC, and the Adviser is Brackenholt Capital Advisors, LLC.

#### 2. **Capital Contributions and Interests**
- **Capital Contributions:** Limited Partners contribute capital to the Fund in exchange for Interests, which are divided into Classes A and B.
- **Interest Classes:** Class A Interests are subject to a Management Fee and an Incentive Fee, while Class B Interests are subject to a Management Fee but not an Incentive Fee.
- **Minimum Investment:** The minimum initial investment is $1,000,000, and subsequent investments must be at least $100,000.

#### 3. **Management and Advisory Agreement**
- **Adviser:** The Adviser is responsible for managing the Fund's investments and operations.
- **Management Fee:** A Management Fee of 1.25% of the net asset value of each Limited Partner's Capital Account is charged annually.
- **Incentive Fee:** An Incentive Fee is charged annually based on the Fund's performance, calculated as 20% of the net increase in the net asset value of each Limited Partner's Capital Account.

#### 4. **Repurchase Program**
- **Repurchase Requests:** Limited Partners can submit Repurchase Requests to the General Partner, subject to certain conditions and limitations.
- **Repurchase Limitations:** The General Partner may reject Repurchase Requests if they would cause the Fund to exceed its leverage limits or if the Fund does not have sufficient cash to effect the repurchase.

#### 5. **Credit Facility**
- **Borrowing:** The Fund has a Credit Facility with Sallowfield Bank, N.A. to borrow up to $100,000,000.
- **Interest and Fees:** The Fund pays interest and fees on borrowings under the Credit Facility, which are used to finance investments and pay expenses.

#### 6. **Tax Considerations**
- **Tax Status:** The Fund is treated as a partnership for U.S. federal income tax purposes.
- **Taxable Income:** Each Limited Partner is taxed on its distributive share of Fund income, whether or not distributed.
- **ERISA Considerations:** The General Partner limits investment by benefit plan investors to less than 25% of the value of each class of equity interests to avoid plan asset status.

#### 7. **Transfers, Term, and Reports**
- **Transfers:** No Interest may be transferred without the prior written consent of the General Partner.
- **Term and Dissolution:** The Fund has no fixed term and continues until dissolved, which can occur upon the determination of the General Partner, the withdrawal or bankruptcy of the General Partner, or a decree of judicial dissolution.
- **Reports:** Limited Partners receive monthly Capital Account statements, quarterly portfolio letters, annual audited financial statements, and Schedules K-1.

#### 8. **Appendices**
- **Directory:** Provides contact information for the Fund, General Partner, Adviser, Administrator, Independent Auditor, Tax Preparer, Custodian, Credit Facility Agent and Lender, Independent Valuation Firm, Counsel, and Cayman Islands Counsel.
- **Executed Side Letter:** A letter agreement between the General Partner, Adviser, and Hallowby College, providing specific terms for the Investor's subscription, including a reduced Management Fee, early repurchase provisions, and reporting requirements.

### Key Takeaways
- **Investment Strategy:** The Fund focuses on senior secured loans to middle-market companies.
- **Fees and Incentives:** Limited Partners pay a Management Fee and an Incentive Fee based on the Fund's performance.
- **Repurchase Program:** Limited Partners can request repurchases of their Interests, subject to certain limitations.
- **Credit Facility:** The Fund has a significant borrowing capacity to finance its investments.
- **Tax Implications:** Each Limited Partner is taxed on its distributive share of Fund income.
- **ERISA Compliance:** The Fund limits investment by benefit plan investors to avoid plan asset status.
- **Reporting Requirements:** Limited Partners receive regular financial and performance reports.

This summary provides a comprehensive overview of the key aspects of the Brackenholt Senior Lending Fund, LP Partnership Agreement, highlighting the investment strategy, fee structure, and operational and regulatory considerations.
