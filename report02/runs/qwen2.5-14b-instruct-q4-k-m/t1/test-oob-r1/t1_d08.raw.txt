### Summary of Key Points from the Memorandum

#### 1. **Introduction and Overview**
   - **Fund Name:** Tarrenfield Oakes Fund III, L.P.
   - **Sponsor:** Tarrenfield Oakes Real Estate Partners III, L.P.
   - **Investment Manager:** Tarrenfield Oakes Investment Management, LLC
   - **Objective:** To acquire, renovate, and operate value-add multifamily and industrial properties in the Sunbelt and Mountain states.
   - **Target IRR:** 12% to 14% net of fees.
   - **Capital Commitments:** $312.5 million as of the Initial Closing on April 30, 2026.
   - **Final Closing Date:** Expected by April 30, 2027.

#### 2. **Investment Strategy**
   - **Property Types:** Multifamily and industrial properties.
   - **Geographic Focus:** Sunbelt and Mountain states.
   - **Investment Criteria:** Properties with significant value-add potential, including interior renovations and leasing improvements.
   - **Leverage:** Substantial property-level leverage, some at floating rates.
   - **Management:** Active management by the Sponsor and its affiliates.

#### 3. **Organizational Structure**
   - **General Partner:** Tarrenfield Oakes Fund III GP, L.L.C.
   - **Limited Partners:** Up to 100 individuals or entities.
   - **Key Persons:** Michael Oakes, Marguerite Soames, and other senior executives.
   - **Investment Manager:** Tarrenfield Oakes Investment Management, LLC.
   - **Administrator:** Ostrander Pell Fund Services, LLC.
   - **Independent Auditor:** Lindenhall Crewe LLP (replaced Wexcombe Adair LLP).

#### 4. **Subscription Facility**
   - **Amount:** $75 million (expandable to $120 million).
   - **Lender:** Ellery Plains Bank, N.A.
   - **Maturity Date:** July 2, 2028.
   - **Purpose:** To defer capital calls and improve reported internal rates of return.

#### 5. **Management Fees and Expenses**
   - **Management Fee:** 1.5% of Adjusted Capital Commitments.
   - **Incentive Fee:** 20% of profits above a 12% preferred return.
   - **Expenses:** Covered by the Fund, including legal, accounting, and administrative costs.

#### 6. **Capital Contributions and Distributions**
   - **Capital Contributions:** Limited Partners must contribute their Capital Commitments in up to 10 installments.
   - **Distributions:** Made quarterly, first to Limited Partners, then to the General Partner.
   - **Return of Capital:** Limited Partners receive their Capital Contributions before the General Partner receives a return of capital.

#### 7. **Investment Management and Control**
   - **Investment Decisions:** Made by the General Partner, with oversight from the Advisory Committee.
   - **Key Persons:** Michael Oakes and Marguerite Soames.
   - **Advisory Committee:** Provides advice and oversight but does not have decision-making authority.

#### 8. **Confidentiality and Use of Information**
   - **Confidentiality:** Limited Partners must keep all information about the Fund and its Investments confidential.
   - **Use of Information:** Limited Partners may use information for their own benefit but must comply with legal requirements.

#### 9. **Banking and Lenders**
   - **Operating Accounts:** Maintained with U.S. commercial banks.
   - **Subscription Facility:** Expected from banks, life insurance companies, agency lenders, and debt funds.

#### 10. **Risk Factors**
   - **Illiquidity:** Interests cannot be withdrawn or redeemed and have no public market.
   - **Value-Add Execution:** Returns depend on successful renovations and achieving projected rents.
   - **Leverage and Interest Rates:** Leverage magnifies losses, and rising rates increase debt service and may prevent refinancing.
   - **Concentration:** Fund concentrates in a few property types and regional markets.
   - **Environmental, Physical, and Insurance Risks:** Exposure to environmental liabilities and natural disasters.
   - **Reliance on the Sponsor:** Limited Partners cannot evaluate Investments in advance or participate in management.
   - **Subscription Facility and Defaults:** Defers capital calls but may require additional funding upon default.
   - **Regulatory and Tax Changes:** Changes in zoning, rent regulation, property tax assessment practices, and federal tax law may reduce returns.
   - **Limited Operating History:** Prior Funds operated in different economic conditions.

#### 11. **Tax and ERISA Considerations**
   - **Partnership Status:** Expected to be treated as a partnership for U.S. federal income tax purposes.
   - **Tax-Exempt and Non-U.S. Investors:** Part of income may be unrelated business taxable income for tax-exempt investors.
   - **ERISA:** Intended to operate as a "real estate operating company" or limit benefit plan investors to less than 25% of each class of equity interests.
   - **State and Local Taxes:** Limited Partners may be required to file returns and pay taxes in states where properties are located.

#### 12. **Reports and Valuation**
   - **Quarterly Reports:** Delivered within 60 days after each of the first three fiscal quarters.
   - **Annual Financial Statements:** Prepared under U.S. generally accepted accounting principles and audited by the independent auditor.
   - **Investment Valuations:** Quarterly fair value and annual appraisals by an independent appraiser.

#### 13. **Recent Developments**
   - **Initial Closing:** Occurred on April 30, 2026, with $312.5 million in Capital Commitments.
   - **Investments:** Acquired a multifamily property in Mesa, Arizona, and contracted to acquire a light industrial property in Aurora, Colorado.
   - **Subscription Facility:** Entered into a $75 million revolving facility with Ellery Plains Bank, N.A.
   - **Change of Independent Auditor:** Lindenhall Crewe LLP replaced Wexcombe Adair LLP as the independent auditor.

   - **Administrator:** Ostrander Pell Fund Services, LLC.
   - **Independent Auditor:** Lindenhall Crewe LLP.
