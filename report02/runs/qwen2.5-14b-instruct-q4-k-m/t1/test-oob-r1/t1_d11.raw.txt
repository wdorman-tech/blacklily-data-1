### Summary of Changes Effective July 1, 2026

The General Partner has amended the terms of the Fund, and the Partnership Agreement has been updated accordingly, effective July 1, 2026 (the "Effective Date"). Limited Partners were notified in writing on May 15, 2026, and the General Partner waived the lock-up, the early redemption charge, and the notice period for redemptions as of June 30, 2026, for requests made on or before June 15, 2026. Any inconsistencies with the changes outlined in this section should be disregarded.

#### 14.1 Redemption Notice Period

The notice period for redemptions has been extended from 30 days to 45 days. A written redemption request for any Redemption Date on or after August 31, 2026, must be received by the Administrator at least 45 days before that Redemption Date. The 10-day notice period for redemptions following a Key Person Event under Section 4.4 remains unchanged.

#### 14.2 Fund-Level Gate

The Fund now imposes a fund-level gate as of each Redemption Date on or after July 31, 2026. If aggregate redemption requests of all Limited Partners as of any Redemption Date exceed 15% of the net asset value of the Fund as of that Redemption Date, determined before giving effect to those redemptions, the General Partner may reduce each request pro rata so that aggregate redemptions equal 15% of that net asset value. The unsatisfied portion of each request is carried forward to the next Redemption Date without priority over requests first made for that date and remains subject to the gate, unless withdrawn by written notice received by the Administrator within 10 Business Days after the Redemption Date as of which it was reduced. The gate applies to all redemptions, including redemptions following a Key Person Event, and is measured at the level of the Fund, not for each Limited Partner individually. The statements in Sections 1.12, 7.4, and 9.12 that the Fund does not limit aggregate redemptions no longer apply.

#### 14.3 Independent Auditor

The General Partner has appointed Marbeck Pennycott LLP as the independent auditor of the Fund, and the board of the Master Fund has appointed Marbeck Pennycott (Cayman), a CIMA-approved auditor, as the auditor of the Master Fund, effective from the Effective Date, beginning with the fiscal year ending December 31, 2026. Brennick Tallow LLP completed its audit of the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, and the change did not result from any disagreement on accounting principles, financial statement disclosure, or audit scope. References in this Memorandum to Brennick Tallow LLP or Brennick Tallow (Cayman) as auditor should be read as references to Marbeck Pennycott LLP or Marbeck Pennycott (Cayman), respectively. Marbeck Pennycott LLP is located at 90 Canal Terrace, Stamford, Connecticut 06902.

#### 14.4 Other Terms

Except as set forth in this Section 14, the terms described in this Memorandum, including the Management Fee, the Incentive Allocation, the lock-up, and the monthly Redemption Dates, remain unchanged.
