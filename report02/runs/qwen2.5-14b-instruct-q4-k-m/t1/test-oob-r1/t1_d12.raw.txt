### Summary of Key Changes in Supplement No. 2

**Supplement No. 2** to the Confidential Offering Memorandum of Vessary Point Commodities Fund Ltd. dated 1 March 2024, as supplemented by Supplement No. 1 dated 2 December 2024, includes several important updates:

1. **Reduction of Class B Management Fee:**
   - **Effective Date:** 1 July 2025
   - **New Rate:** One-twelfth of 1.00% (1.00% per annum) of the Net Asset Value of the Class B Shares
   - **Calculation and Payment:** Monthly in advance as described in Section 4.1 of the Memorandum
   - **Impact:** This reduction applies to all Series of Class B Shares, including those issued before the effective date.

2. **Director Changes:**
   - **Resignation:** Callum Aldersey resigned as a Director effective 30 April 2025.
   - **Appointment:** Ffion Latchmere was appointed as an independent Director effective 1 May 2025.
   - **Background:** Ms. Latchmere is a chartered accountant resident in the Cayman Islands and was head of fund services at a Cayman Islands trust company until 2021.
   - **Compensation:** She receives the independent Director's fee described in Section 4.4 of the Memorandum.

3. **Fund Management Assets:**
   - As of 31 March 2025, the Investment Manager managed approximately US$2.6 billion.

### Key Points to Note

- **Fee Reduction:** The Management Fee for Class B Shares is significantly reduced, which is beneficial for Shareholders holding Class B Shares.
- **Director Transition:** The change in Directors ensures continued oversight and governance of the Fund.
- **No Action Required:** Shareholders are not required to take any action in connection with this Supplement No. 2.

### Additional Information

- **Supplement No. 2** prevails over any inconsistencies with the original Memorandum or Supplement No. 1.
- The Fund continues to operate under the terms and conditions outlined in the Memorandum and previous supplements, with the specified changes taking precedence.

For further details, please refer to the full text of Supplement No. 2 and the original Memorandum.
