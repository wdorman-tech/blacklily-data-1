The provided partnership agreement outlines several key provisions regarding the structure and operation of the partnership, including withdrawal rights, transfers, dissolution, and various administrative and legal matters. Here's a summary of the main points:

### Withdrawal Rights
- **No Redemption Rights:** Limited Partners do not have the right to redeem or withdraw their interests before the dissolution and winding up of the Partnership.
- **Required Withdrawal:** The General Partner may require a Limited Partner to withdraw if it determines that the Limited Partner's continued participation would cause the Partnership's assets to be "plan assets" under ERISA, violate applicable law, or cause a material adverse regulatory or tax consequence.

### Transfers
- **Consent Requirement:** No Limited Partner may transfer any part of its interest without the prior written consent of the General Partner, which may be withheld in its sole discretion.
- **Tax and Regulatory Conditions:** The General Partner will not consent to a transfer that would cause the Partnership to be a publicly traded partnership under Section 7704 of the Code, require registration under the Investment Company Act of 1940, or cause Benefit Plan Investors to hold 25% or more of any class of Interests.

### Dissolution and Liquidation
- **Events of Dissolution:** The Partnership will dissolve upon the expiration of its term, the election of the General Partner with the consent of a Majority in Interest of the Limited Partners, the withdrawal, removal, or bankruptcy of the General Partner, the disposition of all Investments after the Investment Period, or judicial dissolution under Section 17-802 of the Act.
- **Winding Up:** Upon dissolution, the General Partner or a liquidator appointed by a Majority in Interest of the Limited Partners will wind up the Partnership and use commercially reasonable efforts to dispose of all Investments within two years. Proceeds will be applied to creditors, reserves, and then to the Partners.

### Books, Reports, and Service Providers
- **Books and Reports:** The Partnership will keep its books in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles and deliver unaudited quarterly reports and audited annual financial statements to Limited Partners.
- **Independent Auditors:** The financial statements will be audited annually by an independent certified public accounting firm selected by the General Partner.
- **Administrator:** The General Partner has engaged Orrelby Fund Services (US) LLC to maintain the register of Partners and the Capital Accounts, process drawdowns and distributions, perform anti-money laundering procedures, and prepare Partner statements.
- **Custody:** The Partnership does not engage a prime broker and holds its interests and cash in compliance with Rule 206(4)-2 under the Investment Advisers Act of 1940.

### Side Letters
- **Side Letters:** The General Partner may enter into side letters with Limited Partners to establish rights under, or supplement or alter, this Agreement.
- **Most Favored Nations Election:** Limited Partners with a Capital Commitment of $50,000,000 or more may elect to benefit from any side letter provisions granted to other Limited Partners with a lower Capital Commitment, subject to certain exclusions.

### Miscellaneous
- **Entire Agreement:** This Agreement, along with subscription agreements and any side letters, constitutes the entire agreement of the Partners and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings.
- **Amendments:** This Agreement may be amended with the consent of the General Partner and a Majority in Interest of the Limited Partners, except for certain amendments that require the consent of each Limited Partner adversely affected.
- **Legal Counsel:** Haskett Voorlan LLP acted as counsel to the Partnership, the General Partner, and the Manager in connection with the formation of the Partnership and the offering of Interests.
- **ERISA and Tax Matters:** The General Partner will use reasonable best efforts to ensure that Benefit Plan Investors hold less than 25% of the total value of each class of Interests and will hold Investments through blocker corporations to avoid unrelated business taxable income or effectively connected income.
- **Governing Law:** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, and each Partner submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware and waives trial by jury.

This summary provides an overview of the key provisions in the partnership agreement, focusing on withdrawal rights, transfers, dissolution, and various administrative and legal matters.
