  "quote": "The Valuation Committee (the \"Committee\") consists of four voting members: the Chief Financial Officer, the Controller, the Director of Operations and the President of Quenby Holt Trust Company. The Chief Investment Officer, the Head of Private Investments and the Chief Compliance Officer attend as non-voting members. Non-voting members may present information and recommendations but may not vote, and the Head of Private Investments is excused from deliberation on any position for which he or she prepared the valuation recommendation.\n\nA quorum consists of three voting members, one of whom must be the Chief Financial Officer or the Controller."
