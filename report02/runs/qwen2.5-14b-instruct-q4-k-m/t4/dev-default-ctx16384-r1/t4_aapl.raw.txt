**Apple reports third quarter results with record revenue and EPS**

## Results
- Revenue for the quarter was $109.4 billion, a 16% increase year over year.
- Gross margin was 50.1%, including a favorable impact of approximately 2 percentage points from tariff refunds.
- Diluted earnings per share was $2.02, a 29% increase year over year, including a favorable impact of $0.11 from tariff refunds.

## Segments and drivers
- iPhone revenue set a new June quarter record at $54.252 billion, up 21% year over year.
- Mac revenue also set a new June quarter record at $10.352 billion, up 29% year over year.
- Services revenue reached $30.739 billion, a 12% increase year over year.

## Outlook
- No specific guidance or outlook was provided in the earnings release.

## Capital and balance sheet
- The company declared a cash dividend of $0.27 per share, payable on August 13, 2026, to shareholders of record as of August 10, 2026.
- Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities totaled $62.399 billion as of June 27, 2026.

## Watch items
- The impact of tariff refunds on both gross margin and EPS should be monitored, as it contributed significantly to the reported figures.
- The company’s installed base of active devices reached a new all-time high, but specific details on the growth rate or segment breakdown were not provided.
