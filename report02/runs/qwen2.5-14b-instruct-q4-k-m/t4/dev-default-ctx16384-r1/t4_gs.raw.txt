**The Goldman Sachs Group, Inc. Reports Second Quarter 2026 Earnings**

## Results
- **Net Revenues**: $20.34 billion for 2Q26, a 39% increase from 2Q25 and 18% from 1Q26.
- **Net Earnings**: $6.63 billion for 2Q26, a 78% increase from 2Q25 and 18% from 1Q26.
- **EPS**: $20.98 for 2Q26, compared to $10.91 for 2Q25 and $17.55 for 1Q26.
- **ROE**: 23.5% for 2Q26, compared to 21.7% for the first half of 2026.

## Segments and Drivers
- **Global Banking & Markets**: Net revenues of $15.52 billion for 2Q26, a 53% increase from 2Q25 and 22% from 1Q26, driven by higher investment banking fees and FICC revenues.
- **Asset & Wealth Management**: Net revenues of $4.60 billion for 2Q26, a 20% increase from 2Q25 and 13% from 1Q26, primarily due to higher management and other fees and investments gains.
- **Platform Solutions**: Net revenues of $221 million for 2Q26, a 64% decrease from 2Q25 and 46% from 1Q26, reflecting markdowns related to the Apple Card loan portfolio.

## Outlook
- **Dividend Increase**: Quarterly dividend increased to $5.00 per common share for 3Q26 from $4.50.
- **Capital Return**: Returned $5.36 billion of capital to common shareholders in 2Q26, including $4.00 billion in share repurchases and $1.36 billion in dividends.
- **No Guidance Provided**: The release does not provide forward-looking guidance or outlook for future periods.

## Capital and Balance Sheet

- **Supplementary Leverage Ratio (SLR)**: 4.3% for 2Q26, compared to 4.7% for 1Q26.
