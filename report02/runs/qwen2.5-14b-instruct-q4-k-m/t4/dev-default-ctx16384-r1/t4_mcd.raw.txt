**McDonald's Reports Second Quarter 2026 Results**

## Results
- Global Systemwide sales increased 5% (4% in constant currencies) to $37 billion for the quarter.
- Global comparable sales increased 1.3%, with U.S. comparable sales up 0.8% and International Operated Markets up 1.5%.
- Diluted earnings per share was $3.32, a 6% increase (5% in constant currencies).
- Pre-tax charges of $52 million were recorded for the quarter, primarily related to restructuring charges.

## Segments and drivers
- U.S. comparable sales growth was driven by positive check growth, including favorable product mix, partly offset by negative comparable guest counts.
- International Operated Markets saw positive comparable sales growth, led by Germany, Australia, and the U.K., partly offset by France.
- International Developmental Licensed Markets reported positive comparable sales, with Japan leading the growth, while China saw negative comparable sales.

## Outlook
- No specific guidance or outlook was provided in the earnings release.

## Capital and balance sheet
- Free cash flow was not disclosed in the release.
- No specific capital allocation or balance sheet changes were mentioned.

## Watch items
- The committee should monitor the impact of restructuring charges on future earnings.
- The committee should inquire about the strategic implications of Skye Anderson's appointment as President of McDonald’s USA.
- The committee should track the performance of the U.S. market under the new leadership, given the opportunity to raise the bar in the largest market.
