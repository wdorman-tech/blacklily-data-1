**ExxonMobil Reports Second-Quarter 2026 Earnings of $14.5 Billion**

## Results
- **Adjusted EPS of $3.52**: Up from $2.09 in the first quarter.
- **Record Permian Production**: Exceeding all competitors with a 9% CAGR through 2030.
- **Fifth Guyana FPSO Launch**: Production startup planned for 4Q26, increasing capacity by 250 Kbd.
- **Record Diesel Production**: Based on current asset basis, leveraging internal data available to 2014.
- **Cumulative Structural Cost Savings**: $16.3 billion, more than all other IOCs combined.

## Segments and Drivers
- **Upstream**: Earnings of $9.189 billion, up from $6.265 billion in the first quarter, driven by strong reliability and record Permian production.
- **Energy Products**: Earnings of $4.099 billion, up from $2.799 billion in the first quarter, supported by structural cost savings and optimized assets.
- **Chemical Products**: Earnings of $1.214 billion, up from $110 million in the first quarter, benefiting from North American feed advantage and performance chemical margins.
- **Specialty Products**: Earnings of $969 million, up from $651 million in the first quarter, driven by higher basestock margins and growth in high-value products.

## Outlook
- **No Specific Guidance Provided**: The company did not provide specific financial guidance for future periods.
- **Capital Expenditures**: Planned investments are 20% higher than the nearest IOC, with $13.0 billion invested year-to-date.
- **Dividend and Share Repurchases**: Declared a third-quarter dividend of $1.03 per share, payable on September 10, 2026.

## Capital and Balance Sheet
- **Cash Flow**: Free cash flow of $17.2 billion, up from $2.7 billion in the first quarter.
- **Capital Expenditures**: Total cash capital expenditures of $12.974 billion year-to-date, up from $12.539 billion in the same period last year.
- **Shareholder Distributions**: Distributed $9.4 billion, including $4.3 billion in dividends and $5.1 billion in share repurchases.

## Watch Items
- **Middle East Disruptions**: Impact on earnings and production needs to be monitored.
- **Structural Cost Savings**: Ongoing efforts to maintain and extend cost savings should be closely watched.
- **Regulatory and Policy Changes**: Potential impacts on future investment opportunities and emissions reduction plans.
