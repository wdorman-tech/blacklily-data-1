**Alphabet Inc. Reports Q2 2026 Revenue Growth of 24%**

## Results
- Consolidated revenues increased 24% to $119.8 billion, with 23% growth in constant currency.
- Google Services revenues grew 15% to $94.5 billion, driven by 17% growth in Google Search & other and 15% in subscriptions, platforms, and devices.
- Google Cloud revenues surged 82% to $24.8 billion, fueled by demand for AI infrastructure and solutions.
- Operating income rose 30% to $40.8 billion, with an operating margin of 34%.

## Segments and drivers
- Google Search & other revenues increased 17% to $63.3 billion, reflecting strong performance in AI features and search query growth.
- YouTube ads revenues grew 13% to $11.1 billion, supported by high viewership during major events like the FIFA World Cup 2026.
- Google Cloud revenues expanded 82% to $24.8 billion, driven by enterprise AI solutions and infrastructure.

## Outlook
- No specific guidance provided for future periods.

## Capital and balance sheet
- Issued $49.6 billion in equity to scale AI infrastructure and global compute.
- Entered into an ATM Program to sell up to $40.0 billion of Class A and Class C stock for tax obligations.
- Issued senior unsecured notes for $20.3 billion to fund general corporate purposes.
