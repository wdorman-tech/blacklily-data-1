**IBM Reports Second-Quarter Results and Updates Full-Year Guidance**

## Results
- **Revenue**: Second-quarter revenue was $17.2 billion, up 1% year-over-year.
- **Gross Profit**: Gross profit was $9.9 billion, down 1% year-over-year.
- **Pre-tax Income**: Pre-tax income was $2.5 billion, down 5% year-over-year.
- **Net Income**: Net income was $2.2 billion, down 2% year-over-year.

## Segments and Drivers
- **Software**: Revenue was $7.8 billion, up 5% year-over-year. Key drivers included Hybrid Cloud (Red Hat) up 11%, Data up 19%, and Automation up 4%.
- **Consulting**: Revenue was $5.3 billion, flat year-over-year, with both Strategy and Technology and Intelligent Operations segments up 1% at constant currency.
- **Infrastructure**: Revenue was $3.8 billion, down 7% year-over-year, driven by a 42% decline in IBM Z, partially offset by a 37% increase in Distributed Infrastructure.

## Outlook
- **Revenue**: The company expects full-year constant currency revenue growth in the range of 4% to 5%.
- **Free Cash Flow**: IBM anticipates free cash flow to increase by about $1 billion year-over-year for the full year.

## Capital and Balance Sheet
- **Cash Flow**: Second-quarter free cash flow was $2.5 billion, down $0.3 billion year-over-year.
- **Debt**: Total debt, including IBM Financing debt, was $62.0 billion, up $0.7 billion year-to-date.

## Watch Items
- **Currency Impact**: The company expects currency to be neutral to growth for the full year, but the impact of currency fluctuations on quarterly results should be monitored.
- **Segment Performance**: The significant decline in IBM Z revenue and the strong growth in Distributed Infrastructure suggest a need to monitor the strategic shift in the company's infrastructure business.
