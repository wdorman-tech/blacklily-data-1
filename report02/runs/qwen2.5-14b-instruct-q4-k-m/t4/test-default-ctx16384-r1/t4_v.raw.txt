**Visa Inc. Reports Fiscal Third Quarter 2026 Results**

## Results
- **Revenue Growth**: Net revenue increased by 14% to $11.6 billion.
- **Earnings**: GAAP net income was $5.6 billion, or $2.97 per share, and non-GAAP net income was $6.3 billion, or $3.32 per share.
- **Volume Metrics**: Payments volume grew by 10%, cross-border volume excluding intra-Europe transactions increased by 12%, and total cross-border volume rose by 13%.
- **Share Repurchases and Dividends**: Visa repurchased $4.9 billion worth of shares and declared a quarterly dividend of $0.670 per share.

## Segments and Drivers
- **Service Revenue**: Service revenue increased by 14% to $4.9 billion.
- **Data Processing Revenue**: Data processing revenue grew by 17% to $6.0 billion.
- **International Transaction Revenue**: International transaction revenue increased by 6% to $3.9 billion.
- **Other Revenue**: Other revenue rose by 45% to $1.5 billion.

## Outlook
- **No Specific Guidance**: The company did not provide specific financial guidance for future periods.

## Capital and Balance Sheet
- **Cash and Investments**: Cash, cash equivalents, and investment securities totaled $13.9 billion at the end of the quarter.
- **Share Repurchase Program**: Visa has $28.4 billion remaining in its share repurchase authorization.

## Watch Items
- **Litigation Escrow**: Visa deposited $250 million into its litigation escrow account, impacting earnings per share.
- **Regulatory Environment**: Visa noted the impact of complex and evolving global regulations on its future operations.
