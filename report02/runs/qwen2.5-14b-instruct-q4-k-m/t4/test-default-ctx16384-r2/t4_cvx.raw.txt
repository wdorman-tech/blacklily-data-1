**Chevron Reports Second Quarter 2026 Earnings of $12.1 Billion**

## Results
- Reported earnings of $12.1 billion for the second quarter of 2026, up from $2.5 billion in the same period last year.
- Adjusted earnings were $12.0 billion, with a return on capital employed of 21 percent.
- Foreign currency effects decreased earnings by $49 million.
- Cash flow from operations was $22.6 billion, compared to $8.6 billion in the second quarter of 2025.

## Segments and Drivers
- U.S. upstream earnings were $3.5 billion, up from $1.4 billion in the second quarter of 2025, driven by higher liquids realizations and sales volumes.
- International upstream earnings were $4.6 billion, up from $1.3 billion in the second quarter of 2025, due to higher sales volumes and liquids realizations.
- U.S. downstream earnings were $2.4 billion, up from $404 million in the second quarter of 2025, primarily due to higher margins on refined product sales.
- International downstream earnings were $2.5 billion, up from a loss of $1.0 billion in the second quarter of 2025, due to higher margins on refined product sales and favorable timing effects.

## Outlook
- No specific guidance provided for future periods.

## Capital and Balance Sheet
- Capital expenditures were $4.5 billion for the quarter, up from $3.7 billion in the second quarter of 2025.
- Total debt was reduced by $8.4 billion in the quarter, bringing total debt to $37.1 billion.

## Watch Items
- The impact of foreign currency effects on earnings should be monitored, as they decreased earnings by $49 million in the quarter.
- The company's ability to maintain its structural cost reduction targets and achieve additional synergies from the Hess acquisition should be closely watched.
