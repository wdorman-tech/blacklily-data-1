**The Home Depot Reports Second Quarter 2026 Earnings**

## Results
- Second quarter sales increased to $47.9 billion, up 5.7% from $45.3 billion in the same period last year.
- Comparable sales grew by 1.7%, with U.S. comparable sales increasing by 1.3%.
- Net earnings for the quarter were $4.8 billion, compared to $4.6 billion in the second quarter of 2025.
- Adjusted diluted earnings per share were $4.92, up from $4.68 in the prior year.

## Segments and drivers
- Broad-based demand across the business contributed to the strong performance.
- Comparable sales growth was driven by a 2.8% increase in the average ticket, partially offset by a decline in customer transactions.

## Outlook
- The company reaffirms its fiscal 2026 guidance, expecting total sales growth of approximately 2.5% to 4.5%.
- Comparable sales growth is forecasted to be flat to 2.0%.
- Adjusted diluted earnings per share is expected to grow flat to 4.0% from $14.69 in fiscal 2025.

## Capital and balance sheet
- Capital expenditures are expected to be approximately 2.5% of total sales.
- The company plans to open approximately 15 new stores during fiscal 2026.

## Watch items
- The impact of tariffs and trade policy changes on costs and pricing strategies.
- The company's ability to manage inventory levels and maintain on-shelf availability in a dynamic environment.
- The potential for fluctuations in fuel and energy costs affecting profitability.
