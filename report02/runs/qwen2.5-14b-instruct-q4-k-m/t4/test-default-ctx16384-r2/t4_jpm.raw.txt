**JPMorgan Chase Reports Second-Quarter 2026 Net Income of $21.2 Billion**

## Results
- **Net income**: $21.2 billion, up 41% year-over-year (YoY), or up 13% excluding significant items.
- **Net income excluding significant items**: $16.9 billion.
- **Revenue**: $57.3 billion reported, $58.0 billion managed, up 27% YoY, or up 15% excluding significant items.
- **Credit costs**: $2.5 billion, with $2.4 billion net charge-offs and a $149 million net reserve build.

## Segments and drivers
- **Consumer & Community Banking (CCB)**: Net income of $5.3 billion, up 3% YoY; net revenue of $20.3 billion, up 8% YoY, driven by higher asset management fees and deposit-related fees.
- **Commercial & Investment Bank (CIB)**: Net income of $9.7 billion, up 46% YoY; net revenue of $24.9 billion, up 27% YoY, driven by higher Investment Banking fees and Markets revenue.
- **Asset & Wealth Management (AWM)**: Net income of $2.0 billion, up 33% YoY; net revenue of $6.9 billion, up 19% YoY, driven by strong net inflows and higher market levels.

## Outlook
- No specific guidance provided in the earnings release.

## Capital and balance sheet
- **Common equity Tier 1 (CET1) ratio**: 14.1% standard, 14.2% advanced.
- **Total Loss-Absorbing Capacity (TLAC)**: $590 billion.
- **Cash and marketable securities**: $1.5 trillion.

## Watch items
- **Significant items**: The earnings release includes significant items such as a $4.6 billion net gain related to Visa shares and $1.0 billion of gains on certain equity investments, which should be excluded when assessing core performance.
- **Credit quality**: The provision for credit losses was $2.5 billion, with $2.4 billion net charge-offs and a $149 million net reserve build, indicating ongoing monitoring of credit quality is necessary.
