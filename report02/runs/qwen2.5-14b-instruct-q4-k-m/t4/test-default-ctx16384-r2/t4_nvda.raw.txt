**NVIDIA Reports Q2 FY27 Revenue of $96.2 Billion, Up 106% Year-Over-Year**

## Results
- Revenue for Q2 FY27 was $96.2 billion, up 18% from Q1 FY27 and 106% from Q2 FY26.
- Data Center revenue was $89.0 billion, up 18% from Q1 FY27 and 117% from Q2 FY26.
- GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins were both 75.0%.
- GAAP and non-GAAP diluted earnings per share were $2.46 and $2.22, respectively.

## Segments and Drivers
- Data Center segment revenue grew significantly, driven by strong demand for AI infrastructure.
- Edge Computing revenue was $7.2 billion, up 13% from Q1 FY27 and 27% from Q2 FY26.
- The company announced several new products and partnerships, including the NVIDIA Vera Rubin platform and NVIDIA Vera CPU.

## Outlook
- Revenue for Q3 FY27 is expected to be $108.0 billion, plus or minus 2%.
- GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins are expected to be 74.0%, plus or minus 50 basis points.
- GAAP and non-GAAP operating expenses are expected to be approximately $9.2 billion and $9.0 billion, respectively.

## Capital and Balance Sheet
- NVIDIA returned approximately $26.0 billion to shareholders through share repurchases and dividends in Q2 FY27.
- As of the end of Q2 FY27, the company had approximately $99.0 billion remaining under its share repurchase authorization.
- The company expects GAAP and non-GAAP tax rates to be between 16.0% and 18.0% for FY27.

## Watch Items
- Monitor the impact of the company's significant revenue growth on its ability to maintain gross margins and operating expenses.
- Keep an eye on the company's guidance for Q3 FY27, particularly the assumption that there will be no Data Center compute revenue from China.
- Consider the potential risks associated with the company's reliance on third-party manufacturing and supply chain disruptions.
