**Johnson & Johnson reports Q2 2026 results, raises 2026 outlook**

## Results
- Reported sales grew 6.6% to $25.3 billion in Q2 2026.
- Adjusted EPS increased to $2.90, up 4.7% from the prior year.
- Free cash flow estimated at ~$8.7 billion, a significant increase from $6.2 billion in Q2 2025.

## Segments and drivers
- **Innovative Medicine**: Operational sales grew 6.8%, driven by strong performance in Oncology, Immunology, and Neuroscience.
- **MedTech**: Operational sales increased 3.6%, with growth in Surgery, Cardiovascular, Vision, and Orthopaedics.

## Outlook
- Raised 2026 guidance for estimated reported sales to $101.1 billion, a 7.3% increase.
- Adjusted EPS guidance increased to $11.68, a 8.2% rise from the previous guidance.

## Capital and balance sheet
- Free cash flow estimated at ~$8.7 billion for the year-to-date 2026.
- No specific capital allocation or balance sheet details provided in the release.

## Watch items
- Committee should monitor the impact of divestitures and acquisitions on future operational growth.
- Questions should be raised about the company’s ability to meet the raised guidance, particularly in light of the ongoing regulatory and competitive landscape.
