**Meta Reports 28% Q2 2026 Revenue Growth**

## Results
- **Revenue**: $60.80 billion, up 28% year-over-year.
- **Net Income**: $15.85 billion, down 14% year-over-year.
- **Operating Margin**: 31%, down from 43% in Q2 2025.
- **Free Cash Flow**: $784 million, down from $8.55 billion in Q2 2025.

## Segments and Drivers
- **Family of Apps (FoA)**: Revenue of $60.37 billion, up 28% year-over-year.
- **Reality Labs**: Revenue of $431 million, up 16% year-over-year.
- **Advertising Revenue**: $59.36 billion, up 27% year-over-year, driven by a 14% increase in ad impressions and a 12% increase in average price per ad.

## Outlook
- **Q3 2026 Revenue**: Expected to be in the range of $61-64 billion.
- **Full-Year 2026 Expenses**: Expected to be in the range of $165-169 billion, including $2.4 billion in legal charges.
- **Capital Expenditures**: Expected to be in the range of $130-145 billion.

## Capital and Balance Sheet
- **Capital Expenditures**: $31.08 billion for Q2 2026.
- **Cash, Cash Equivalents, and Marketable Securities**: $90.26 billion as of June 30, 2026.
- **Long-Term Debt**: $83.66 billion as of June 30, 2026.

## Watch Items
- **Legal Proceedings**: The company has $2.4 billion in charges related to legal proceedings, impacting expenses.
- **Headcount Reduction**: A May 2026 headcount reduction, with 8,000 employees impacted, will no longer be reflected in headcount by the end of Q3 2026.
