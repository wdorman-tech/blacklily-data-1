**Morgan Stanley Reports Second Quarter 2026 Record Revenues and EPS**

## Results
- **Revenue and Profit Growth**: Net revenues increased to $21.3 billion from $16.8 billion, and net income applicable to Morgan Stanley rose to $5.6 billion from $3.5 billion.
- **EPS and ROTCE**: Earnings per diluted share were $3.46 compared to $2.13, and return on tangible common equity (ROTCE) was 26.6% compared to 18.2%.
- **Expense Efficiency**: The expense efficiency ratio improved to 65% from 71%.
- **Capital Position**: The Standardized Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) capital ratio was 14.8%.

## Segments and Drivers
- **Institutional Securities**: Reported record net revenues of $11.0 billion, up from $7.6 billion, driven by strong performance in Equity and Investment Banking.
- **Wealth Management**: Delivered record net revenues of $8.9 billion, up from $7.8 billion, with a pre-tax margin of 30.5% and net new assets of $148 billion.
- **Investment Management**: Net revenues were $1.6 billion, up from $1.5 billion, primarily due to higher average assets under management (AUM).

## Outlook
- **No Specific Guidance**: The release did not provide specific guidance or outlook for future periods.

## Capital and Balance Sheet
- **Share Repurchase Program**: The firm repurchased $1.5 billion of its outstanding common stock and reauthorized a multi-year share repurchase program of up to $20 billion.
- **Dividend Increase**: The Board of Directors declared a $1.15 quarterly dividend per share, a 15-cent increase from the previous quarter.

## Watch Items
- **Expense Efficiency Ratio**: The firm's expense efficiency ratio improved significantly. Committee should monitor if this trend continues and understand the drivers.
- **Capital Management**: The firm's CET1 capital ratio decreased slightly from 15.0% to 14.8%. Committee should monitor the firm's capital management strategies and their impact on financial flexibility.
