**Microsoft Reports Strong Q4 Earnings Growth Driven by Cloud and AI**

## Results
- Revenue reached $90.0 billion, up 18% year-over-year (17% in constant currency).
- Operating income was $40.6 billion, up 18% year-over-year.
- Net income was $35.8 billion on a GAAP basis, up 31% year-over-year, and $35.3 billion on a non-GAAP basis, up 22% year-over-year.
- Diluted earnings per share was $4.81 on a GAAP basis, up 32% year-over-year, and $4.74 on a non-GAAP basis, up 23% year-over-year.

## Segments and drivers
- Microsoft Cloud revenue was $59.3 billion, up 27% year-over-year.
- Productivity and Business Processes revenue was $37.8 billion, up 14% year-over-year.
- Intelligent Cloud revenue was $39.3 billion, up 32% year-over-year (31% in constant currency).
- More Personal Computing revenue was $12.9 billion, down 4% year-over-year (down 5% in constant currency).

## Outlook
- Microsoft will provide forward-looking guidance during the earnings conference call.

## Capital and balance sheet
- The company returned $10.2 billion to shareholders through dividends and share repurchases in Q4.
- Total assets increased to $758.4 billion as of June 30, 2026, from $619.0 billion as of June 30, 2025.
