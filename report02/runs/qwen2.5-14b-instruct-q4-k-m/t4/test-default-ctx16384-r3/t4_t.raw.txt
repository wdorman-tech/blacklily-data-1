**AT&T Reports Strong Q2 Earnings with Continued Investment in Advanced Connectivity**

## Results
- Revenues increased by 2.3% to $31.6 billion year over year.
- Adjusted EPS was $0.65, up from $0.54 in the year-ago quarter.
- Adjusted EBITDA grew by 5.2% to $12.3 billion year over year.
- Free cash flow was $4.7 billion, up from $4.4 billion in the year-ago quarter.

## Segments and drivers
- Advanced Connectivity service revenues grew by 5.1% year over year, with operating income up 20.3% and EBITDA up 8.0%.
- Added over 1 million Advanced Connectivity customers, including 646,000 internet net adds (367,000 fiber and 279,000 fixed wireless) and 432,000 postpaid phone net adds.
- Latin America segment revenues increased by 16.1% year over year, driven by favorable foreign exchange rates and postpaid wireless subscriber growth.

## Outlook
- Reiterates full-year 2026 guidance for adjusted EBITDA growth in the 3% to 4% range and adjusted EPS of $2.25 to $2.35.
- Plans to return $45 billion+ to shareholders through dividends and share repurchases during 2026-2028.
- Capital investment expected to be in the $23 billion to $24 billion range annually during 2026-2028.

## Capital and balance sheet
- Total debt was $144.0 billion at the end of Q2.
- Net debt was $126.4 billion.
- Returned $4.1 billion to shareholders, including approximately $2.2 billion in common share repurchases.

## Watch items
- Monitor the impact of the company's accelerated share repurchase program on its financial metrics and leverage ratios.
- Keep an eye on the progress of fiber network expansion and the associated capital investment, which is critical for future growth.
- Assess the company's ability to meet its long-term financial targets, particularly in light of the continued decline in legacy service revenues.
