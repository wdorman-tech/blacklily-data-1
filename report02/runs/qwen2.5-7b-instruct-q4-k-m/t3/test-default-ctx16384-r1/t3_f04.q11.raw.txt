  "quote": "The Best Execution Committee is chaired by the Chief Operating Officer, Dmitri Halloway. Its other members are the Head of Trading, Marcus Ebbing; the Chief Risk Officer, Yuki Ashbury; the Director of Portfolio Operations, Leopold Sarkis; and the CCO. A majority of members constitutes a quorum.\nThe Committee meets quarterly. At each meeting it reviews transaction cost analysis prepared by Orrinmoor Analytics, which compares each execution with the evaluated price supplied by the primary pricing service at the time of trade and with composite dealer quotations; the number of competitive quotations obtained, against a target of at least three quotations for investment grade trades over $1 million par when market conditions permit; the share of volume executed with each dealer and through each electronic platform; settlement failures; and use of minority-owned, women-owned and veteran-owned broker-dealers against client goals. Minutes record the Committee's conclusions and any follow-up."
