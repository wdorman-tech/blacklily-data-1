# Results

- Reported earnings of $12.1 billion for the second quarter 2026.
- Return on capital employed (ROCE) of 21.4%.
- Record U.S. production; worldwide production increased 20% from last year.
- Crude unit throughput at U.S. refineries was a record 1.07 million barrels per day.
- Signed a 20-year power agreement with Microsoft for a West Texas data center.

## Segments and drivers

- **U.S. Upstream:** Earnings of $3,541 million, up 73% from the year-ago period, driven by higher liquids realizations and sales volumes.
- **International Upstream:** Earnings of $4,641 million, up 221% from the year-ago period, due to higher sales volumes and liquids realizations.
- **U.S. Downstream:** Earnings of $2,411 million, up 481% from the year-ago period, primarily due to higher margins on refined product sales.
- **International Downstream:** Earnings of $2,457 million, up 197% from the year-ago period, driven by higher margins on refined product sales and an asset sale gain.

## Outlook

- No guidance or outlook provided in the release.

## Capital and balance sheet

- Total debt reduced by $8.4 billion, to $37,075 million, strengthening the balance sheet.
- Net debt-to-CFFO ratio of 0.6x, indicating strong cash generation.

## Watch items

- **Production Trends:** Monitor the impact of geopolitical events on production volumes.
- **Cost Reductions:** Track the progress of structural cost reduction initiatives and their impact on earnings.
- **Asset Sales:** Keep an eye on the progress of asset sales and their impact on the balance sheet and earnings.
