# Alphabet Inc.: Second Quarter 2026 Results

## Results
- **Revenues**: Increased 24% to $119.8 billion, or 23% in constant currency.
- **Google Services**: Revenues increased 15% to $94.5 billion, driven by 17% growth in Google Search & other, 15% in Google subscriptions, platforms, and devices, and 13% in YouTube ads.
- **Google Cloud**: Revenues increased 82% to $24.8 billion, led by an increase in Google Cloud Platform (GCP) across enterprise AI Solutions and enterprise AI Infrastructure, as well as core GCP services.
- **Operating Income**: Increased 30% to $40.8 billion.
- **Net Income**: Increased 298% to $112.1 billion.
- **Earnings Per Share (EPS)**: Increased 294% to $9.11.

## Segments and Drivers
- **Google Services**: Contributed $94.5 billion in revenues, with strong growth in Search & other, subscriptions, platforms, and devices, and YouTube ads.
- **Google Cloud**: Saw significant acceleration in growth, with revenues increasing 82% to $24.8 billion, driven by enterprise AI Solutions and Infrastructure.
- **Other Bets**: Revenues increased to $382 million, but operating income decreased to a loss of $1.8 billion.

## Outlook
- No specific guidance or outlook provided in the release.

## Capital and Balance Sheet
- **Equity Capital Raise**: Issued $49.6 billion in Class A and Class C stock and mandatory convertible preferred stock for general corporate purposes, including scaling AI infrastructure.
- **Senior Unsecured Notes**: Issued $20.3 billion in senior unsecured notes for general corporate purposes.
- **Dividends**: Declared a quarterly cash dividend of $12.15 per share on Series A and B mandatory convertible preferred stock, and $0.22 per share on Class A, Class B, and Class C stock.

## Watch Items
- **AI Investments**: Gemini Enterprise adoption is strong, with nearly 90% of the Fortune 100 using it.
- **YouTube Engagement**: Over 1.7 billion unique viewers watched World Cup-related videos during the FIFA World Cup 2026.
- **Revenue Growth**: Monitor the impact of AI on Search query growth and the acceleration in Google Cloud revenues.
