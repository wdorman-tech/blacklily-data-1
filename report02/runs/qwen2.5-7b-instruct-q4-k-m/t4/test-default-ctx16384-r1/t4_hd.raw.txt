# The Home Depot, Inc.: Second Quarter Fiscal 2026 Results

## Results
- **Sales**: $47.9 billion, up $2.6 billion, or 5.7% from the second quarter of fiscal 2025.
- **Comparable Sales**: 1.7% increase.
- **Net Earnings**: $4.8 billion, or $4.79 per diluted share, compared to $4.6 billion, or $4.58 per diluted share, in the same period of fiscal 2025.
- **Adjusted Diluted Earnings Per Share**: $4.92, up from $4.68 in the same period of fiscal 2025.

## Segments and Drivers
- **Broad-Based Demand**: The company saw demand across all business segments, with a focus on smaller projects.
- **Investments and Customer Service**: Investments and focus on customer service were highlighted as key drivers of the quarter's success.

## Outlook
- **Total Sales Growth**: Approximately 2.5% to 4.5%.
- **Comparable Sales Growth**: Approximately flat to 2.0%.
- **New Stores**: Approximately 15 new stores.
- **Gross Margin**: Approximately 33.1%.
- **Operating Margin**: Approximately 12.4% to 12.6%.
- **Adjusted Operating Margin**: Approximately 12.8% to 13.0%.

## Capital and Balance Sheet
- **Capital Expenditures**: Approximately 2.5% of total sales.
- **Total Assets**: $109,384 million.
- **Total Liabilities**: $92,767 million.
- **Total Stockholders' Equity**: $16,617 million.

## Watch Items
- **Amortization Impact**: The release notes an expected 40 basis point impact from acquired intangible asset amortization on the adjusted operating margin.
- **Supply Chain and Input Costs**: The company expects IEEPA tariff refunds to offset some unplanned fuel, energy, and other product input costs.
- **New Store Impact**: The addition of 15 new stores could affect comparable sales growth in the near term.
