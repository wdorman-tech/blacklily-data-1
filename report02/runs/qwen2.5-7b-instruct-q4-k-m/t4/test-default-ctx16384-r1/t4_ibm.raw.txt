# IBM Second-Quarter 2026 Results

## Results
- **Revenue:** $17.2 billion, up 1% year-over-year (YoY), with constant currency growth of 4-5%.
- **Gross Profit:** $9.9 billion, down 1% YoY.
- **Pre-tax Income:** $2.5 billion, down 5% YoY.
- **Net Income:** $2.2 billion, down 9% YoY.
- **Diluted Earnings Per Share:** $2.27, down 3% YoY.

## Segments and Drivers
- **High-Growth Portfolio:** Software segment, particularly Red Hat, the Watsonx portfolio, HashiCorp, and Confluent, grew 5% with strong performance.
- **Infrastructure:** Distributed Infrastructure grew 37% with an order backlog of nearly $500 million.
- **Consulting:** Flat revenue, up 1% at constant currency.
- **Revenue Guidance:** Full-year constant currency revenue growth expected in the range of 4-5%.
- **Free Cash Flow:** Expected to increase by about $1 billion year-over-year.

## Outlook
- **Revenue Growth:** Full-year constant currency revenue growth expected in the range of 4-5%.
- **Free Cash Flow:** Expected to increase by about $1 billion year-over-year.
- **Investment Priorities:** Accelerating changes to the go-to-market model, investing in AI and quantum computing, and generating free cash flow.

## Capital and Balance Sheet
- **Cash Flow:** Generated net cash from operating activities of $2.6 billion in the second quarter, up $0.9 billion YoY.
- **Free Cash Flow:** $2.5 billion, down $0.3 billion YoY.
- **Cash Position:** Ended the quarter with $8.2 billion of cash, restricted cash, and marketable securities, down $6.3 billion from year-end 2025.
- **Acquisitions:** Invested $10.5 billion in acquisitions year-to-date.

## Watch Items
- **Segment Performance:** Monitor the performance of high-growth segments like Red Hat and Watsonx, as well as the Distributed Infrastructure order backlog.
- **Revenue Guidance:** Assess the accuracy of the full-year revenue growth guidance, particularly in light of revenue headwinds late in the quarter.
- **Free Cash Flow:** Track the company's ability to generate and manage free cash flow, especially as it invests in new initiatives.
