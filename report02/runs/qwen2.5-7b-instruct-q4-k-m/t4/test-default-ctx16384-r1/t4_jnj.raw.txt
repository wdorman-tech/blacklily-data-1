# Johnson & Johnson Reports Q2 2026 Results, Raises 2026 Outlook

## Results
- Reported sales growth of 6.6% to $25.3 billion.
- Earnings per share (EPS) of $2.27.
- Adjusted EPS of $2.90.
- Raised 2026 guidance with estimated reported sales of $101.1 billion or 7.3% at the midpoint, and increased adjusted EPS guidance by $0.13 to $11.68 or 8.2% at the midpoint.

## Segments and Drivers
- **Innovative Medicine**: Worldwide operational sales grew 6.8%, driven by products like DARZALEX, CARVYKTI, TECVAYLI, and RYBREVANT/LAZCLUZE.
- **MedTech**: Worldwide operational sales grew 3.6%, driven by wound closure products, biosurgery products, and trauma.

## Outlook
- Raised 2026 guidance for adjusted operational sales to 6.2% – 6.8% at the midpoint.
- Increased adjusted operational EPS guidance to $11.50 – $11.65 at the midpoint.
- Increased adjusted EPS guidance to $11.60 – $11.75 at the midpoint.

## Capital and Balance Sheet
- Free cash flow estimated at ~$8.7 billion for the second quarter.

## Watch Items
- **TREMFYA approvals**: Approval for inhibiting the progression of structural joint damage in adults with active psoriatic arthritis.
- **Notable announcements**: FDA approvals for CAPLYTA for the prevention of relapse in schizophrenia and Dual Energy THERMOCOOL SMARTTOUCH SF platform for inhibiting the progression of structural joint damage in adults with active psoriatic arthritis.
