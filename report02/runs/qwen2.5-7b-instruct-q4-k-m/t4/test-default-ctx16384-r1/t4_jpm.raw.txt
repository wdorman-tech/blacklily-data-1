# JPMorgan Chase & Co.: Q2 2026 Earnings Release

## Results
- **Net income of $21.2 billion ($7.70 per share)**
- **Net income excluding significant items of $16.9 billion ($6.14 per share)**
- **Reported revenue of $57.3 billion**
- **Managed revenue of $58.0 billion**
- **Provision for credit losses of $2.5 billion**
- **Net charge-offs of $2.4 billion**
- **Net reserve build of $149 million**

## Segments and drivers
- **Consumer & Community Banking (CCB):**
  - **Net revenue of $20.3 billion (up 8%)**
  - **Card Services & Auto net revenue of $7.8 billion (up 12%)**
  - **Home Lending net revenue of $1.3 billion (up 3%)**
- **Commercial & Investment Banking (CIB):**
  - **Net revenue of $24.9 billion (up 27%)**
  - **Investment Banking revenue of $3.9 billion (up 45%)**
  - **Markets revenue of $12.1 billion (up 35%)**
- **Asset & Wealth Management (AWM):**
  - **Net revenue of $6.9 billion (up 19%)**
  - **Assets under management of $5.1 trillion (up 18%)**

## Outlook
- **No specific guidance or outlook provided in the release**

## Capital and balance sheet

  - **Standardized ratio of 14.1%**

- **Total Loss-Absorbing Capacity: $590 billion**

## Watch items
