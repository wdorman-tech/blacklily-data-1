# Meta Platforms, Inc.: Second Quarter 2026 Earnings Release

## Results
- **Revenue**: $60.80 billion, up 28% year-over-year.
- **Income from operations**: $18.78 billion, down 8% year-over-year.
- **Family daily active people (DAP)**: 3.60 billion, up 3% year-over-year.
- **Ad impressions**: Increased by 14% year-over-year.
- **Average price per ad**: Increased by 12% year-over-year.

## Segments and drivers
- **Family of Apps**: Revenue of $60.37 billion, up 24% year-over-year.
  - **Advertising revenue**: $59.36 billion, up 27% year-over-year.
- **Reality Labs**: Revenue of $431 million, up 16% year-over-year.
- **Operating margin**: 31%, down from 43% year-over-year.
- **Pro forma revenue (constant currency)**: $60.12 billion, up 27% year-over-year.

## Outlook
- **Third quarter 2026 revenue guidance**: $61-64 billion.
- **Full year 2026 expense outlook**: $165-169 billion.
- **Operating income**: Expected to be above 2025 levels.
- **Capital expenditures**: $130-145 billion.

## Capital and balance sheet
- **Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities**: $90.26 billion.
- **Long-term debt**: $83.66 billion.
- **Free cash flow**: $784 million.

## Watch items
- **Family daily active people (DAP)**: Monitor the 3% year-over-year growth, as it is a key driver of revenue.
- **Operating margin**: Keep an eye on the decline to 31%, as it may indicate cost pressures.
- **Legal proceedings**: Note the $2.4 billion charges related to legal proceedings, which could impact future financial results.
