# Morgan Stanley Second Quarter 2026 Earnings Results

## Results
- **Net Revenues:** $21.3 billion (up 27% from $16.8 billion in 2025)
- **Net Income Applicable to Morgan Stanley:** $5.6 billion (up 58% from $3.5 billion in 2025)
- **Earnings Per Diluted Share:** $3.46 (up 62% from $2.13 in 2025)
- **Return on Tangible Common Equity (ROTCE):** 26.6% (up from 18.2% in 2025)

## Segments and Drivers
- **Institutional Securities:**
  - **Net Revenues:** $11.0 billion (up 45% from $7.6 billion in 2025)
  - **Record Revenues Driven by Equity and Investment Banking:** Equity net revenues up 69%, Investment Banking net revenues up 58%
- **Wealth Management:**
  - **Net Revenues:** $8.9 billion (up 14% from $7.8 billion in 2025)
  - **Record Net New Assets:** $148 billion, with $74 billion from IPOs in the Workplace channel
- **Investment Management:**
  - **Net Revenues:** $1.6 billion (up 6% from $1.5 billion in 2025)
  - **Asset Management Fees Increased:** Driven by higher average AUM and positive flows

## Outlook
- **No Guidance Provided:** The release does not provide any outlook or guidance for future periods.

## Capital and Balance Sheet
- **Standardized Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) Capital Ratio:** 14.8%
- **Book Value Per Share:** $67.80
- **Tangible Book Value Per Share:** $53.18

## Watch Items
- **Expense Efficiency Ratio:** 65% (down from 71% in 2025), indicating improved operating leverage.
- **Net New Assets in Wealth Management:** $148 billion, with a significant portion from IPOs, which may not be sustainable in the long term.
- **Provision for Credit Losses:** $98 million, down from $196 million in 2025, reflecting portfolio growth and individual assessments.
