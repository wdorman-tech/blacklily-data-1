# Microsoft Corporation: Fourth Quarter and Fiscal Year 2026 Results

## Results
- **Revenue**: $90.0 billion, up 18% (17% in constant currency)
- **Operating Income**: $40.6 billion, up 18%
- **Net Income**: $35.8 billion, up 31% on a GAAP basis, and $35.3 billion, up 22% on a non-GAAP basis
- **Diluted Earnings per Share**: $4.81, up 32% on a GAAP basis, and $4.74, up 23% on a non-GAAP basis

## Segments and Drivers
- **Microsoft Cloud and AI**: Revenue of $59.3 billion, up 27% year-over-year, with Azure and other cloud services revenue increasing 43%
- **Productivity and Business Processes**: Revenue of $37.8 billion, up 14%, driven by 16% growth in Microsoft 365 Commercial cloud revenue (14% on a reported basis)
- **More Personal Computing**: Revenue of $12.9 billion, down 4% (5% in constant currency), with Windows OEM and Devices revenue decreasing 7%

## Outlook
- **No guidance or outlook provided** in the release.

## Capital and Balance Sheet
- **Revenue**: $331.8 billion, up 18% (16% in constant currency)
- **Operating Income**: $155.2 billion, up 21% (19% in constant currency)
- **Net Income**: $133.7 billion, up 31% on a GAAP basis, and 22% (20% in constant currency) on a non-GAAP basis
- **Diluted Earnings per Share**: $17.95, up 32% on a GAAP basis, and 22% (21% in constant currency) on a non-GAAP basis

## Watch Items
1. **Revenue Guidance**: The release does not provide any guidance for future quarters, which could impact investor expectations.
2. **Cloud Services Growth**: Continued strong growth in Azure and other cloud services, but monitor for potential slowing in the broader market.
3. **Personal Computing Segment**: Declining revenue in More Personal Computing, which may indicate broader market challenges or competitive pressures.
