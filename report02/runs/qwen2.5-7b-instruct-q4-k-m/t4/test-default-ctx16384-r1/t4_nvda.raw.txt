# NVIDIA Corporation: Q2 Fiscal 2027 Earnings Release

## Results
- **Revenue:** $96.2 billion, up 106% from a year ago.
- **Data Center Revenue:** $89.0 billion, up 117% from a year ago.
- **GAAP and Non-GAAP Gross Margin:** 75.0%.
- **GAAP and Non-GAAP Earnings per Diluted Share:** $2.46 and $2.22, respectively.

## Segments and Drivers
- **Data Center:** Second-quarter revenue was $89.0 billion, up 18% from the previous quarter and up 117% from a year ago.
- **Edge Computing:** Second-quarter revenue was $7.2 billion, up 13% from the previous quarter and up 27% from a year ago.
- **Key Highlights:** NVIDIA announced the NVIDIA Vera Rubin platform is ramping into full production, and unveiled the NVIDIA Spectrum-6 switch systems, among other advancements.

## Outlook
- **Revenue:** $108.0 billion, plus or minus 2%.
- **GAAP and Non-GAAP Gross Margins:** 74.0%, plus or minus 50 basis points.
- **GAAP and Non-GAAP Operating Expenses:** Approximately $9.2 billion and $9.0 billion, respectively.

## Capital and Balance Sheet
- **Share Repurchase and Dividends:** NVIDIA returned approximately $26.0 billion to shareholders in the form of shares repurchased and cash dividends.
- **Share Repurchase Authorization:** Approximately $99.0 billion remaining under the share repurchase authorization.
- **Cash and Cash Equivalents:** $22.443 billion at the end of the quarter.

## Watch Items
- **Vera Rubin Platform:** Monitor the progress of the Vera Rubin platform, particularly its ramp-up into full production and its impact on Data Center revenue.
- **AI Infrastructure Buildout:** Keep an eye on the momentum of the AI infrastructure buildout, including the partnerships and deployments announced, as they could significantly impact future revenue streams.
