# AT&T Inc.: Second-Quarter 2026 Earnings Results

## Results
- Revenues totaled $31.6 billion, up 2.3% from the year-ago quarter.
- Diluted EPS from continuing operations was $0.66, versus $0.62 in the year-ago quarter; adjusted EPS was $0.65, versus $0.54 in the year-ago quarter.
- Operating income was $7.0 billion; adjusted operating income was $7.5 billion.
- Income from continuing operations was $5.0 billion, up 3.6% year over year; adjusted EBITDA was $12.3 billion, up 5.2% year over year.
- Cash from operating activities from continuing operations was $10.8 billion, versus $9.8 billion in the year-ago quarter.
- Capital expenditures related to continuing operations were $5.7 billion; capital investment was $6.1 billion.
- Free cash flow was $4.7 billion, versus $4.4 billion in the year-ago quarter.

## Segments and Drivers
- **Advanced Connectivity**: Added over 1 million Advanced Connectivity customers, driven by year-over-year increases in net adds across fiber, fixed wireless, and postpaid phone subscribers. Advanced Connectivity service revenue of $23.5 billion, up 5.1% year over year.
- **Legacy**: Revenues continued to decline year over year, down 25.9% due to the decommissioning of the copper-based network. Operating income and EBITDA were $523 million, down $436 million year over year.
- **Latin America**: Segment revenues were up 16.1% year over year, primarily driven by favorable foreign exchange rates and postpaid wireless subscriber growth.

## Outlook
- **Service Revenue Growth**: Expected to grow in the low-single-digit range annually, with Advanced Connectivity service revenue growth in the mid-single-digit range annually.
- **Adjusted EBITDA**: Expected to grow in the 3% to 4% range in 2026, improving to 5% or better in 2028.
- **Adjusted EPS**: Expected to be $2.25 to $2.35 in 2026 with a double-digit 3-year CAGR through 2028.

## Capital and Balance Sheet
- **Capital Investment**: Expected to be in the $23 billion to $24 billion range annually during 2026-2028.
- **Free Cash Flow**: Expected to be $18 billion+ in 2026, $19 billion+ in 2027, and $21 billion+ in 2028.
- **Net Debt-to-Adjusted EBITDA**: Targeted to return to a level consistent with the 2.5x range within approximately three years following the closing of the transaction with EchoStar.

## Watch Items
- **Customer Growth**: Monitor the continued growth in Advanced Connectivity customers, particularly in fiber and fixed wireless.
- **Legacy Network Decommissioning**: Track the progress of decommissioning the copper-based network and its impact on legacy revenues and operating expenses.
- **Foreign Exchange Impact**: Keep an eye on foreign exchange rates and their impact on Latin America segment revenues.
