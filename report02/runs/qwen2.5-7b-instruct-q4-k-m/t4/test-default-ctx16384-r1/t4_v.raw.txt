**Visa Inc.: Fiscal Third Quarter 2026 Results**

## Results
- **GAAP Net Income:** $5.6 billion or $2.97 per share (7% increase over prior year)
- **Non-GAAP Net Income:** $6.3 billion or $3.32 per share (8% increase over prior year)
- **Net Revenue:** $11.6 billion, up 14% year-over-year, or 13% on a constant-dollar basis
- **Payments Volume:** 10% increase year-over-year
- **Cross-Border Volume (excluding intra-Europe):** 12% increase year-over-year
- **Processed Transactions:** 10% increase year-over-year

## Segments and Drivers
- **Strong Growth in Payments Volume, Cross-Border Volume, and Processed Transactions:** Reflects resilient consumer and business spending.
- **Net Revenue Growth:** Driven by year-over-year growth in payments volume, cross-border volume, and processed transactions.

## Outlook
- **No Guidance Provided:** The company did not provide any outlook or guidance for future periods.

## Capital and Balance Sheet
- **Cash, Cash Equivalents, and Investment Securities:** $13.9 billion as of June 30, 2026
- **Share Repurchases and Dividends:** $6.2 billion in share repurchases and dividends for the quarter
