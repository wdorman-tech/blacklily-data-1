- **Q3 2026, gross of fees:** 2.26%

### Fee Change

Effective January 1, 2027, the fee for the Private Credit Sleeve will be reduced to 1.10% annually, from the current rate of 1.25%. The new fee will be billed quarterly in arrears on quarter-end net asset value (NAV).

### Required Disclosure

Private credit investments are illiquid and are valued using estimates that may not reflect the price at which a loan could actually be sold. These investments may lose value.
