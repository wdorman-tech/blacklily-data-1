I hope this letter finds you well. I am writing to provide you with an update on the performance and activities of your ESG Core Equity mandate with fossil-fuel reserves exclusion for the quarter ending June 30, 2026.

### Performance Overview

During the second quarter of 2026, your portfolio experienced a net return of -3.36%, which is net of advisory fees. The gross return, before fees, was -3.16%. For comparison, the Developed Markets Core Equity Index (unscreened) returned -2.95% during the same period. Year-to-date, your portfolio has returned 0.92% net of fees, and over the trailing 12 months, it has delivered a net return of 6.18%.

### Commentary

1. **Energy Exclusion Impact:** The exclusion of fossil-fuel reserves had a minor impact on performance, costing about 0.5% relative to the comparison index. This was due to the rally in oil-linked stocks in May, which the portfolio did not participate in.

2. **Environmental Impact:** The weighted average carbon intensity of the portfolio is 58% below that of the comparison index, reflecting our commitment to environmental criteria.

3. **Proxy Season:** We voted against 11 director nominees due to weak climate disclosure, aligning with our ESG principles.

### Addressing Samuel's Concern

Samuel, you had asked whether the ESG screen costs money. The exclusion of fossil-fuel reserves does have a small impact on performance, but it is important to us that your portfolio aligns with your values. We believe that long-term, this approach can lead to better risk-adjusted returns.
