**Quarterly Letter to the Ferreira-Lindqvist Household**

Dear Dr. Heitor Ferreira and Dr. Annika Lindqvist,

I hope this letter finds you well. I am Barnaby Wrenfield, your Senior Portfolio Manager at Treleaven Ormesby Advisors, LLC. I am writing to provide you with an update on your International Developed Quality Equity strategy for the quarter ending June 30, 2026.

### Performance Overview

For the second quarter of 2026, the strategy returned -1.9% net of fees, which is slightly better than the -3.2% return of the comparison index (developed markets ex-US equity index, net dividends). Year to date, the strategy has returned 4.6% net of fees, and since its inception on May 3, 2021, it has delivered an annualized return of 6.3% net of fees.

### Commentary

In the quarter, we made some strategic adjustments to the portfolio. We halved our positions in Nordic banks in response to the April rally, reducing the financials allocation to 14.5% from 19.0% at the end of March. Additionally, we added to our Japanese holdings following the May sell-off, making Japan the largest country weight at 23.5% of the portfolio. At the end of the quarter, cash levels were 4.4%, and the number of holdings remained unchanged at 38 names.

### Fee Change

Effective October 1, 2026, the fee rate for the first $2,000,000 of household assets under management will be reduced from 0.90% to 0.80% per year. The fee for assets above $2,000,000 will remain at 0.65% per year. This change is part of a firm-wide repricing approved by the partners in June and will be reflected in the Q4 2026 invoice, which will be billed on September 30, 2026, based on the value of the accounts as of that date.
