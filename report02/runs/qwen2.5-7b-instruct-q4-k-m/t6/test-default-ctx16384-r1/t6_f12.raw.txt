**Quarterly Letter to the Wexcombe Household**

Dear Everett and Lucia,

I hope this letter finds you well. I am Renata Oyelaran-Hess, your Senior Wealth Advisor at Hollick Varlow Advisory Group, LLC. I am writing to provide you with an update on your Liquidity Reserve account for the quarter ending September 30, 2026.

### Performance

In the third quarter of 2026, your account returned 1.3% net of fees, which is slightly higher than the 1-3 year US Treasury index return of 1.2%. Year to date, your account has returned 3.6% net of fees, and over the trailing 12 months, it has returned 4.7% net of fees.

### Commentary

- **September Maturity Roll:** We rolled the September maturity into a new 3-year Treasury rung with a yield to maturity of 3.72%. This move helps us maintain a balanced ladder and ensure we are capturing the best yields available.
- **Ladder Duration:** The ladder duration is now 1.7 years, up from 1.5 years at the end of June. This slight increase in duration reflects our ongoing strategy to balance yield and safety.
- **Agency Holdings:** We reduced the agency holdings to 12% of the ladder from 20%. The decision was made because the callable agency spreads were too tight to justify the additional risk. We have reinvested the proceeds into Treasury bills, which offer a safer and more predictable return.

### Fee Change

Effective November 1, 2026, the advisory fee for your account will decrease from 0.40% per year to 0.30% per year. This change reflects the move to a lower fee schedule for the Liquidity Reserve account, while your equity account fee remains unchanged.

### Required Disclosure

Yield to maturity reflects the yield at the time of purchase, assumes the security is held to maturity, and is not a forecast of the return you will receive.
