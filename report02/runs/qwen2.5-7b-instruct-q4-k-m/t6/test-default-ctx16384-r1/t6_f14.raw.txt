**Quarterly Letter to the Delacourt-Ambrose Household**

Dear Mrs. Delacourt and Mr. Ambrose,

I hope this letter finds you well. I am writing to provide you with an update on the Delacourt-Ambrose Charitable Remainder Unitrust for the first quarter of 2026.

### Performance

For the quarter ending March 31, 2026, the Delacourt-Ambrose Charitable Remainder Unitrust returned 2.2% net of fees. This compares to a 1.8% return for the 55% global equity index / 45% US aggregate bond index. Year to date, the return is 2.2%, and since the inception of the trust on October 2, 2017, the annualized return has been 6.8% net of fees.

### Unitrust Payment

The first 2026 unitrust payment of $31,250 was made on March 31, 2026. This payment is based on the 5% unitrust amount, which is paid quarterly. The January 2, 2026, valuation of the trust was $2,500,000, and the payment is divided into four equal installments.

### Asset Allocation

To ensure the trust can meet future obligations, we have increased the cash and Treasury bills allocation to 6.5% of the trust assets. This pre-funds the June and September payments without selling risk assets. Additionally, we have trimmed the high-yield bond allocation to 3.5% from 7.5%, as spreads are near their narrowest since 2021.

### Fee Change

Effective June 1, 2026, the advisory fee for the first $3,000,000 of trust assets will decrease from 1.00% to 0.90% per year. The fee for assets above $3,000,000 remains unchanged at 0.70% per year. The new rate will be prorated on the Q2 invoice.

### Required Disclosure

Unitrust payments are set by the trust instrument as a fixed percentage of the trust's net assets, which are revalued each year. These payments are not a measure of investment performance and may rise or fall with the value of the trust.
