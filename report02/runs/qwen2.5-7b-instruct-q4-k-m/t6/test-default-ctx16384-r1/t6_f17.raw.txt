- **Commodities:** We have reduced the commodities allocation to 5% of the sleeve, following the recent gold rally. The policy range for commodities is now 4% to 12%.

### Fee Change
Effective December 1, 2026, the advisory fee for the Real Assets sleeve will increase from 0.60% per year to 0.75% per year. This change is due to the shift in the sleeve's composition, moving from ETFs to directly held TIPS and exchange-traded futures managed by the firm. The weighted fund expenses in the sleeve will fall from 0.14% to 0.03%, offsetting most of the increase.
