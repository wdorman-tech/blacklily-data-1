**Quarterly Letter to the Okafor-Lindgren Household**

Dear Adaora and Nils,

I hope this letter finds you well. I am writing to provide you with an update on the performance and recent changes in your North Carolina UTMA custodial account for Mateo Okafor-Lindgren, with Adaora Okafor as custodian.

### Performance Update

For the second quarter of 2026 (April 1 through June 30, 2026), the account returned 3.7% net of fees. This is slightly lower than the comparison index, which returned 4.1% during the same period. Year to date, the account has returned 5.3% net of fees, and since its inception on September 16, 2019, it has generated an annualized return of 8.2% net of fees.

### Recent Changes

1. **Glide Path Adjustment**: On June 1, we took the annual glide path step, reducing the equity target from 75% to 70%. This adjustment is in line with our strategy to balance risk and growth as Mateo approaches his college years.
   
2. **Asset Rebalancing**: We moved $14,000 from equities into Treasuries maturing in 2031. This move is specifically earmarked for Mateo’s first-year college costs, ensuring that the funds will be available when needed.

3. **Cash Position**: As of the end of June, the cash position stands at 2.5% after the recent rebalance.

### Fee Change

Effective October 1, 2026, the advisory fee for the UTMA account will change to 0.80% per year. This new rate applies to the UTMA account, which is now aggregated with Adaora and Nils's accounts for breakpoint purposes. The change is a result of a householding request from the parents, which was approved in June.

### Required Disclosure

Assets in a custodial account under the Uniform Transfers to Minors Act are the irrevocable property of the minor, pass to the minor's control at the age set by state law, and may reduce eligibility for need-based financial aid.
