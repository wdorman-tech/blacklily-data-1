I hope this letter finds you well. I am writing to provide an update on the performance and activities of the Harlan-Osei Family Foundation’s endowment model for the first quarter of 2026.

### Performance

For the quarter ending March 31, 2026, the endowment model returned -3.1% net of fees, which is slightly better than the -3.5% return of the comparison index. The gross-of-fees return was -2.9%. As of March 31, 2026, the year-to-date return is -3.1%, and the trailing five-year return is 6.6% annualized, net of fees.

### Commentary Points

- **Grants and Asset Allocation:** During Q1, we distributed $212,500 from the short-term reserve to support the foundation’s grant-making activities. No equity sales were made during the March market decline, and we remain on track to meet the 5% minimum payout for the year.
- **Fixed Income:** The fixed income allocation increased to 27.5% of assets after adding Treasury bonds to capture the yield spike. Our target was 25%, so this adjustment is within our policy parameters.
- **Global Equity:** The global equity allocation decreased to 57.8% of assets, remaining within the 5-point band around the 60% target. No rebalancing was triggered during the quarter.

### Fee Change

Effective May 1, 2026, the advisory fee for foundation assets above $10,000,000 will be reduced from 0.60% per year to 0.45% per year. The first $10,000,000 will remain at 0.75% per year. This change reflects our ongoing commitment to competitive pricing and value for our clients.
