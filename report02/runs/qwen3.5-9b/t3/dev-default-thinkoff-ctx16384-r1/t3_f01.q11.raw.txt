  "quote": "No Supervised Person may accept gifts with an aggregate value exceeding $250 per calendar year from any single person or entity that does business with, or seeks to do business with, the Firm, including clients, custodians, fund sponsors and vendors. The same limit applies to gifts given by a Supervised Person on behalf of the Firm. Every gift with a value above $50, given or received, must be reported in the Compliance Platform within ten business days. A gift that exceeds the limit must be returned or, if return is impractical, donated to charity at the direction of the CCO.\n\nBusiness entertainment is permitted when the host is present and the purpose is business. Business entertainment with an estimated cost exceeding $400 per person per event requires the prior approval of the CCO."
