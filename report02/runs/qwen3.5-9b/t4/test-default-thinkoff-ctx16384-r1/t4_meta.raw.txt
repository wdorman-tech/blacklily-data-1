**Meta Reports Strong Revenue Growth Driven by AI and Ad Pricing Despite Elevated Legal Charges**

## Results
*   Meta reported second quarter 2026 revenue of $60.80 billion, representing a 28% increase from the prior year period. On a constant currency basis, revenue would have increased by 27%.
*   Costs and expenses totaled $42.03 billion, an increase of 55% year-over-year. This figure includes $2.40 billion in charges related to legal proceedings and $1.18 billion in severance expenses connected to the May 2026 headcount reduction.
*   Income from operations was $18.775 billion, reflecting an operating margin of 31%, down from 43% in the prior year period. Net income decreased by 14% to $15.848 billion, while diluted earnings per share fell 13% to $6.18.
*   Family daily active people averaged 3.60 billion for June 2026, an increase of 3% year-over-year. Ad impressions delivered across the Family of Apps increased by 14%, while the average price per ad rose by 12%.

## Segments and drivers
*   The Family of Apps segment generated $60.37 billion in revenue with income from operations of $23.394 billion. Advertising revenue within this segment was $59.363 billion.
*   Reality Labs reported revenue of $431 million and an operating loss of $4.619 billion for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.
*   Total costs and expenses include significant one-time items: $2.40 billion in legal proceeding charges recognized in the second quarter and $1.18 billion in severance expenses.

## Outlook
*   For the third quarter of 2026, total revenue is expected to be in the range of $61-64 billion. This guidance assumes foreign currency will act as an approximately 1% headwind to year-over-year growth based on current exchange rates.
*   Full year 2026 total expenses are now expected to be in the range of $165-169 billion, with the lower end raised to incorporate the legal charges recognized in the second quarter.
*   The company anticipates its tax rate for the remaining quarters of 2026 to be between 15-17%, an increase from the prior outlook of 13-16%, absent changes to the tax landscape.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   Capital expenditures, including principal payments on finance leases, were $31.08 billion for the quarter. The company anticipates full year 2026 capital expenditures to be in the range of $130-145 billion.
*   Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities stood at $90.26 billion as of June 30, 2026. Long-term debt was $83.66 billion at the same date.
*   The capital return program included dividend and dividend equivalent payments of $1.35 billion during the quarter.

## Watch items
*   Monitor the impact of youth-related trials scheduled for this year in the U.S., which management notes may ultimately result in a material loss due to ongoing scrutiny on youth-related issues in several markets.
*   Track whether operating income delivered this year exceeds 2025 operating income, as previously stated in the CFO commentary.
*   Observe if actual third quarter revenue falls within the $61-64 billion guidance range given the noted foreign currency headwind assumption.
