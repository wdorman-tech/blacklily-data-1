**Morgan Stanley Delivers Record Second Quarter Revenues and Strong Capital Position Amid Active Markets**

## Results
*   The Firm reported net revenues of $21.3 billion for the second quarter ended June 30, 2026, compared with $16.8 billion a year ago.
*   Net income applicable to Morgan Stanley was $5.6 billion, or $3.46 per diluted share, versus $3.5 billion, or $2.13 per diluted share, for the same period last year.
*   Return on tangible common equity was 26.6%, while the expense efficiency ratio stood at 65% for the first half of the year.
*   The Firm repurchased $1.5 billion of its outstanding common stock during the quarter and declared a quarterly dividend of $1.15 per share.

## Segments and drivers
*   Institutional Securities generated record net revenues of $11.0 billion, driven by an 58% increase in Investment Banking revenues due to higher completed M&A transactions and advisory activity.
*   Equity net revenues reached a record level with a 69% increase from a year ago, supported by strong client engagement and favorable market conditions across regions including Asia.
*   Wealth Management delivered record net revenues of $8.9 billion and added net new assets of $148 billion for the quarter, with total client assets reaching the $10 trillion milestone.
*   Investment Management reported net revenues of $1.6 billion, primarily driven by asset management fees on higher average AUM, alongside positive long-term net flows of $7.5 billion.

## Outlook
*   The release does not provide specific numerical guidance for future quarters or full-year targets beyond the current reporting period.
*   Management noted that active markets and consistent execution across all three regions drove exceptional results, suggesting continued momentum in Institutional Securities.
*   The Firm stated it continues to accrete capital, providing incremental flexibility to invest in core businesses while generating strong returns for shareholders.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   The Standardized Common Equity Tier 1 capital ratio was 14.8%, while the Advanced Approach CET1 capital ratio was 16.2%.
*   Tier 1 leverage was 6.0% under the leverage-based capital requirement, and the supplementary leverage ratio (SLR) was 4.9%.
*   The effective tax rate for the current quarter was 23.1%, reflecting a slight increase from the prior year period.

## Watch items
*   Monitor the impact of higher mark-to-market losses on corporate loans, inclusive of hedges, which caused Other net revenues to decrease from a year ago in Institutional Securities.
*   Track the cumulative impact of consistent lending growth in the securitized products business and credit corporates driving Fixed Income results, alongside potential volatility in credit portfolios.
*   Observe the transition regarding deferred cash-based compensation plans (DCP), where hedges are now recorded in compensation expense or other comprehensive income rather than excluding them from non-GAAP measures.
