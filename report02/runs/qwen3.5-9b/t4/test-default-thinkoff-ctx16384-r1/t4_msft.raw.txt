**Microsoft Cloud and AI Strength Fuels Fourth Quarter Results**

## Results
*   Revenue for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, was $90.0 billion, representing an 18% increase compared to the corresponding period of last fiscal year.
*   Operating income reached $40.6 billion, marking an 18% increase from the prior year period.
*   Net income on a GAAP basis was $35.8 billion, which increased 31%, while non-GAAP net income was $35.3 billion, increasing 22%.
*   Diluted earnings per share were $4.81 on a GAAP basis (a 32% increase) and $4.74 on a non-GAAP basis (a 23% increase).

## Segments and drivers
*   Microsoft Cloud revenue totaled $59.3 billion, up 27% year-over-year, driven by Azure and other cloud services revenue which increased 43%.
*   Revenue in Productivity and Business Processes was $37.8 billion, an increase of 14%, highlighted by a 24% rise in Microsoft 365 Consumer cloud revenue.
*   Revenue in More Personal Computing decreased 4% to $12.9 billion, primarily due to a 7% decline in Windows OEM and Devices revenue and a 10% drop in XBOX content and services revenue.

## Outlook
*   The release states that Microsoft will provide forward-looking guidance in connection with the quarterly earnings announcement on its earnings conference call and webcast.
*   No specific numerical guidance or outlook figures are provided within this written text of the release.
*   The company notes that actual results could differ materially due to intense competition, substantial investments in cloud and AI strategy, and evolving legal and regulatory requirements.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   Microsoft returned $10.2 billion to shareholders in the form of dividends and share repurchases during the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2026.
*   Total cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments stood at $76.8 billion as of June 30, 2026.
*   Commercial remaining performance obligation increased 84% to $678 billion.

## Watch items
*   The release notes that several discrete items impacted financial results when compared to forward-looking guidance provided on April 29, 2026, including a $3.2 billion gain from an investment in Anthropic and lower-than-expected expenses related to the Voluntary Retirement Program.
*   Management considers non-GAAP measures in conjunction with GAAP financial results, specifically noting that non-GAAP results exclude the impact from investments in OpenAI, which resulted in a net income increase of $480 million for the quarter.
