**AT&T Q2 2026: Investment Strategy Drives Record Advanced Connectivity Growth and Accelerated Capital Returns**

## Results
*   Revenues totaled $31.6 billion, representing a 2.3% increase from the year-ago quarter.
*   Diluted EPS from continuing operations was $0.66, while adjusted EPS was $0.65; both figures reflect increases versus the year-ago quarter of $0.62 and $0.54, respectively.
*   Operating income reached $7.0 billion, with adjusted operating income at $7.5 billion, compared to $6.5 billion in the prior year period.
*   Adjusted EBITDA was $12.3 billion, up 5.2% year over year, supported by cash from operating activities of $10.8 billion and free cash flow of $4.7 billion.

## Segments and drivers
*   Advanced Connectivity service revenues grew 5.1% year over year to $23.5 billion, driving a 20.3% increase in operating income and an 8.0% rise in EBITDA*.
*   The Company added over 1 million Advanced Connectivity customers, comprising 646,000 total internet net adds (367,000 fiber and 279,000 fixed wireless) and 432,000 postpaid phone net adds.
*   Legacy segment revenues declined 25.9% year over year as the Company continues to decommission its copper-based network, resulting in a 45.5% decrease in operating income.
*   Latin America segment revenues increased 16.1% year over year, primarily driven by favorable foreign exchange rates and postpaid wireless subscriber growth.

## Outlook
*   AT&T maintains its outlook for improved growth in adjusted EBITDA*, adjusted EPS*, and higher free cash flow* through 2028, with service revenue growth projected in the low-single-digit range annually.
*   The Company plans to return $45 billion+ to shareholders during 2026-2028 through dividends and share repurchases, including approximately $10 billion of share repurchases in 2026.
*   Advanced Connectivity EBITDA* is expected to grow in the mid-to-high-single-digit range annually, with legacy EBITDA* anticipated to turn negative after 2027 until direct costs associated with the copper-based network are substantially eliminated.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   Total debt stood at $144.0 billion at the end of the second quarter, resulting in net debt* of $126.4 billion.
*   Capital investment* totaled $6.1 billion for the quarter, compared to $5.1 billion in the year-ago quarter, reflecting ongoing spending for strategic initiatives.
*   The Company returned $4.1 billion to shareholders this quarter, including approximately $2.2 billion in common share repurchases under the 2024 authorization.

## Watch items
*   Monitor the impact of the first-quarter acquisition of Lumen’s mass markets fiber business on incremental customer costs and whether these expenses persist beyond the integration period.
*   Track the timing of the closing of the transaction with EchoStar, as the release notes that the net debt-to-adjusted EBITDA ratio* target depends on this event.
*   Observe the asset abandonment charge associated with the reprioritization of the Company’s spectrum strategy and its effect on operating expenses in future quarters.
