**Alphabet Q2 2026 Results Driven by Massive Equity Gains and Accelerating Cloud Growth**

## Results
*   Consolidated Alphabet revenues increased 24% to $119.8 billion, representing a 23% increase in constant currency, marking the company's 12th consecutive quarter of double-digit revenue growth.
*   Google Services revenues rose 15% to $94.5 billion, with specific growth of 17% in Search & other, 15% in subscriptions, platforms, and devices, and 13% in YouTube ads.
*   Google Cloud revenues accelerated significantly by 82% to $24.8 billion, driven by demand for enterprise AI Solutions and infrastructure.
*   Consolidated Alphabet operating income increased 30% while the operating margin expanded two percentage points to 34%.
*   Net income available to common stockholders surged 298%, with diluted EPS reaching $9.11, largely due to a net gain of $98.0 billion from unrealized gains on equity securities.

## Segments and drivers
*   Google Cloud growth was led by increases in the Google Cloud Platform (GCP) across enterprise AI Solutions and enterprise AI Infrastructure, alongside core GCP services.
*   Search query growth is being driven by popular AI features, while the Gemini App reported 950 million monthly active users and Gemini models process 22 billion API tokens per minute.
*   YouTube viewership for major world events remains strong, with over 1.7 billion unique viewers watching World Cup-related videos during the FIFA World Cup 2026.
*   Nearly 90% of the Fortune 100 companies are now using Gemini Enterprise, indicating wide adoption of the company's AI offerings.

## Outlook
*   The release does not provide specific numerical guidance for future quarters or full-year revenue targets.
*   Management stated they have exciting momentum across the board but did not offer quantitative projections for upcoming periods.
*   No explicit outlook regarding capital expenditure plans beyond general corporate purposes was provided in this document.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   In June 2026, the company issued a combination of Class A stock, Class C stock, and mandatory convertible preferred stock generating aggregate net proceeds of $49.6 billion for general corporate purposes.
*   The company also entered into an equity distribution agreement to sell up to $40.0 billion of Class A and Class C stock through an At-the-Market Program, though no shares had been sold as of June 30, 2026.
*   Senior unsecured notes were issued in the second quarter of 2026 for net proceeds of $20.3 billion to be used for general corporate purposes.

## Watch items
*   The release states that fluctuations in the value of investments may be affected by market dynamics and could significantly contribute to the volatility of other income and expense in future periods, given the massive gain recorded this quarter.
*   Management noted that proceeds from equity issuances are intended for capital expenditures to scale AI infrastructure and global compute; monitoring the pace of these expenditures against the $49.6 billion raised is necessary.
*   The release indicates that certain costs related to shared AI research and development, corporate initiatives, and fines are not allocated to segments, requiring careful review of Alphabet-level activities in future filings.
