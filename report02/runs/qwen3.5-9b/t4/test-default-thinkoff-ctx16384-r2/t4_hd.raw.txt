**The Home Depot Reports Strong Q2 Sales and Reaffirms Fiscal 2026 Guidance**

## Results
*   The Company reported second quarter fiscal 2026 sales of $47.9 billion, representing an increase of $2.6 billion or 5.7% from the same period in fiscal 2025.
*   Net earnings for the quarter were $4.8 billion, or $4.79 per diluted share, compared with net earnings of $4.6 billion, or $4.58 per diluted share, in the second quarter of fiscal 2025.
*   Comparable sales for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 increased 1.7%, while comparable sales in the U.S. specifically increased 1.3%.
*   Adjusted(1) diluted earnings per share for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 were $4.92, compared with adjusted diluted earnings per share of $4.68 in the same period of fiscal 2025.

## Segments and drivers
*   Management stated that broad-based demand was observed across the business as customers continued to engage in smaller projects.
*   The Company attributes this quarter's results to investments across the business and associates' focus on customer service.
*   Fiscal 2026 guidance includes IEEPA tariff refunds, which are expected to partially offset unplanned fuel, energy, and other product input costs throughout the fiscal year.

## Outlook
*   The Company reaffirms its fiscal 2026 guidance, including total sales growth of approximately 2.5% to 4.5% and comparable sales growth of approximately flat to 2.0%.
*   Diluted earnings-per-share are guided to grow approximately flat to 4.0% from $14.23 in fiscal 2025, while adjusted(1) diluted earnings-per-share are guided to grow approximately flat to 4.0% from $14.69 in fiscal 2025.
*   The Company reaffirms plans for approximately 15 new stores and an effective tax rate of approximately 24.3% for the full year.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   At the end of the second quarter, the company operated a total of 2,364 retail stores and over 1,340 SRS locations across all 50 states, the District of Columbia, Puerto Rico, the U.S. Virgin Islands, Guam, 10 Canadian provinces and Mexico.
*   The Company employs over 470,000 associates.
*   Capital expenditures are guided to be approximately 2.5% of total sales for fiscal 2026.

## Watch items
*   Monitor the impact of IEEPA tariff refunds on fuel, energy, and other product input costs as these factors are explicitly noted in the guidance explanation.
*   Track the execution of approximately 15 new store openings against the backdrop of a dynamic environment mentioned by leadership.
*   Observe the performance of adjusted(1) operating margin, which is guided between 12.8% and 13.0%, noting that this non-GAAP measure excludes amortization expense from acquired intangible assets.

(1) As used in this earnings release, adjusted operating income, adjusted operating margin, and adjusted diluted earnings per share are non-GAAP financial measures.
