**IBM Reports Second Quarter 2026 Results with Updated Full-Year Revenue Growth Guidance**

## Results
*   IBM reported second-quarter 2026 revenue of $17.2 billion, representing a flat year-over-year change at GAAP and a one percentage point decline in pre-tax income margin to 14.4%.
*   Operating (non-GAAP) pre-tax income for the quarter was $3.3 billion, reflecting a three percentage point increase in margin compared to the prior year period.
*   Free cash flow for the quarter was $2.5 billion, down $0.3 billion year-over-year, while net cash from operating activities reached $2.6 billion, an increase of $0.9 billion year-over-year.
*   The company returned $1.6 billion to shareholders in dividends during the second quarter and ended the period with $8.2 billion in cash, restricted cash, and marketable securities.

## Segments and drivers
*   Software revenues totaled $7.8 billion, up five percent year-over-year, driven by strong performance in Hybrid Cloud (Red Hat) which grew eleven percent, and Data which rose nineteen percent.
*   Consulting revenues were $5.3 billion, remaining flat year-over-year but increasing one percent at constant currency, with Strategy and Technology and Intelligent Operations both posting a one percent increase at constant currency.
*   Infrastructure revenues declined seven percent to $3.8 billion, primarily due to a forty-two percent drop in IBM Z sales, though Distributed Infrastructure grew thirty-seven percent and Power and Storage built an order backlog of nearly $500 million.
*   Financing revenues increased twelve percent to $0.2 billion, rising eleven percent at constant currency.

## Outlook
*   Management now expects full-year 2026 constant currency revenue growth in the range of four-to-five percent, with currency expected to be neutral to growth for the year at current foreign exchange rates.
*   The company continues to expect full-year free cash flow to increase by about $1 billion year-over-year and anticipates improved pre-tax income margin expansion for the full year.
*   No specific guidance was provided regarding third-quarter results or adjusted earnings per share figures beyond the updated full-year revenue expectations.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   IBM invested $10.5 billion in acquisitions during the first six months of 2026, while debt, including IBM Financing debt of $13.0 billion, totaled $62.0 billion as of June 30, 2026.
*   Capital expenditures for the quarter were $229 million, and the company purchased marketable securities totaling $1.259 billion while disposing of $1.152 billion in investments.
*   The balance sheet shows total assets of $152.1 billion, with goodwill at $74.6 billion and intangibles, net at $14.0 billion.

## Watch items
*   Monitor the divergence between GAAP revenue performance, which was flat, and the management's confidence in capturing growth opportunities as AI adoption moves from experimentation to enterprise-scale deployment.
*   Track the significant decline in IBM Z revenues of forty-two percent to determine if this contraction is a temporary cyclical fluctuation or indicative of broader structural shifts in mainframe demand.
*   Observe the reduction in cash and marketable securities by $6.3 billion year-over-year alongside heavy acquisition spending to assess liquidity management strategies under current economic conditions.
