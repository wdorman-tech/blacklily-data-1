**NVIDIA Reports Record Q2 FY27 Revenue of $96.2 Billion with Data Center Growth of 117%**

## Results
*   NVIDIA reported second-quarter fiscal 2027 revenue of $96.2 billion, representing an increase of 18% from the previous quarter and 106% from a year ago.
*   Data Center revenue reached $89.0 billion for the quarter, up 117% from a year ago and 18% from the prior quarter.
*   GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins were both 75.0% for the second quarter of fiscal 2027.
*   GAAP diluted earnings per share were $2.46, while non-GAAP diluted earnings per share were $2.22.
*   The company returned approximately $26.0 billion to shareholders through share repurchases and cash dividends during the quarter.

## Segments and drivers
*   Data Center revenue growth was driven by the ramp of the NVIDIA Vera Rubin platform into full production with racks running at partners including CoreWeave, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, and Nebius.
*   The NVIDIA Spectrum-6 switch systems supporting both pluggable and co-packaged optics are arriving across the world's gigascale AI factories as part of the Vera Rubin platform.
*   Strategic partnerships were announced to establish independent compute financing platforms with Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs, and KKR to mobilize over $500 billion of third-party capital for AI infrastructure buildout.
*   Edge Computing revenue was $7.2 billion, up 13% from the previous quarter and 27% from a year ago, supported by partnerships with Microsoft to reinvent the Windows PC with NVIDIA RTX Spark.

## Outlook
*   For the third quarter of fiscal 2027, revenue is expected to be $108.0 billion, plus or minus 2%.
*   GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins are expected to be 74.0%, plus or minus 50 basis points.
*   NVIDIA is not assuming any Data Center compute revenue from China in its outlook for the third quarter of fiscal 2027.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   As of the end of the second quarter, the company had approximately $99.0 billion remaining under its share repurchase authorization.
*   The next quarterly cash dividend is expected to be $0.25 per share on October 1, 2026, to all shareholders of record on September 10, 2026.
*   For the full year fiscal 2027, NVIDIA expects GAAP and non-GAAP tax rates to be between 16.0% and 18.0%, excluding any discrete items and material changes to NVIDIA's tax environment.

## Watch items
*   Monitor the execution of the $500 billion third-party capital mobilization plan subject to definitive agreements with major financial institutions, as this represents a significant expansion of the AI infrastructure financing ecosystem.
*   Verify whether the absence of assumed Data Center compute revenue from China in the Q3 outlook impacts actual results given the company's global footprint and recent geopolitical developments.
*   Track the deployment of NVIDIA Vera CPUs for SpaceXAI agentic AI applications and the rollout of the NVIDIA DSX platform to ensure these new product launches translate into sustained revenue growth beyond the current quarter.
