**Visa Delivers Strong Q3 2026 Results with 14% Revenue Growth and Robust Volume Expansion**

## Results
*   Visa reported GAAP net income of $5.6 billion, representing a 7% increase over the prior year, while non-GAAP net income reached $6.3 billion, an 8% increase.
*   Net revenue totaled $11.6 billion for the quarter, reflecting a 14% year-over-year increase or 13% on a constant-dollar basis.
*   GAAP earnings per share were $2.97, up 10%, whereas non-GAAP earnings per share stood at $3.32, marking an 11% rise.
*   The company executed share repurchases and dividends totaling $6.2 billion during the period.

## Segments and drivers
*   Payments volume increased 10% year-over-year on a constant-dollar basis for the three months ended June 30, 2026, while processed transactions rose 10%.
*   Cross-border volume excluding intra-Europe transactions grew 12% on a constant-dollar basis, and total cross-border volume expanded 13%.
*   Data processing revenue increased 17% to $6.0 billion, international transaction revenue grew 6% to $3.9 billion, and other revenue surged 45% to $1.5 billion.
*   Client incentives amounted to $4.7 billion, an 18% increase over the prior year, while GAAP operating expenses rose 19% primarily driven by personnel expenses.

## Outlook
*   Management stated that consumer and business spending remains resilient, with the strategy continuing to deliver strong performance across consumer payments, commercial, and money movement solutions.
*   The company is designing, building, and shipping products at an increased velocity to position Visa, its clients, and the ecosystem to capture future opportunities.
*   No specific numerical guidance or outlook figures were provided in this release regarding future quarters or full-year targets.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   Cash, cash equivalents, and investment securities stood at $13.9 billion as of June 30, 2026.
*   The company had $28.4 billion of remaining authorized funds for share repurchases as of the quarter end.
*   Total assets decreased to $94.59 billion from $99.627 billion in the prior year period, while total liabilities declined to $59.412 billion from $61.718 billion.

## Watch items
*   Monitor the trajectory of litigation provisions, which included a $237 million charge for the interchange multidistrict litigation case and a $253 million provision in the current quarter's operating expenses.
*   Track the impact of foreign currency fluctuations on reported earnings, noting that GAAP earnings per share growth was approximately 9% on a constant-dollar basis excluding these impacts.
*   Observe the trend in client incentives, which rose 18% to $4.7 billion, as this category significantly influences net revenue calculations and could affect future margin compression if growth outpaces incentive costs.
