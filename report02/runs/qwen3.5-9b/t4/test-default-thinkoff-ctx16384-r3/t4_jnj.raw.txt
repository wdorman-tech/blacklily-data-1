**Johnson & Johnson Raises 2026 Guidance Following Strong Q2 Reported Sales Growth and Operational Performance**

## Results
*   Second-quarter 2026 reported sales grew 6.6% to $25.3 Billion, driven by operational growth of 5.6% and adjusted operational growth of 5.7%.
*   Diluted earnings per share for the quarter were $2.27 on a GAAP basis, while adjusted diluted EPS stood at $2.90.
*   The company increased its full-year 2026 guidance, estimating reported sales between $100.8 Billion and $101.4 Billion with a midpoint of $101.1 Billion, representing a 7.3% increase versus the prior year.
*   Adjusted EPS guidance for 2026 was raised by $0.13 to a range of $11.60 to $11.75, with a midpoint of $11.68, reflecting an 8.2% increase versus the prior year.

## Segments and drivers
*   Innovative Medicine worldwide operational sales grew 6.8%, primarily driven by DARZALEX, CARVYKTI, TECVAYLI, RYBREVANT/LAZCLUZE in Oncology, TREMFYA and Other Immunology in Immunology, and SPRAVATO and CAPLYTA in Neuroscience.
*   MedTech worldwide operational sales grew 3.6%, supported by wound closure products and biosurgery products in Surgery, electrophysiology products and Shockwave in Cardiovascular, contact lenses in Vision, and trauma in Orthopaedics.
*   Growth in Innovative Medicine was partially offset by STELARA and REMICADE in Immunology, as well as IMBRUVICA and ZYTIGA in Oncology.
*   MedTech growth was negatively impacted by net acquisitions and divestitures of 10 basis points, while Innovative Medicine growth was negatively impacted by divestitures of 10 basis points.

## Outlook
*   Johnson & Johnson does not provide GAAP financial measures on a forward-looking basis due to uncertainty regarding legal proceedings, unusual gains and losses, acquisition-related expenses, and purchase accounting fair value adjustments.
*   The company raised its adjusted operational sales guidance for 2026 to a range of 6.2% – 6.8% versus the prior year, with a midpoint of 6.5%.
*   Adjusted operational EPS guidance for 2026 was increased to a range of $11.50 – $11.65, with a midpoint of $11.58, representing a 7.3% increase versus the prior year.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   Second-quarter free cash flow was estimated at approximately $8.7 Billion for the year-to-date period as of July 15, 2026.
*   The company announced an investment of more than $1 Billion to strengthen U.S. Vision manufacturing in Jacksonville, Florida.
*   DePuy Synthes appointed Christina Zamarro as Chief Financial Officer.

## Watch items
*   Monitor the impact of divestitures on Innovative Medicine growth, which negatively impacted operational sales by 10 basis points in the quarter.
*   Track the outcome of legal proceedings and unusual gains or losses, as the company explicitly states it cannot predict these GAAP measures with reasonable certainty for forward-looking periods.
*   Observe the execution of the planned separation of the Orthopaedics business and the ability to realize anticipated benefits from this strategic move.
