I am writing to provide an update on your Hollinrake Revocable Trust account for the second quarter of 2026. We understand that seeing a decline in your portfolio can be concerning, especially when you have been asking why the protective measures are not preventing losses during this period. Please know that our goal remains to preserve your capital while seeking growth over time.

Your account experienced a net return of -2.4% for the quarter ended June 30, 2026. This figure includes the cost of fees and option premiums. For context, the gross-of-fees return was -2.2%, and the comparison index for the same period returned -1.6%. Since our inception on January 2, 2020, your account has generated an annualized net return of 5.9%. Year-to-date through June 30, 2026, the net return is -0.7%.

The market environment in Q2 presented specific challenges for our hedging strategy. To manage risk as equity prices fluctuated, we rolled our put spread to a September expiry with strikes at 95% and 80% of the index level. We also adjusted our short call strike to 104% of the index level, which is higher than the previous roll at 106%. These adjustments were designed to fund the wider put spread and maintain protection. Consequently, your net equity exposure after hedges stood at 71% as of June 30, down from 78% at the end of March. While these tools help limit losses in downturns, they also cap gains during strong rallies, which is why returns may be lower than the underlying index in certain periods.

We are also writing to inform you of a change to your advisory fee structure. Effective September 15, 2026, the annual rate for all assets in the Hedged Equity strategy will increase from 0.85% to 1.00%. This adjustment reflects our transition from monthly overlay rolls by a subadviser to daily management by our own options desk, allowing us to respond more quickly to market changes. As required by Section 5 of your advisory agreement, this letter serves as the necessary thirty-day written notice. You may terminate your agreement without penalty at any time before the effective date if you choose not to proceed with the new rate.

Options strategies can limit both losses and gains, the hedge may not perform as intended in all market conditions, and the cost of the options will reduce returns in periods when the hedge is not needed.
