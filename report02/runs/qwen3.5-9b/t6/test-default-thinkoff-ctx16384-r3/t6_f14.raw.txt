Dear Mrs. Delacourt and Mr. Ambrose,

I am pleased to share the quarterly update for your Delacourt-Ambrose Charitable Remainder Unitrust for the first quarter of 2026. This letter reflects the performance of your income-oriented balanced strategy, which maintains a diversified allocation of 55% global equity and 45% fixed income.

During this reporting period, your portfolio generated a return of 2.2% net of fees. For context, the gross-of-fees return was 2.4%. Your performance outpaced the comparison index, which returned 1.8% over the same timeframe. Since the inception of the trust on October 2, 2017, your annualized return has been 6.8% net of fees. Year-to-date through March 31, 2026, the net return remains at 2.2%.

We successfully executed our plan to fund upcoming distributions without needing to sell any risk assets. We increased our holdings in cash and Treasury bills to 6.5% of trust assets. This position allows us to pre-fund the June and September unitrust payments smoothly. Additionally, we adjusted our bond portfolio by trimming high-yield bonds from 7.5% down to 3.5%. This move was made because credit spreads have narrowed significantly, reaching levels not seen since 2021.

As scheduled, the first quarterly unitrust payment of $31,250 was made on March 31. This amount represents 5% of the trust's net assets as valued on January 2, 2026. The total annual distribution will be paid in four equal installments throughout the year.

We are also writing to inform you of a change to our advisory fee structure that aligns your account with our firm's standard schedule for individual clients. This adjustment applies specifically to the first $3,000,000 of trust assets. The annual rate will decrease from 1.00% to 0.90%. Assets above this threshold remain at the unchanged rate of 0.70%, though your current balance does not yet reach that level. This new fee rate will take effect on June 1, 2026. Please note that the invoice for the second quarter will reflect a prorated amount based on this change.

Unitrust payments are set by the trust instrument as a fixed percentage of the trust's net assets as revalued each year, will rise or fall with the value of the trust, and are not a measure of investment performance.

Thank you for your continued trust in our partnership. We remain committed to providing steady income and preserving capital for your future needs.
