1.  **Analyze the Request:**
    *   Role: Marketing review analyst at an SEC-registered investment adviser.
    *   Task: Review draft marketing materials against a fixed checklist (Rule 206(4)-1 derived).
    *   Input: A draft marketing piece (invitation to a retirement income workshop).
    *   Output Format: JSON object with key "flags", containing a list of objects. Each object has keys "category" (C1-C9), "quote" (verbatim text), "reason" (one sentence).
    *   Constraints: No markdown, no preamble, no commentary. Only the JSON object.
    *   Checklist Rules:
        *   C1: Testimonial/endorsement without disclosure (client status, compensation, conflicts).
        *   C2: Gross performance without net performance shown with equal prominence.
        *   C3: Performance of portfolio other than private fund without 1-, 5-, 10-year periods.
        *   C4: Performance time periods/results not fair and balanced (favorable window).
        *   C5: Hypothetical/model/backtested/projected performance without criteria/assumptions/risks.
        *   C6: Material statement of fact untrue or unsubstantiated (absolute claims, top/best without support).
        *   C7: Third-party rating/ranking without date, period, source, compensation.
        *   C8: Performance of selected positions/subset without total portfolio performance.
        *   C9: Benefits described without fair and balanced treatment of material risks/limitations.
