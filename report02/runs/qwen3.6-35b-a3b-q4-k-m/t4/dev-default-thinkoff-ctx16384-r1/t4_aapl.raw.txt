**Apple Reports Record June Quarter Revenue of $109.4 Billion and Diluted EPS of $2.02**

## Results

*   Total company revenue for the fiscal third quarter ended June 27, 2026, was $109.4 billion, representing a 16 percent increase year over year.
*   Diluted earnings per share reached $2.02, up 29 percent from the prior year period; this figure included a favorable impact of approximately $0.11 from tariff refunds.
*   Gross margin for the quarter was 50.1 percent, which included a favorable impact of approximately 2 percentage points from tariff refunds.
*   Operating cash flow set a new June quarter record, as noted by management, though the specific dollar amount is not explicitly stated in the provided text.

## Segments and drivers

*   iPhone revenue was $54.3 billion, contributing significantly to the double-digit growth across product categories mentioned by leadership.
*   Services revenue reached a record $30.7 billion for the June quarter, supporting the overall top-line expansion.
*   Mac revenue grew to $10.4 billion, while Wearables, Home and Accessories revenue was $7.9 billion; iPad revenue was $6.2 billion.
*   Geographic performance showed double-digit growth in every region, with Americas net sales at $45.8 billion, Europe at $29.4 billion, Greater China at $18.8 billion, Japan at $6.6 billion, and Rest of Asia Pacific at $8.9 billion.

## Outlook

*   The release contains no specific financial guidance or quantitative outlook for future quarters beyond the current period results.
*   Management highlighted the introduction of "all-new Siri AI" and new child safety features at WWDC26 as key software innovations, but provided no revenue projections tied to these launches.
*   Forward-looking statements regarding future business plans are included in the standard legal disclaimer, but no specific targets or estimates are offered.

## Capital and balance sheet

*   Cash and cash equivalents increased to $39.5 billion at quarter-end from $35.9 billion at the prior fiscal year-end.
*   Total shareholders’ equity rose to $107.5 billion compared to $73.7 billion at the start of the fiscal year.
*   The board declared a cash dividend of $0.27 per share, payable on August 13, 2026, to shareholders of record as of August 10, 2026.

## Watch items

*   Gross margin benefited from approximately 2 percentage points of tariff refunds; the committee should ask how sustainable this benefit is and what the normalized margin would be without these refunds.
*   Inventories increased significantly to $11.1 billion from $5.7 billion at the prior year-end; the committee should monitor whether this buildup indicates slowing demand or strategic inventory positioning for new product cycles.
*   Operating expenses, specifically Research and development, rose to $11.7 billion from $8.9 billion in the prior year quarter; the committee should assess if this spending acceleration aligns with the stated AI initiatives and long-term return profiles.
