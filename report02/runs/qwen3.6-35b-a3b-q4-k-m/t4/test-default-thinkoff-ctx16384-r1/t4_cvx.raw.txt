**Chevron Reports Record Second-Quarter Earnings Driven by Hess Integration and High Commodity Prices**

## Results

*   Reported earnings for the second quarter of 2026 were $12.1 billion, or $6.11 per diluted share, compared to $2.5 billion in the prior-year period.
*   Adjusted earnings, which exclude special items and foreign currency effects, totaled $12.0 billion, or $6.06 per diluted share for the second quarter of 2026.
*   Cash flow from operations reached $22.6 billion in the second quarter of 2026, a significant increase from $8.6 billion in the same period last year.
*   Free cash flow was $18.1 billion for the second quarter of 2026, while adjusted free cash flow stood at $15.4 billion for the same period.

## Segments and drivers

*   Upstream earnings were $8.2 billion in the second quarter of 2026, supported by record U.S. production and higher liquids realizations; worldwide net oil-equivalent production increased 20 percent year-over-year to 4,070 thousand barrels per day.
*   Downstream earnings totaled $4.9 billion in the second quarter of 2026, driven by higher margins on refined product sales and record crude unit throughput at U.S. refineries of 1.07 million barrels per day.
*   The company achieved its structural cost reduction target six months early, capturing $3 billion in annual run-rate savings, and delivered $1.5 billion of annual run-rate synergies from the Hess Corporation acquisition within one year of closing.

## Outlook

*   The release does not provide specific financial guidance or outlook for future quarters; it notes that Chevron publishes a "Sensitivities and Forward Guidance" document updated quarterly on its website.
*   Chevron signed a 20-year power purchase agreement with Microsoft to provide 2.67 gigawatts of behind-the-meter power for a West Texas data center, positioning the company to generate resilient cash flows through this new energy business.
*   The company aims to reduce structural costs by $3 billion to $4 billion by the end of 2026 as part of its ongoing cost discipline program.

## Capital and balance sheet

*   Total debt was reduced by a record $8.4 billion in the second quarter of 2026, bringing total debt to $37.1 billion at June 30, 2026.
*   The net debt-to-CFFO ratio improved to 0.6x at June 30, 2026, down from 0.8x in the second quarter of 2025.
*   Chevron declared a quarterly dividend of $1.78 per share, payable on September 10, 2026, to common stockholders of record as of August 19, 2026.

## Watch items

*   International upstream production faced curtailments in the Partitioned Zone between Saudi Arabia and Kuwait due to the Middle East conflict; how will geopolitical instability further impact international output and logistics?
*   International downstream refinery crude unit inputs decreased 10 percent year-over-year due to supply disruptions from the Middle East conflict; what is the timeline for restoring these volumes to pre-conflict levels?
*   The company announced agreements to sell its 50 percent interest in the Singapore Refining Company and other downstream assets, with a transaction expected to close in 2027; are there regulatory or counterparty risks that could delay this closing?
