**Alphabet Reports Q2 2026 Revenue of $119.8 Billion, Driven by Google Cloud Acceleration and Significant Equity Gains**

## Results

*   Consolidated Alphabet revenues for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, increased 24% to $119.8 billion, with constant currency revenue growth of 23%.
*   Net income available to common stockholders rose 298% to $112.1 billion, resulting in diluted EPS of $9.11, compared to $2.31 in the prior year period.
*   Operating income increased 30% to $40.8 billion, expanding the operating margin by 2 percentage points to 34%.
*   Other income reflected a net gain of $98.0 billion, primarily driven by net unrealized gains on equity securities.

## Segments and drivers

*   Google Services revenues grew 15% to $94.5 billion, supported by 17% growth in Google Search & other, 15% in Google subscriptions, platforms, and devices, and 13% in YouTube ads.
*   Google Cloud revenues accelerated significantly, increasing 82% to $24.8 billion, led by growth in Google Cloud Platform enterprise AI Solutions, enterprise AI Infrastructure, and core services.
*   Alphabet-level activities, primarily reflecting shared AI research and development expenses, resulted in an operating loss of $5.8 billion, up from a $3.4 billion loss in the prior year period.

## Outlook

*   The release does not provide specific financial guidance or outlook for future quarters; it only notes that actual results may differ materially from forward-looking statements based on risks detailed in SEC filings.

## Capital and balance sheet

*   In June 2026, the company issued Class A stock, Class C stock, and mandatory convertible preferred stock for aggregate net proceeds of $49.6 billion to scale AI infrastructure.
*   The company also issued senior unsecured notes for net proceeds of $20.3 billion in the second quarter of 2026 for general corporate purposes.
*   As of June 30, 2026, total cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities stood at $242.5 billion, while long-term debt increased to $98.2 billion from $46.5 billion at year-end 2025.
