**The Home Depot Reports Second-Quarter Fiscal 2026 Sales Growth of 5.7% and Reaffirms Full-Year Guidance**

## Results

*   Total sales for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 reached $47.9 billion, an increase of $2.6 billion, or 5.7%, compared to the second quarter of fiscal 2025.
*   Net earnings for the quarter were $4.8 billion, or $4.79 per diluted share, compared with net earnings of $4.6 billion, or $4.58 per diluted share, in the same period of fiscal 2025.
*   Adjusted(1) diluted earnings per share for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 were $4.92, compared with adjusted diluted earnings per share of $4.68 in the same period of fiscal 2025.
*   Comparable sales for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 increased 1.7%, driven by a 1.3% increase in comparable sales in the U.S.

## Segments and drivers

*   The company reported broad-based demand across the business, with customers continuing to engage in smaller projects during the quarter.
*   Comparable customer transactions decreased 1.0% for the quarter, while comparable average ticket increased 2.8%.
*   Operating income for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 was $6.8 billion, compared with operating income of $6.6 billion in the same period of fiscal 2025.

## Outlook

*   The company reaffirms its fiscal 2026 guidance, which includes IEEPA tariff refunds expected to partially offset unplanned fuel, energy, and other product input costs throughout the fiscal year.
*   Fiscal 2026 guidance projects total sales growth of approximately 2.5% to 4.5% and comparable sales growth of approximately flat to 2.0%.
*   Guidance includes an expected gross margin of approximately 33.1%, an operating margin of approximately 12.4% to 12.6%, and adjusted(1) diluted earnings-per-share to grow approximately flat to 4.0% from $14.69 in fiscal 2025.

## Capital and balance sheet

*   At the end of the second quarter, the company operated a total of 2,364 retail stores and over 1,340 SRS locations across all 50 states, the District of Columbia, Puerto Rico, the U.S. Virgin Islands, Guam, 10 Canadian provinces and Mexico.
*   Cash and cash equivalents at the end of the second quarter were $2.1 billion, compared with $2.8 billion at the end of the second quarter of fiscal 2025.
*   Total stockholders’ equity at the end of the second quarter was $16.6 billion, compared with total stockholders’ equity of $10.7 billion at the end of the second quarter of fiscal 2025.

## Watch items

*   The release notes that comparable customer transactions decreased 1.0% for the quarter; what specific initiatives are being implemented to reverse this decline in foot traffic?
*   Guidance includes IEEPA tariff refunds to offset input costs; how sensitive is the gross margin outlook to potential changes in these refund expectations or further trade policy shifts?
*   The company reports adjusted(1) operating margin guidance of approximately 12.8% to 13.0% for fiscal 2026, excluding an expected approximately 40 basis point impact from acquired intangible asset amortization; what is the trajectory of this amortization expense in subsequent quarters?

(1) The Company reports its financial results in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP). As used in this earnings release, adjusted operating income, adjusted operating margin, and adjusted diluted earnings per share are non-GAAP financial measures.
