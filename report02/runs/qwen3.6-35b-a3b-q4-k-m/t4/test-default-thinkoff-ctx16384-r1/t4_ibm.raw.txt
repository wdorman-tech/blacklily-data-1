**IBM Reports Second-Quarter Revenue Growth Driven by Software, While Updating Full-Year Outlook**

## Results

*   Total revenue for the second quarter of 2026 was $17.2 billion, representing a one percent year-over-year increase. GAAP diluted earnings per share from continuing operations were $2.27, down two percent compared to the prior year period.
*   Operating (non-GAAP) diluted earnings per share for the quarter were $2.93, an increase of five percent year-over-year. The company reported a gross profit margin of 57.7 percent on a GAAP basis and 59.4 percent on an operating (non-GAAP) basis.
*   Net income from continuing operations was $2.2 billion for the quarter. Pre-tax income from continuing operations stood at $2.5 billion, with a pre-tax income margin of 14.4 percent.
*   For the first six months of 2026, total revenue reached $33.1 billion, up five percent year-over-year. GAAP diluted earnings per share for the year-to-date period were $3.55.

## Segments and drivers

*   Software revenue grew five percent to $7.8 billion, driven by a 19 percent increase in Data revenues and an 11 percent rise in Hybrid Cloud (Red Hat) revenues. Transaction Processing revenues declined eight percent.
*   Consulting revenues were flat at $5.3 billion, with both Strategy and Technology and Intelligent Operations reporting flat results on a reported basis, though both grew one percent at constant currency.
*   Infrastructure revenues decreased seven percent to $3.8 billion. While IBM Z revenues fell 42 percent, Distributed Infrastructure revenues surged 37 percent, building an order backlog of nearly $500 million.

## Outlook

*   The company now expects full-year constant currency revenue growth in the range of four-to-five percent. At current foreign exchange rates, currency is expected to be neutral to growth for the year.
*   IBM continues to expect free cash flow to increase by about $1 billion year-over-year for the full year 2026.
*   Management expects improved pre-tax income margin expansion for the full year, citing productivity efforts and AI-driven operational efficiencies.

## Capital and balance sheet

*   The company generated net cash from operating activities of $2.6 billion in the second quarter, up $0.9 billion year-over-year. Free cash flow was $2.5 billion, down $0.3 billion year-over-year.
*   IBM ended the second quarter with $8.2 billion in cash, restricted cash, and marketable securities, a decrease of $6.3 billion from year-end 2025.
*   Total debt, including IBM Financing debt of $13.0 billion, totaled $62.0 billion, an increase of $0.7 billion year-to-date. The company invested $10.5 billion in acquisitions during the first six months of the year.

## Watch items

*   Software segment performance shows divergence: while Data and Hybrid Cloud grew significantly, Transaction Processing declined eight percent. How will the company mitigate the decline in legacy transaction processing as it scales high-growth AI and cloud offerings?
*   Infrastructure results highlight a structural shift with IBM Z down 42 percent offset by Distributed Infrastructure up 37 percent. Can the $500 million backlog in Distributed Infrastructure sustain growth to fully offset the continued contraction in mainframe revenues?
*   The company reported $10.5 billion in acquisitions for the first half of the year while debt increased. What is the integration status of these recent acquisitions, and how will the company manage the associated amortization charges within its full-year margin expansion guidance?
