**Johnson & Johnson Raises 2026 Guidance on Strong Q2 Sales Growth and Pipeline Momentum**

## Results

*   Reported second-quarter 2026 sales reached $25.3 billion, representing a 6.6% increase from the prior year period. Operational sales growth was 5.6%, while adjusted operational sales growth was 5.7%.
*   Second-quarter reported earnings per share (EPS) were $2.27, down 0.9% from the previous year. Adjusted EPS for the quarter was $2.90, reflecting a 4.7% increase compared to the prior year period.
*   Net earnings for the quarter totaled $5.5 billion, a slight decrease of 0.1% year-over-year. Adjusted net earnings were $7.1 billion, up 5.7% from the comparable period in 2025.
*   The company reported second-quarter free cash flow of approximately $8.7 billion, compared to $6.2 billion in the same quarter last year.

## Segments and drivers

*   Innovative Medicine sales grew 7.8% reported, driven by strong performance in Oncology (DARZALEX, CARVYKTI, TECVAYLI, RYBREVANT/LAZCLUZE), Immunology (TREMFYA), and Neuroscience (SPRAVATO, CAPLYTA). This growth was partially offset by declines in STELARA, REMICADE, IMBRUVICA, and ZYTIGA.
*   MedTech sales increased 4.5% reported, with operational growth of 3.6%. Key drivers included wound closure products, biosurgery products, electrophysiology products, Shockwave, contact lenses, and trauma segments.
*   Regional performance showed U.S. sales growing 7.3% reported, while International sales grew 5.7% reported, with operational growth of 3.4%.

## Outlook

*   Johnson & Johnson raised its full-year 2026 estimated reported sales guidance to a midpoint of $101.1 billion, representing 7.3% growth at the midpoint.
*   The company increased its full-year 2026 adjusted EPS guidance by $0.13 to a midpoint of $11.68, reflecting 8.2% growth at the midpoint.
*   Adjusted operational EPS for 2026 is now estimated at a midpoint of $11.58, representing 7.3% growth.

## Capital and balance sheet

*   The company generated approximately $8.7 billion in free cash flow during the second quarter of 2026.
*   Johnson & Johnson announced an investment of more than $1 billion to strengthen U.S. vision manufacturing in Jacksonville, Florida.
*   The company entered into agreements to acquire Firefly Bio, Inc., and miniature radiofrequency tracking technology to expand its oncology pipeline and joint reconstruction portfolio.

## Watch items

*   Management states it cannot predict GAAP financial measures for 2026 due to uncertainties regarding legal proceedings and acquisition-related expenses. What are the primary components of "special items" excluded from adjusted figures that could cause significant variance between reported and adjusted results?
