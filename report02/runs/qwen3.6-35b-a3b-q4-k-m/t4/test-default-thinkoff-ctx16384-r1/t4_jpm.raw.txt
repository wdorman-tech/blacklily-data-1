**JPMorgan Chase Reports Second-Quarter 2026 Net Income of $21.2 Billion, Driven by Record Revenue and Significant Investment Gains**

## Results

*   Reported net income for the second quarter of 2026 was $21.2 billion, or $7.70 per share. This figure includes significant items: a $4.6 billion net gain related to Visa shares ($1.27 per share) and $1.0 billion in gains on certain equity investments ($0.29 per share).
*   Net income excluding these significant items was $16.9 billion, or $6.14 per share. The release notes that results excluding significant items are non-GAAP financial measures.
*   Reported revenue was $57.3 billion, while managed revenue (a non-GAAP measure) was $58.0 billion. Both reported and managed revenue increased 28% and 27%, respectively, compared to the second quarter of 2025.
*   Noninterest expense was $27.3 billion, up 15% from the prior year period. The reported overhead ratio was 48%, while the managed overhead ratio (non-GAAP) was 47%.
*   Return on tangible common equity (ROTCE), a non-GAAP measure, was 29% for the quarter. ROTCE excluding significant items was 23%.

## Segments and drivers

*   **Commercial & Investment Bank (CIB):** Net income was $9.7 billion, up 46% year-over-year. Markets revenue surged 35% to $12.1 billion, driven by an 86% increase in Equity Markets revenue. Investment Banking fees rose 30% to $3.3 billion, contributing to a total CIB net revenue of $24.9 billion, up 27%.
*   **Consumer & Community Banking (CCB):** Net income was $5.3 billion, up 3%. Card Services and Auto net revenue grew 12% to $7.8 billion, supported by higher revolving balances. The Card Services net charge-off rate was 3.34%, and debit and credit card sales volume increased 10% year-over-year.
*   **Asset & Wealth Management (AWM):** Net income was $2.0 billion, up 33%. Assets under management reached $5.1 trillion, up 18% year-over-year. Revenue increased 19% to $6.9 billion, driven by higher average market levels and strong net inflows of $50 billion in long-term AUM.

## Outlook

*   The release does not provide specific financial guidance or quantitative outlook for future quarters.
*   Chairman and CEO Jamie Dimon highlighted that revenue in each line of business hit a new record, citing elevated market activity and rigorous execution as key drivers.
*   Management noted that while the U.S. economy has shown resiliency with stronger business investment and hiring, risks such as geopolitical tensions, sticky inflation, and large global fiscal deficits remain shifting below the surface.

## Capital and balance sheet

*   Basel III common equity Tier 1 capital was $303 billion, resulting in a Standardized ratio of 14.1% and an Advanced ratio of 14.2%.
*   Total Loss-Absorbing Capacity stood at $590 billion, with average loans of $1.5 trillion and cash and marketable securities of $1.5 trillion.
*   The Firm declared a common dividend of $4.0 billion ($1.50 per share) and executed $6.2 billion in common stock net repurchases during the quarter.

## Watch items

*   **Expense Growth vs. Revenue:** Noninterest expense grew 15% year-over-year, outpacing the growth in reported revenue (28%) but tracking closely with managed revenue (27%). The release attributes this to higher compensation and technology costs; the committee should monitor whether expense discipline can be maintained as revenue volatility persists.
*   **Credit Quality in Card Services:** CCB Card Services net charge-offs were $2.2 billion, up $70 million year-over-year, with a net charge-off rate of 3.34%. Given the release notes this was predominantly driven by Card Services, monitoring the trajectory of consumer credit health is essential.
*   **Reliance on Significant Items:** A substantial portion of reported earnings ($5.6 billion) came from non-recurring gains (Visa and equity investments). The committee should assess the sustainability of core operating performance, reflected in the $16.9 billion net income excluding these items.
