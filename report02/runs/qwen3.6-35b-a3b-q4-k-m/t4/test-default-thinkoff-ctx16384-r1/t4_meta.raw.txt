**Meta Reports Q2 2026 Revenue of $60.8 Billion, Driven by 28% Growth Despite Margin Compression and Heavy Capital Spend**

## Results

*   Total revenue for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, was $60.80 billion, representing a 28% increase year-over-year; on a constant currency basis, revenue increased by 27%.
*   Net income was $15.85 billion, down 14% from the prior year period, while diluted earnings per share declined 13% to $6.18.
*   Total costs and expenses rose 55% year-over-year to $42.03 billion, driven by a $2.40 billion charge for legal proceedings and $1.18 billion in severance expenses related to the May 2026 headcount reduction.
*   Operating income decreased to $18.78 billion from $20.44 billion in the prior year, resulting in an operating margin of 31% compared to 43% previously.

## Segments and drivers

*   Family daily active people (DAP) averaged 3.60 billion, a 3% increase year-over-year, while ad impressions grew 14% and average price per ad increased 12%.
*   Advertising revenue within the Family of Apps segment reached $59.36 billion, contributing to total segment revenue of $60.37 billion; however, operating income for this segment fell to $23.39 billion from $24.97 billion in the prior year.
*   Reality Labs reported revenue of $431 million and an operating loss of $4.62 billion, with cumulative six-month losses widening slightly to $8.65 billion from $8.74 billion in the prior year period.

## Outlook

*   The company expects third quarter 2026 total revenue to be between $61 billion and $64 billion, assuming foreign currency acts as an approximately 1% headwind to growth.
*   Full year 2026 total expenses are now projected to be in the range of $165 billion to $169 billion, a raise from previous guidance to incorporate the second quarter legal charges.
*   Capital expenditures for full year 2026 are expected to be between $130 billion and $145 billion, narrowed from the prior outlook of $125 billion to $145 billion.

## Capital and balance sheet

*   Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities totaled $90.26 billion as of June 30, 2026, while long-term debt increased to $83.66 billion from $58.74 billion at year-end 2025.
*   Free cash flow for the quarter was $784 million, calculated by subtracting purchases of property and equipment ($30.12 billion) and principal payments on finance leases ($962 million) from operating cash flow.
*   The company returned $1.35 billion to shareholders through dividend and dividend equivalent payments during the quarter.

## Watch items

*   Operating margins compressed significantly despite revenue growth; the committee should ask how much of the 55% rise in costs is structural versus one-time, particularly given the $2.40 billion legal charge and $1.18 billion severance expense included in the period.
*   Reality Labs continues to generate substantial losses with limited revenue growth; the release notes that headcount includes approximately 8,000 employees impacted by the May reduction who will no longer be reflected by the end of Q3, raising questions about future cost trajectories for this segment.
*   Management highlighted ongoing scrutiny on youth-related issues and scheduled trials in the U.S. that may result in a material loss; the committee should monitor the potential financial impact of these regulatory matters on future provisions and cash flow.
