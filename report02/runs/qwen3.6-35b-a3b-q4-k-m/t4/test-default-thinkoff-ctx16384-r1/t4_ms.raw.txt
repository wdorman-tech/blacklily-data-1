**Morgan Stanley Reports Record Second Quarter Net Revenues of $21.3 Billion and EPS of $3.46**

## Results

*   The Firm reported net revenues of $21.3 billion for the second quarter ended June 30, 2026, compared with $16.8 billion in the same period last year. Net income applicable to Morgan Stanley was $5.6 billion, or $3.46 per diluted share, versus $3.5 billion, or $2.13 per diluted share, a year ago.
*   Pre-tax income reached $7.3 billion for the quarter, up from $4.6 billion in the prior-year period. The expense efficiency ratio was 65% for the second quarter and the first half of the year.
*   Return on tangible common equity (a non-GAAP measure) was 26.6%, compared with 18.2% a year ago. Book value per share ended at $67.80, up from $61.59 in the prior-year quarter.
*   The effective tax rate for the current quarter was 23.1%.

## Segments and drivers

*   Institutional Securities reported record net revenues of $11.0 billion, driven by a 69% increase in Equity net revenues to $6.3 billion and a 58% rise in Investment Banking net revenues to $2.4 billion. Fixed Income net revenues increased 13% to $2.5 billion.
*   Wealth Management delivered record net revenues of $8.9 billion, supported by asset management fees on elevated assets and higher net interest income. The business added a record $148 billion in net new assets, bringing total fee-based client assets to $3.0 trillion.
*   Investment Management reported net revenues of $1.6 billion, primarily driven by asset management fees on higher average assets under management (AUM). AUM reached $2.0 trillion, with positive long-term net flows of $7.5 billion for the quarter.

## Outlook

The release does not provide specific financial guidance or outlook for future periods.

## Capital and balance sheet

*   The Standardized Common Equity Tier 1 capital ratio was 14.8% at the end of the second quarter, down from 15.0% in the prior-year period.
*   The Firm repurchased $1.5 billion of outstanding common stock during the quarter. The Board reauthorized a multi-year common equity share repurchase program of up to $20 billion, beginning in the third quarter of 2026.
*   The Board declared a quarterly dividend of $1.15 per share, payable on August 14, 2026.

## Watch items

*   Institutional Securities reported Other net revenues of $(152) million, primarily due to higher mark-to-market losses on corporate loans inclusive of hedges; the committee should monitor the sustainability of these trading losses relative to core revenue growth.
*   Wealth Management transactional revenues excluded mark-to-market gains on investments associated with Deferred Cash-Based Compensation (DCP) that were present in the prior year; the release notes that DCP hedges are now transitioned to derivatives, impacting compensation expense rather than net revenues, which may alter period-over-period comparability.
*   Provision for credit losses decreased to $98 million from $196 million a year ago, driven by portfolio growth and individual assessments for corporate and commercial real estate loans; the committee should assess whether this decline reflects improved credit quality or timing of specific loan impairments.
