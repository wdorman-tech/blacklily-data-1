**Microsoft Reports Q4 Revenue of $90.0 Billion Driven by 27% Cloud Growth and AI Adoption**

## Results

*   For the quarter ended June 30, 2026, revenue was $90.0 billion, an increase of 18% compared to the prior year period. On a constant currency basis, revenue increased 17%.
*   Operating income for the quarter was $40.6 billion, representing an 18% increase year-over-year.
*   Net income was $35.8 billion on a GAAP basis, up 31%, and $35.3 billion on a non-GAAP basis, up 22%. Non-GAAP results exclude the impact from investments in OpenAI.
*   Diluted earnings per share were $4.81 on a GAAP basis, an increase of 32%, and $4.74 on a non-GAAP basis, an increase of 23%.

## Segments and drivers

*   Microsoft Cloud revenue reached $59.3 billion, increasing 27% year-over-year. Azure and other cloud services revenue grew by 43%. Commercial remaining performance obligation increased 84% to $678 billion.
*   Productivity and Business Processes revenue was $37.8 billion, up 14%. Microsoft 365 Commercial cloud revenue increased 14% on a reported basis, while Microsoft 365 Consumer cloud revenue rose 24%. LinkedIn revenue increased 12%, and Dynamics 365 revenue grew 13%.
*   Intelligent Cloud revenue was $39.3 billion, up 32%. More Personal Computing revenue decreased 4% to $12.9 billion, driven by a 10% decline in XBOX content and services revenue and a 7% drop in Windows OEM and Devices revenue. Search advertising revenue excluding traffic acquisition costs increased 10%.

## Outlook

*   The release states that Microsoft will provide forward-looking guidance in connection with this quarterly earnings announcement on its earnings conference call and webcast.
*   No specific numerical guidance or outlook figures are provided within the text of this earnings release.

## Capital and balance sheet

*   Microsoft returned $10.2 billion to shareholders in the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2026 through dividends and share repurchases.
*   As of June 30, 2026, total cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments were $76.8 billion. Total stockholders’ equity stood at $442.4 billion.
*   Additions to property and equipment for the quarter were $35.8 billion, reflecting continued infrastructure investment.

## Watch items

*   The release notes that discrete items resulted in a benefit of $0.27 on diluted earnings per share, including a $3.2 billion gain from the investment in Anthropic. How sustainable is this level of investment-related income given the volatility of such assets?
*   XBOX content and services revenue decreased 10%, partially offset by severance expense and impairment charges. What are the specific drivers behind the XBOX decline, and are the associated impairment charges one-time or indicative of a longer-term trend in that segment?
