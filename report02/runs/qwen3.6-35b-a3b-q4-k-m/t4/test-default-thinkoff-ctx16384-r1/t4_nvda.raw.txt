**NVIDIA Reports Second-Quarter Fiscal 2027 Revenue of $96.2 Billion, Driven by Data Center Growth**

## Results

*   Total revenue for the quarter ended July 26, 2026, was $96.2 billion, representing an 18% increase from the previous quarter and a 106% increase from a year ago.
*   GAAP net income was $59.7 billion, while non-GAAP net income was $54.0 billion for the second quarter of fiscal 2027.
*   Diluted earnings per share were $2.46 on a GAAP basis and $2.22 on a non-GAAP basis.
*   The company returned approximately $26.0 billion to shareholders through share repurchases and cash dividends during the quarter.

## Segments and drivers

*   Data Center revenue reached $89.0 billion, up 117% from a year ago, driven by the ramp of the NVIDIA Vera Rubin platform into full production with partners including CoreWeave, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, and Nebius.
*   Edge Computing revenue was $7.2 billion, up 27% from a year ago, supported by new partnerships for Windows PC AI initiatives and the expansion of the DRIVE Hyperion robotaxi-ready platform ecosystem.
*   The company announced strategic partnerships with Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs, and KKR to establish independent compute financing platforms aimed at mobilizing over $500 billion of third-party capital for AI infrastructure buildout.

## Outlook

*   NVIDIA expects revenue for the third quarter of fiscal 2027 to be $108.0 billion, plus or minus 2%, noting it is not assuming any Data Center compute revenue from China in this outlook.
*   GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins for the third quarter are expected to be 74.0%, plus or minus 50 basis points.
*   For the full year fiscal 2027, NVIDIA expects GAAP and non-GAAP tax rates to be between 16.0% and 18.0%, excluding discrete items and material changes to the tax environment.

## Capital and balance sheet

*   As of the end of the second quarter, the company had approximately $99.0 billion remaining under its share repurchase authorization.
*   Cash and cash equivalents increased to $22.4 billion from $10.6 billion at the beginning of the fiscal year, while total assets grew to $320.3 billion.
*   Long-term debt increased to $32.4 billion from $7.5 billion at the start of the fiscal year, following proceeds related to the issuance of debt.

## Watch items
