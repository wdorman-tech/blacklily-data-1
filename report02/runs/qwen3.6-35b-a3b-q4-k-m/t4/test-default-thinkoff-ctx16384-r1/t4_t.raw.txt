**AT&T Reports Strong Q2 2026 Results with Record Subscriber Adds and Reiterated Full-Year Guidance**

## Results

*   Consolidated revenues for the second quarter totaled $31.6 billion, an increase of 2.3% from the year-ago quarter, driven by growth in Advanced Connectivity fiber and wireless revenues.
*   Diluted earnings per share from continuing operations were $0.66, compared to $0.62 in the prior year period; adjusted diluted EPS was $0.65, versus $0.54 in the year-ago quarter.
*   Operating income was $7.0 billion, while adjusted operating income was $7.5 billion, both up from $6.5 billion in the second quarter of 2025.
*   Adjusted EBITDA reached $12.3 billion, representing a 5.2% increase year over year, with free cash flow of $4.7 billion compared to $4.4 billion in the prior year period.

## Segments and drivers

*   Advanced Connectivity service revenue grew 5.1% year over year to $23.5 billion, supported by a record quarter for combined fiber and fixed wireless net adds totaling 646,000.
*   The company added more than 1 million Advanced Connectivity customers, including 432,000 postpaid phone net adds with a churn rate of 0.86%, while total fiber locations reached 38.6 million.
*   Legacy segment revenues declined 25.9% to $1.6 billion as the company continues decommissioning its copper-based network, though operating expenses in this segment decreased by 10.8%.

## Outlook

*   The company reiterates its full-year 2026 adjusted EPS guidance of $2.25 to $2.35 and expects free cash flow of $18 billion or more for the year.
*   Management maintains its long-term outlook for service revenue growth in the low-single-digit range annually through 2028, with Advanced Connectivity service revenue expected to grow in the mid-single-digit range.
*   AT&T plans to return approximately $45 billion to shareholders during 2026-2028 through dividends and share repurchases, including accelerating share repurchases to approximately $10 billion in 2026.

## Capital and balance sheet

*   Total debt stood at $144.0 billion at the end of the second quarter, resulting in net debt of $126.4 billion after accounting for cash and time deposits.
*   Capital investment totaled $6.1 billion in the quarter, while capital expenditures related to continuing operations were $5.7 billion, with plans to maintain annual capital investment in the $23 billion to $24 billion range through 2028.
*   The company returned $4.1 billion to shareholders during the quarter, including approximately $2.2 billion in common share repurchases under its existing authorization.

## Watch items

*   The release notes that legacy EBITDA is expected to turn negative after 2027 until AT&T substantially eliminates direct costs associated with operating its copper-based network; how quickly regulatory approvals for decommissioning are obtained could impact this timeline.
*   Operating expenses increased due to an asset abandonment charge related to spectrum strategy reprioritization and higher bad debt expenses driven by subscriber growth; the committee should monitor whether these one-time charges recur or if bad debt trends persist with continued subscriber expansion.
