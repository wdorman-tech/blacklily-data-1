**Visa Reports Fiscal Third Quarter 2026 Net Revenue of $11.6 Billion, Up 14% Year-Over-Year**

## Results

*   GAAP net income for the fiscal third quarter ended June 30, 2026, was $5.6 billion, or $2.97 per share, representing a 7% and 10% increase over the prior year period, respectively.
*   Non-GAAP net income for the quarter was $6.3 billion, or $3.32 per share, reflecting an 8% and 11% increase over the prior year’s results on a reported and constant-dollar basis, respectively.
*   Net revenue totaled $11.6 billion, an increase of 14% year-over-year, or 13% on a constant-dollar basis, driven by growth in payments volume, cross-border volume, and processed transactions.
*   GAAP operating expenses were $4.8 billion, a 19% increase over the prior year, primarily due to higher personnel expenses; non-GAAP operating expenses increased 17% year-over-year.

## Segments and drivers

*   Payments volume for the three months ended June 30, 2026, increased 10% on a constant-dollar basis over the prior year, while total processed transactions reached 71.7 billion, also up 10%.
*   Cross-border volume excluding intra-Europe transactions increased 12% on a constant-dollar basis, and total cross-border volume increased 13% on a constant-dollar basis for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.
*   Data processing revenue rose 17% to $6.0 billion, international transaction revenue grew 6% to $3.9 billion, and other revenue surged 45% to $1.5 billion.

## Outlook

The release does not provide specific financial guidance or quantitative outlook for future periods. Management commentary indicates that consumer and business spending remains resilient and the company is positioning itself to capture growth opportunities across consumer payments, commercial solutions, and value-added services.

## Capital and balance sheet

*   Cash, cash equivalents, and investment securities stood at $13.9 billion as of June 30, 2026.
*   The company repurchased approximately 14.5 million shares of class A common stock for $4.9 billion during the quarter, leaving $28.4 billion in remaining authorized funds for share repurchases.
*   Total cash, cash equivalents, restricted cash, and restricted cash equivalents decreased to $18.8 billion from $25.0 billion at the beginning of the nine-month period, largely due to financing activities including $16.4 billion in stock repurchases and $3.9 billion in dividends paid.

## Watch items

*   GAAP operating expenses increased 19% year-over-year, significantly outpacing revenue growth; the committee should monitor whether this expense trajectory is sustainable as personnel and marketing costs continue to rise.
*   The company recorded a $250 million deposit into its litigation escrow account in June 2026; investors should track future developments in the interchange multidistrict litigation case and any potential impacts on accrued liabilities or cash flows.
