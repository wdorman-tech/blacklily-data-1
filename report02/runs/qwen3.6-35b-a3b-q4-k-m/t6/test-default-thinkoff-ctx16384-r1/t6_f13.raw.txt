Our goal has always been to protect capital during downturns while participating in upside potential, and the hedging mechanisms we employ are designed specifically for this purpose.

To manage risk more effectively, we have adjusted the options structure within your account. We rolled the put spread to a September expiry with long strikes at 95% and short strikes at 80% of the index level. To help fund this wider protection, we raised the short call strike to 104% of the index level, up from the previous 106%. This adjustment reflects our ongoing effort to balance cost efficiency with robust downside protection. As a result of these hedges, your net equity exposure at the end of June was 71%, down from 78% at the end of March. This lower exposure helps cushion the portfolio against further volatility in the equity markets.
