**Apple reports record June quarter revenue of $109.4 billion and diluted EPS of $2.02, driven by double-digit growth in iPhone, Mac, and Services.**

## Results
*   Total net sales for the fiscal 2026 third quarter ended June 27, 2026, were $109.4 billion, up 16 percent year over year.
*   Diluted earnings per share was $2.02, up 29 percent year over year; the release notes this figure included a favorable impact of $0.11 from tariff refunds.
*   Company gross margin was 50.1 percent, including a favorable impact of approximately 2 percentage points from tariff refunds.
*   Net income for the quarter was $29.8 billion, compared to $23.4 billion in the prior year period.
*   The board declared a cash dividend of $0.27 per share, payable on August 13, 2026.

## Segments and drivers
*   iPhone revenue reached $54.3 billion, Mac revenue was $10.4 billion, and Services revenue was $30.7 billion, all setting new June quarter records.
*   Revenue grew in every geographic segment, with the Americas generating $45.8 billion and Europe generating $29.4 billion.
*   Greater China revenue was $18.8 billion, up from $15.4 billion in the prior year quarter.
*   The company stated that its installed base of active devices reached a new all-time high across all major product categories and geographic segments.

## Outlook
*   The release does not provide specific financial guidance or outlook figures for the next quarter.

*   The company notes it assumes no obligation to update forward-looking statements, which speak only as of the date they are made.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   Cash and cash equivalents stood at $39.5 billion, while marketable securities totaled $107.0 billion ($22.9 billion current and $84.1 billion non-current).
*   Total shareholders’ equity increased to $107.5 billion from $73.7 billion at the start of the fiscal year.
*   For the nine months ended June 27, 2026, the company reported $62.1 billion in repurchases of common stock and $11.8 billion in payments for dividends and dividend equivalents.

## Watch items
*   Monitor the sustainability of gross margin, as the 50.1 percent figure includes a favorable impact of approximately 2 percentage points from tariff refunds.
*   Assess the impact of the $0.11 per share favorable impact from tariff refunds on diluted EPS, which was $2.02 for the quarter.
*   Review the increase in inventories to $11.1 billion from $5.7 billion at the start of the fiscal year, alongside the $5.5 billion cash outflow for inventory changes in the nine-month period.
