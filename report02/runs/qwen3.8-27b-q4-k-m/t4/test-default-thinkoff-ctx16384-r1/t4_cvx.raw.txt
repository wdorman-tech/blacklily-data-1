**Chevron reports Q2 2026 reported earnings of $12.1 billion and record U.S. production, driven by higher commodity prices and Hess integration synergies.**

## Results
*   Reported earnings for the second quarter of 2026 were $12.1 billion, or $6.11 per diluted share. Adjusted earnings (non-GAAP) for the same period were $12.0 billion, or $6.06 per diluted share.
*   Return on capital employed (ROCE) for the second quarter of 2026 was 21.4 percent, compared to 6.2 percent in the second quarter of 2025.
*   Cash flow from operations (CFFO) for the second quarter of 2026 was $22.6 billion. Free cash flow (non-GAAP) for the quarter was $18.1 billion.
*   The company achieved its structural cost reduction target six months early, capturing $3 billion in annual run-rate savings.
*   Chevron delivered $1.5 billion of annual run-rate synergies related to the Hess Corporation acquisition within one year of closing, exceeding the initial target by 50 percent.

## Segments and drivers
*   Upstream earnings for the second quarter of 2026 were $8.2 billion. U.S. upstream earnings were $3.5 billion, driven by higher liquids realizations and sales volumes. International upstream earnings were $4.6 billion, aided by higher sales volumes and liquids realizations.
*   Net oil-equivalent production for the second quarter of 2026 was 4,070 MBOED, a 20 percent increase from the second quarter of 2025. This increase was largely due to legacy Hess assets and growth in the Permian Basin and Gulf of America.
*   Downstream earnings for the second quarter of 2026 were $4.9 billion. U.S. downstream earnings were $2.4 billion, supported by higher margins on refined product sales. International downstream earnings were $2.5 billion, benefiting from higher margins and favorable timing effects.
*   U.S. refinery crude unit throughput was a record 1.07 million barrels per day, with crude unit utilization at more than 97 percent.

## Outlook
*   The release does not provide specific forward-looking financial guidance or outlook figures for future quarters.
*   Chevron signed a 20-year power purchase agreement with Microsoft to provide 2.67 gigawatts of behind-the-meter power for a West Texas data center.
*   The company aims to reduce structural costs by $3-4 billion by the end of 2026, having already achieved $3 billion in annual run-rate savings.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   Total debt was reduced by a record $8.4 billion in the second quarter of 2026.
*   The debt-to-CFFO ratio for the second quarter of 2026 was 0.8x. The net debt-to-CFFO ratio (non-GAAP) was 0.6x.
*   The Board of Directors declared a quarterly dividend of $1.78 per share, payable September 10, 2026.

## Watch items
*   Monitor the impact of the Middle East conflict on international operations, as the release notes curtailments in the Partitioned Zone and supply disruptions affecting international downstream inputs.
*   Assess the sustainability of the $3 billion in structural cost savings, as the program aims for a total of $3-4 billion by the end of 2026.
*   Track the progress of the Singapore downstream asset sale, which is expected to close in 2027, and the potential participation in West Qurna 2 and Nasiriyah oilfield developments in Iraq.
