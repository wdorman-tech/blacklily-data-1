**Alphabet Q2 2026 revenues grew 24% to $119.8 billion, driven by an 82% surge in Google Cloud and a $98.0 billion net gain on equity securities.**

## Results
*   Consolidated revenues increased 24% year-over-year to $119.8 billion; the release notes this is the 12th consecutive quarter of double-digit revenue growth.
*   Non-GAAP constant currency revenues increased 23% year-over-year, excluding the effect of foreign exchange rate movements and hedging activities.
*   Operating income increased 30% to $40.8 billion, with operating margin expanding by 2 percentage points to 34%.
*   Net income available to common stockholders increased 298% to $112.1 billion, and diluted EPS increased 294% to $9.11.
*   Other income reflected a net gain of $98.0 billion, primarily resulting from net unrealized gains on equity securities.

## Segments and drivers
*   Google Services revenues increased 15% to $94.5 billion, led by 17% growth in Google Search & other and 15% in Google subscriptions, platforms, and devices.
*   Google Cloud revenues increased 82% to $24.8 billion, driven by growth in Google Cloud Platform (GCP) across enterprise AI Solutions and enterprise AI Infrastructure.
*   Google Cloud operating income increased to $8.8 billion from $2.8 billion in the prior year period.
*   Alphabet-level activities, which primarily reflect expenses related to shared AI research and development, resulted in a loss of $5.8 billion.

## Outlook
*   The release does not provide specific financial guidance or outlook figures for future periods.
*   Management stated that AI investments are redefining what is possible across the business, citing wide adoption of Gemini Enterprise by nearly 90% of the Fortune 100.
*   The company noted that Gemini models process 22 billion API tokens per minute and the Gemini App has 950 million monthly active users.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   In June 2026, the company issued Class A and Class C stock and mandatory convertible preferred stock for aggregate net proceeds of $49.6 billion for general corporate purposes, including AI infrastructure capital expenditures.
*   The company issued senior unsecured notes for net proceeds of $20.3 billion in the second quarter of 2026.
*   Total cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities increased to $242.5 billion as of June 30, 2026, from $126.8 billion as of December 31, 2025.

## Watch items
*   Monitor the impact of the $98.0 billion net gain on equity securities, which the release notes could significantly contribute to the volatility of Other Income (Expense) in future periods.
*   Assess the execution of the $49.6 billion equity raise and $20.3 billion debt issuance, specifically regarding the stated use for scaling AI infrastructure and global compute.
*   Track the utilization of the At-the-Market Program, under which the company may sell up to $40.0 billion of Class A and Class C stock, as no shares had been sold as of June 30, 2026.
