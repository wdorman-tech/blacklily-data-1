**The Home Depot reported Q2 FY2026 sales of $47.9 billion and reaffirmed full-year guidance, citing broad-based demand and smaller project engagement.**

## Results
*   Net sales for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 were $47.9 billion, an increase of 5.7% from the second quarter of fiscal 2025.
*   Net earnings for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 were $4.8 billion, or $4.79 per diluted share, compared with $4.6 billion, or $4.58 per diluted share, in the same period of fiscal 2025.
*   Adjusted diluted earnings per share (non-GAAP) for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 were $4.92, compared with $4.68 in the same period of fiscal 2025.
*   Comparable sales for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 increased 1.7%, with U.S. comparable sales increasing 1.3%.
*   Six-month net sales for the period ended August 2, 2026, were $89.6 billion, an increase of 5.3% from the prior year period.

## Segments and drivers
*   The company attributes results to investments across the business and associate focus on customer service, noting customers continued to engage in smaller projects.
*   Comparable customer transactions decreased 1.0% in the second quarter of fiscal 2026, while comparable average ticket increased 2.8%.
*   Customer transactions and average ticket measures do not include results from HD Supply or SRS.
*   The company operated 2,364 retail stores and over 1,340 SRS locations at the end of the second quarter.

## Outlook
*   The company reaffirms its fiscal 2026 guidance, which includes IEEPA tariff refunds expected to partially offset unplanned fuel, energy, and other product input costs.
*   Guidance projects total sales growth of approximately 2.5% to 4.5% and comparable sales growth of approximately flat to 2.0% for fiscal 2026.
*   Diluted earnings-per-share is guided to grow approximately flat to 4.0% from $14.23 in fiscal 2025, while adjusted diluted earnings-per-share (non-GAAP) is guided to grow approximately flat to 4.0% from $14.69 in fiscal 2025.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   Cash and cash equivalents were $2.1 billion at the end of the second quarter of fiscal 2026, compared with $2.8 billion at the end of the second quarter of fiscal 2025.
*   Net cash provided by operating activities for the six months ended August 2, 2026, was $11.4 billion, compared with $9.0 billion in the prior year period.
*   Capital expenditures for the six months ended August 2, 2026, were $1.7 billion, consistent with the prior year period.

## Watch items
*   Monitor the impact of IEEPA tariff refunds on offsetting unplanned fuel, energy, and product input costs throughout fiscal 2026.
*   Assess the divergence between declining comparable customer transactions (-1.0%) and rising average ticket (+2.8%) in the second quarter of fiscal 2026.
*   Track the integration of SRS Distribution, Inc., which contributed $125 million to acquired intangible asset amortization in the second quarter of fiscal 2026.
