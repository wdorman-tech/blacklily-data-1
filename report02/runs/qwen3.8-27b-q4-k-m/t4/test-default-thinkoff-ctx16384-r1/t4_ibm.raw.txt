**IBM reports Q2 2026 GAAP revenue of $17.2 billion and raises full-year constant currency revenue growth guidance to 4–5%.**

## Results
*   GAAP revenue from continuing operations was $17.2 billion, up 1% year-over-year; GAAP net income was $2.2 billion, down 1% year-over-year.
*   Operating (non-GAAP) pre-tax income was $3.3 billion, up 3% year-over-year; Operating (non-GAAP) diluted EPS was $2.93, up 5% year-over-year.
*   GAAP gross profit margin was 57.7%, down 1.0 point year-over-year; Operating (non-GAAP) gross profit margin was 59.4%, down 0.7 points year-over-year.
*   The company generated $2.6 billion in net cash from operating activities, up $0.9 billion year-over-year.

## Segments and drivers
*   Software revenue was $7.8 billion, up 5% year-over-year, driven by 11% growth in Hybrid Cloud (Red Hat) and 19% growth in Data.
*   Infrastructure revenue was $3.8 billion, down 7% year-over-year, with Distributed Infrastructure up 37% and IBM Z down 42%.
*   Consulting revenue was $5.3 billion, flat year-over-year, but up 1% at constant currency.
*   Power and Storage within Distributed Infrastructure grew at a record pace, building an order backlog of nearly $500 million.

## Outlook
*   The company now expects full-year 2026 constant currency revenue growth in the range of 4–5%.
*   Full-year 2026 free cash flow is expected to increase by about $1 billion year-over-year.
*   The company expects improved pre-tax income margin expansion for the full year.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   IBM ended Q2 2026 with $8.2 billion in cash, restricted cash, and marketable securities, down $6.3 billion from year-end 2025.
*   Total debt, including IBM Financing debt of $13.0 billion, was $62.0 billion, up $0.7 billion year-to-date.
*   The company returned $1.6 billion to shareholders in dividends during the second quarter.

## Watch items
*   Monitor the impact of the $10.5 billion invested in acquisitions this year on integration costs and debt levels.
*   Assess the sustainability of the 37% growth in Distributed Infrastructure against the 42% decline in IBM Z.
*   Evaluate how the planned $10 billion investment in quantum computing over the next five years affects capital allocation priorities.
