**Johnson & Johnson reports Q2 2026 reported sales of $25.3 billion and raises full-year 2026 guidance.**

## Results
*   Q2 2026 reported sales were $25.3 billion, representing a 6.6% increase year-over-year.
*   Q2 2026 reported diluted EPS was $2.27, a 0.9% decrease from the prior year.
*   Non-GAAP adjusted EPS for Q2 2026 was $2.90, up 4.7% from the prior year.
*   Non-GAAP free cash flow for Q2 2026 was approximately $8.7 billion, compared to $6.2 billion in the prior year.
*   The company increased its full-year 2026 estimated reported sales guidance to a midpoint of $101.1 billion, or 7.3% growth.

## Segments and drivers
*   Innovative Medicine reported Q2 2026 sales of $16.4 billion, with operational sales growth of 6.8% excluding translational currency.
*   Growth in Innovative Medicine was driven by DARZALEX, CARVYKTI, TECVAYLI, RYBREVANT/LAZCLUZE, TREMFYA, SPRAVATO, and CAPLYTA.
*   MedTech reported Q2 2026 sales of $8.9 billion, with operational sales growth of 3.6% excluding translational currency.
*   MedTech growth was driven by wound closure, biosurgery, electrophysiology, Shockwave, contact lenses, and trauma products.

## Outlook
*   Full-year 2026 adjusted EPS guidance was increased by $0.13 to a midpoint of $11.68, representing 8.2% growth.
*   Full-year 2026 adjusted operational EPS guidance was increased by $0.18 to a midpoint of $11.58, representing 7.3% growth.
*   The company stated it does not provide GAAP financial measures on a forward-looking basis due to uncertainties in legal proceedings and acquisition-related expenses.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   Non-GAAP free cash flow for the second quarter of 2026 was estimated at approximately $8.7 billion.
*   The company announced an investment of more than $1 billion to strengthen U.S. Vision manufacturing in Jacksonville, Florida.
*   The company announced the acquisition of Firefly Bio, Inc. to expand its oncology pipeline.

## Watch items
*   Monitor the impact of STELARA, which negatively impacted Innovative Medicine operational growth by approximately 760 basis points in Q2 2026.
*   Assess the execution of the planned separation of the Orthopaedics business, cited as a risk factor in the release.
*   Track the commercial uptake of newly approved products, including TREMFYA for psoriatic arthritis and CAPLYTA for schizophrenia relapse prevention.
