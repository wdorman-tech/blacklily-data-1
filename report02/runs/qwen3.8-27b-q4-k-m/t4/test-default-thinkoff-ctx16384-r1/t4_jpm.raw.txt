**JPMorgan Chase reports Q2 2026 net income of $21.2 billion, driven by record revenues across all business lines and significant one-time gains.**

## Results
*   Reported net income was $21.2 billion ($7.70 per share), while net income excluding significant items (non-GAAP) was $16.9 billion ($6.14 per share).
*   Reported net revenue was $57.3 billion; managed net revenue (non-GAAP) was $58.0 billion, up 27% year-over-year.
*   Noninterest expense was $27.3 billion, resulting in a reported overhead ratio of 48% and a managed overhead ratio (non-GAAP) of 47%.
*   Return on common equity (ROE) was 24%, and return on tangible common equity (ROTCE, non-GAAP) was 29%, or 23% excluding significant items.

## Segments and drivers
*   Commercial & Investment Bank (CIB) net income was $9.7 billion, up 46% year-over-year, with Markets revenue up 35% and Investment Banking fees up 30%.
*   Consumer & Community Banking (CCB) net income was $5.3 billion, up 3% year-over-year, driven by Card Services & Auto revenue growth of 12%.
*   Asset & Wealth Management (AWM) net income was $2.0 billion, up 33% year-over-year, with assets under management (AUM) reaching $5.1 trillion, up 18% year-over-year.
*   Corporate net income was $4.2 billion, up $2.5 billion year-over-year, primarily due to a $4.6 billion net gain related to Visa shares.

## Outlook
*   The release does not provide specific quantitative guidance or outlook figures for future periods.
*   Management noted that market sentiment remains constructive for continued activity, particularly in Investment Banking.
*   CEO Jamie Dimon cited risks including geopolitical tensions, sticky inflation, and elevated asset prices, stating the firm prepares for a wide range of scenarios.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   Basel III common equity Tier 1 (CET1) capital ratio was 14.1% (Standardized) and 14.2% (Advanced).
*   The firm distributed $4.0 billion in common dividends and $6.2 billion in common stock net repurchases during the quarter.
*   Book value per share was $133.01, up 9% year-over-year; tangible book value per share (non-GAAP) was $113.35, up 10% year-over-year.

## Watch items
*   Monitor the sustainability of CIB revenue growth, as Markets revenue increased 35% year-over-year, with Equity Markets up 86%, driven by elevated client activity.
*   Assess the impact of the $4.6 billion Visa gain and $1.0 billion equity investment gains on the baseline, as these significant items account for the difference between reported and non-GAAP net income.
*   Review credit costs, which were $2.5 billion, including a $149 million net reserve build primarily in Wholesale, against a backdrop of average loans up 10% year-over-year.
