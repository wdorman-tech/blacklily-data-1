**Meta Q2 2026 revenue grew 28% to $60.8 billion, but net income declined 14% to $15.8 billion due to a 55% surge in costs and expenses.**

## Results
*   Revenue for the three months ended June 30, 2026, was $60,801 million, an increase of 28% year-over-year. On a constant currency basis (non-GAAP), revenue would have increased by 27% year-over-year.
*   Net income was $15,848 million, a decrease of 14% year-over-year. Diluted earnings per share (EPS) was $6.18, down 13% from $7.14 in the prior year period.
*   Income from operations was $18,775 million, a decrease of 8% year-over-year. The operating margin was 31%, compared to 43% in the second quarter of 2025.
*   Total costs and expenses were $42,026 million, an increase of 55% year-over-year. This figure includes $2.40 billion in charges related to legal proceedings and $1.18 billion in severance expenses associated with the May 2026 headcount reduction.

## Segments and drivers
*   Family of Apps (FoA) revenue was $60,370 million, while Reality Labs (RL) revenue was $431 million for the quarter.
*   FoA income from operations was $23,394 million, down from $24,971 million in the prior year period. RL recorded an operating loss of $4,619 million, compared to a loss of $4,530 million in the prior year period.
*   Family daily active people (DAP) averaged 3.60 billion in June 2026, a 3% year-over-year increase. Ad impressions increased 14% year-over-year, and the average price per ad increased 12% year-over-year.

## Outlook
*   For the third quarter of 2026, total revenue is expected to be in the range of $61–64 billion. This guidance assumes foreign currency is an approximately 1% headwind to year-over-year total revenue growth.
*   Full year 2026 total expenses are now expected to be in the range of $165–169 billion. This range incorporates the $2.4 billion in legal charges recognized in the second quarter.
*   Full year 2026 capital expenditures, including principal payments on finance leases, are anticipated to be in the range of $130–145 billion, narrowed from the prior outlook of $125–145 billion.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities totaled $90.26 billion as of June 30, 2026. Long-term debt was $83.66 billion as of the same date.
*   Cash flow from operating activities was $31.86 billion for the quarter. Free cash flow (non-GAAP) was $784 million.
*   Dividend and dividend equivalent payments were $1.35 billion for the quarter.

## Watch items
*   The release notes that youth-related trials are scheduled for this year in the U.S., which may ultimately result in a material loss. The committee should monitor the status of these legal proceedings.
*   Headcount was 75,472 as of June 30, 2026, a 1% decrease year-over-year. The release states that approximately 8,000 employees impacted by the May 2026 reduction will no longer be reflected in headcount by the end of the third quarter of 2026.
*   The company expects its tax rate for the remaining quarters of 2026 to be between 15–17%, an increase from the prior outlook of 13–16%, absent changes to the tax landscape.
