**Morgan Stanley reports record Q2 2026 net revenues of $21.3 billion and diluted EPS of $3.46, driven by strong Institutional Securities performance.**

## Results
*   Net revenues for the second quarter ended June 30, 2026, were $21.3 billion, compared with $16.8 billion for the same period in 2025.
*   Net income applicable to Morgan Stanley was $5.6 billion, or $3.46 per diluted share, compared with $3.5 billion, or $2.13 per diluted share, a year ago.
*   Return on tangible common equity (non-GAAP) was 26.6% for the quarter, up from 18.2% in the prior year period.
*   The expense efficiency ratio was 65% for the first half of 2026, demonstrating operating leverage while the firm continued to invest in its businesses.

## Segments and drivers
*   Institutional Securities reported record net revenues of $11.0 billion, reflecting strong performance in Equity driven by robust client engagement and strength in Investment Banking.
*   Wealth Management delivered record net revenues of $8.9 billion, generating a pre-tax margin of 30.5%, with net new assets of $148 billion for the quarter.
*   Investment Management reported net revenues of $1.6 billion, primarily driven by asset management fees on higher average assets under management (AUM).

## Outlook
*   The release does not provide specific financial guidance or outlook figures for future periods.
*   Management stated the firm continues to accrete capital, providing incremental flexibility to invest in core businesses while generating strong returns for shareholders.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   The Standardized Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) capital ratio was 14.8% as of the press release date.
*   The firm repurchased $1.5 billion of outstanding common stock during the quarter as part of its Share Repurchase Program.
*   The Board of Directors reauthorized a multi-year common equity share repurchase program of up to $20 billion, beginning in the third quarter of 2026.

## Watch items
*   Monitor the impact of the March 2026 workforce management action, which resulted in $178 million in severance costs across all business segments.
*   Assess the sustainability of Wealth Management net new assets, noting that just over half of the $148 billion in Q2 2026 inflows were related to IPOs of certain clients in the Workplace channel.
*   Review the change in presentation for Wealth Management transactional revenues, which no longer excludes mark-to-market gains associated with deferred cash-based compensation (DCP) beginning in Q1 2026.
