**Microsoft reports Q4 FY26 revenue of $90.0 billion, up 18%, driven by 43% growth in Azure and other cloud services.**

## Results
*   Revenue for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, was $90.0 billion, an 18% increase year-over-year (17% in constant currency).
*   Operating income was $40.6 billion, up 18% year-over-year.
*   GAAP net income was $35.8 billion, up 31%; non-GAAP net income, which excludes the impact from investments in OpenAI, was $35.3 billion, up 22%.
*   GAAP diluted earnings per share (EPS) was $4.81, up 32%; non-GAAP diluted EPS was $4.74, up 23%.
*   The release notes that discrete items, including a $3.2 billion gain from an investment in Anthropic, resulted in a $0.27 benefit to diluted EPS compared to forward-looking guidance provided on April 29, 2026.

## Segments and drivers
*   Intelligent Cloud revenue was $39.3 billion, up 32% (31% in constant currency), driven by a 43% increase in Azure and other cloud services revenue.
*   Productivity and Business Processes revenue was $37.8 billion, up 14%, with Microsoft 365 Commercial cloud revenue up 14% on a reported basis.
*   More Personal Computing revenue was $12.9 billion, down 4% (5% in constant currency), with Windows OEM and Devices revenue down 7% and XBOX content and services revenue down 10%.
*   Microsoft Cloud revenue was $59.3 billion, up 27%, and commercial remaining performance obligation increased 84% to $678 billion.

## Outlook
*   The release states that Microsoft will provide forward-looking guidance in connection with this quarterly earnings announcement on its earnings conference call and webcast.
*   No specific numerical guidance figures are provided in the text of the release itself.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   Microsoft returned $10.2 billion to shareholders in the form of dividends and share repurchases in the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2026.
*   Total cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments were $76.8 billion as of June 30, 2026.
*   Net cash from operations was $55.4 billion for the quarter, while additions to property and equipment were $35.8 billion.

## Watch items
*   The release highlights a $3.2 billion gain from an investment in Anthropic and lower-than-expected expenses related to the Voluntary Retirement Program as discrete items impacting results; the committee should assess the sustainability of these one-time benefits.
*   More Personal Computing revenue decreased 4%, with specific declines in Windows OEM and Devices (-7%) and XBOX content and services (-10%); the committee should monitor the trajectory of these hardware and gaming segments.
*   The release notes that non-GAAP results exclude the impact from investments in OpenAI, which resulted in a $480 million increase to net income in the quarter; the committee should evaluate the ongoing volatility and materiality of these investment impacts on reported earnings.
