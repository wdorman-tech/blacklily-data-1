**NVIDIA reports Q2 FY27 revenue of $96.2 billion, up 106% year-over-year, with Data Center driving the majority of growth.**

## Results
*   Revenue for the second quarter ended July 26, 2026, was $96.2 billion, an increase of 18% from the previous quarter and 106% from the same period last year.
*   GAAP net income was $59.7 billion, while non-GAAP net income was $54.0 billion for the quarter.
*   GAAP diluted earnings per share were $2.46, compared to non-GAAP diluted earnings per share of $2.22.
*   Both GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins stood at 75.0% for the quarter.
*   Operating expenses were $8.4 billion on a GAAP basis and $8.2 billion on a non-GAAP basis.

## Segments and drivers
*   Data Center revenue reached $89.0 billion, representing a 117% increase from the prior year and an 18% increase from the previous quarter.
*   Edge Computing revenue was $7.2 billion, up 27% year-over-year and 13% quarter-over-quarter.
*   The release highlights the ramp of the Vera Rubin platform into full production, with racks running at partners including CoreWeave, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure.
*   NVIDIA announced strategic partnerships with Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs, and KKR to mobilize over $500 billion of third-party capital for AI infrastructure buildout.

## Outlook
*   For the third quarter of fiscal 2027, revenue is expected to be $108.0 billion, plus or minus 2%.
*   The company states it is not assuming any Data Center compute revenue from China in this outlook.
*   GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins are expected to be 74.0%, plus or minus 50 basis points.
*   GAAP operating expenses are expected to be approximately $9.2 billion, while non-GAAP operating expenses are expected to be approximately $9.0 billion.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   NVIDIA returned approximately $26.0 billion to shareholders in the second quarter through share repurchases and cash dividends.
*   As of the end of the second quarter, the company had approximately $99.0 billion remaining under its share repurchase authorization.
*   The next quarterly cash dividend of $0.25 per share is scheduled to be paid on October 1, 2026.

## Watch items
*   The release notes that non-GAAP financial measures no longer exclude stock-based compensation expense beginning in the first quarter of fiscal 2027, which alters the basis for adjusted figures.
*   The outlook explicitly excludes Data Center compute revenue from China, creating a specific variable to monitor for potential upside or downside relative to the $108.0 billion target.
*   The company expects GAAP and non-GAAP tax rates for full-year fiscal 2027 to be between 16.0% and 18.0%, excluding discrete items and material changes to the tax environment.
