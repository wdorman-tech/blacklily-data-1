**AT&T reports Q2 2026 revenue of $31.6 billion and adjusted EPS of $0.65, reiterating full-year guidance while accelerating share repurchases to approximately $10 billion.**

## Results
*   Revenues totaled $31.6 billion, up 2.3% from the year-ago quarter, driven by growth in Advanced Connectivity fiber and wireless revenues.
*   Diluted EPS from continuing operations was $0.66, compared to $0.62 in the year-ago quarter; adjusted EPS (non-GAAP) was $0.65, versus $0.54 in the prior year period.
*   Operating income was $7.0 billion, while adjusted operating income (non-GAAP) was $7.5 billion.
*   Income from continuing operations was $5.0 billion, up 3.6% year over year; adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP) was $12.3 billion, up 5.2% year over year.
*   Free cash flow (non-GAAP) was $4.7 billion, compared to $4.4 billion in the year-ago quarter.

## Segments and drivers
*   Advanced Connectivity service revenue grew 5.1% year over year to $23.5 billion, with operating income up 20.3% to $7.3 billion.
*   The segment added 646,000 total consumer and business internet net adds, comprising 367,000 fiber and 279,000 fixed wireless connections.
*   Postpaid phone net adds were 432,000, with postpaid phone churn of 0.86%.
*   Legacy segment revenues declined 25.9% year over year to $1.6 billion as the company continues to decommission its copper-based network.

## Outlook
*   AT&T reiterates its long-term outlook for 2026-2028, including adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP) growth of 3% to 4% in 2026, improving to 5% or better in 2028.
*   Adjusted EPS (non-GAAP) is guided to $2.25 to $2.35 in 2026, with a double-digit 3-year CAGR through 2028.
*   The company expects to return $45 billion or more to shareholders during 2026-2028 through dividends and share repurchases.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   Total debt was $144.0 billion at the end of the second quarter, resulting in net debt (non-GAAP) of $126.4 billion.
*   Capital expenditures related to continuing operations were $5.7 billion; capital investment (non-GAAP) was $6.1 billion.
*   The company returned $4.1 billion to shareholders in the quarter, including approximately $2.2 billion in common share repurchases.

## Watch items
*   Monitor the execution of the plan to sell a controlling interest in the subsidiary holding the acquired Lumen mass markets fiber business, which is currently classified as held-for-sale.
*   Assess the impact of the asset abandonment charge associated with the reprioritization of the spectrum strategy on future operating expenses.
*   Track the timeline for the net debt-to-adjusted EBITDA ratio (non-GAAP) to return to the target 2.5x range within approximately three years following the EchoStar transaction closing.
