**Visa reports fiscal Q3 2026 GAAP net income of $5.6 billion and non-GAAP net income of $6.3 billion, with net revenue up 14% year-over-year.**

## Results
*   GAAP net income was $5.6 billion, or $2.97 per share, representing a 7% and 10% increase, respectively, over the prior year.
*   Non-GAAP net income was $6.3 billion, or $3.32 per share, representing an 8% and 11% increase, respectively, over the prior year.
*   Net revenue increased 14% to $11.6 billion, or 13% on a constant-dollar basis.
*   GAAP operating expenses rose 19% to $4.8 billion, primarily driven by personnel expenses and special items.
*   Non-GAAP operating expenses increased 17% to $3.9 billion, driven by marketing and personnel expenses.

## Segments and drivers
*   Service revenue grew 14% to $4.9 billion, while data processing revenue rose 17% to $6.0 billion.
*   International transaction revenue increased 6% to $3.9 billion, and other revenue rose 45% to $1.5 billion.
*   Payments volume increased 10% on a constant-dollar basis for the three months ended June 30, 2026.
*   Cross-border volume excluding intra-Europe transactions grew 12% on a constant-dollar basis, while total cross-border volume increased 13%.

## Outlook
*   The release does not provide specific financial guidance or outlook figures for future periods.
*   Management commented that consumer and business spending remains resilient and that the strategy continues to deliver strong performance across consumer payments, commercial, and money movement solutions.
*   The company stated it is designing, building, and shipping products at an increased velocity to position itself to capture opportunities ahead.

## Capital and balance sheet
*   Visa repurchased approximately 14.5 million shares of class A common stock for $4.9 billion during the quarter, with $28.4 billion of remaining authorized funds for repurchases as of June 30, 2026.
*   The board declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.670 per share of class A common stock, payable on September 1, 2026.
*   Cash, cash equivalents, and investment securities totaled $13.9 billion at June 30, 2026.
*   Visa deposited $250 million into its litigation escrow account on June 25, 2026, which has the same economic effect on earnings per share as repurchasing class A common stock.

## Watch items
*   Monitor the impact of the $563 million in severance costs and $237 million litigation provision associated with the interchange multidistrict litigation case on future operating expenses.
*   Assess the sustainability of the 45% growth in other revenue and the 17% increase in data processing revenue relative to the 14% growth in net revenue.
*   Track the remaining $28.4 billion in authorized share repurchase funds and the pace of future buybacks given the $4.9 billion spent in the current quarter.
