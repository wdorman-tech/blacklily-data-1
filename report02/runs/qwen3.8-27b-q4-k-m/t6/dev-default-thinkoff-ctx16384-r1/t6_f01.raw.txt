For the quarter, your portfolio returned -1.42% net of fees. For context, the gross-of-fees return was -1.19%. This performance compares favorably to your policy blend benchmark, which returned -1.87% over the same period. Year to date, your portfolio is up 2.36% net of fees. During the quarter, we made several adjustments to manage risk and position the portfolio for the future. We used these gains to increase our international developed equity allocation from 13% to 17%. Additionally, we shortened the duration of our bond holdings from 6.0 years to 5.1 years to reduce sensitivity to interest rate changes. At the end of the quarter, we held 3.4% of the portfolio in cash.
