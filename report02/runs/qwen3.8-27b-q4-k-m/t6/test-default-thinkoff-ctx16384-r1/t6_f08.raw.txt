Dear Priya and Samuel,

I hope this letter finds you both well. I am writing to share the results for your ESG Core Equity mandate for the second quarter of 2026, which ended on June 30. As of that date, your household assets stood at $6,412,300.

During the quarter, your portfolio returned -3.36% net of fees. For context, the gross-of-fees return was -3.16%. The unscreened Developed Markets Core Equity Index returned -2.95% over the same period. While the quarter was challenging, your year-to-date return remains positive at 0.92% net of fees, and your trailing twelve-month return is 6.18% net of fees.

You asked in May whether our environmental screens cost money. I want to answer that directly: yes, they do. In Q2, our exclusion of fossil-fuel reserves cost the portfolio approximately 0.5% against the comparison index, primarily because oil-linked stocks rallied in May. However, this trade-off reflects your values. Our portfolio’s weighted average carbon intensity is 58% below that of the comparison index. Additionally, during proxy season, we voted against 11 director nominees due to weak climate disclosure, ensuring your capital supports companies with stronger governance standards.

I am also pleased to inform you of a new fee breakpoint that benefits your household. Effective July 1, 2026, we are introducing a reduced rate of 0.60% on household assets above $5,000,000. This is a new tier, not a change to your existing rates. Your resulting fee schedule will be 0.90% on the first $2,000,000, 0.75% on assets from $2,000,000 to $5,000,000, and 0.60% on assets above $5,000,000. This new rate will first be applied to your third-quarter invoice, which is billed in arrears.

Applying environmental, social and governance criteria may cause the portfolio to forgo opportunities available to strategies that do not use such criteria, and its returns may differ, sometimes significantly, from those of the broader market.

Thank you for your continued trust. Please let me know if you have any questions about these results or the fee structure.
