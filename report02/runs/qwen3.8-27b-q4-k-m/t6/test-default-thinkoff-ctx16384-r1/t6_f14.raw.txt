Dear Mrs. Delacourt,

I am writing to share the results for the Delacourt-Ambrose Charitable Remainder Unitrust for the first quarter of 2026. It has been a privilege to manage this trust for you and Mr. Ambrose since 2017, and I appreciate your continued confidence in our firm.

For the period ending March 31, 2026, the trust achieved a return of 2.2% net of fees. For context, the gross return before fees was 2.4%. This performance compares favorably to the 1.8% return of the benchmark index, which reflects our 55% global equity and 45% fixed income allocation. Since the trust’s inception in October 2017, the annualized return net of fees has been 6.8%.

During the quarter, we made several strategic adjustments to protect the trust’s income stream. We raised our allocation to cash and Treasury bills to 6.5% of trust assets. This move allowed us to pre-fund the upcoming June and September unitrust payments without needing to sell risk assets. Additionally, we reduced our exposure to high-yield bonds from 7.5% to 3.5%, as credit spreads have narrowed significantly.

As scheduled, the first unitrust payment of $31,250 was made on March 31. This amount represents 5% of the trust’s January 1 valuation of $2,500,000, paid in four equal installments.

I also want to inform you of a change to our fee structure. Effective June 1, 2026, the advisory fee on the first $3,000,000 of trust assets will decrease from 1.00% to 0.90% per year. This adjustment aligns the trust with our standard individual client schedule. The fee for assets above $3,000,000 remains unchanged at 0.70% per year. This new rate will be prorated on your second-quarter invoice.

Unitrust payments are set by the trust instrument as a fixed percentage of the trust's net assets as revalued each year, will rise or fall with the value of the trust, and are not a measure of investment performance.

Please let me know if you have any questions regarding these results or the fee change. I look forward to continuing to serve you and Mr. Ambrose.
