Dear Reiko,

I hope this letter finds you well. I am writing to share the results for your Preferred and Hybrid Income sleeve for the second quarter of 2026, covering the period from April 1 through June 30.

During this quarter, the sleeve returned 1.6% net of fees. For context, the gross return before fees was 1.8%. This performance compares favorably to the US preferred securities index, which returned 1.1% over the same period. Since the inception of this strategy in August 2022, the annualized return has been 5.2% net of fees. Year-to-date through June 30, the sleeve has grown by 3.9% net of fees.

We made several adjustments to the portfolio to better align with your income needs. As of June 30, the sleeve’s current yield stands at 6.4%. We also reduced the portfolio’s sensitivity to interest rate changes, cutting the duration to worst from 5.0 years to 4.2 years. Additionally, we sold two fixed-to-floating bank preferred securities that had their first call dates in November 2026. We reinvested the $186,000 in proceeds into $25-par utility preferreds. This move helps manage the risk that issuers may redeem these securities before maturity, a feature known as call risk.

I am also pleased to inform you of a fee reduction for this specific sleeve. Effective July 1, 2026, the advisory fee will decrease from 0.70% per year to 0.55% per year. This change reflects our move to a lower-cost fixed income trading platform. Please note that this fee adjustment applies only to the Preferred and Hybrid Income sleeve; the fees for the balanced portion of your IRA remain unchanged. You will see this new rate reflected on your Q3 invoice.

We remain committed to providing steady income for your monthly expenses while managing risk. Please let me know if you have any questions about these updates or the portfolio’s performance.

Sincerely,
