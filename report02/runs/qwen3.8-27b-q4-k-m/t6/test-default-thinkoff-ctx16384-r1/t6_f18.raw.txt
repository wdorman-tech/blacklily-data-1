Dear Adaora and Nils,

I hope this letter finds you well. I am writing to share the performance and recent updates for Mateo’s North Carolina UTMA custodial account for the second quarter of 2026.

For the period from April 1 through June 30, the account returned 3.7% net of fees. For context, the gross return before fees was 3.9%. This result compares to the 4.1% return of our age-based policy blend benchmark. Year-to-date through June 30, the account is up 5.3% net of fees. Since the account’s inception in September 2019, it has achieved an annualized return of 8.2% net of fees.

As we approach Mateo’s college years, we executed our annual glide path step on June 1. This adjustment reduced the equity target from 75% to 70%. I understand you asked in May whether 70% equities is too high with five years remaining until college. The glide path is designed to gradually shift assets toward stability as the target date approaches. By lowering the equity allocation now, we are reducing volatility while maintaining growth potential, which aligns with our moderate risk track. To support this shift, we moved $14,000 from equities into Treasuries maturing in 2031. These funds are earmarked for first-year college costs. Following this rebalance, the account holds 2.5% in cash.

Additionally, I am pleased to confirm a change to your advisory fee structure. In May, you requested that we aggregate Mateo’s UTMA account with your personal accounts for breakpoint purposes. This request was approved in June. Effective October 1, 2026, the advisory fee for the UTMA account will decrease from 1.00% per year to 0.80% per year. This new household rate will apply starting with the fourth-quarter invoice.

Please remember that assets in a custodial account under the Uniform Transfers to Minors Act are the irrevocable property of the minor, pass to the minor's control at the age set by state law, and may reduce eligibility for need-based financial aid.

Thank you for your continued trust. Please let me know if you have any questions about these updates.
