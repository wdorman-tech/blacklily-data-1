Dear Dr. Harlan-Osei and the Board of the Harlan-Osei Family Foundation,

I am writing to provide an update on the performance of your Endowment Model portfolio for the first quarter of 2026, covering the period from January 1 through March 31.

During this quarter, the portfolio returned -3.1% net of fees. For context, the gross-of-fees return was -2.9%. This result compares favorably to the policy portfolio benchmark, which returned -3.5% over the same period. Year-to-date through March 31, the net return remains at -3.1%. Looking at the longer term, the trailing five-year annualized return, net of fees, stands at 6.6%.

Several key actions were taken during the quarter to manage liquidity and risk. We paid $212,500 in grants from the short-term reserve, which allowed us to avoid selling any equity positions during the market decline in March. This strategy keeps us on pace to meet the 5% minimum payout requirement. Additionally, we added U.S. Treasuries during the recent yield spike, bringing the fixed income allocation to 27.5% of assets, slightly above the 25% target. Following the market decline, global equities now represent 57.8% of the portfolio. This remains within the acceptable five-point band around our 60% target, so no rebalancing was triggered.

I also wish to inform you of a change to our fee structure. Effective May 1, 2026, the advisory rate for foundation assets above $10,000,000 will decrease from 0.60% per year to 0.45% per year. The rate for the first $10,000,000 remains unchanged at 0.75% per year. This adjustment reflects a reset of the foundation and endowment schedule following our annual fee review. The new rate will be prorated on your second-quarter invoice.
